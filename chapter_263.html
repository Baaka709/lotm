Chapter 263

Chapter 263 - Spiritual Perception and Attempt

746 words · 5 min read

Under the dim crimson moon, Klein rose from bed and immediately treated the dream seriously. A Seer could not simply dismiss a dream involving the bloody door from the underground structure.

“I dreamed of the blood-stained door in that room.”

Miss Bodyguard appeared in the mirror and calmly explained, “The influence of the evil spirit’s aura. It will gradually weaken until it disappears.”

Reassured, Klein returned to bed. This time, he slept without encountering the dream again.

The next morning, he enjoyed a simple breakfast while reading the newspapers, as was customary for gentlemen of the Northern Continent. One report announced that the Civil Servant Unified Examination Bill had passed. Klein immediately thought of Benson, who had been preparing for the examination long before others. The schedule was rushed, suggesting the King and Prime Minister were eager to implement the system, but that worked in Benson’s favor.

Klein quietly wished his brother success, though he refrained from his usual prayer because Miss Bodyguard was nearby and he had claimed to be a believer of the God of Steam and Machinery.

The newspapers also reported a new National Atmospheric Pollution Council and efforts to regulate pollution from chemical factories. Klein noticed that an advertisement connected to the Ernst Firm was gone and concluded there would be no reason to attend the next Beyonder gathering.

Meanwhile, in Tingen, Benson and Melissa were also reading about the examination bill.

“Yes,” Benson confirmed when Melissa asked whether it had passed.

But Benson quickly turned her attention toward another piece of news: the Backlund Polytechnic would be restructured into a university and begin accepting students the following year. It would emphasize technical subjects instead of grammar and classical literature, have relatively cheap tuition, and offer scholarships.

“Melissa, I think you can try.”

She hesitated, but Benson encouraged her. He believed her interest in machinery and steam made the university a perfect opportunity. He also assured her that their finances were secure enough that they did not need to let money determine their future.

“I’m planning on resigning tomorrow and will focus on preparing for the exam,” Benson told her. “Most of the positions are said to be in Backlund. That’s my goal. I hope we can go there together.”

Melissa eventually nodded.

After she left for the bathroom, Benson’s cheerful expression disappeared. He quietly took out his silver pocket watch and stared at it with a sigh.

---

Klein spent the rest of Sunday searching Backlund’s public libraries for information about Viscount Pound. The family had left surprisingly little historical material, and after six exhausting hours he found nothing useful.

He decided he needed either a noble-history expert or access to police records that might reveal the current address of Baronet Pound.

When he returned home, Miss Bodyguard appeared in the mirror.

“The employment period is over.”

Klein acknowledged that their three days together had ended. He then asked whether she would be interested in clues concerning the Pound family.

She nodded.

“Through Maric?” Klein asked.

She nodded again, bowed elegantly, and disappeared.

Later that night, Klein touched Rosago’s All-Black Eye. Horrific roars immediately flooded his mind, while black threads spread from his body. He released the artifact only after barely enduring its corrupting influence.

Then he performed his ritual and ascended to the gray fog carrying Azik’s copper whistle and the iron cigarette case.

Above the gray fog, Klein examined the All-Black Eye and discovered something unexpected: it had become completely quiet. Its corruption remained, but it was dormant.

“As expected, it’s impossible to separate them directly...”

He then summoned a new puppet.

A stiff, lifeless figure in a hooded robe appeared across the table. It was obviously not a real person and could never deceive the Tarot Club, but Klein had another idea.

Holding the All-Black Eye, he allowed his spirituality to touch the black threads connecting the artifact to the puppet.

The puppet moved.

It worked.

He could use the Nimblewright Master’s ability through the All-Black Eye to create a controllable false Tarot Club member. The technique consumed enormous spirituality and could not sustain a second puppet, but it was enough.

Klein practiced controlling the puppet’s hands, throat, and mouth until he could make it speak.

When he was satisfied, he smiled at the fake member.

“Welcome, new member. Which tarot card do you wish to draw?”

The puppet raised a hand to its chin and answered in a hoarse voice:

“World!

“I choose the card ‘The World.’”

Key Events

  • Klein learns that the dream of the bloody door is residual influence from the evil spirit.
  • Benson plans to resign and prepare for the new civil-service examination, while Melissa considers attending the new Backlund university.
  • Klein spends hours unsuccessfully searching for information about Viscount Pound.
  • Miss Bodyguard’s employment period ends, but she agrees to hear about Pound-family clues through Maric.
  • Klein discovers that Rosago’s All-Black Eye has become dormant rather than losing its corruption.
  • He successfully uses the All-Black Eye to control a fake body through the Nimblewright Master’s abilities.
  • Klein creates a new Tarot Club persona and names it The World.
