Chapter 257

Chapter 257 - 2-081

1,160 words · 7 min read

Klein struggled to maintain his composure after hearing the Apothecary explain his reason for choosing the pathway. Fortunately, his iron mask concealed his expression.

The Apothecary continued enthusiastically, explaining that he loved Backlund because of the sheer number of prostitutes around its music halls, theaters, streets, and alleys. He even looked forward to the possibility of encountering impoverished aristocratic women working in the poorer districts.

Klein quickly changed the subject.

He asked whether the Apothecary’s teacher had objected to his decision.

The Apothecary shook his head. His teacher had simply taught him everything he knew about herbs and then kicked him out the door, ordering him to travel the world and experience life. The teacher had not even told him where to obtain the ingredients for the next potion.

The Apothecary then asked Klein to help locate Spring of the Elves marrow crystals. If Klein found them, he could sell them to him for a profit. Since Klein was apparently so lucky, perhaps he could finally help the Apothecary obtain what he needed.

Klein agreed, then asked whether he could exchange the material for the Apothecary potion formula.

The Apothecary immediately refused.

He could only teach the formula to his own students.

Klein suspected this was an actual rule of the Life School of Thought, perhaps supported by some oath or mystical agreement.

However, the Apothecary offered another piece of information: the Apothecary formula itself could be obtained in Backlund.

Klein was delighted.

Before Eye of Wisdom could dismiss them, Klein asked whether the Apothecary possessed medication for mental disorders and related problems. He was primarily asking for Miss Bodyguard’s sake, wanting to find something that might reassure her after the influence of the True Creator had left her in a potentially unstable state.

The Apothecary said that he had a sedative for sale at ten soli each. He would bring four to the next gathering and insisted that they were much more effective than hospital sedatives because they contained additional spiritual materials.

Klein was surprised by the price, since ordinary hospital sedatives and those circulating from official Beyonder organizations were much cheaper.

He then asked Eye of Wisdom whether he could attend another gathering.

Eye of Wisdom agreed and gave him the method for finding future meetings. Klein needed to watch the fifth page of the *Backlund Morning Post* for advertisements placed by the Ernst Firm. When such an advertisement appeared, it meant a gathering would take place at eight the next evening.

The exact knocking rhythm was encoded into the advertisement’s numbers: heavy strikes, light strikes, long pauses, and short pauses each corresponded to one part of the code.

Eye of Wisdom then explained why he organized these underground gatherings.

Independent Beyonders lived difficult lives. They had to remain secretive, avoid official organizations, and constantly fear losing control or becoming insane. Even those who survived those dangers still faced the problem of finding potion ingredients, mystical objects, and reliable knowledge.

The gathering existed so they could help one another.

Unfortunately, the era had very little accessible mysticism, and many Beyonder ingredients were extremely rare.

Klein silently thought that his own situation was much better. He had The Hanged Man, who knew the locations of primitive islands, and The Sun, who lived in a place where powerful monsters could provide useful ingredients.

Klein thanked Eye of Wisdom respectfully.

Then he remembered another matter.

He asked whether anyone possessed mystical items like the ominous object Black Snake had sold him, but without severe negative effects. He wanted something capable of protecting him.

His intention was to first identify something suitable and learn its price, then obtain the necessary money later.

The Apothecary said he had nothing like that and would not sell such an item even if he did.

Eye of Wisdom, however, admitted that he had several. He asked Klein to describe roughly what kind of item he wanted.

Klein requested something stronger than himself, preferably something with mystical abilities rather than simply improved combat power.

Eye of Wisdom then mentioned an item formerly designated 2-081.

The number immediately shocked Klein.

It sounded exactly like the code of a leaked Sealed Artifact.

Eye of Wisdom explained that many mystical items with dangerous side effects were collected and sealed by the orthodox Churches. Their numbered designations reflected their danger grades: Grade 0 was the most dangerous, followed by Grades 1, 2, and 3.

Grade 0 artifacts were rumored to be capable of destroying entire countries or even the world.

The seven orthodox Churches exchanged information about Grade 0 and Grade 1 artifacts, so those designations were unique across the Churches. Grade 2 and Grade 3 numbers, however, could repeat because each Church assigned them separately.

Klein was surprised that even Grade 0 and Grade 1 information was shared, but Eye of Wisdom said this agreement had reportedly been established around the end of the Fourth Epoch or beginning of the Fifth.

His 2-081 had come from the Church of the God of Knowledge and Wisdom.

The artifact greatly enhanced understanding and comprehension. It could rapidly identify an enemy’s Beyonder powers, reveal their strengths and weaknesses, and even allow the user to imitate those abilities. The more powerful the opponent, the greater the chance of failure, and even successful imitation would differ significantly from the original.

Klein was impressed. Perhaps true knowledge really could approach omnipotence.

But the artifact had a frightening flaw.

It forced the user’s mind into a state of accelerated thought. If the effect continued beyond the safe limit, the user would become mentally impaired.

The Apothecary sarcastically warned Eye of Wisdom that his obsession with displaying his collection might eventually get him robbed and killed.

Eye of Wisdom merely laughed and suggested that perhaps he deliberately advertised his collection to tempt greedy people into attacking him—similar to Roselle’s idea of entrapment.

He then admitted that 2-081 was not for sale. It was his treasured possession, bought for 6,800 pounds. Comparable Grade 2 items might cost anywhere from 4,000 to 20,000 pounds, depending on their abilities and drawbacks, although many could not realistically be purchased with money at all.

Instead, Eye of Wisdom recommended two other items.

The first was a brooch capable of repelling and purifying spirits while granting access to several Sun-domain spells. Its flaw was that the wearer would never feel coolness and would constantly experience the warmth of a southern summer.

The second was a shape-changing hat. It allowed the wearer to move underwater for at least half an hour and use certain Water- or Wind-domain spells. Its drawback was an increasing thirst and desire for water, followed by gradual weakening after returning to land.

Similar items cost between 1,500 and 3,500 pounds, sometimes far more, and were extremely difficult to obtain.

Klein nodded and said he would consider the choices and decide at the next gathering.

Then he smiled deliberately and admitted that he did not have that much money at present.
