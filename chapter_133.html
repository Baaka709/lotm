Chapter 133

1,155 words · 7 min read

Elizabeth immediately became nervous after Klein confirmed that the strange dreams were connected to something supernatural.

“Can you divine the specific reason? Even better, can you divine a way to solve it?”

Klein inwardly noted that divination could usually provide only a broad direction, often wrapped in symbolism that was difficult to interpret correctly. Fortunately, he considered himself far more capable than an ordinary Seer.

“Since this concerns dreams, I suggest using a related form of divination.”

Elizabeth eagerly agreed.

Klein pointed toward the sofa. “I’ll need you to sleep here and let the dream appear. Is that a problem?”

“No, I trust you,” Elizabeth replied without hesitation, though she nervously added that she could not guarantee the dream would appear.

“It’s only an attempt,” Klein reassured her.

Instead of using the sofa, Elizabeth folded her arms on the table and lowered her head onto them, explaining that she slept like this at school whenever she was tired.

“You can pretend I’m not here,” Klein said with a smile.

The room gradually became silent. Once Klein confirmed through her aura that she had fallen asleep, he took out a semicircular silver charm covered with Hermes inscriptions, symbols, and numbers.

It was the Dream Charm he had successfully created the previous morning.

He had also completed two Slumber Charms and two Requiem Charms, intentionally making each type with a different shape so he could distinguish them by touch during battle.

“Crimson.”

The ancient Hermes incantation echoed softly through the room. The Dream Charm became almost weightless in Klein’s hand. After he injected his spirituality into it and placed it on the table, a transparent flame silently enveloped the silver and transformed into deep, serene black flames.

The darkness spread over both Klein and Elizabeth.

Klein entered Cogitation and extended his spirituality toward the illusory sphere of light representing Elizabeth’s dream. The surrounding darkness seemed endless. He quickly touched the sphere.

Reality twisted.

The peaceful room vanished, replaced by a yellow-brown plain covered with human and horse corpses, fresh blood, and discarded weapons.

Elizabeth stood there dressed in an elegant gown and fishnet hat. Upon seeing Klein, she immediately became excited.

“Mr. Moretti, we meet again! I suspected that the Klein Moretti on the divination club register was you when Selena and I first came. I came several more times but always missed you because of lessons... Then during summer break, my parents dragged me to Lamud Town... You can help me, right?”

Klein was briefly stunned by her chatter.

So she had actually suspected I worked here and had come looking for me?

Her excitement seemed completely genuine. That reminded him that dreams revealed people’s most honest sides—everyone’s except Mr. Fool’s.

Then the dream changed.

A nearly two-meter-tall knight in black armor approached, dragging a huge broadsword across the ground. Metal scraped against metal with every step. Two glowing red eyes stared from behind the helmet.

Klein instantly recognized what he was dealing with.

The will of a wraith, but not yet an evil spirit.

According to the Nighthawks’ classifications, ordinary vengeful spirits were the weakest, followed by shadows and wraiths. Evil spirits were much more dangerous, with the strongest said to possess power comparable to High-Sequence Beyonders.

Klein stepped forward, shielding Elizabeth.

Then he stomped his foot.

The dream shattered instantly.

Countless fragments of light scattered like fireflies before Klein’s spirituality returned to his body. He opened his eyes in the Red Agate Room and saw the Dream Charm almost completely burned away.

His heart ached.

Using these charms is practically burning money!

The pure silver required for charms belonging to the Evernight Goddess’s domain meant that, even without considering his labor, each charm consumed roughly six to eight soli worth of materials.

Klein felt a little better when he remembered that Beyonders of the Eternal Blazing Sun Church used gold instead.

Elizabeth slowly woke.

“Mr. Moretti, were there any results?”

“Yes,” Klein answered solemnly. “Your nightmares should disappear within a week.”

He had already decided to report the matter to the Captain and send someone to Lamud Town.

Elizabeth brightened immediately.

“Really? That’s great! Thank you!”

Then she suddenly remembered something and frowned.

“What’s wrong?” Klein asked.

“Nothing. I just remembered that I need to go home.”

She placed a prepared one-soli note on the table, picked up her hat, and left with a slightly hesitant goodbye.

Once outside and sure no one was watching, Elizabeth suddenly flung her arms around and groaned.

“Pins and needles! How numbing...”

---

At Blackthorn Security Company, Dunn rubbed his forehead as he looked at Klein.

“Did you suddenly return because you encountered another supernatural incident?”

Klein cleared his throat.

“Yes.”

“What happened this time?”

Klein carefully arranged his thoughts.

“There are two matters. First, I discovered Monster Ademisaul hiding in a corner of the underground market, trembling in fear while I was buying materials for my charms.”

He deliberately emphasized the connection to his expensive materials, hoping to hint at reimbursement.

Dunn did not seem to notice.

“What happened to Ademisaul?”

Klein explained that Ademisaul had dreamed of countless corpses and blood, including his own body, and had consequently become terrified.

Dunn considered it.

“As a Seer, what do you think that symbolizes?”

“A disaster,” Klein answered cautiously. “A disaster affecting a wide area. But I don’t have enough information to know more, and not every part of a dream necessarily has symbolic meaning.”

Dunn rubbed his forehead again.

“I’ll report this to the Holy Cathedral and see what they think. This isn’t something I’m particularly knowledgeable about.”

Klein then turned to Elizabeth’s case.

“Lamud Town... Is that lady a believer of the Goddess?”

“Yes.”

“Then there shouldn’t be any problems. Let’s go to Lamud Town now and try to have dinner there. Bring Frye too. His abilities should be useful if corpses and ghosts are involved.”

Dunn explained that if Elizabeth belonged to another faith, the matter would have to be handled by the appropriate organization, while cases involving people without allegiance to the three major Churches could fall to the Machinery Hivemind.

After thinking for a moment, Dunn added, “We also have three men on the mission. We can request Sealed Artifact 3-0782.”

“3-0782?”

Klein eventually remembered it: the Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem.

The artifact continuously purified corpses and spirits within a fifteen-meter radius. Ghosts and corpses could be destroyed in less than a minute.

But there was a horrifying drawback.

The artifact also purified the souls of ordinary humans. A normal person exposed to it for an hour would become an idiot whose only thought was praising the Sun. Beyonders could endure roughly six hours.

Klein was impressed that Dunn remembered its designation, while privately feeling his own memory was somehow worse.

Then Dunn leaned back and studied him with his deep gray eyes.

“You went to the Divination Club again today?”

He paused.

“Did you feel any changes over the past two days?”
