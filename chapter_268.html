Chapter 268 - The Cemetery and the Hospital

748 words · 5 min read

When the steam metro reached the southern bank of the Tussock River, Klein rented a carriage to Aston Cemetery on the outskirts of South Borough. The cemetery belonged to the Church of the God of Steam and Machinery, and under the darkness of dusk, its trees seemed like monsters with claws and fangs.

When the carriage driver heard Klein say he wanted to visit a friend, he became nervous. Klein quickly explained that his friend was the cemetery keeper, and the driver immediately left.

Klein waited until night had completely fallen before entering the cemetery. The cold wind had driven away much of the haze, while the crimson moon cast a faint glow over the graves.

After climbing the iron fence, Klein found a secluded grave and selected it for his first experiment. The tombstone carried a humorous epitaph asking a passerby to “help me up.”

A very humorous gentleman... I choose you!

Klein leaned beneath the trees and patiently waited, repeatedly tossing Azik’s copper whistle into the air.

Twenty minutes passed.

Nothing happened.

He concluded that the whistle might not affect corpses whose souls had been properly laid to rest through religious ritual. But he realized his experiment had another weakness: the corpse had been buried for a long time.

He needed a recently buried body.

Klein carefully avoided the cemetery guardians and eventually found a grave whose burial ceremony had been completed that very day. He waited for half an hour.

Again, nothing happened.

That gave him a much clearer conclusion. Azik’s copper whistle could not affect corpses that had undergone the ritual of putting the soul to rest. Rather than being disappointed, Klein reminded himself that the whistle was primarily a messenger-summoning item, so this weakness was not surprising.

His second experiment would concern corpses that had died recently but had not yet been laid to rest.

A hospital morgue was the ideal location.

Klein left the cemetery and walked through the dark streets. The surroundings reminded him of the night he had once walked from the cemetery after resurrecting. The memory made him sigh, and he suddenly began running to dispel the melancholy.

Later, disguised in a gray-blue worker’s uniform and cap, he infiltrated St. Estin’s Hospital in the Backlund Bridge area.

The hospital was a charity institution belonging to the Church of the God of Steam and Machinery, and many poor people died there. Some corpses remained overnight in the underground morgue, especially during the colder seasons.

Klein used his Nighthawk experience and Clown agility to sneak past the staff. He unlocked the morgue with a piece of wire and entered.

The temperature inside was lower than in the corridor. Most corpses were wrapped in bags and stored in iron cabinets, while several lay on tables awaiting examination.

Klein waited about ten minutes before deciding that it was time.

He opened his pocket watch, prepared himself, and stopped enveloping Azik’s copper whistle with spirituality.

He immediately felt that the surroundings had become unnaturally quiet.

About two minutes later, a corpse suddenly sat upright.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

The iron cabinets around him began violently banging as other corpses reacted.

“Crimson!”

Klein instantly activated a Requiem Charm and threw it forward. Icy-blue flames and peaceful darkness spread across the morgue, forcing the corpses back into place.

He used a second charm, then a third to make certain everything had stabilized.

The experiment worked.

The copper whistle could affect recently dead corpses that had not undergone a soul-resting ritual, including certain zombies. At the same time, surrounding the whistle with spirituality could suppress its negative effect.

Klein prepared to leave, but footsteps suddenly sounded outside.

The elderly janitor had been attracted by the noise.

Klein leaped upward and clung to the space between the door and ceiling. The janitor unlocked the door and entered, holding a lantern. He inspected the room but discovered nothing unusual.

Klein silently dropped behind him and escaped.

The janitor eventually returned to his guardroom, reassuring himself that there were no such things as zombies or wraiths.

Meanwhile, Klein walked through the quiet streets with a smile.

Now that he understood the whistle better, he could focus on acquiring the ingredients needed for his Magician advancement. If he obtained an appropriate Beyonder weapon, he might trade it for the material he needed. He also had formulas such as Telepathist, Bard, and Light Suppliant that could be exchanged for valuable resources.

His Clown potion was digesting faster than he had expected as well.

Key Events

  • Klein tests Azik’s copper whistle on old corpses buried through religious rituals and gets no reaction.
  • A second test on a recently buried corpse also produces no result.
  • He concludes that properly laid-to-rest corpses are unaffected by the whistle.
  • Klein infiltrates St. Estin’s Hospital to test the whistle on recently deceased corpses.
  • A corpse rises and other bodies react when Klein stops suppressing the whistle’s effect.
  • Klein uses three Requiem Charms to restore the morgue to normal.
  • He confirms that spirituality can shield the whistle’s negative influence.
  • Klein begins planning how to trade formulas and resources for the materials needed for his Magician advancement.
