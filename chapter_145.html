Chapter 145

Chapter 145

1,074 words · 7 min read

Klein stared at the diary entry in disbelief.

Some pathways could be exchanged after Sequence 5?

That contradicted everything the Nighthawks had taught him. He had always understood that once a Beyonder chose a pathway, changing it would lead to strange powers but also madness and an inability to advance. Yet Roselle’s diary described a hidden exception.

Klein did not blindly trust the information. Roselle’s surprise seemed genuine, but he might have been deceived or misunderstood what he had been told.

I need to verify this. For now, I’ll memorize it.

If true, the structure of Sequence pathways was far more complicated than he had imagined.

The Nighthawks possessed the complete Sleepless pathway and a relatively complete Corpse Collector pathway up to Sequence 4, while the Church of the God of Steam and Machinery similarly possessed the Savant pathway and much of Mystery Pryer. If Roselle was right, those pathways could substitute for one another at High Sequence.

Then what about the Seer pathway? Could Apprentice or Marauder be its compatible partner?

Klein suspected that the first several Seer Sequences each contributed a separate ability, all of which would combine at Sequence 4, perhaps preventing any later pathway substitution.

He turned back to the diary.

The pages were not actually chronological. One entry described tensions among the Eternal Blazing Sun, Lord of Storms, and God of Knowledge and Wisdom, along with hostility between the Evernight Goddess and the God of Combat. Roselle noted that such rivalries could potentially be exploited.

Another entry described his participation in a gathering of an ancient organization.

Then came the revelation that nearly made Klein lose his composure:

The second Blasphemy Slate was in that organization’s hands.

Roselle had seen the legendary object personally and discovered that it concealed an unimaginable secret. He even mused that perhaps one day he would create his own set of Blasphemy Slates, each containing an ultimate secret.

Klein stared at the page in frustration.

Why didn’t you write the organization’s name?

He immediately connected this with Roselle’s later creation of twenty-two cards, each corresponding to a pathway to godhood.

Perhaps those cards represented the ultimate secrets hidden within each pathway.

Where were they now?

Klein suppressed his curiosity and turned his attention to the others.

“You didn’t need to wait for me.”

“It’s our honor,” Alger replied respectfully.

Audrey then reported something important.

“Mr. Fool, the proposal for selecting civil servants through examinations has gained the support of the King and Prime Minister. It should soon pass both the House of Lords and House of Commons, and is expected to be implemented early next year.”

Alger reflexively mocked the politicians.

“It looks like the King and Prime Minister still know how to use their brains.”

Klein quietly thought of Benson. Once examinations became available, his brother would finally have a chance to improve his career. Yet Benson would still be competing against university graduates for many positions, and Klein worried about how quickly the opportunity might pass.

Audrey then turned to Alger.

“Mr. Hanged Man, I investigated the matter you asked about. The King has been persuaded by the Prime Minister and will not seek revenge against Feysac at the East Balam coast for the time being. I believe you can now give me the additional payment you promised.”

Alger considered it.

“What would you like?”

“Clues about the Psychology Alchemists, or clues about the main ingredients of the Telepathist potion. Of course, that can wait until after The Sun gives me the formula.”

“No problem.”

Derrick listened in confusion.

Almost everything they discussed—Kings, Prime Ministers, governments, Feysac, Psychology Alchemists—was foreign to him. He began to suspect that Justice and The Hanged Man might not even come from the same world he knew.

Yet that also made The Fool’s power seem even more astonishing.

He could gather people from incredibly distant places despite the monsters and darkness separating them.

Perhaps The Fool really is an ancient god.

Audrey was about to become a silent observer when she remembered something.

“I recently came into contact with a Beyonder circle and heard about a powerful person called Mr. A. Mr. Fool, Mr. Hanged Man, Mr. Sun, do you know anything about him?”

Klein did not.

Mr. A? I only know a Mr. Z.

Could the similarity mean that Mr. A also belonged to the Aurora Order?

Klein remained silent, unwilling to damage his image by offering an uncertain answer.

Alger, however, knew something.

“The Aurora Order is hostile toward the Churches of the Lord of Storms, Eternal Blazing Sun, and God of Knowledge and Wisdom. I happen to know quite a bit about them.”

He explained that the Aurora Order possessed five Saints and twenty-two Oracles. The Oracles used the alphabet as their code names, from Mr. A through Mr. X. They were all Beyonders, ranging from Sequence 7 to Sequence 5.

They were skilled at hiding themselves, and if an Oracle died, someone else would take his position.

“I cannot guarantee that your Mr. A is the same Mr. A, but there is a good possibility.”

Audrey instantly became more cautious.

“Thank you, Mr. Hanged Man. You no longer need to compensate me.”

“No,” Alger replied. “I want to ask for your help with this information and provide additional compensation.”

“What help?”

Alger hesitated briefly.

“I received information that Qilangos, also known as Rear Admiral Hurricane, has secretly gone ashore and infiltrated Backlund. I don’t know what he intends to do, but I want you to help locate him. You don’t need to put yourself in danger afterward.”

Audrey’s eyes widened.

“Rear Admiral Hurricane Qilangos? One of the seven great pirate admirals?”

She could barely maintain her Spectator state.

This was exactly the kind of person she had dreamed of encountering after becoming a Beyonder—someone who had existed in the legendary stories and adventures told among the nobility.

Alger immediately became serious.

“He’s a Sequence 6 Wind-blessed of the Sailor pathway. He also possesses a miraculous item comparable to a Sealed Artifact. He’s crafty and cruel. Do not attempt to confront him.”

Then Alger looked toward The Fool.

“Mr. Fool, can I ask your adorer to assist me at a critical moment? I’ll offer a price that interests you.”

The only adorer I have is myself...

Klein suppressed the absurdity and answered calmly.

“That depends on whether my adorer happens to be in Backlund.”

“Alright.”

Alger withdrew his gaze, disappointed but still quietly hopeful.
