Chapter 212

Chapter 212 - Avenger

985 words · 6 min read

The cold September wind swept through the cemetery in northern Loen, jolting Klein fully awake.

He looked down at himself and gave a rueful smile. There were still secrets surrounding his transmigration, and his strange resurrection ability seemed limited. He estimated that he might only have two more resurrections left, and if his body were completely destroyed, he had no idea whether it would work at all.

After buttoning his dirt-covered suit, Klein realized he was still wearing the newest shirt and tuxedo Benson and Melissa had bought for him.

“Benson and Melissa really have no idea how to save money...”

He pushed himself upright with his Clown abilities and looked at his own tombstone.

*The best elder brother.*

*The best younger brother.*

*The best colleague.*

The words made his chest ache as he imagined what Benson and Melissa must have gone through. This grief felt almost worse than watching Dunn die.

Klein quickly covered the coffin and restored the grave. Resurrection could never be discovered by ordinary people, and especially not by the Nighthawks, Mandated Punishers, or Machinery Hivemind. In this world, actual gods existed, and such an event could never be dismissed as an ordinary miracle.

While restoring the grave, Klein sensed Azik’s copper whistle beneath the soil. He dug it out, cleaned it, and kept it rather than immediately summoning the messenger.

Instead, he used the pendulum still wrapped around his wrist to locate Dunn’s grave.

After wandering through the cemetery, he finally found the Captain’s tombstone beneath the moonlight.

Dunn’s photograph showed the familiar gentle expression, and beneath it was his epitaph:

*The true guardian,*

*The most trusted partner,*

*The Captain forever.*

Klein stared silently until his vision blurred. For an instant, he could almost see Dunn turning toward him and saying in his mellow voice:

“We saved Tingen.”

Klein spoke quietly to the grave, joking about Dunn’s weakened mental state and his strange assumptions during the final battle. He pointed out that Dunn had failed to ask what powerful offensive abilities Klein possessed, yet had probably guessed that Klein had something more than the single Sealed Artifact he claimed to be carrying.

Then his expression crumbled.

“I have no idea what I am now. Maybe I’m just an evil spirit that has clawed its way back from hell to seek revenge...”

Tears streamed down his face.

“Captain... We miss you too!”

After mourning in silence, Klein found a secluded place and entered the world above the gray fog.

He needed answers.

He wrote his first divination statement:

*The person who killed me.*

After repeating it seven times, he entered the dreamlike state created by Cogitation.

The image formed slowly: brand-new leather boots, pale hands, and Saint Selena’s urn.

Then the vision revealed the murderer’s face.

A middle-aged man with short dark-blond hair, one blind eye, another eye so dark blue it was nearly black, and a face as smooth and rigid as a carved statue.

Klein immediately recognized him.

Ince Zangwill.

The former Archbishop of the Evernight Church, the failed Gatekeeper, and the fugitive carrying Sealed Artifact 0-08.

“It’s him!”

Suddenly, countless fragments of previous events connected in Klein’s mind. He now understood why the murderer had taken Saint Selena’s ashes while leaving Dunn’s Beyonder characteristic untouched.

Klein carefully reasoned through the Captain’s earlier revelation about Beyonder Characteristics. The remains of dead Beyonders could serve as the equivalent of incomplete potions. Since Ince Zangwill was a Sequence 5 Gatekeeper, he could theoretically use the corresponding remains and supplementary ingredients to advance.

For a High-Sequence advancement, however, a special ritual was necessary.

Saint Selena had been a Saint, meaning her ashes corresponded to a powerful Sequence. As a former Archbishop, Ince would certainly know exactly which Sequence she belonged to and what ingredients were required.

The truth became clear.

His real target had never been Klein.

It had been Saint Selena’s ashes.

Ince Zangwill had orchestrated the entire disaster in order to obtain them and advance to Sequence 4.

Klein performed another divination to verify the theory.

The pendulum swung clockwise.

Success.

His deduction was correct.

But another question immediately arose.

Ince was only a Sequence 5. He could not possibly have created such an enormous chain of coincidences by himself. He could not have made Megose visit the Nighthawks at exactly the right moment.

That meant Sealed Artifact 0-08 was responsible.

The artifact looked like an ordinary quill, but Klein suspected its true ability involved recording events that would inevitably occur. Yet it could not simply grant wishes directly; otherwise Ince could have written that the urn grew wings and flew straight into his hands.

There had to be restrictions.

Perhaps 0-08 possessed little direct combat ability but could subtly force people to act according to the events it described without realizing they were being manipulated.

If so, the artifact was terrifying enough to explain every coincidence.

Even Megose, carrying the child of an evil god, had been drawn into its arrangements.

That was why Grade 0 Sealed Artifacts were classified as the most confidential and dangerous objects imaginable.

Klein failed to divine this theory directly because he lacked sufficient information. He therefore moved on to his final question: the city where Ince Zangwill was currently located.

Because of Sealed Artifact 0-08 and the possibility that Ince had already become a Demigod, Klein could not obtain his precise location. He could only divine the general area.

The vision revealed a vast, somewhat muddy river crossed by a grand bridge. Ports lined both banks, crowded with goods, workers, houses, carriages, and strange machinery. To the northeast stretched dense districts dominated by modern Loen architecture and Gothic structures.

Farther east, the chimneys became more numerous and the air smokier. To the west, the land rose toward magnificent buildings, Gothic clock towers, and grand castles.

Then a bell rang.

Klein immediately recognized the city.

The “Land of Hope.”

The “City of Cities.”

Backlund.
