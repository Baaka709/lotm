Chapter 178

Chapter 178 - The Subsequent Ideas

898 words · 6 min read

After escaping Qilangos, Fors and Xio discussed their next move. Fors was surprised when Xio suggested involving the police, since Beyonders normally handled such matters themselves.

Xio explained that Darkholme’s gruesome death would inevitably attract the attention of one of the major supernatural organizations—the Mandated Punishers, Nighthawks, Machinery Hivemind, or a military department. They could anonymously leak information identifying Qilangos as the murderer and let the entire city hunt him. Their goal was never to capture the powerful pirate themselves, but merely to discover his whereabouts. The more Beyonders searching for him, the safer their investigation would become. Eventually, Qilangos would be forced into making a mistake, allowing them to claim the bounty for providing his location.

Fors admitted that this was unusually rational for Xio. Xio acknowledged that she had personally felt the terrifying presence of Death when Qilangos approached. The sheer danger had instinctively warned her that charging directly at him would be suicidal.

Fors agreed to inform Audrey first, then report Darkholme’s murder to the police. They would continue investigating Qilangos indirectly, hoping to determine his range of activity and, more importantly, why he had come to Backlund.

Fors explained that even if Qilangos constantly changed locations or disguises, knowing his objective would eventually expose him. Her experience as a novelist had taught her that once the key point of a mystery was identified, the rest usually became much easier.

Xio was astonished that Fors could produce such a logical deduction. Fors simply teased her, saying that she was not stupid—just lazy, while Xio preferred thinking with her muscles.

Their next problem was contacting Audrey. Audrey’s messenger was Susie, who apparently took several walks each day. They therefore had to wait around Count Hall’s estate for the golden retriever instead of attempting to sneak inside.

Fors refused to infiltrate the mansion. Audrey’s father was a hereditary count, one of the most powerful members of the House of Lords, and deeply involved in banking, coal, steel, and other major industries. A man of that status was almost certainly protected by Beyonders and possessed valuable supernatural items. Sneaking inside would be nothing like infiltrating the homes of impoverished nobles.

Eventually, Xio and Fors concealed themselves near the estate and waited.

Susie finally emerged from a hidden opening in the wall. Almost immediately, a black male dog appeared and began chasing after her. Xio could practically see Susie’s disgust and decided to intervene.

“My ruling is for you to leave her alone!”

The black dog fled with its tail between its legs. Susie relaxed, barked politely, and wagged her tail, secretly relieved that she had not been forced into speaking to the humans.

...

Audrey soon received the latest intelligence from Xio and Fors. She closed her piano and paced around the room as she considered the situation.

Qilangos was extremely dangerous. If Xio and Fors continued investigating him recklessly, they could easily be killed. Their investigation could also expose Audrey’s own involvement.

She decided that their plan to involve the authorities was the safest choice. The Tarot Club meeting was also only two hours away, and Audrey wondered what Mr. Fool would advise. If he was not interested in intervening directly, she could discuss the matter with The Hanged Man.

For the first time, Audrey fully appreciated just how dangerous her situation had become. Someone had already been killed.

...

At three in the afternoon, Audrey’s vision returned from a crimson haze, and she found herself once again within the mysterious world above the gray fog.

The vast palace, ancient bronze table, and ever-present Fool surrounded by thick fog immediately gave her a powerful sense of security. Compared with the terrifying Qilangos, she felt that she and Mr. Fool existed on completely different levels.

She cheerfully greeted Mr. Fool, The Hanged Man, and The Sun.

After greetings were exchanged, Klein noticed that Audrey wanted to speak and gave her permission.

Her first concern was not Qilangos, however. She asked whether Mr. Fool’s mysterious “adorer” had received the three hundred pounds that had been promised.

Klein replied that he had not paid close attention but assumed the money had been received because the adorer had not asked for further assistance.

He was secretly delighted, knowing that the entire three hundred pounds was safely sitting in his anonymous bank account.

Audrey then turned toward Alger and reported that there had been progress in the search for Qilangos. Unfortunately, the pirate had detected their investigation immediately after they found his trail and had killed one of the people involved.

She described Xio and Fors’s plan to continue investigating while drawing the attention of the authorities.

Alger accepted the information and promised to pay close attention.

He then addressed Mr. Fool directly.

He asked for permission to use Mr. Fool’s name to report his findings if he discovered Qilangos’s true purpose and the powerful mystical item that the pirate intended to obtain. He emphasized that he was not asking for additional assistance, merely permission to convey information through a ritual.

Alger believed that if his discoveries became important enough, Mr. Fool’s adorer would naturally intervene.

Audrey immediately regretted not having made the same request earlier. Being able to report information directly might allow her to receive Mr. Fool’s guidance from time to time.

Klein considered Alger’s request.

Then, under the attention of the other Tarot Club members, he nodded.

“You may.”

Key Events

  • Xio and Fors decide to involve Backlund’s official Beyonder organizations in the search for Qilangos rather than confront him themselves.
  • Their goal is to determine Qilangos’s whereabouts and, more importantly, uncover the reason for his presence in Backlund.
  • Fors emphasizes that discovering his objective will eventually expose him regardless of how well he hides or disguises himself.
  • Xio and Fors wait outside Count Hall’s estate for Susie so they can secretly pass information to Audrey.
  • Audrey receives the new intelligence and decides that Qilangos is too dangerous for Xio and Fors to continue pursuing without involving greater forces.
  • At the Tarot Club, Audrey reports the Qilangos situation to Alger.
  • Alger asks Mr. Fool for permission to report Qilangos’s true objective and the mystical item he seeks through a ritual.
  • Klein grants Alger permission to report such information directly.
