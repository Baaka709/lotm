Chapter 274

Chapter 274 - Beyonder Weapon

656 words · 4 min read

The hooded woman introduced the first weapon.

“The Whip of Slowness.”

It was a replica of a mystical item that could continuously create invisible restraints, gradually slowing an opponent the longer a battle continued.

Its effects would last for another two years.

The price was 450 pounds or the Barbarian potion formula.

Klein wondered whether The Sun would look strange wielding a whip, then asked whether its restraint could affect spirits such as wraiths.

The woman shook her head.

“No.”

The second weapon was the Axe of Hurricane.

It increased agility and running speed, had a fifteen-percent chance of paralyzing an enemy, and a five-percent chance of producing lightning. In a thunderstorm, the lightning effect increased to fifty percent.

The weapon’s effects would last for no more than a year.

Klein immediately saw enormous potential.

The City of Silver was constantly surrounded by flashes of lightning. If that environment counted as a thunderstorm, The Sun could potentially cause lightning with half his attacks.

Even without that bonus, the axe was an excellent combat weapon.

The one-year limitation was not especially important because Derrick would probably be much stronger by then and might need another weapon anyway.

Klein decided to buy it.

Before he could bid, another man offered 520 pounds.

Klein raised it to 530.

The rival increased the offer to 550.

Klein calmly bid 560.

The other man stopped.

“Alright then, it’s yours.”

Klein secretly exhaled.

After no one else bid, the weapon was transferred to him after Eye of Wisdom inspected it.

The axe was heavy and iron-black, with a sharp edge and numerous spiritual symbols and runes embedded in its surface. Klein could sense a slight sting when touching it.

He considered how the weapon could be traded to The Sun for the Mist Treant materials he needed.

With his remaining finances, he still lacked the resources to simply purchase all his Magician ingredients, so he would have to rely on formulas, knowledge, or other assets.

The Whip of Slowness later sold for 480 pounds.

A Sequence 6-level Beyonder ingredient called Heart of Featherman failed to sell when its price reached 1,600 pounds.

Near the end of the gathering, another man offered a Sequence 9 Corpse Collector formula.

Klein recognized that the formula was normally associated with the Church of the Evernight Goddess, the Church of Combat, and the Numinous Episcopate. He wondered what kind of person might be selling it.

The formula sold quickly.

Klein realized that Sequence 9 formulas were particularly easy to sell because people liked acquiring them for children or students. Such formulas gave future Beyonders more pathway choices.

He then asked whether anyone possessed the complete spinal fluid of a Dark Patterned Black Panther.

No one answered.

“I will provide payment that no Beyonder would refuse.”

Still, there was no response.

Klein gave up for the moment.

The Apothecary then offered his strange medicines for sale. The weapon seller asked him whether the dangerous beast in the sewers had been eliminated.

The Apothecary laughed.

“You asked the right person!”

Then he admitted that he had no idea.

He had reported the matter to the police and never returned.

Klein silently mocked him for his survival instinct.

Eventually all the Apothecary’s medicines were sold, and Eye of Wisdom announced the end of the gathering.

Klein had been assigned fifth to leave through a secret basement passage.

Relieved that Eye of Wisdom apparently had not recognized him, Klein returned the robe, removed his mask, and safely traveled to East Borough.

In a sealed apartment, he summoned himself and carried the Axe of Hurricane above the gray fog.

He sent a message to The Sun.

“The World has sacrificed the weapon to me. You can prepare the bestowment ritual.”

In the City of Silver, Derrick immediately prepared the ceremony.

He wondered what kind of weapon he would receive—a straight sword, broadsword, or rapier.

Then the ritual revealed his reward.

An axe.

  • The Whip of Slowness sells for 480 pounds.
