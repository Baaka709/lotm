Chapter 191

Chapter 191 - Unclear Motives

952 words · 6 min read

Qilangos managed to evade his pursuers by using the wind to cross a man-made lake. He intended to fake an escape toward the Tussock River before heading into Hillston, Backlund’s financial district, where the city’s enormous population would make him almost impossible to find.

Before he could disappear, however, reality suddenly changed.

The colors around him became unnaturally vivid, as though the world had been painted over with thick pastel colors. The trees became intensely green, the fruit unnaturally red, and the darkness beneath the water impossibly black. Above the obscured crimson moon appeared countless transparent, indescribable figures and strange streaks of color containing mysterious knowledge.

Qilangos suddenly found himself suspended in midair.

Dark water rose toward his feet, while pale hands stretched upward from beneath the surface.

He immediately realized that he had been ambushed by an extremely powerful enemy.

A gigantic skeletal creature appeared before him, roughly four meters tall with black flames burning inside its eyes.

Qilangos remained calm. Creeping Hunger transformed into a brilliant golden form, and he spread his arms.

A pillar of pure radiance descended from the sky and struck the skeleton. The blazing power belonged to the Priest of Light, a Sun pathway ability particularly effective against undead creatures. The skeleton was destroyed, its black flames extinguished as its body dissolved.

Qilangos did not have time to celebrate.

Another skeletal giant appeared.

Then another.

Then dozens more.

Soon more than a hundred four-meter-tall skeletons surrounded him, each staring with black flames burning in its eye sockets. Meanwhile, the dark water below continued rising, with countless pale hands reaching toward him.

Qilangos had been completely surrounded.

...

Back at Duke Negan’s mansion, Cardinal Ace Snake ordered the Mandated Punishers to spread out and pursue Qilangos. He himself created a typhoon and flew after the fleeing pirate.

Alger immediately followed.

He refused to abandon even the smallest possibility of personally killing Qilangos.

But after only a short distance, he heard Ace Snake’s voice carried through the wind:

“There’s no need to continue the pursuit.”

Alger stopped and looked toward the sky.

Ace Snake was hovering above the forest and lake, his expression stern.

Alger rushed toward the Cardinal and then noticed something impossible.

On the calm lake near the bank floated a familiar figure.

Qilangos.

Alger recognized the pirate immediately. At first he thought he was hallucinating because Qilangos had escaped successfully moments earlier.

Then Qilangos’s face began rotting.

Yellow-green fluid seeped from his flesh. His skin peeled away piece by piece until only a skull remained. His eyeballs dropped from their sockets.

Then the rest of his body collapsed.

Within less than twenty seconds, one of the Seven Pirate Admirals was dead.

Alger was stunned.

Qilangos was a Sequence 6 Wind-blessed and the owner of Creeping Hunger. Yet someone had killed him so quickly and so completely that Alger could barely comprehend what he had witnessed.

Who could do this?

And why?

Ace Snake immediately questioned Alger about whether he had shared his information with anyone else.

Alger explained that he had reported it directly to the Cardinal and that the people who handled the original information had not opened the letter during transfer. He could guarantee that no one in that chain had deliberately leaked it, though he could not eliminate the possibility that someone else had independently obtained the same information.

As Alger considered the possibilities, one answer became increasingly convincing.

Mr. Fool’s adorer.

Only the Tarot Club had received Audrey’s information in time, and only someone connected to Mr. Fool could apparently have acted with such perfect timing and such inexplicable motives.

Alger became increasingly convinced that Mr. Fool’s mysterious adorer had killed Qilangos.

Ace Snake reached a different conclusion.

He told the arriving Mandated Punishers that Qilangos had been killed by either a High-Sequence Beyonder or someone using a Sealed Artifact of comparable strength. Based on the remains and the method of death, he believed the attacker probably belonged to the Death pathway.

He suggested the attacker could be a member of the Numinous Episcopate, a secret organization historically connected to Death and attempts to revive the ancient deity.

The killer’s motive, however, was completely unclear.

Alger heard the words “High-Sequence Beyonder” and mentally connected them with Mr. Fool’s adorer.

A Demigod.

The thought terrified him.

If someone of that power stood behind Mr. Fool, then betrayal would be meaningless. Alger imagined himself suffering the same horrifying fate as Qilangos and immediately felt a chill.

He then realized that his only sensible choice was complete loyalty.

At the same time, he felt immense relief.

Qilangos was dead, meaning no one could threaten him with the secret he had feared losing.

...

Inside Duke Negan’s mansion, Audrey was still discussing the assassination with her mother and other nobles when Count Hall appeared.

She quietly joined him on the balcony.

He told her that the matter had been resolved and that she no longer needed to worry.

He also asked whether she had told anyone that Baron Gramir was actually an impostor.

Audrey firmly denied it.

She silently reflected that she had only told an existence almost equivalent to a god.

She explained that after warning her father, she had gone elsewhere and then joined her mother, providing an easy explanation for her movements.

Count Hall then delivered the shocking news.

Qilangos was dead.

Someone had killed him, but nobody knew why.

There was no clear motive, suggesting that the killer might belong to a secret and extremely powerful organization.

Audrey immediately connected the clues.

An unclear motive, extraordinary power, and impossible timing.

Could the killer have been Mr. Fool’s adorer?

Could it have been the Tarot Club?

The realization suddenly dawned on her.
