Chapter 193

Chapter 193 - Coming To A Close

898 words · 6 min read

The report on Qilangos’s death in the Tingen Morning Post was remarkably brief, listing only the time, location, people involved, and final outcome. Klein noted that information traveled surprisingly quickly in this world thanks to Roselle’s inventions and the telegraph network. The news from Backlund had reached Tingen overnight, allowing the morning papers to publish it immediately.

As Klein thought through the situation, he became increasingly convinced that Azik was probably safe. If Azik had been seriously injured or captured during the pursuit, Audrey or Alger would almost certainly have tried to report it to Mr. Fool. Since neither had contacted him, things were likely under control. Klein decided that if he still received no response from Azik or prayers from the Tarot Club by nightfall, he would use the copper whistle to send Azik another letter.

Around him, other passengers discussed Qilangos. Some spoke about how pirate admirals terrorized the seas, others wondered why Qilangos had come to Backlund, and several were fascinated by the enormous bounty.

One passenger mentioned the kingdom’s ten-thousand-pound reward. Another calculated what he could do with such money, including buying nurseries and investing in railroads and colonial companies. Someone else pointed out that Intis, Feysac, Feynapotter, and various merchant groups had also placed bounties on Qilangos.

Klein was stunned by the ten-thousand-pound figure. Even with his new salary, accumulating that much would take him roughly twenty years under impossible conditions of saving every penny.

He folded the paper with some disappointment and accepted that the Qilangos incident was essentially over. All that remained were loose ends, particularly the promised pages of Roselle’s diary from Alger.

...

In Backlund’s Cherwood Borough, Fors and Xio discussed the sudden end of their investigation.

Fors joked that her money seemed to disappear constantly, though the royalties from *Stormwind Mountain Villa* were still providing enough income to keep her comfortable. Otherwise, she might have been forced to return to practicing medicine.

Xio worried that their involvement with Qilangos could draw attention from the official Beyonder organizations, but Fors explained that the authorities would likely focus on Xio rather than herself. Xio had been the one directly involved in the police report and the investigation around Darkholme, while Fors could still maintain her public identity as a popular author.

Fors admitted that the experience had also given her valuable inspiration for a future novel about a brutal murder.

Then a newspaper boy suddenly shouted the headline:

“Extra! Extra! Rear Admiral Hurricane Qilangos killed in Backlund!”

Both women froze.

Xio could hardly believe that Qilangos was already dead. She had expected the investigation to uncover his purpose, gather powerful Beyonders, and eventually ambush him. Instead, he had died before they even understood what he was doing.

Xio immediately bought a copy of the Tussock Times. The report stated that Qilangos had been killed by Duke Negan’s bodyguard.

Fors gently mocked Xio for believing everything printed in a newspaper. Xio eventually reasoned that perhaps the official organizations had somehow deduced Qilangos’s motives early and successfully coordinated an ambush.

Whatever the explanation, she and Fors could finally return to their ordinary lives—although they decided to avoid the police station whose sphere of influence they had previously entered.

Xio then worried about their payment from Audrey. They had not actually completed the mission of discovering Qilangos’s purpose.

Fors reassured her that their efforts had still forced Qilangos into action, contributing indirectly to his downfall. Given Audrey’s generosity, she expected them to receive at least half the promised reward.

Both women wondered who would claim the ten-thousand-pound bounty.

Fors then warned Xio not to contact Audrey immediately. The circumstances surrounding Qilangos’s death were far too strange, and suddenly seeking out Audrey could attract unwanted attention. Xio was surprised that Fors had somehow predicted she was considering going straight to the Empress Borough.

Fors simply laughed.

...

After a busy morning, Klein returned to the Blackthorn Security Company and reported that everyone connected to Lanevus who had been assigned to him appeared innocent. They were simply victims and had no involvement in any Beyonder-related incidents.

Dunn ordered him to stop that line of investigation temporarily and focus on the more promising suspects after the other Nighthawks completed their own investigations. They could not dedicate the entire team to Lanevus and neglect other threats.

Klein was about to leave for lunch when Rozanne knocked and announced that someone had arrived with a mission.

Dunn suggested hearing the request first and rejecting it if it proved too troublesome.

They entered the reception area and found two women sitting on the sofa. Both wore plain black hats and dresses.

One was plump and fair-skinned, her face concealed behind a veil. Klein immediately felt that he recognized her but could not place her.

The thinner woman spoke.

“The mission we would like to entrust to you is for you to track and monitor Madam Sharon and find evidence of her crimes.”

Klein immediately understood.

The silent, veiled woman was Madam Maynard herself—the wife of the murdered parliamentarian and daughter of the New Party’s leader.

She apparently could not accept the police conclusion surrounding her husband’s death. Convinced that Sharon was responsible, she had privately hired the security company to investigate the widow and uncover evidence against her.

Klein could only shake his head and laugh inwardly.

The case had come back to him in a very unexpected way.
