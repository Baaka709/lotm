Chapter 169 - New Abilities

1,042 words · 7 min read

After the color returned to Klein’s eyes, Crestet encouraged him to move around and discover the abilities granted by the Clown potion.

Klein began with a simple punch. The force behind it surprised him so much that he nearly fell forward like a carriage stopping suddenly. Yet instead of losing his balance, he instinctively coordinated his spine, tendons, ligaments, and muscles to correct his center of gravity and remain standing.

He quickly realized that the greatest physical improvement was not simply strength, agility, or speed, but coordination. His body had become extraordinarily balanced and controllable. He felt almost like a roly-poly toy and joked internally that he could now perform circus tricks or even walk a tightrope. The Clown potion truly lived up to its name.

After testing his strength and speed, Klein estimated that his physical abilities were now around Gawain’s level. With specialized training, he believed he could become competent in combat within only a month or two instead of the half-year he had originally expected.

Crestet agreed that the Clown potion was particularly suited to combat. He then asked Klein what voices he had heard during his advancement.

Klein truthfully mentioned hearing the word “Hornacis,” but deliberately concealed “Flegrea.” He wanted to observe Crestet’s reaction first and only reveal more if the deacon willingly discussed the Hornacis mountain range and the Nation of the Evernight.

Instead, Crestet warned him about a far more dangerous possibility. High-Sequence Beyonders could exert influence over lower-Sequence Beyonders on the same pathway. Some portions of a pathway, he explained, already contained aspects of the realm of demigods. The murmurs and howls heard by low-level Beyonders could therefore be intentionally transmitted by powerful beings, potentially carrying malicious intent.

This danger was even greater when the pathway belonged to an evil god.

Crestet revealed that he had spoken with Dunn about Klein’s teammate who had recently lost control.

Old Neil.

The Hidden Sage immediately came to Klein’s mind. He solemnly promised that he would not be tempted or corrupted by the mysterious voices.

At the same time, Klein began wondering whether this explained why the Church only openly provided certain pathways, such as Sleepless and Corpse Collector, while concealing many others. Perhaps pathways connected to gods or powerful beings were too dangerous. The Mystery Pryer and Seer pathways might remain available because their early stages offered useful support without giving the entities associated with those pathways too much influence.

Yet Klein still could not explain why the Church also concealed potion names, their characteristics, and the precautions required when consuming them.

Before leaving, Klein asked Crestet how one should act as a Clown. Did he have to join a circus?

Crestet laughed and explained that this would be formalism. A potion name did not merely describe a job; it represented a group of people sharing particular traits. Just as a Seer could be understood as someone capable of perceiving fate while remaining respectful toward it, a Clown had to embody the essence of the role rather than mechanically imitate the profession.

The specific rules also differed slightly from person to person, so another Beyonder’s experience could never serve as a perfect template.

Klein concluded that he could therefore act as a Clown in his ordinary life as long as he understood its essence.

Crestet cautiously agreed—in theory.

After saying farewell, Klein drew the crimson moon over his chest and wished him the Goddess’s blessing. As Crestet prepared to leave, Klein suddenly felt an inexplicable intuition.

Crestet would step forward with his left foot.

A moment later, Crestet did exactly that.

One step. Two steps. Three.

Klein watched him disappear into the corridor, stunned by what had just happened.

The Clown potion had given him the ability to intuitively predict another person’s next action.

Klein immediately recognized how powerful this could be when combined with his enhanced coordination, speed, agility, and strength. Even though the intuition was fleeting and could not necessarily be relied upon constantly, predicting an opponent’s movement at a critical moment could easily turn defeat into victory.

He also remembered another possibility: once he had reduced the negative influence of the Clown potion through Cogitation, he could attempt the ritual to summon himself again.

Klein then searched his mind for other abilities. The Nighthawks’ confidential records stated that some potions granted innate knowledge of spells after advancement, but he sensed nothing of the sort. Apparently, the Clown did not possess the spellcasting ability described in those records.

He wondered whether “crafty” instead referred to becoming better at using facial expressions and body language to deceive others.

The thought reminded him of the suited clown from the Secret Order he had encountered previously. That man’s strange and varied spells had been overwhelmingly powerful. Klein concluded that the clown he had fought must have been a Sequence 7 and that his clown-like appearance had merely disguised his identity. If that man had also avoided the influence of Sealed Artifact 2-049, ten Kleins probably would not have been enough to defeat him.

However, Klein discovered that the Clown was not entirely without offensive tricks.

He picked up the paper containing the potion formula, flicked it into the air, and watched in amazement as it hardened like a dagger and pierced directly into the wall.

Klein immediately thought of carrying tarot cards in the future. They could serve both as divination tools and as weapons.

After cleaning up the alchemy room and burning the potion formula, Klein left and sealed the secret door behind him.

He had no desire to start entertaining people with absurd tricks in the wake of Old Neil’s death. For now, he intended to use Cogitation to reduce the influence of the potion.

As he walked through the corridor toward the Blackthorn Security Company, Klein reflected on how much his position had changed. He was no longer merely a support member. Since Old Neil’s death, he was now the only remaining member of the Tingen Nighthawks capable of providing that kind of support.

The Holy Cathedral would probably send another Mystery Pryer or Seer to the team.

Klein eventually reached the stairs and emerged into the recreation room.

Warm sunlight poured through the oriel window, pure and bright against the darkness of the basement.
