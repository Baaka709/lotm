Chapter 256 - Meeting the Apothecary Again

975 words · 6 min read

Before leaving home, Klein used divination to check whether visiting the Bravehearts Bar would be beneficial. The answer was negative. He then cautiously asked whether anyone was monitoring him.

Miss Bodyguard’s ethereal voice answered from behind him.

“No.”

Klein instinctively looked back but saw nothing. He could not help laughing inwardly. MI9 apparently did not even consider him a suspect anymore. After Rosago failed to implicate him, they had seemingly concluded that a detective who spent his days tracking down missing cats could hardly be involved in assassinating an ambassador or defeating a Sequence 5 Beyonder.

Comforted by that conclusion, Klein put on his half top hat and carried his black, silver-inlaid cane to the Bravehearts Bar on Iron Gate Street.

The bar was as rowdy as ever. After buying a Southville beer, Klein made his way to Card Room 3, where Kaspars was gambling with enormous piles of cash and gold coins. The arms dealer complained that he preferred real money to poker chips because the weight and texture of actual currency intoxicated him almost like women did.

When Kaspars saw Klein, he immediately asked why he had come back.

Klein wanted information about another gathering like the previous one. Kaspars was surprised because he knew Klein had already reached an arrangement with Maric, but Klein clarified that this was unrelated to hiring bodyguards. He had become genuinely interested in the underground Beyonder gatherings.

Kaspars informed him that there would be another gathering that night, organized by the same person as before. He charged Klein one pound for access.

After half an hour, they took the same route as before to the same dark house. Klein wore the iron half-mask and followed Kaspars through the strange entrance procedure, eventually putting on the hooded robe that concealed his face.

This time, however, Klein felt completely different.

The gathering no longer intimidated him. He calmly observed the room and sensed Miss Bodyguard’s familiar cold presence nearby. He also concluded that there was probably no Sequence 5 Beyonder present and possibly not even a Sequence 6.

Then the round-faced Apothecary spoke.

“Black Snake has apparently died in the sewers. Those beasts are still rampant.”

Klein immediately became alert.

Black Snake was the man who had previously sold him the remains of a Listener, allowing him to make the Language of Foulness. Since Black Snake was suspected of belonging to the Aurora Order, his death was significant. He had also been fairly strong, yet had apparently died while simply clearing beasts from the sewers.

Klein remembered the strange thumping he had heard when investigating Zreal’s corpse and the way part of Zreal’s body had already been eaten by sewer creatures. Those events had happened far away in Iron Carbon Street, making him wonder whether they were connected.

The news frightened the other Beyonders as well.

The Apothecary asked what he should do. After considering it, Klein advised him to abandon the herbs he had been growing in the sewers and never return there.

The Apothecary hesitated. The herbs would soon mature, and the beasts normally remained in the deepest parts of the sewers.

Klein explained that Black Snake might have had an organization backing him. His death would almost certainly attract an investigation, and the Apothecary would not want to become entangled in it.

The Apothecary accepted the advice.

Klein went further and suggested anonymously reporting the sewer beasts to the police. If creatures powerful enough to kill Black Snake were lurking beneath Backlund, eventually they might cause a major disaster. Letting the authorities deal with them would allow the Beyonders to benefit without taking the risk themselves.

Eye of Wisdom immediately applauded the idea. They might fear official Beyonders, he said, but that did not mean they could not make use of them.

Klein smiled inwardly. As an official Beyonder himself, he naturally approached the situation differently from these isolated underground practitioners.

The gathering continued with people trading materials and requesting items, but nothing particularly useful attracted Klein’s attention. He deliberately held back his own requests and preferred to observe the group longer.

As the meeting progressed, Eye of Wisdom gradually arranged for members to leave at three-minute intervals. Klein noticed that there were at least five exits, with attendants directing different people through different routes to prevent them from revealing each other’s identities.

Eventually, only three people remained: Eye of Wisdom, Klein, and the round-faced Apothecary.

Eye of Wisdom chuckled and remarked that Klein appeared to be lucky.

The Apothecary widened his eyes in recognition. He remembered Klein as the man who had bought the ominous item from Black Snake and assumed he had already died.

Klein simply replied that perhaps he was naturally lucky.

Eye of Wisdom warned him not to rely excessively on luck. No matter how many times a person survived through fortune, one failure could still result in death.

Klein explained that this was precisely why he had come to the gathering: he wanted to acquire useful items and improve his ability to protect himself.

The Apothecary suddenly lamented that he should have listened to his teacher.

That word immediately caught Klein’s attention.

The Apothecary explained that his teacher had advised him to choose a pathway that would make him sufficiently lucky, but he had instead chosen to become a medicine concocter. As a result, he had been unable to obtain his next potion ingredients for two years.

Klein immediately noticed how closely the advice resembled the Monster pathway’s concept of luck. The connection made it increasingly likely that the Apothecary came from the Life School of Thought.

When Klein asked why he had chosen the pathway anyway, the Apothecary straightened proudly.

“This is a man’s choice!”

Once he learned that he could concoct medicine that improved his performance in a certain personal area, he had chosen the pathway without hesitation.
