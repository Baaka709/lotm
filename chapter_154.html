Chapter 154

957 words · 6 min read

Dunn studied Klein’s eyes for several moments before leaning back and slowly exhaling.

“Are you certain?”

There was a trace of surprise in his expression. He had evidently expected the special application, just not this soon.

Klein smiled. “I’m certain, Captain. When you completely master a potion, you’ll feel a special, almost magical sensation. There’s no mistaking it.”

“Special... magical sensation...” Dunn repeated, his brows drawing together.

Klein immediately realized what that implied. Dunn had advanced twice without truly digesting his potions. Since the Captain knew nothing of the acting method, he could only rely on time and instinct to slowly reduce the danger of losing control. In a sense, Dunn had been unconsciously acting all along.

After thinking for nearly a minute, Dunn finally said, “Perhaps it would be better to wait another year.”

Klein understood. A longer wait would make his advancement less conspicuous, especially since Daly’s own unusually rapid progression had already given the Church a precedent. Still, Klein had another reason for refusing to wait.

“At first, I wanted to wait until next year. There’s still much I need to improve, especially my combat skills. But, Captain, don’t you think we’ve encountered too many coincidences over the past two months?”

He listed them one after another: while pursuing kidnappers, they unexpectedly discovered the Antigonus Notebook; when the delivery of Sealed Artifact 2-049 was delayed, Ray Bieber remained in Tingen and attempted to digest its power at the harbor; attending a birthday banquet had led him into Hanass Vincent’s incident; investigating a library had brought him face-to-face with an Aurora Order member.

“I don’t know what these coincidences mean, but they make me feel unsafe. That’s why I want to strengthen myself as quickly as possible.”

Klein deliberately hinted at the possibility of a hidden manipulator without revealing his own knowledge. He wanted the Nighthawks to start looking at these events from a broader perspective.

Dunn leaned forward, steepling his fingers before his mouth. After quietly considering Klein’s words, he finally said in his deep, gentle voice, “Very perceptive... Perhaps there really is something lurking in the dark.”

“You can submit the special application.”

“Alright.”

Klein rose with a smile, but Dunn stopped him before he left.

“Hold on. Pay attention to your choice of words.”

Klein nodded. He had expected Dunn might suggest that he secretly advance to Sequence 8 and only formally report it years later, but that would be too dangerous. Regardless of whether the application was normal or special, the Holy Cathedral would still investigate the advancing Beyonder. Concealing an advancement could drag the entire Tingen Nighthawks into trouble.

With his mysticism lessons finished, Klein went to the clerks’ office and began writing his application. Two newer clerks were there, but they quickly left after realizing he wanted privacy. Klein appreciated their caution and finished drafting the document within minutes before typing it on the Akerson Model 1346 typewriter.

As he was completing it, Leonard Mitchell emerged from the restroom. Leaning against the doorway, he curiously asked what Klein was writing.

“Special application.”

“To advance to Sequence 8?”

Leonard was startled when Klein confirmed that he had already fully digested the potion.

“You’re already done?”

Klein raised an eyebrow and reminded him of something Leonard had once said—that certain people were simply special, capable of doing what others could not.

“Such as me. Such as you.”

Leonard grew serious and asked whether Klein thought the advancement was too risky. Klein again described the strange coincidences they had encountered, prompting Leonard to mutter that perhaps he needed to hurry as well.

Then Leonard’s expression suddenly brightened.

“Aren’t you going to share your experience with us? The experience of quickly grasping a potion while avoiding the danger of losing control?”

Klein readily agreed. He had intended to use this opportunity to discreetly guide his fellow Nighthawks toward the acting method, though he could not explain it as openly as he had to Dunn.

Leonard immediately dragged him to the recreation room, where Frye, Kenley, and Seeka Tron were playing cards.

“Everyone!” Leonard dramatically announced. “Let me introduce Mr. Klein Moretti, who has fully grasped his potion in a month and a half!”

Klein found the introduction painfully theatrical. The others were skeptical until Klein confirmed that he had indeed felt a clear indication that his potion was completely digested.

Seeka, usually reserved and elegant, became visibly eager.

“How did you do it?”

Klein sat down and explained that he had found inspiration in a maxim of the Mystery Pryers.

“Do as you wish, but do no harm?”

Leonard supplied the phrase.

Klein agreed and explained that, according to confidential information, following that principle reduced a Mystery Pryer’s chance of losing control. Madam Daly’s example had then given him an even clearer understanding. She had advanced from Corpse Collector to Spirit Medium in only two years because she had been determined to become a true Spirit Medium.

Klein said his own experience at the Divination Club had helped him develop corresponding Seer principles. By strictly following those principles and genuinely trying to become a real Seer, he discovered that his potion digestion accelerated dramatically.

The others fell into thought, clearly taking his words seriously.

“I’m going to submit my special application,” Klein said. “If you have any questions, ask me privately.”

He then returned to Dunn’s office, signed the application, and pressed his thumbprint onto it before handing it over.

Dunn carefully read the document.

“I’ll submit it to the Holy Cathedral as soon as possible. Be prepared for an examination. Perhaps next week, or the week after.”

Klein nodded solemnly.

“Alright.”

After leaving the office and closing the door behind him, Klein could not help wondering what kind of examination awaited him.
