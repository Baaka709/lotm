Chapter 273

Chapter 273 - Handing Out A Formula

612 words · 4 min read

Five minutes before eight on Wednesday night, Klein reached the alley behind Bravehearts Bar.

He had disguised himself carefully in a gray-blue worker’s uniform, cap, beard, and half-mask. The clothing had been specially modified to contain numerous pockets for charms, herbs, and other materials.

He wanted nothing to connect this identity with the mysterious newcomer who had previously bought Black Snake’s item.

It would be best if Eye of Wisdom and the Apothecary don’t recognize me.

Using his Clown abilities, Klein deliberately changed his facial expressions, movements, physical habits, and even his walking posture.

He then approached the gathering’s entrance and performed the secret knocking sequence.

The door opened almost immediately.

A hidden eye inspected him from head to toe before the attendant handed him a black hooded robe.

Klein put it on, making his way toward the living room with an unfamiliar gait.

The room was dark, illuminated by a single candle, and filled with Beyonders.

Unlike his previous visits, Klein deliberately sat on a high stool in the center.

He wanted everything about this identity to be completely different.

When Eye of Wisdom announced the start of the gathering, Klein spoke first in a hoarse voice.

“I want to sell potion formulas.

“They are Sequence 9 Bard and Sequence 8 Light Suppliant from the Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun.

“The former is 220 pounds and the latter is 450 pounds.”

Klein had deliberately chosen these formulas. The Spectator and Telepathist formulas were connected to the active Psychology Alchemists, making them risky to sell. His own Seer pathway was even more sensitive, while the Eternal Blazing Sun’s Beyonders were relatively uncommon in Backlund due to the influence of the Lord of Storms Church.

As Klein waited, he reflected on the strengths and weaknesses of low-Sequence pathways.

Different pathways possessed highly specialized abilities. A Spectator might lack direct combat strength, but could manipulate an opponent so subtly that the enemy might die without understanding what had happened.

Likewise, even a comparatively weak Beyonder could defeat a stronger opponent if they understood the enemy’s weaknesses in advance.

Such situations were dangerous enough that grouped attacks could be extremely effective.

The Shepherd pathway stood out because of its versatility and lack of obvious weaknesses.

Klein hoped to reach Sequence 7 soon. He believed that with his future abilities, he would be able to fight a Sequence 6 for a time and perhaps survive against a Sequence 5.

Eventually, the Apothecary spoke.

“I can consider buying it.”

A second man also showed interest, suggesting he might give the formulas to his child.

The man offered 200 pounds for the Bard formula and 400 for Light Suppliant.

Klein pretended to think before adding a condition: the buyer could not resell the formulas at this particular gathering.

The buyer agreed, with Eye of Wisdom witnessing the arrangement.

Klein handed the formulas to an attendant, who gave them to Eye of Wisdom for verification.

Eye of Wisdom removed an elaborate ring containing a dark green gemstone shaped like an eye. When he touched the formulas with it, brilliant golden light emerged, producing a holographic projection.

“Authentic and effective!”

Klein immediately recognized that the artifact appeared to possess a form of notarization ability.

The deal was completed, and Klein received a stack of ten-pound notes.

He carefully counted the money three times.

The Apothecary then announced that he had brought sedatives, though Klein deliberately ignored him so as not to reveal his identity.

Afterward, a hooded woman introduced two Beyonder weapons.

Klein’s attention sharpened immediately.

The weapon she sold last time had been exactly what he needed, and he hoped these new items might satisfy The Sun’s requirements.

Key Events

  • Klein disguises himself heavily to avoid being recognized at Eye of Wisdom’s gathering.
  • He offers the Sequence 9 Bard and Sequence 8 Light Suppliant formulas.
  • A wealthy attendee buys both formulas for 200 and 400 pounds respectively.
  • Klein adds a condition preventing the buyer from reselling them at the same gathering.
  • Eye of Wisdom uses a powerful ring to verify that the formulas are authentic.
  • Klein receives 600 pounds in cash.
  • The Apothecary again mentions sedatives and searches unsuccessfully for Spring of the Elves marrow crystals.
  • A hooded woman introduces two Beyonder weapons, catching Klein’s attention.
