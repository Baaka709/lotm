Chapter 272

Chapter 272 - Observing Each Other

604 words · 4 min read

Klein immediately considered the circumstances suspicious. The serial murders had already reached eleven victims, and yet the police were using a private detective to assist the investigation.

Isengard Stanton invited Klein into his carriage. A young brown-haired man sat inside as his assistant. Stanton deliberately kept the carriage door open and did not order the driver to move.

“What would you like to talk about?”

Stanton wanted to know what Klein had learned from following Lopez.

Klein initially invoked professional confidentiality.

Stanton offered one pound.

Then two.

Klein accepted and revealed the only useful thing he had heard.

“Ma’am Lopez attempted to order her subordinates to tell Capim that he’s not to send anyone over in the next few days.”

Stanton immediately understood the importance.

“Capim?”

“You know Capim?”

Stanton explained that Capim was one of Cherwood Borough’s most controversial magnates. Young girls frequently disappeared and later turned up in legal or illegal brothels. Rumors repeatedly connected these disappearances to Capim, yet he remained free because evidence was lacking and he had powerful connections.

Klein felt disgusted.

As he left, Stanton complimented his fighting ability and asked his name.

“Sherlock Moriarty.”

After Klein left, Stanton examined him more carefully and analyzed his unusual appearance. The cultured gold-rimmed glasses did not fit well with the intentionally rough beard and crude presentation.

Perhaps Klein was hiding something.

Meanwhile, Klein had been observing Stanton with Spirit Vision.

His emotions remained abnormally stable: rational blue thought and spiritually dominant purple colors appeared almost continuously, with very little emotional fluctuation.

Either Stanton was exceptionally talented at controlling himself, or he was a Beyonder.

Klein eventually transferred to another carriage and headed toward Iron Gate Street.

At Bravehearts Bar, he found Kaspars drinking.

“Is Maric here?”

Klein held Azik’s copper whistle in his pocket and used spirituality to suppress its effects.

The instant he mentioned Maric, several gazes turned toward him.

Once he asked whether Maric had come, those gazes shifted toward Kaspars.

Kaspars replied that Maric had not appeared that day or the previous day.

Klein immediately stopped suppressing the copper whistle.

Someone else was clearly searching for Maric.

The discovery caused him to reconsider Miss Bodyguard.

Why would a Sequence 5 Beyonder accept a three-day protection job for only a thousand pounds? Such a person would normally have enough influence and resources to avoid taking ordinary jobs.

Klein began to suspect that Miss Bodyguard and Maric might be hiding from a powerful organization. If something strong enough to hunt a Sequence 5 existed, it likely possessed Holy Artifacts or multiple Sequence 5 Beyonders.

Perhaps Maric had been exposed to the Machinery Hivemind.

Klein decided not to investigate further.

He casually claimed that he had wanted to play cards with Maric.

Kaspars mentioned that he would be playing Texas that evening.

Klein declined and left without asking about other Beyonder gatherings.

He later returned to his East Borough apartment and used divination above the gray fog to make sure no one had followed him.

Afterward, he returned to Minsk Street, collected the newspapers from his mailbox, and began reading.

Then he found an advertisement from the Ernst Firm.

To ordinary readers, it was simply a list of purchase prices for goods such as flour, butter, lard, cream, and tea.

To Klein, it was the code for a Beyonder gathering.

The meeting would be held at eight the following evening.

The door code was seven hard knocks, one light knock, six long intervals, and one short interval.

Klein leaned back and smiled.

He was eager to sell some potion formulas and use them to acquire valuable ingredients or mystical items.

Key Events

  • Stanton pays Klein for information about Lopez and identifies Capim as a major criminal suspect.
  • Stanton suspects Klein’s unusual appearance may conceal his real identity or background.
  • Klein detects unusually controlled emotional states from Stanton and suspects he may be a Beyonder.
  • At Bravehearts Bar, Klein realizes someone else is looking for Maric.
  • Klein theorizes that Miss Bodyguard and Maric may both be hiding from a powerful organization.
  • Klein avoids probing further into Maric’s situation.
