Chapter 180

Chapter 180 - A Smart Person Always Overthinks

920 words · 6 min read

Audrey immediately became attentive when Mr. Fool asked about the City of Silver’s beliefs. She was curious about the mysterious city but had always avoided prying into The Sun’s privacy. Now that Mr. Fool himself had raised the topic, she eagerly listened.

Derrick explained that the City of Silver did not worship any of the seven orthodox gods, Death, or the hidden evil entities known to The Hanged Man, such as the Primordial Demoness, Hidden Sage, Chained God, or True Creator. Instead, they worshipped the omnipotent and omniscient Creator.

Klein was not surprised. He deliberately asked, “Even though He abandoned you?”

The word immediately shocked Alger.

Abandoned?

His thoughts connected this statement with the mysterious “Forsaken Land of the Gods,” a place the Church of Storms had spent generations trying to understand and locate. Alger suddenly realized that The Sun was very likely from that legendary land—and that Mr. Fool had somehow been capable of drawing a person from there into the Tarot Club.

The realization left Alger deeply shaken.

Derrick answered that the City of Silver still believed they would eventually regain the Creator’s favor, perhaps on the day the sun rose again. He explained that their people had once been ruled by the giants’ royal family and worshipped the Giant King Aurmir, but had later been saved by the Creator. They would never betray their Lord again.

The mention of the Giant King immediately caught Klein’s attention. The name belonged to an ancient being remembered in legends and myths, and Klein wondered whether Aurmir had been the ancient God of Combat, especially since the current Church of the God of Combat controlled the Warrior pathway associated with the giants.

Klein then asked whether the City of Silver still performed sacrifices to the Creator.

Derrick answered yes, but said that since their abandonment, they had never received a response.

Klein asked him to describe the ritual in detail.

Alger immediately became extremely excited. He wondered whether Mr. Fool was attempting to investigate the truth behind the City of Silver’s abandonment or determine whether the Creator still existed. To Alger, this felt like being granted access to secrets normally reserved for gods.

Derrick explained their sacrificial ceremonies. The City of Silver built magnificent altars marked with the Creator’s symbol and performed sacrifices after successful harvests of Black-Faced Grass. They offered captured monsters from the darkness, first reciting the Creator’s honorific title and prayers, then dancing before killing the monsters and allowing their spirituality and tainted blood to cover the altar.

If no monsters were available, they used a sinner imprisoned on the lowest floor of the City of Silver.

They then prepared the first harvested Black-Faced Grass as food and presented it to the Creator before ending the ritual with collective hymns of praise.

Klein carefully analyzed every part of the ceremony using his own mysticism knowledge. He concluded that since he intended to offer sacrifices to himself, he would not need such elaborate timing or an extravagant altar. The critical element seemed to be creating a channel through the spirituality and blood of creatures containing Beyonder powers—assuming the being on the other end could actually receive the offering.

He then asked what language they used for the prayers and requested the exact wording.

Derrick explained that they used Jotun, the common language of the City of Silver. Their prayers were:

“Your devoted believers pray for your attention.

“We pray for you to take their offerings.

“We pray for you to open the gates to your Kingdom.”

Klein listened silently, deliberately maintaining an inscrutable posture while the gray fog swirled around him. He did not reveal what conclusions he had reached.

Alger accepted the silence as natural. Secrets concerning a deity could not simply be handed directly to mortals. Derrick likewise decided that he needed to grow stronger so that he could eventually obtain information valuable enough to exchange for Mr. Fool’s guidance.

After further discussion, Klein ended the Tarot Club gathering. Audrey, Alger, and Derrick disappeared from the ancient bronze table.

Once alone, Klein considered his own development. After advancing to Sequence 8, he discovered that he could connect to more crimson stars and therefore potentially invite additional members into the Tarot Club.

At least two more, he estimated.

He had no intention of rushing. Just as before, he would observe potential candidates and consider recommendations from Justice and The Hanged Man before deciding.

Klein then thought about the strange requirements behind summoning members into the space above the gray fog. He remembered that when he first encountered The Sun praying, a crystal ball had been visible before Derrick, but it had disappeared after Klein drew him into the mysterious realm.

This made Klein wonder whether each crimson star might correspond to a special item in the real world, with possession of such an item being necessary to establish a connection. Perhaps Justice and The Hanged Man had similar objects.

He considered another possibility: perhaps even someone without such an item could eventually be pulled into the gray fog if they sincerely recited Mr. Fool’s honorific name while Klein heard their prayer.

It was something he intended to test in the future.

Klein then left the palace above the gray fog. Because he had finally mastered the overflowing powers of the Clown potion and eliminated its negative effects, he decided to attempt his own summoning ritual once again.

As he descended through the madness and distant ravings, Klein felt both anticipation and apprehension.

What would he summon this time?
