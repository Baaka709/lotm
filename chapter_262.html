Chapter 262

Chapter 262 - Dream

814 words · 5 min read

Klein immediately decided that retreat was the only sensible option.

The evil spirit inside the underground room was likely more dangerous than Rosago or Qilangos and might have existed there for centuries or even thousands of years. It could potentially be comparable to a High-Sequence Beyonder.

Even with Miss Bodyguard’s strength, Klein did not believe they had any chance of defeating it.

Greed would only get them killed.

When Miss Bodyguard asked what they should do next, Klein quickly reasoned through the options.

He could ask Carter to contact the police, but simply saying there was an “evil spirit” might not make the authorities take the matter seriously. A poorly prepared expedition could lead to massive casualties and perhaps even free the spirit.

Worse, Klein himself had now seen the bizarre statues and secrets of the structure.

Someone might want to silence him.

He decided to investigate the former owners of the property and learn more about the viscount family before taking further action. If necessary, he could eventually obtain explosives or anonymously inform the authorities.

But there was no reason to rush.

Miss Bodyguard suggested that he find someone capable of exorcising the evil spirit. Even if there were no mystical items inside, the remains left behind after an evil spirit vanished could be extraordinarily valuable.

Klein refused.

“The risk is too high. I think my life and health are more important.”

He added that Miss Bodyguard was the strongest person he knew, and even she did not appear confident in defeating the creature.

Miss Bodyguard finally commented,

“You still have some sense.”

She floated away, and Klein followed.

After exiting the ancient chamber, he closed the stone door, leaving the inverted candlesticks and bizarre divine statues sealed within their darkness.

Back in the basement, Millet Carter anxiously awaited his report.

Klein deliberately frightened him with a harmless explanation.

“There are a lot of snakes in there, and many places have collapsed. I need to gather information and manpower before attempting another exploration. You absolutely shouldn’t send anyone inside.”

Carter was alarmed enough to agree.

He asked whether the snakes might escape, and whether Klein knew anyone who could deal with them.

Klein reassured him that the cold season would keep the snakes inactive as long as no one disturbed them.

Then he asked about the former owner of the house.

“Viscount Pound.”

Carter explained that the Pound family had gained its title during the Battle of the Violated Oath. The family had once been prominent but began declining decades later. Its heirs repeatedly disappeared or died, forcing distant relatives to inherit the title. The current Viscount Pound had squandered most of the family fortune and had been demoted to baronet.

Klein immediately recognized the historical significance.

The Battle of the Violated Oath had been a major religious war roughly six centuries earlier. Feynapotter had originally supported both Mother Earth and the God of Knowledge and Wisdom, but conflicts between their churches had eventually led Loen and Intis to intervene. Feysac later entered the war, but the conflict ended with several regions becoming independent.

Because the war involved religion, Beyonders from the churches must have played important roles.

Klein wondered whether the later collapse of the Pound family might have been connected to the underground structure.

Unfortunately, Carter did not know where Baronet Pound currently lived.

Klein returned home, ate a surprisingly successful bowl of homemade Feynapotter-style noodles, and performed a divination about whether he should notify the police.

The result was negative.

That night, Klein fell into an ordinary dream before realizing with a jolt that he was dreaming.

Someone was invading his dream.

He found himself in a blazing yellow desert.

A colossal black-and-gold dragon descended from the sky, its enormous wings blotting out the sun. Its scales were immense, and its dark-golden eyes were terrifying.

The dragon opened its mouth and released a beam of pure light, annihilating enormous sections of desert.

Then a giant knight sprang into the air.

He was three or four meters tall, dressed in black armor stained with blood. He wielded a huge broadsword, and countless bluish-white flames condensed into spear-like projections that shot toward the dragon.

The knight leaped onto the dragon’s head and brought his sword down.

Lightning-like sword rays intertwined.

The ground shook.

The dragon crashed down, spilling dark-golden blood.

Then the scene changed.

A huge bloody door appeared—the same door Klein had seen deep within the underground structure.

It slowly opened.

Behind it sat a man of ordinary height on a high-backed chair.

As the dream moved closer, Klein realized that the man was the same knight who had slain the dragon. Yet he was no longer gigantic.

He suddenly raised his head.

His handsome young face was covered in horrifying signs of decay, and his eyes were cold and emotionless.

Klein jolted awake.

Crimson moonlight was shining through the curtains.

Key Events

  • Klein decides the imprisoned evil spirit is far too dangerous to confront.
  • He plans to investigate the Pound family and possibly use the police or explosives later.
  • Klein deceives Millet Carter by claiming the underground danger consists mainly of snakes and collapsed passages.
  • Carter reveals that the property belonged to Viscount Pound, whose family declined mysteriously after once being prominent.
  • Klein connects the Pound family’s history to the ancient structure and the Battle of the Violated Oath.
  • Klein divines that he should not immediately inform the police.
  • During sleep, Klein enters a mysterious dream involving a giant black-and-gold dragon and a gigantic armored knight.
  • The knight kills the dragon, then appears seated before the bloody door from the underground structure.
  • The knight’s face has become horribly decayed, and Klein awakens in fear.
  • The dream strongly links the underground structure, the bloody door, and the mysterious knight.
