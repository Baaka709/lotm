The gray fog remained as eternal and unchanging as ever, with illusory crimson stars scattered throughout the vast space at different distances. Seated within the colossal palace, Klein summoned a yellow-brown goatskin and carefully rewrote the incantation for his experiment.

He altered the ritual so that the candle represented himself, enclosed the room in a spiritual wall, and used various ingredients to simulate a summoning ritual. At the bottom, he wrote his divination question:

“There will be danger if the ritual above is carried out outside of this world.”

Holding his topaz pendant over the statement, Klein entered a state of Cogitation and repeated the question seven times. When he opened his eyes, the pendant was slowly spinning counterclockwise.

A negative answer.

“There will be danger…” meant no—there would be no danger.

“I can give it a try then.”

Klein returned to his bedroom and began the ritual. The sealed room darkened except for the dim mint-scented candle he had designated as his representation. After dripping the ritual ingredients onto the flame, he stepped back and loudly shouted in Jotun:

“I!”

Then he switched to Hermes.

“I summon in my name: The Fool that doesn’t belong to this era, the mysterious ruler above the gray fog; the King of Yellow and Black who wields good luck.”

The candle immediately erupted into strange motion. Its flame spun violently, while the surrounding fragrance formed a vortex that rapidly absorbed Klein’s spirituality. Enduring the discomfort, he finished the incantation.

The flame suddenly became still, now covered by a gray radiance about the size of a palm.

“I didn’t summon anything... Oh right, perhaps I’ll need to respond to it above the gray fog? It’s really quite troublesome to summon myself...”

After regaining his composure, Klein returned above the gray fog. There, he noticed rippling light emerging from the mysterious symbol carved into the back of the seat of honor—the symbol combining a pupil-less eye, secrecy, and distorted lines representing change.

He reached toward it.

Immediately, he heard his own summoning incantation echo through the palace. Surging spirituality and the rippling light condensed into an illusory, shapeless door. It trembled as though desperate to open.

Klein instinctively willed it to move.

The boundless gray fog and enormous palace seemed to surge forward with him, sending faint ripples toward the mysterious door. Yet no matter how strongly he pushed, it remained immovable, surrounded by absolute silence.

“The Door of Summoning has yet to take shape?”

Klein quickly analyzed the failure and realized that his spirituality was insufficient. He could not yet create a complete Door of Summoning. Perhaps after advancing to Sequence 8, becoming a Clown, and surviving the dangerous initial stage, he would have enough strength to try again.

Even so, the experiment had given him tremendous confidence. This was the first time the mysterious space above the gray fog had directly responded to him, aside from the earlier incident involving his divination of the Eternal Blazing Sun.

“There will come a day when I’ll understand all the secrets here!”

Filled with excitement, Klein descended from the gray fog, returned to his bedroom, extinguished the candle, dismantled the ritual, and removed the spiritual wall. A gust of wind passed through the room as he yawned. Completely exhausted, he fell into bed and quickly drifted into sleep.

In his hazy dream, Klein suddenly found himself sitting in his own living room, holding a copy of the Tingen City Honest Paper.

*Don’t tell me Captain is here again?*

The thought had barely formed when the door creaked open.

Dunn Smith entered in his familiar black windbreaker, carrying his cane and pipe. His black top hat remained on his head, beneath which his deep gray eyes quietly watched Klein. Dunn sat down on the single-seat sofa, crossed one leg over the other, removed his hat, and leaned back.

He simply stared.

Klein immediately understood that he was dreaming, but he pretended not to notice. He kept reading the newspaper.

One minute passed.

Then two.

Then five.

Dunn remained silent, his gaze fixed on Klein.

*Captain, what are you trying to do?*

Trying to make the dream seem natural, Klein continued reading, occasionally glancing at Dunn. Ten minutes passed. Then fifteen. The Captain still had not moved.

Unable to endure the pressure, Klein put the newspaper aside and began cleaning instead. He wiped the dining table, the coffee table, then other furniture, silently pleading for the Nightmare to leave.

*Look, my dream is completely ordinary and boring. There’s nothing worth observing. Hurry up and leave! Why don’t you pretend to be a ghost and I’ll pretend to be scared? Then you can finish your Nightmare business!*

But Dunn continued staring.

Klein cleaned everything he could think of, eventually even cleaning his room. The exhausting part of the nightmare was not the work—it was knowing that Dunn Smith was quietly watching every movement.

At last, Dunn uncrossed his legs and stood. He picked up his cane, put his hat back on, and walked toward the door.

Klein held his breath until the Captain finally left.

He instinctively raised a hand and waved goodbye.

“Phew...”

Once the nightmare returned to normal, Klein could finally relax.

*That really was such a nightmare!*

...

Meanwhile, in West Backlund, Audrey entered Philip’s Department Store with her maid Annie and her golden retriever, Susie.

Philip’s was among the most prestigious department stores in the Loen Kingdom, admitting only nobles and wealthy members. Luxurious carriages lined the entrance, and the location had become as much a social gathering place as a shopping venue.

Audrey gracefully greeted the various noblewomen she encountered, smoothly complimenting dresses, discussing political accomplishments, and exchanging appropriate pleasantries. In the past, her stubbornness and arrogance had made such interactions difficult. Now, as a Spectator, she could read much of what people were thinking and feeling from their expressions, allowing her to respond almost effortlessly.

On the second floor, she entered a ready-made dress shop. The petite blond attendant behind the counter was actually Xio Derecha, the Arbiter.

Audrey subtly signaled Susie. The golden retriever immediately ran toward another counter, and Annie followed after her.

With the distraction secured, Audrey approached Xio while pretending to inspect dresses.

“Why did you arrange to meet me here?” Xio whispered as she continued loudly presenting the clothing.

Instead of answering directly, Audrey asked about the shop’s usual attendant.

Xio explained that she had persuaded the woman to take the morning off.

Audrey secretly removed a folded piece of paper from her handbag and passed it to Xio. On it was a remarkably detailed portrait.

“Rear Admiral Hurricane, Qilangos, has snuck into Backlund. This is his portrait. I hope you can find him for me. Oh, and don’t alert him.”

Xio unfolded the paper and saw a lifelike image of a man in his thirties with a distinctive broad chin.

Audrey recalled proudly that her art teacher had once praised her skill.

Then she added the most tempting part:

“The Kingdom offers a reward of ten thousand pounds for Qilangos. If he’s arrested, even someone who only provides clues should receive a few hundred pounds.”

Just as Audrey expected, Xio’s eyes immediately lit up with delight.

Key Events

  • Klein divines that performing his modified summoning ritual outside the gray fog would not be dangerous.
  • His ritual produces a response from the gray fog and briefly manifests an incomplete “Door of Summoning.”
  • Klein realizes he lacks sufficient spirituality to complete the door and decides to try again after advancing to Sequence 8.
  • Klein dreams of Dunn silently watching him for an unbearably long time before finally leaving.
  • In Backlund, Audrey secretly meets the Arbiter Xio Derecha at a department store.
  • Audrey gives Xio a portrait of Rear Admiral Hurricane Qilangos and asks her to secretly track him.
  • Xio becomes highly interested after Audrey reveals the enormous bounty offered for Qilangos.
