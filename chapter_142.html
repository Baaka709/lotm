Chapter 142

Chapter 142

1,053 words · 7 min read

Klein knew better than to begin by bluntly asking Daxter for the Telepathist formula. A Spectator would never be fooled so easily.

Instead, he started with Hood Eugen, the Psychology Alchemists member living in the asylum.

“Has Hood Eugen behaved unusually recently?”

Daxter studied Klein’s expression and gestures before answering.

“No. He’s completely normal. Frankly, I think he could leave the asylum and behave like an ordinary person whenever he wishes, but he chooses to remain. He seems to spend his time treating other patients. Those with chaotic, violent, or abnormal thoughts have been improving. Perhaps Hood Eugen is using them to train his Beyonder powers.”

Klein immediately began analyzing the implication.

If Hood Eugen was a Psychiatrist, probably Sequence 7 or higher, his continued stay in the asylum might indeed be a form of acting. The Psychology Alchemists apparently had not discovered the true acting method, because even a relatively important member like Hood Eugen seemed unaware of it.

Klein then realized something more significant.

The Church of the Evernight Goddess was far older than the Psychology Alchemists, and yet as far as Klein knew, it had never openly described the acting method either. That seemed impossible.

Spirit Medium Daly was a perfect example. She had apparently digested her potion remarkably quickly by behaving in accordance with its name, yet nobody around her seemed to realize that her experience represented a reproducible principle.

Klein reasoned that someone within the Church must have discovered this long ago—or that the higher-ups deliberately kept it hidden.

Unless Daly had independently rediscovered the method, there was simply no way an ancient organization like the Evernight Church could have accumulated centuries of successful examples without eventually recognizing the pattern.

He decided he would later search the Church’s historical records for Beyonders who had rapidly digested their potions and examine what had happened to them afterward.

Daxter eventually asked whether Hood Eugen’s behavior represented a problem.

“Not right now,” Klein replied. “It simply made me think of something else.”

He then asked whether the Psychology Alchemists had taken any recent action.

“Nothing important,” Daxter answered. “There was only a small gathering in Awwa for exchanging items and experiences.”

Klein then turned to Daxter himself.

“How are you doing?”

Daxter’s expression became serious.

“Not well. I still hear ravings and experience illusions. If I weren’t a psychiatrist, I might think I had developed a mental disorder. I’ve followed Hood Eugen’s and your advice and ignored them, which has helped. But they still disturb my sleep, and I’ve become irritable and short-tempered. It feels as though another personality is growing inside me. I’m afraid I might lose control someday.”

Klein had expected this.

“You don’t need to worry. You’re now a subsidiary member of the Nighthawks, and that comes with benefits. We have methods that can help Beyonders resist losing control. They aren’t perfect, but they can certainly help.”

He then exaggerated slightly about his own experience.

“You’re speaking to someone who used only a month to free himself from illusions and ravings. Hood Eugen and the other cadres should have told you how difficult that normally is.”

Daxter calmly exposed the weakness in Klein’s claim.

“There’s a little lie in what you said, but it’s mostly true. What do you want from me?”

Klein smiled.

“It isn’t something only I want.”

He deliberately allowed Daxter to assume that the Nighthawks themselves had an interest in the request.

Daxter considered his answer carefully.

“If your method really works, and what you want is within my reach, I’ll do my best.”

Klein answered directly.

“We want the Telepathist formula.”

He already had a plan. The formula would be exchanged for Klein’s personal experience in controlling the potion’s negative effects. Klein would verify and remember the formula during the transaction, then report the deal to Dunn and gain Nighthawk merit for it.

One formula could therefore serve two purposes.

Daxter looked at him for a moment before agreeing.

“I’ll try to obtain it. But I can’t promise how long it will take. If it becomes too dangerous, I hope I can replace it with something else.”

“No problem.”

Klein then explained the acting method in broad terms.

“The key to resisting loss of control lies in the name of the potion. You have to understand what that name truly means. That understanding cannot come simply through thought; it has to come through experience.

“For example, as a Spectator, you must understand that you are a spectator, not an actor. You need to determine for yourself how a Spectator should behave, experiment until you discover the underlying principles, and then follow them strictly.”

Daxter listened carefully.

“That’s an entirely new way of looking at things. I’d call what you described a theory, like a theory of theater or opera. I’ll try it, and I hope it works.

“If it really does, I’ll do my best to obtain the Telepathist formula.”

“May the Goddess protect you.”

Klein drew the crimson moon over his chest.

He deliberately did not ask Daxter for the Psychiatrist formula. Daxter did not yet hold a high enough position within the Psychology Alchemists, and pressing him further could expose him.

Instead, Klein intended to strengthen Daxter’s position gradually and collect greater rewards later.

Once the conversation ended, Klein quietly checked the corridor before leaving and entering the Nighthawks’ shooting range.

The moment the door locked behind him, his expression became grave.

His earlier thoughts about the acting method had led him to another realization.

He had learned two crucial facts separately:

The Church of the Evernight Goddess had destroyed the Antigonus family.

And the Antigonus family had controlled most of the Seer pathway.

Why, then, had the Evernight Church apparently only obtained the Sequence 9 Seer formula?

That made no sense.

The Antigonus family should have possessed a large portion of the higher Seer Sequence formulas. If the Aurora Order had once obtained the Clown formula from an Antigonus magical notebook, then the Church that annihilated the entire family should have obtained even more.

Even if the family had hidden its most valuable possessions at the highest peak of the Hornacis mountain range, the Evernight Church should still have gained substantial spoils.

After all, they were the ones who killed the family.

And the dead could be made to speak.
