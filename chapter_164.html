Chapter 164 - Miserable Wretches

1,078 words · 7 min read

Fresh blood streamed beneath Old Neil’s door as Klein stared at it in horror. A cough came from within, followed by Neil’s raspy voice.

“Dunn, why are you here?”

Dunn calmly replied that they had heard Neil was sick and had come to visit. The house fell silent. Then Neil suddenly shouted in anger and terror, “No! You’re lying!” His voice weakened almost immediately.

“Yes, I know my condition isn’t quite right.”

Neil hurriedly insisted that he had never harmed anyone or betrayed the Nighthawks’ secrets. At worst, he had filed dishonest expense claims. He desperately reminded Klein of the Mystery Pryers’ maxim he had always followed: “Do as you wish, but do no harm.” He begged them to leave, swearing that he would be normal again by tomorrow and would never hurt anyone.

Dunn gently asked what he had been trying to accomplish.

Neil hesitated before answering with renewed hope. He was trying to resurrect Celeste, the woman he had once failed to save. He believed his failure years ago had only been because he lacked sufficient mystical knowledge. Now, he claimed, he had finally found the correct method. If he had been a High-Sequence Beyonder back then, everything would have been simple. His voice became tearful as he pleaded again for Dunn to leave him alone.

Dunn asked how he intended to resurrect her.

Neil eagerly explained his plan. He would use the “Alchemical Life” method to create an immortal body, something associated with the Sequence 4 Beyonders of the Church of Mother Earth, then summon Celeste’s spirit from the spirit world and pray for divine assistance to unite the spirit with the new body.

“Isn’t it a great idea?”

Dunn forced a smile and agreed that it was, asking Neil to open the door so they could help. Neil immediately became frightened again. He begged Dunn to leave, even promising not to steal his coffee beans anymore. He promised Klein and Royale that he would never again make them help with his fraudulent expense claims.

Dunn continued speaking gently, insisting they were not there to punish him. Neil was their teammate, and because he was sick, they had to see him with their own eyes. After all, the Nighthawks already had countless missions to handle, including monitoring the asylum and responding to sudden incidents. Neil hesitated, then finally agreed.

The door creaked open.

Klein wiped his eyes and looked inside. The carpet was soaked in blood and tangled with hair. The entire living room was covered in the same sticky, bloody substance—the floor, ceiling, furniture, and even the piano. Neil’s head hung suspended from the ceiling by a thick strand of the substance. Four additional eyes, cold and devoid of eyelashes, stared from his forehead and cheeks.

The piano keys moved by themselves, producing a beautiful melody.

“Dunn, look. I’m really okay,” Neil said with a radiant smile. “Royale, Klein, you think so too, right?”

When he opened his mouth, Klein saw the same bloody, hairy substance filling it.

Dunn continued talking as though nothing were wrong, asking Neil where he had learned the resurrection ritual. Neil said that he had heard it as though someone had whispered it directly into his ears. He had tested the first part and confirmed that it worked. It was a gift from God.

Then his voice suddenly faltered.

“He is... He is the Hidden Sage...”

Klein immediately recognized the name. The Hidden Sage was the non-anthropomorphic god worshipped by the Moses Ascetic Order, an existence associated with resurrection, evil, corruption, and the complete Mystery Pryer pathway.

Mentioning the name seemed to awaken something inside Neil. He looked around and finally seemed to understand what had become of him. His six eyes focused on Dunn, and he gave a bitter smile.

“So it turns out—it turns out that I’ve already become a monster...”

He begged them to let him go. He would disappear into the mountains, never harm anyone, and quietly continue his ritual. But then something illusory seemed to shatter before Klein’s eyes.

Neil’s four extra eyes suddenly flashed darkly and fixed themselves upon Dunn.

“You’re pulling me into a dream! No, it’s useless! My eyes can see through all of that!”

The blood coating the room began to writhe violently, like some enormous creature opening its jaws. Neil’s head blurred into overlapping images. Klein reached into his pocket for his Slumber Charm.

Then everything abruptly became still.

The bloody substance settled like a perfectly calm lake.

Neil’s expression emptied itself of hatred, fear, desire, and every other emotion. He became utterly peaceful.

At some point, Dunn had thrown Sealed Artifact 3-0611 into the blood.

The Peaceful Hair Strands had taken effect.

Neil’s four unnatural eyes slowly closed. Dunn, Klein, and Royale immediately drew their guns and aimed at his head. Yet Neil’s face suddenly filled with terrible fear as he struggled against the artifact’s effect. His extra eyes vanished, leaving only the old, familiar Neil Klein remembered: white hair, deep wrinkles, and turbid crimson eyes.

Neil began recalling their shared past.

“Dunn, do you remember the time I saved you...?”

“Royale, do you remember when I helped you redeem your family’s lives...?”

“Klein, do you remember how I taught you mysticism every day? Do you remember when we talked about how to make claims? Do you remember how I made you hand-ground coffee? Do you remember when we fought against a Mandated Punisher Rampager?”

His desperate voice echoed through Klein’s mind. Klein’s hand trembled around his revolver. He could barely force himself to pull the trigger.

Bang! Bang!

Two silver demon-hunting bullets tore through Neil’s head.

Klein watched the familiar face collapse into despair as the bullets shattered his skull, spraying blood and flesh across the room. Slowly, the sticky substance coating the surroundings began withdrawing, flowing back toward Neil’s ruined head before his remains collapsed to the ground.

Silence followed.

Neil’s corpse rapidly deteriorated into a mass of rotten flesh. Yet amid the blood and meat, Klein could still see a pair of crimson, crystal-clear eyes filled with unbearable pain.

Everything felt unreal.

Klein stood frozen, unable to accept how quickly they had gone from trying to save their teammate to killing him. Dunn stepped forward, his normally upright body stooped beneath the weight of what they had done.

Looking down at Old Neil’s remains, he murmured heavily, “We are guardians, but also a bunch of miserable wretches that are constantly fighting against threats and madness.”

Key Events

  • Old Neil admits he knows something is wrong with him but insists he has never intentionally harmed anyone.
  • He reveals that he is attempting to resurrect Celeste through an “Alchemical Life” body and a ritual to reunite her spirit with it.
  • Neil reveals that the Hidden Sage taught or communicated the resurrection method to him, exposing the source of his corruption.
  • Neil realizes that he has already become a monster, but then detects Dunn’s attempt to pull him into a dream and launches an attack.
  • Dunn uses Sealed Artifact 3-0611, the Peaceful Hair Strands, to suppress Neil’s emotions and immobilize him.
  • Before dying, Neil recalls his memories and bonds with Dunn, Royale, and Klein, making Klein hesitate to shoot.
  • Dunn, Klein, and Royale kill Old Neil with silver demon-hunting bullets.
  • Dunn reflects bitterly on the Nighthawks’ existence as “guardians” who are themselves miserable people constantly fighting madness.
