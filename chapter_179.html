Chapter 179 - Praising Mr. Fool

823 words · 5 min read

Alger was relieved when Mr. Fool granted him permission to report Qilangos-related discoveries through a ritual. Klein, meanwhile, was genuinely curious about both Qilangos’s objective and the mysterious item powerful enough to elevate a Sequence 6 Wind-blessed to combat strength comparable to a Sequence 4.

Although Klein had become more confident now that he possessed actual allies and had Azik somewhere in Backlund, he remained cautious about relying on Azik. Their relationship was cooperative rather than binding, and Klein still did not know exactly how powerful the amnesiac Azik was. If his lost memories had also taken away knowledge necessary for his abilities, his strength might have been significantly reduced.

Still, Azik’s enormous skeletal messenger reassured Klein that even if he could not defeat Qilangos and the Creeping Hunger directly, he would probably be capable of protecting himself and potentially rescuing Audrey, Alger, and the others.

Alger then revealed that he expected to obtain another batch of Emperor Roselle’s diary pages soon. Because Pritz Harbor was under the Backlund diocese of the Church of Storms, Alger could remain in the capital under the pretense of reporting on his previous voyage. Given the Church’s long history in Backlund and its enormous information network, he was confident he could acquire more diary pages under the guise of research.

Klein was pleased by the prospect, although he was already worried about what he would need to offer Alger in return. It was impossible to know in advance whether the coming diary pages would be valuable enough to satisfy him.

Audrey then asked for permission to similarly contact Mr. Fool directly through rituals whenever she obtained timely and useful information.

Klein immediately approved her request.

Derrick, The Sun, received the same permission.

The discussion then turned to Audrey’s search for the complete pituitary gland of a Rainbow Salamander, one of the main ingredients needed for the Telepathist potion. Alger did not possess it, while Derrick had only encountered the creature in textbooks.

Audrey was secretly envious of the structured knowledge available through the City of Silver’s books compared with the fragmented information she had to gather through conversations and Beyonder gatherings.

Alger then said that he might be able to obtain the Rainbow Salamander’s pituitary gland—but only if Qilangos was successfully located. He proposed treating the ingredient as additional compensation owed to Audrey. He explained that Rainbow Salamanders were nearly extinct and could mainly be found on primitive islands in the Sea of Fog, Berserk Sea, or Sonia Sea. Very few people possessed the coordinates to those islands.

Audrey immediately became excited and admitted that she had always dreamed of traveling to such untouched islands.

Klein was also intrigued. He remembered an entry from Emperor Roselle’s diary describing how the Emperor had discovered an unnamed island filled with supernatural creatures while crossing the Sea of Fog with his Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. It might have been one of the “primitive islands,” but Roselle had unfortunately left no coordinates in the diary page Klein possessed.

Derrick, meanwhile, found the discussion increasingly bewildering. The Sea of Fog, Berserk Sea, primitive islands, and other geographical references made him feel that Audrey and Alger inhabited a completely different world from his isolated City of Silver.

Audrey then asked whether the near-extinction of Rainbow Salamanders meant the Spectator pathway would eventually be cut off.

Alger assured her that substitute materials would inevitably exist.

When Audrey asked what they were, Alger refused to disclose the information for free. He suggested that the Psychology Alchemists might know.

Audrey ultimately decided not to pursue the transaction. She reasoned that the information was not immediately useful and that becoming too deeply involved in Alger’s pursuit of Qilangos would be dangerous.

Klein, however, was disappointed. He was extremely curious about the hidden information behind Alger’s claim.

He began considering what the near-extinction of Rainbow Salamanders implied. If the Psychology Alchemists still distributed formulas requiring the creature’s organs instead of listing substitute materials, perhaps they possessed the coordinates to certain primitive islands—or were cooperating with an organization that did.

After the trading discussion ended, Klein turned his attention to The Sun.

He asked whether the City of Silver still worshipped gods.

The question was carefully chosen. Klein himself lacked access to advanced ritual knowledge, especially the deeper principles behind sacrificial rituals. Since he needed to understand how offerings dedicated to himself worked, particularly because he intended to use the mysterious space above the gray fog to transfer materials, he needed to obtain this knowledge from somewhere.

He had considered asking Daly, but that might expose his unusual interest. Azik was another possibility, though Klein could not guarantee that his forgotten memories included the relevant ritual knowledge. The Sun therefore offered another route.

Klein planned to frame his questions around gods and theology so that his inquiries would appear perfectly natural.

Derrick answered respectfully:

“We still believe in the Lord that created everything, the omnipotent and omniscient God.”
