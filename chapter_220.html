Chapter 220

Chapter 220 - Kleins Sacrificial Trial

882 words · 6 min read

Audrey immediately accepted The Fool’s proposed “attempt,” though she maintained her usual elegant restraint. She remembered that whenever Mr. Fool had used that particular word in the past, the result had been extraordinary.

Alger reacted very differently. The word made him extremely wary. He immediately wondered what The Fool wanted, what His true purpose was, and whether the proposed experiment would benefit or threaten him.

After thinking about Qilangos’s grotesquely rotting corpse, he respectfully replied, “Your wish is my wish.”

Derrick watched the two carefully, confused by their strong reactions.

Klein explained that the experiment would make the transfer of the Beyonder material easier, safer, and more confidential.

“Do you remember the sacrificial ritual described by The Sun?”

Alger remembered. His instincts immediately tightened because, in the Fifth Epoch, sacrificial rituals were commonly associated with evil gods and devils rather than orthodox deities.

Klein then revealed his plan: Alger would sacrifice the Rainbow Salamander’s pituitary gland to The Fool, and Klein would bestow it upon Audrey. This would allow the two parties to exchange the material without directly revealing themselves.

Audrey was astonished by the simplicity and grandeur of the method. To her, the Tarot Club seemed to possess an almost divine means of exchanging mystical materials.

Alger remained cautious and asked what he had to do.

Klein explained that the experiment might fail and that Alger would need to prepare a simple altar bearing the symbol of the pupil-less eye and distorted lines associated with The Fool.

The symbol appeared before the members in a curtain of light.

Klein had already conducted earlier experiments and concluded that his ritual could create an illusory gate above the gray fog, resembling the Door of Summoning. He could not stabilize the channel by himself, but the mysterious space above the gray fog should be able to complete the process. He estimated a roughly ninety percent chance of success.

The remaining uncertainty was whether materials possessing ordinary spirituality would be enough, or whether genuine Beyonder materials would be necessary.

Klein decided that Justice and The Hanged Man could bear the experimental costs. After all, the Tarot Club itself had been born from an attempt, and further attempts were inevitable.

He instructed them to remember the symbol, telling Audrey cheerfully that she could simply pray to him if she forgot.

The second stage was equally straightforward. They were to follow the normal ritual procedure without extra herbs or holy oil, chant The Fool’s name in ancient Hermes or Jotun, and recite:

“Your devoted servant prays for your attention,

I pray for you to take his offerings.

I pray for you to open the gates to your Kingdom.”

Afterward, the spiritual materials were to be combined with the natural power generated by the invocation. If that failed, they were to replace them with actual Beyonder materials and repeat the process.

Alger immediately interpreted the ritual as evidence that The Fool was trapped or restricted in some way. He imagined that the repeated “attempts” were designed to help The Fool gradually overcome those restrictions and perhaps eventually descend into the real world.

Yet the existence of a powerful adorer complicated that theory. Why would The Fool need the Tarot Club to conduct these experiments when His adorer could perform similar actions? Alger concluded that secrets remained hidden from him and that perhaps the adorer was being watched by dangerous existences.

Klein asked when Alger intended to perform the sacrifice, explaining that the stable passage would only remain available for a very short time.

Alger answered that he had the Rainbow Salamander material and numerous spiritually active materials. He would immediately attempt the first version after the gathering and, if necessary, seek more Beyonder materials.

He then glanced toward Audrey.

She understood at once.

“If any additional Beyonder materials are required, I’ll compensate you afterward,” she promised.

Klein agreed and told Audrey that once the sacrifice succeeded, he would inform her of the bestowment ceremony.

Audrey immediately imagined how useful the same system might be for future money and item transfers.

Then she raised another matter. She had discovered two suitable women in Backlund—both Beyonders, both connected to their own circles and resources, both capable of keeping secrets. She asked whether they could join the Tarot Club.

Alger immediately wondered whether Justice was trying to strengthen her influence inside the organization. Derrick also became curious.

Klein hesitated. His original plan had been for the members to remain ignorant of each other’s identities, allowing them to develop separate networks and protecting the Tarot Club if one member were captured.

But Audrey’s proposal was attractive. Meeting Beyonders with different circles and resources was precisely what he needed to uncover information about the Secret Order and Lanevus.

Still, Klein was not sure whether these potential members could be summoned merely by reciting his name.

To preserve his mysterious image, he gave a measured response.

“It will require a certain amount of examination.

“Miss Justice, use a clandestine method that wouldn’t expose yourself to let them know of my name to garner interest in them.”

Audrey immediately agreed.

After a few final exchanges, the gathering ended. The Sun, The Hanged Man, and Justice returned to their respective locations.

Klein remained alone above the gray fog, waiting for Alger to perform the sacrificial ritual.

Key Events

  • Klein proposed using a sacrificial ritual to transfer Audrey’s Rainbow Salamander material through The Fool.
  • He supplied the ritual method, including the required symbol and prayer, and planned to test whether ordinary spiritual materials were sufficient.
  • Alger interpreted the experiment as further evidence that The Fool was constrained and was testing methods to overcome those restrictions.
  • Audrey agreed to cover any additional Beyonder-material costs if the initial attempt failed.
  • Audrey proposed inviting two other Backlund Beyonders to join the Tarot Club.
  • Klein cautiously approved the idea pending discreet examination and indirect contact.
  • After the gathering ended, Klein remained above the gray fog to await Alger’s sacrificial attempt.
