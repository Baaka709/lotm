Chapter 144

977 words · 6 min read

Audrey had actually obtained three pages of Roselle’s diary, but the characters on the last page were too complicated to memorize. Her ability to retain the contents only extended a little beyond two pages, so she decided to leave the rest for the next gathering.

Klein immediately understood why she had brought up the new diary pages.

“Miss Justice, what do you need?”

Audrey answered carefully, “You know that I’ll soon fully digest the Spectator potion. I hope to obtain the Telepathist formula so that I can prepare the ingredients in advance. I know two pages may not be worth the formula, so I can give you another page and also pay some money.”

She immediately realized how inappropriate that sounded and hurriedly corrected herself.

“That’s not what I meant! Mr. Fool, you can decide the compensation yourself!”

Klein considered the proposal. He already had a plan involving an anonymous account and a disguised identity, allowing Audrey to provide money indirectly without knowing who ultimately received it.

Before he could answer, however, The Sun suddenly spoke.

“The Telepathist potion formula? Telepathist, which is followed by Psyche Analyst?”

Audrey immediately noticed something unusual. Derrick had used the ancient title “Psyche Analyst” instead of the modern “Psychiatrist.”

Derrick continued without realizing the significance.

“I can obtain the formula.”

He explained that the pathway came from the Dragon race and that the City of Silver had once been ruled by the Giant King’s imperial family. Since Giants and Dragons were sworn enemies, the City of Silver possessed the Sequence 9, 8, and 7 formulas of the Dragon-related pathway.

Klein nearly covered his face.

He had explicitly warned Derrick not to reveal his origins, yet the boy had casually exposed the City of Silver and its connection to ancient pathways.

Still, Klein learned something valuable: the Spectator pathway had originated from the Dragon race. That explained why the symbol associated with Justice was a dragon.

Klein could easily have stopped the conversation by withholding the gray fog’s translation, but he deliberately allowed Derrick to continue. Maintaining the image of an unfathomable Fool was more important.

Audrey and Alger were both stunned.

The Giant King. Dragons. The City of Silver.

Yet The Fool remained completely calm, sitting behind his gray fog as if he already knew everything.

He knows... He knew all of this.

Audrey eventually asked, “The City of Silver? I’ve never heard of it. Where is it?”

Derrick responded with an equally direct question.

“If you aren’t residents of the City of Silver, which city-state are you from?”

Audrey immediately realized the uncomfortable implication and fell silent. Alger also misunderstood Derrick’s question as a deliberate refusal to reveal their locations.

Derrick recognized the awkwardness and stopped pursuing the matter.

Instead, he proposed his own exchange.

“I’ll try to obtain the Telepathist formula as soon as possible. I want to exchange it for the beginning Sequence of the Sun pathway.”

“Sun pathway? Sequence 9 Bard?” Alger asked.

“Probably,” Derrick replied. “But I don’t know enough about it.”

Klein immediately intervened. He did not want anyone else taking over a deal that could benefit him.

“I believe Miss Justice doesn’t have the Bard formula.”

Audrey nodded.

Klein then announced his three-way arrangement.

“I will give The Sun the Bard formula. The Sun will give the Telepathist formula to Miss Justice as soon as possible. Try to complete it within the next two gatherings. Miss Justice, give me the new pages of Roselle’s diary. Then the deal is complete.”

He deliberately added another condition: Derrick might not be able to receive Audrey’s eventual compensation directly, in which case Klein could compensate him while Audrey transferred money to one of Klein’s anonymous “adorers.”

This carefully redirected attention toward the mysterious location of The Sun and away from Klein’s own financial interests.

Alger immediately became suspicious.

Where exactly is The Sun? Could he be somewhere in the Southern Continent?

Audrey, meanwhile, was thrilled. Her chance to obtain the Telepathist formula had finally appeared.

“I have no objections.”

“Neither do I,” Derrick replied.

Alger was the only one excluded from the transaction, though he would eventually gain access to the Bard formula as well.

Klein was pleased that he had delayed the issue of compensation until a later gathering.

He extended his hand.

The Bard formula appeared.

“Main ingredients: a Crystal Sunflower, or an adult Flint Bird’s tail feather, or a Fire Bird’s tail feather... a piece of Siren Rock or a Singing Sunflower.

“Supplementary ingredients: a blade of Midsummer Grass, five drops of July Wine Juice, a blade of Elf Dark Leaf...”

Derrick listened closely, first frowning at the unfamiliar ingredients before relaxing.

Klein understood immediately.

The City of Silver had preserved ancient names for ingredients, while his own formula came directly from the Eternal Blazing Sun and therefore contained ancient names together with replacement ingredients from different eras.

Audrey then transferred the two diary pages she had memorized onto the mysterious goatskin, and Klein received them.

He began reading at once.

The first entries were ordinary notes about Matilda’s pregnancy, attractive village-born maids, Countess Florais’s private party, and an Archbishop Fan Estin seeking Roselle’s help.

But the entry from November 9th changed everything.

Roselle recorded that Archbishop Estin had revealed a secret of the Sequence pathways.

After reaching Sequence 5, a Beyonder could potentially switch to an equivalent Sequence from one of one or two related pathways.

In other words, pathway substitution became possible from the Mid-Sequence onward, including the transition toward High-Sequence.

But the replacement had to come from compatible pathways. Using an unrelated potion could result in semi-insanity at best and make further advancement impossible.

Roselle specifically noted several compatible examples, including the Sleepless and Corpse Collector pathways, as well as the Church’s Savant and Mystery Pryer pathways.

Klein stared at the information, immediately recognizing how significant this hidden rule could be.

  • Klein receives Audrey’s diary pages and discovers Roselle’s revelation that compatible Beyonders can switch between certain related pathways from Sequence 5 onward.
