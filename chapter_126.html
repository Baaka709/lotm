Chapter 126

Chapter 126

958 words · 6 min read

Klein’s hunger nearly distracted him, but confirming Trissy’s whereabouts mattered more. No one knew what terrible measures the transformed Instigator might take in her revenge against society.

He hurried upstairs, locked his bedroom, and prepared a spirituality wall before traveling above the gray fog. Though he was already familiar with the madness that accompanied the journey, the rituals of the night had left him somewhat tired. At the long bronze table, he wrote the divination statement: “Trissy’s whereabouts.”

After repeatedly reciting it, Klein entered a dream. Through the mist, he saw a steam locomotive belching smoke and sparks, with clean leather seats inside. Near a window sat Trissy, wearing a checkered fishnet hat. Klein tried repeatedly to determine the train number, but the dream refused to reveal it. Eventually the pressure forced him awake.

“I could only confirm that Trissy took the steam locomotive and left Tingen. There weren’t any more clues...”

He felt that the mysterious space above the gray fog had helped eliminate interference, but had not made the divination itself all-powerful. Since Dunn was already arranging telegrams and placing Trissy on wanted lists across the country, Klein decided not to report the incomplete divination. Without a train number, other divination methods would likely fail as well.

He returned to his room with one thought dominating his mind: mutton.

“I must add some fennel... Praise the Lady!”

The next morning, Klein arrived at Blackthorn Security Company shortly before nine. Rozanne greeted him excitedly with good news: the Captain’s request had been approved, and two police officers experienced with supernatural incidents would be transferred to work as clerks. Rozanne would finally no longer need to remain awake through so many nights.

Klein sincerely congratulated her, then went underground for his mysticism lessons. Passing the Captain’s office, he noticed Dunn, Leonard, and the others were still present, suggesting their investigation had yielded nothing useful. Dunn was busy sending telegrams, so Klein decided to speak with him later.

Then Klein suddenly stopped.

He stared at the gas lamps and realized he had made a crucial mistake.

His divination had shown Trissy aboard a steam locomotive, and he had unconsciously assumed the image represented the present. But this world did not have the modern nighttime train schedules he was accustomed to. Steam locomotives barely carried passengers after dark.

The vision was not from the previous night.

It was from the future.

Trissy was going to leave Tingen that day—or perhaps the next.

Klein immediately turned back upstairs and found Leonard in the entertainment room. After pulling him aside, he told him directly, “I divined that Trissy will leave on a steam locomotive.”

Leonard checked his pocket watch. “The earliest train is at seven in the morning... No time to waste! I’ll tell the Captain that I received a reliable tip.”

Leonard quickly informed Dunn, who immediately gathered the other Nighthawks and sent a telegram before they rushed out toward the railway station.

Watching them leave, Klein felt conflicted. The realization reminded him of the lesson he had learned from the death of the suited clown: mistakes in interpretation could be just as dangerous as ignorance. This time, he had recognized his mistake himself, making the lesson sink even deeper.

He entered the duty room and encountered Old Neil, who had just prepared some hand-ground coffee.

“Would you like one?”

“Alright.”

Old Neil smiled and prepared it with Klein’s usual three sugar cubes and spoonful of milk, then complained that Klein’s sweet tooth would harm his teeth and health. Klein defended his preferences, explaining that while he liked sweet coffee, he preferred rose salt, black pepper, fennel, and similar seasonings with meat.

As they prepared to begin their studies, Klein mentioned that the Captain and others were heading for the steam locomotive station based on his information.

Old Neil immediately laughed. “Under such circumstances, the possibility of failure is much higher than success. Trissy should be a Sequence 7 Beyonder... Don’t rely on divination, divination isn’t all-powerful. You’ll only obtain symbolic signs which are very easy to interpret wrongly or ignore something.”

Klein remembered his own error and nodded.

“Yeah, divination isn’t all-powerful.”

The moment he said it, an indescribable change occurred within him. His mind, body, and spirit entered a strange state. Then he heard an illusory shattering sound, as though something inside him had dissolved and merged completely with his spirituality.

He immediately understood.

The Seer potion had finally been completely digested.

Far away, at Wienia—the first town downstream from Tingen and the first railway stop on the route toward Backlund—Trissy prepared to escape. She had changed into a long beige dress and a woman’s circular hat, with fine fishnet gauze covering half her face and making her features difficult to recognize.

Before leaving, she had sent a telegram to her partner in Tingen, warning them to remain cautious. She claimed she had used stolen money to purchase a ticket to Backlund.

Trissy had deliberately avoided boarding directly at Tingen. Her instincts and extensive experience as an Assassin told her that leaving from a less obvious station would make pursuit more difficult.

A steam locomotive arrived with a shrill whistle, trailing smoke and sparks.

Trissy carried no luggage. She boarded the first cabin, already planning to leave after three stations and reach Backlund by another route.

Meanwhile, beneath Saint Selena Cathedral, Klein sat with his eyes closed, experiencing the final effects of the potion’s digestion. In his mind, illusory stars appeared one after another, each seemingly linked to him and drawing together as though they wished to fuse.

The strange hunger and thirst eventually faded.

Klein opened his eyes.

His mind felt clearer, lighter, and purer than before.

He now understood that he had become a true, complete Seer.

Key Events

  • Klein divines that Trissy has boarded a steam locomotive, but cannot determine the train number.
  • He realizes the vision depicts the future rather than the previous night and warns Leonard.
  • Dunn and the Nighthawks rush to the railway station based on Klein’s information.
  • Old Neil reminds Klein that divination is symbolic and can easily be misinterpreted.
  • Klein’s Seer potion finishes digesting completely during this realization.
  • Trissy secretly travels from Wienia toward Backlund and plans to leave the train after several stations.
  • Klein awakens beneath Saint Selena Cathedral as a fully matured Seer.
