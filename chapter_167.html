Chapter 167

Chapter 167 - Holy Artifact

725 words · 5 min read

Crestet Cesimir instructed Klein to swear upon the Holy Artifact before revealing the Church’s secrets. He placed the silver suitcase on his knees, and its cover dissolved away as darkness seemed to be drawn into it. Inside lay a pure white bone sword covered in mysterious symbols. The sword radiated a brilliant glow, but its strange presence immediately seized Klein’s attention, drawing his gaze toward it until he nearly lost himself in a trance.

Crestet moved the sword, snapping Klein back to his senses, and ordered him to place his hand upon it. Klein cautiously approached and touched the weapon. A cold sensation entered his mind, instantly calming his distracting thoughts and worries.

He then repeated Crestet’s oath in Hermes, swearing before the Evernight Goddess that he would never reveal the details of the “acting method” to anyone who did not already know it, accepting whatever punishment the Goddess deemed appropriate should he violate the oath. As he finished, Klein sensed a faint connection forming between himself and some distant existence through the bone sword. He withdrew his hand, drew the crimson moon over his chest, and praised the Goddess alongside Crestet.

After sealing the sword away, Crestet explained the secret behind the acting method. For countless generations, the Church’s Beyonders had searched for ways to reduce the danger of losing control. The key lay in the names of the potions themselves.

Each potion name corresponded to a particular group or role with its own rules and behavioral principles. By following those rules, a Beyonder gradually became more like the group represented by the potion’s name. In other words, they were “acting” as that role.

Klein immediately connected the explanation to the Seer principles he had developed himself. Crestet confirmed the connection, but emphasized an important limitation: every person’s spirituality was unique. While Beyonders taking the same potion shared the same fundamental rules, the specific way those rules manifested could differ from person to person. Therefore, another person’s experience could only serve as a guide rather than an exact template.

Crestet further explained that following the acting method not only improved one’s control over the potion but actually caused the potion to be digested, much like food. Once a potion was truly digested, its owner would experience a distinctive and mysterious sensation.

Klein then asked about the origins of potion names. Crestet confirmed that the ancient names had been recorded on the Blasphemy Slate, but the names used in the modern era had also been derived from divine revelations and the accumulated experiences of generations of Beyonders.

Klein asked why the Church did not simply teach the acting method to every Nighthawk if it was so effective. Crestet repeated that the matter was a Church secret, one whose true reason would only become accessible to someone who reached the level of an archbishop or high-ranking deacon.

The conversation ended with Crestet ordering Klein to return upstairs and send the other Nighthawks down one by one for the final stage of their examination. Klein realized this separation was likely intended to prevent the acting method from being casually spread.

He returned to the Blackthorn Security Company and found Dunn near the basement entrance. Klein told him that Crestet had requested Frye and the others come down for a conversation. Dunn confirmed that this was the final step and therefore meant Klein had encountered no problems.

Klein suddenly remembered that Royale was guarding Chanis Gate, Leonard was supervising the asylum, and Kenley was on break. Dunn froze before admitting that he had forgotten about them. Fortunately, he explained, having a high-ranking deacon conduct the examination meant there was no need for lengthy communication with the Holy Cathedral. Crestet could make the decision directly and even provide Klein with the Clown potion formula and its principal ingredients.

Klein could barely contain his excitement.

After another hour and a half, Kenley emerged from the alchemy room looking puzzled. Klein was then summoned downstairs once more.

Crestet was standing this time, his black windbreaker moving slightly in the basement breeze, his raised collar hiding his chin in shadow. He looked directly at Klein and smiled.

“Nighthawk Klein Moretti, I announce in the name of the Goddess that you have passed the examination of the Holy Cathedral.

“Congratulations. With your contributions, you can immediately advance to become a Sequence 8 Beyonder!”

Key Events

  • Klein swears before the Holy Artifact, a mysterious white bone sword, never to reveal the acting method to those who do not know it.
  • Crestet explains that the acting method is based on following the rules and characteristics associated with a potion’s name.
  • He reveals that acting gradually digests the potion and reduces the risk of losing control, while individual adaptations are still necessary.
  • The Church keeps the acting method secret because its deeper reasons are restricted to its highest-ranking members.
  • Crestet completes the remaining examinations of the Nighthawks and confirms that Klein has no problems.
  • Dunn explains that because Crestet is a high-ranking deacon, he can immediately approve Klein’s advancement and provide the Clown potion formula and ingredients.
  • Crestet officially declares that Klein has passed the Holy Cathedral’s examination and can immediately advance to Sequence 8.
