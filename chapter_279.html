Chapter 279

Chapter 279 - Extreme Joy Begets Sorrow

634 words · 4 min read

Deep beneath Millet Carter’s property, Klein carefully inspected the underground hall once again, searching for additional entrances. He refused to enter the passage leading toward the innermost chamber, while Sharron’s cold wind once more drove the newly appearing snakes away.

Satisfied that there were no other usable routes, Klein retreated toward the main entrance with the three zombies.

Sharron began placing explosives.

“She looks very professional,” Klein murmured.

The zombies offered no response because Klein continuously used spirituality to shield Azik’s copper whistle, preventing its influence from exciting them.

Klein wondered where Sharron had acquired such expertise. Was demolition somehow connected to her Beyonder pathway?

He was still thinking when Sharron suddenly lit the fuse.

Hey, hey, hey! Why didn’t you warn me?

Klein immediately ran into the corridor.

“It’s very safe here,” Sharron said as she appeared beside him.

“Will dust fall from the tremors over here?”

“Yes.”

“That’s good.”

The fuse hissed.

“One,” Sharron suddenly said.

Then the explosion struck.

Rumble!

The ground violently shook. Dust and debris poured from above, Klein’s ears buzzed, and boulders and earth rapidly collapsed across the entrance.

Klein immediately checked Azik’s copper whistle.

Its surface remained cold and calm.

The evil spirit had not escaped.

Relieved, Klein examined himself and saw that his face was filthy.

That was convenient.

He could now give Millet Carter a convincing explanation for the collapse.

After confirming the entrance was completely sealed, Klein realized that only spirit-like beings, such as Sharron and certain Apprentice-path Beyonders, would be able to pass through the rubble. That meant Sharron had effectively eliminated most potential competitors.

Klein led the three zombies back toward the basement.

Millet Carter was pacing anxiously when they emerged.

“What happened inside?”

Klein deliberately panted.

“The structure was too old. We were driving away the snakes, but a little movement caused it to collapse. The whole area gave way. Fortunately, we were close enough to the entrance to escape.”

Millet was horrified.

Klein took him inside and showed him the completely blocked passage.

Millet sighed with relief that he had hired a professional instead of sending workers blindly into the ruins.

“All right. This commission ends here. I’ll pay you the rest.”

Millet counted his money and discovered that he had only about thirty pounds in cash.

“Fortunately, I still have other forms of money. Do you mind taking gold coins?”

Klein smiled.

“I don’t mind any form of money.”

Millet removed a small box from his clothing and handed Klein five gold coins along with the remaining notes.

Klein counted everything carefully before giving Millet a polite bow.

He was pleased.

The underground structure was sealed, Millet was satisfied, Sharron had obtained what she wanted, and Klein had escaped without serious consequences.

After reaching the street, the three zombies silently walked away in another direction.

Klein smiled and waved after them.

Sharron really had not charged him anything for the zombies.

He returned home, changed clothes, and went to the Quelaag Club for shooting practice and dinner.

Later, while strolling through Minsk Street, he suddenly sensed something unusual.

Two policemen accompanied by a police dog were investigating the area.

Klein suspected the serial murder investigation was responsible. He considered turning away but the officers had already seen him.

“Routine investigation.”

Before Klein could finish responding, the police dog began growling and barking.

Gunpowder.

Klein realized that the smell left from his afternoon shooting practice was probably responsible.

He calmly explained that he had found a revolver and holster in the street and intended to hand them over.

The officers took the weapon.

“You have to come back to the police station with us.”

Klein smiled brightly.

“Certainly. But I have a request. Please inform my lawyer, Mr. Jurgen Cooper. I will only accept a body search with him around.”

Key Events

  • Sharron expertly places explosives and collapses the underground entrance without releasing the evil spirit.
  • Klein confirms through Azik’s copper whistle that the evil spirit remains contained.
  • Klein convinces Millet Carter that the ancient structure collapsed naturally while they were dealing with snakes.
  • Carter pays the remainder of Klein’s commission, including five gold coins.
  • The three zombie assistants leave after the job.
  • Klein returns to the Quelaag Club and later encounters police officers investigating with a dog.
  • The police dog reacts to gunpowder on Klein, causing him to be taken to the police station.
  • Klein immediately requests the presence of his lawyer, Jurgen Cooper.
