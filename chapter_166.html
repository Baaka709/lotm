Chapter 166 - Examination

996 words · 6 min read

Klein felt a cold breeze rising from the basement as he stood at the entrance, his tense emotions finally reaching their peak. The examination he had been waiting for had arrived. Once he passed and advanced to Sequence 8, he would finally possess genuine combat strength, with divination and Flaring Sun Charms giving him better odds of surviving dangerous situations. He had even avoided withdrawing the three hundred pounds Miss Justice had transferred to his anonymous account, fearing that a financial audit might expose the mysterious source of the money.

Dunn explained that the examiner was Crestet Cesimir, one of the nine high-ranking deacons of the Nighthawks. The Holy Cathedral clearly regarded Klein as important. Dunn assured him that Crestet was only Sequence 5 and had not yet become a demigod, though his holy item gave him combat power comparable to a newly advanced Sequence 4 Beyonder. His title in the Beyonder world was the “Goddess’s Sword.”

The examination would take place in the alchemy room—the same laboratory where Old Neil had once prepared Klein’s Seer potion. The gloomy association with Neil briefly weighed on him as he descended into the basement.

Inside, the long tables had been pushed aside, leaving a wide open space. Two high-backed chairs faced each other. A man with short golden-brown hair and dark green eyes sat waiting, his raised collar casting his chin into shadow. Beside him rested a silver suitcase, large enough to hold a sword.

“Hello, Your Grace,” Klein greeted him.

Crestet smiled and invited him to sit. His first test, he explained, would be to determine how well Klein had mastered his potion.

The moment Crestet began observing him, Klein suddenly felt the gas-lamp light disappear into darkness. Exhaustion overwhelmed him as though his body had suddenly reached the point of sleep, yet his mind remained unnaturally tense. In the silent darkness, he heard ordinary sounds—the drip of water, conversations from the Blackthorn Security Company, wind moving through the stairway—but nothing beyond what he should naturally have perceived.

“Excellent.”

Crestet’s voice broke through the darkness, and the room immediately returned to normal.

Klein realized that the examiner had influenced him without him even noticing. This was the terrifying ability of a Sequence 5 Beyonder and a high-ranking deacon.

Crestet announced that Klein had achieved a level beyond outstanding in potion mastery. The next test would determine whether the remaining spirit of the potion had left any hidden problems in his mind or subtly changed his character. He gave Klein three minutes to prepare.

Klein entered Cogitation and carefully cleared away his negative thoughts. Three minutes later, Crestet announced that he would begin singing.

He hummed a pleasant melody, but it gradually became horribly discordant. The sounds transformed into a nightmare of scratching blackboards, bubble wrap, electric drills, and countless other unbearable noises. The chaotic racket filled Klein with an urge to vent his frustration through violence.

Fortunately, his frequent exposure to madness and terrifying ravings had trained his willpower. He suppressed the destructive impulses almost immediately. At the same time, he deliberately displayed annoyance, tension, frustration, and insecurity. Appearing completely unaffected would itself have looked suspicious.

Eventually the singing stopped, and silence returned.

Crestet praised him again, explaining that there were no latent problems in his soul. Even if Klein had wanted to beat him up or stuff something into his mouth, that reaction would have been perfectly normal.

Klein honestly replied that he would never dare.

Crestet then declared that Klein had passed all the tests. Only the question-and-answer session remained.

His gaze suddenly deepened, seeming capable of seeing directly into Klein’s spirit. He asked whether Klein’s experience in the Divination Club had helped him quickly master the potion.

“Yes,” Klein answered, without revealing unnecessary details.

Crestet then asked about the inspiration Klein had supposedly received from the Mystery Pryers’ maxim and Daly’s example. Klein confirmed it and carefully constructed his explanation. He claimed that one of his teammates, a Mystery Pryer, had told him that following their maxim reduced the risk of losing control. He had also heard that Madam Daly wanted to become a true Spirit Medium and had reached Sequence 7 within two years.

Combining those examples, Klein said, he had decided to become a “real Seer” and developed several principles of his own. The result had exceeded his expectations, allowing him to digest the potion very quickly. He described the sensation of fully mastering it as something special and magical, while carefully pretending that he only vaguely understood the true meaning behind the “acting method.”

The old Klein from Earth might have panicked while delivering so many carefully constructed half-truths before such a powerful Nighthawk. But after everything he had experienced since transmigrating, lying had become almost effortless.

Crestet’s penetrating gaze finally relaxed. “Don’t worry, it’s not an illusion.”

Klein felt relieved.

Crestet explained that Dunn had endorsed his account, and he believed Klein really was a genius with strong logic and sharp senses. He then asked whether Klein had shared his experience with his teammates.

Klein admitted that he had. Since they were comrades who faced danger together, he wanted to help them reduce their chances of losing control. He had no reason to keep such information from teammates, though he had deliberately withheld it from the clerks.

Crestet sat upright and smiled.

“Although you haven’t even been with the Nighthawks for two months, I believe your understanding toward partners is much better than many others.”

He then revealed that he intended to share more information with Klein. However, Holy Cathedral regulations required Klein to swear to the Goddess that he would never reveal their conversation to anyone who did not already know about the information.

“That should be fine, right?”

Klein immediately realized what this meant.

He had passed.

“No problem!”

Although he would not be able to directly teach others the acting method, he could still find ways to pass information indirectly to Miss Justice and Mr. Hanged Man.

Key Events

  • Klein begins the Holy Cathedral’s examination, learning that high-ranking deacon Crestet Cesimir, the “Goddess’s Sword,” will evaluate him.
  • Crestet tests Klein’s potion mastery and confirms that he has reached an exceptional level without suffering obvious mental contamination.
  • A second test uses unbearable sounds to provoke violent impulses, but Klein successfully suppresses them while deliberately displaying believable negative emotions.
  • Crestet questions Klein about how he mastered the Seer potion and why he developed his understanding of the acting method.
  • Klein maintains his carefully constructed explanation without revealing the true source of his knowledge.
  • Crestet accepts Klein’s answers, praises his intelligence and loyalty to his teammates, and confirms that Dunn has endorsed him.
  • Crestet prepares to share more confidential information with Klein, provided Klein swears secrecy to the Goddess.
  • Klein realizes that although he cannot openly teach the acting method, he may be able to pass its principles indirectly to Miss Justice and Mr. Hanged Man.
