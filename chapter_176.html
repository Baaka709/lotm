Chapter 176 - Letter

1,038 words · 6 min read

For Klein, summoning a messenger through a ritual had become almost as natural as breathing. He quickly arranged the necessary ingredients, lit the candle representing himself, and briefly entertained the ridiculous thought that he was holding a candlelight vigil for his own sake before immediately dismissing the idea.

He sprinkled Black Rotten Flower powder, associated with Death, onto the candle and then added Full Moon Essence Oil, favored by the Evernight Goddess. The surroundings suddenly became quiet as an invisible mystical force surged through the room.

Klein stepped back and began the summoning incantation in ancient Hermes, calling upon the wandering spirit and messenger belonging to Daly Simone.

A strange creature appeared.

The wall behind his desk rippled like translucent water, and a horrifying face emerged. It had no eyes, nose, or eyebrows—only thick lips surrounding a mouth packed with irregular teeth. A long red tongue extended from it, and at its tip were five tiny fingers that repeatedly stretched and retracted as though waiting to receive a message.

Klein was immediately struck by the contrast between Daly’s messenger and Azik’s enormous skeletal messenger. While Azik’s messenger had been nearly four meters tall, Daly’s looked almost tiny by comparison. He wondered whether the difference came from the mystical items involved, from Azik’s own strength, or from some deeper connection to the Corpse Collector pathway.

He also realized he had forgotten to ask Daly about the Sequence 4 and Sequence 3 names of the Corpse Collector pathway. If Azik really belonged to that pathway, those names might reveal more about his identity. Klein resolved to ask next time.

He handed the folded letter to the messenger’s strange hand. The tongue immediately retracted, swallowing the letter, and the entire translucent face sank back into the wall and disappeared.

Klein found the method surprisingly convenient and impressive before ending the ritual.

On Monday morning in Backlund’s Empress Borough, Xio Derecha and Fors Wall secretly met Audrey’s messenger.

Instead of a terrifying supernatural creature, they encountered Susie—a golden retriever.

Xio stared at the dog and seriously asked whether she was Miss Audrey’s messenger, immediately realizing how ridiculous it sounded. Fors joked that Susie might be a magical creature, though Xio had difficulty imagining why one would look so much like an ordinary dog.

Susie silently indicated the leather pouch strapped around her body.

Xio carefully removed it while Fors examined the material and recognized the expensive crocodile leather as the work of a high-end designer. Xio immediately became extremely cautious with it, as though she were handling an antique.

Inside was a letter.

After reading it, Fors burned it completely with her cigarette, while Xio complained that it contained no additional information. She then took out an investigation report covering the previous few days and ordered Susie, in an exaggeratedly stern tone, to deliver it directly to Audrey.

Susie sat upright and wagged her tail enthusiastically before racing away.

...

At the Hall family villa, Audrey was opening a letter from her brother Alfred, who was currently in the Balam Empire in the Southern Continent. A package had arrived alongside it.

Susie soon entered the room and proudly presented the leather pouch.

“You really are an excellent messenger!” Audrey praised her.

Susie explained that Xio was very serious and reminded her of a hunter who had once trained them. Audrey noticed how naturally Susie spoke Loen now, although her phrasing and logic were still slightly unusual.

Once the room was secured, Audrey read the investigation report from Xio and Fors. They had found that several vagrants had disappeared around the Backlund Bridge borough, but there was no proof that Rear Admiral Hurricane, Qilangos, was responsible.

Audrey carefully considered how to respond. She wanted to encourage Xio by promising four hundred and fifty pounds for successfully tracking down Qilangos, while also considering her efforts to gather the ingredients needed for the Telepathist potion. At present, only the Farsman Rabbit’s spinal fluid had been found; the Rainbow Salamander’s pituitary gland was still missing.

She reminded herself that there was at least progress: she had completely digested the Spectator potion and, once she obtained the remaining ingredients, would be able to advance to Sequence 8.

Audrey wrote her reply, placed it in the pouch, and sent Susie back.

Then she turned her attention to Alfred’s letter.

Her brother encouraged her to visit the Southern Continent and described the Balam Empire’s colonial regions enthusiastically. He praised their sunlight, fresh air, seafood, cultures, and sense of freedom while criticizing Backlund’s gloomy weather, polluted atmosphere, crowds, and endless social gatherings.

He clarified that his departure was not simply running away from home. He wanted to establish himself independently, while believing their elder brother regarded him as a major rival for their father’s inheritance. Their father, a Duke, had already demonstrated his willingness to break with tradition, such as when he sold half the family’s farmland and pasture to enter banking despite strong opposition.

Alfred admitted that he still missed Backlund, especially his parents and Audrey, whose smile had once brightened his life. He believed that Audrey must have become one of Backlund’s most dazzling young women, though his career meant he would not return for another two years.

He also invited Audrey and their aunt to visit the coastal regions of the Balam Empire, particularly because the warmer climate would be good for their aunt’s painful winter joints. Along with the invitation, Alfred had sent gifts reflecting Balam’s culture, including distinctive yellow silk and ornaments associated with the worship of Death. Remembering Audrey’s former interest in mysticism, he promised to look for more mysterious objects for her.

Audrey’s response was affectionate but deliberately misleading. She told Alfred that she had grown up considerably since he last saw her and that she was no longer interested in mysticism.

She silently added the reason: such things were dangerous.

After hearing too many stories about tragedies involving mystical objects through Beyonder gatherings and from Xio and Fors, Audrey had developed a much healthier caution.

Instead, she told Alfred that she was now fascinated by biology and specifically interested in the Rainbow Salamander.

She asked him to find out where one could be found—or whether a complete preserved corpse was available.
