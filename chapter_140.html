Chapter 140

Chapter 140

1,193 words · 7 min read

“The origins of this drop of golden liquid.”

Klein repeated the divination statement seven times while holding the goatskin and the illusory golden liquid. He was unsure whether an object created only from his own perception could truly support divination, but curiosity drove him to test it.

His vision darkened as he entered Cogitation.

Then he saw it.

In a ruined, indistinct dreamscape, a brilliant golden sun suddenly appeared.

A low, resonant sound echoed through the void. Pure, scorching light exploded outward.

Boom!

Klein was violently expelled from the dream. His body rolled aside as golden flames engulfed him. His thoughts dissolved into chaos, unable to form a coherent idea.

Then the entire world above the gray fog shook.

The magnificent palace trembled violently. Stone pillars cracked, the ancient bronze table shattered into pieces, and the entire mysterious space seemed on the verge of collapse.

The terrifying disturbance lasted only a few seconds.

Then everything became silent.

The flames consuming Klein gradually died away. He lay on the floor with charred skin, groaning until his mind slowly cleared. Using the armrest of the high-backed chair, he struggled to stand and stared in disbelief at the damaged palace.

A mere divination had nearly killed him.

What had he seen?

Something unimaginably powerful.

Klein quickly realized that without the protection of the mysterious space above the gray fog, there might not have been anything left of him.

Could that golden liquid be divine blood?

Had he accidentally glimpsed the Eternal Blazing Sun—or perhaps one of His powerful angels?

The thought that he might have looked directly at a god sent another wave of fear through him.

Those who know nothing fear nothing. But those who court death eventually die.

Klein immediately decided he could never casually divine information concerning high-level entities again. If another such existence reacted deliberately, even the mysterious space might not be able to save him.

As his body gradually recovered, Klein restored the palace and bronze table by thought alone.

At least I learned something.

He concluded that only powers approaching the level of divine angels could seriously interfere with the space above the gray fog.

He also added another principle to his mental rules as a Seer:

Never randomly divine matters involving high-level entities.

He should be equally careful with Spirit Vision. Looking directly at something that should never be observed could be fatal in the outside world, where he lacked the gray fog’s protection.

Then Klein realized that the reckless divination had left something behind.

Knowledge.

Because he had remained in a divination state while confronting what appeared to be the Eternal Blazing Sun, fragments of information had instinctively entered his mind.

He used dream divination to organize them and began writing.

The first lesson was simple:

Never look directly at God.

Then came several pieces of astonishing information.

He learned about a Pure White Angel.

He also obtained the method for creating a Flaring Sun Charm, a relatively powerful Sun-domain charm whose effectiveness could last for a year. Instead of directly praying to the Eternal Blazing Sun, its creation could use Sealed Artifact 3-0782 as a substitute, siphoning power from the Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem.

He also learned that the Eternal Blazing Sun was extremely hostile toward both the Lord of Storms and the God of Knowledge and Wisdom.

Then came potion formulas.

The Sequence 9 Bard formula included ingredients such as Crystal Sunflower or a Flint Bird’s or Fire Bird’s tail feather, alongside supplementary materials such as Midsummer Grass and July Wine Juice.

The Sequence 8 Light Suppliant formula also appeared, with several possible substitutes for its main ingredients.

He received partial information on the Priest of Light potion.

Finally, there was something far more important.

Sequence 4—Unshadowed.

Its main ingredient could be the golden blood extracted from the Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem, with another possible combination involving Sun Divine Bird feathers and Holy Brilliance Rock.

Klein stared at what he had written.

He had gained far more than he had ever expected.

The Nighthawks had told him that the Sun pathway began with Bard, followed by Light Suppliant and then Solar High Priest. Now he possessed complete formulas for the first two Sequences, including alternative ingredients from different eras.

And he had obtained them directly through an utterly reckless divination.

The Hanged Man might once have been useful for solving the Sun’s request, since the Lord of Storms’ Church and the Eternal Blazing Sun Church had opposed one another for thousands of years. Their members should naturally possess knowledge of each other’s pathways.

But Klein no longer needed him.

Miss Justice, Mr. Hanged Man, my Sun friend... your Fool nearly became a charred corpse for this information.

Despite the joke, Klein still felt a lingering fear.

He examined the other formulas carefully.

Could Priest of Light be an ancient name corresponding to Solar High Priest? He could not determine its Sequence number.

And Sequence 4 Unshadowed was the first High-Sequence formula he had ever obtained. Unfortunately, the supplementary ingredients were incomplete.

Klein then reconsidered 3-0782.

If its extraordinary purification power really came from divine blood, then its strength was far greater than the Nighthawks had realized. It might even be powerful enough to deserve a higher classification.

The original assessments had focused on practical dangers—whether it retained signs of life, whether it harmed nearby humans, how difficult its effects were to control, and whether it was useful against spirits and corpses.

No one had understood its true origin.

Perhaps the Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem could even fight evil spirits.

Klein sighed at the irony. He was an official Nighthawk of the Evernight Goddess and could never openly become its owner, nor could he casually carry Sun-related ingredients.

Still, perhaps one day he could make a Flaring Sun Charm and siphon some of 3-0782’s power.

But not now.

He checked the mysterious space again and found no further disturbance.

The Eternal Blazing Sun had apparently not tracked him down.

Klein repeated the warning to himself.

Do not look directly at God.

Do not casually investigate high-level entities.

But questions remained.

Why was the Eternal Blazing Sun so hostile toward the Lord of Storms and the God of Knowledge and Wisdom?

And what exactly was the Pure White Angel?

His head throbbed from the amount of information he had gained, and he realized far more time had passed than expected.

He could not remain away from the real world any longer.

Klein had originally believed that a few minutes of divination above the gray fog would be harmless, especially with a spirituality wall isolating him. He had never imagined that a single accident could nearly destroy both him and the mysterious palace.

Before descending, he enveloped himself in spirituality, bracing himself for the possibility of an unexpected purification attack—or damage to the Sacred Emblem.

The crimson moonlight returned.

Klein opened his eyes and found himself once again in the sparse forest, surrounded by weeds.

The Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem remained intact in his hand.

Only after several tense seconds did he finally relax.

He let out a long breath.

Phew...

The reckless experiment had left him utterly exhausted.
