Chapter 266

Chapter 266 - The Worlds Commission

494 words · 3 min read

Sequence 0?

Klein could barely process what he had just learned.

There was an existence above Sequence 1. Sequence 0 was equivalent to a True God. And apparently every pathway had its own Sequence 0.

Could anyone become a god by consuming the right potion, completing the corresponding ritual, and mastering the acting method?

The thought was so astonishing that Klein nearly lost control of his expression.

He wondered whether the final ingredients for a Sequence 0 potion might involve the godhood, divine blood, or physical remains of the corresponding deity.

He forced himself to calm down before the others noticed how deeply shocked he was.

Klein hid Roselle’s diary and turned toward Justice.

“Miss Justice, well done. You finished Bakerland in less than a week. According to the agreement, what kind of payment do you want?”

Alger was stunned.

Bakerland was already dead?

Audrey, secretly pleased by his surprise, calmly explained the financial arrangement. The bounty for Rear Admiral Qilangos was thirty thousand pounds, of which fifteen thousand belonged to Mr. Fool’s adorer. Killing Bakerland had cost ten thousand pounds, leaving another five thousand still owed.

Because her finances were temporarily strained, Audrey could only pay the remaining five thousand in several months.

Klein was shocked by the revelation that Qilangos’s bounty had been so large.

He silently considered the fact that the money should technically go to Azik. Since he had no way to contact Azik in the near future, however, he decided it would be better to use the money to strengthen himself and repay Azik later through assistance.

He accepted.

Then The World spoke.

He asked for several rare Beyonder ingredients: the true root and at least sixty milliliters of juice from a Mist Treant, marrow crystals from the Spring of the Elves, and the complete spinal fluid of a Dark Patterned Black Panther.

The Sun responded first.

He knew where Mist Treants could be found and could eventually help acquire the material, but he needed to complete his advancement and gain enough strength to join the appropriate patrol team.

In exchange, Derrick wanted a weapon capable of improving his combat strength.

Klein immediately thought of an inscribed steel sword he had once seen at Eye of Wisdom’s gathering, but unfortunately someone else had already purchased it.

Audrey warned The Sun that the requested materials roughly corresponded to a Sequence 7 and would normally be worth several hundred pounds. Alger estimated the value somewhat higher.

Derrick simply thanked them.

Audrey then offered the Psychiatrist formula and related information in exchange for The World’s requested ingredients.

Alger offered another proposal: the Sequence 6 Wind-blessed formula of the Sailor pathway. Because it was considerably more valuable than the ingredients, The World would need to make up the difference if they traded.

Klein immediately suspected that Alger was trying to conceal the fact that he was advancing quickly through the Sailor pathway.

The World accepted.

Then he announced that he had another request.

Key Events

  • Klein struggles to process the revelation that Sequence 0 represents True Godhood.
  • Audrey explains that Qilangos’s bounty was 30,000 pounds and that she still owes 5,000 pounds connected to Bakerland’s death.
  • Klein decides to temporarily use Azik’s eventual payment to strengthen himself.
  • The World requests rare Beyonder ingredients, including Mist Treant juice.
  • The Sun agrees to help obtain Mist Treant materials after completing his advancement.
  • The Sun requests a powerful weapon in exchange.
  • Justice offers the Psychiatrist pathway information.
  • The Hanged Man offers the Wind-blessed formula for the Sailor pathway.
