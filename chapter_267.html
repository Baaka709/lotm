Chapter 267 - Singing to the Same Tune

766 words · 5 min read

The World’s next request was carefully chosen.

“I want to get information about all the abject nobles in the Loen Kingdom, including their current addresses. The more details, the better.”

Klein deliberately avoided mentioning Baronet Pound so that Justice would not connect The World with Detective Sherlock Moriarty’s investigation.

Audrey was surprised.

“What do you want to do?”

The World laughed.

“That’s not something you need to worry about. I’m only searching for some things. I won’t do them any harm. I can swear on this point and allow Mr. Fool to bear witness.”

Audrey agreed to gather what information she could. She knew fragments about various disgraced nobles from balls and social gatherings, but obtaining a complete list would require searching books, consulting contacts, and checking records.

Three or four days would be enough.

The World accepted.

He offered secret Fourth Epoch history, knowledge of Beyonder pathways, and potion formulas as possible payment, but acknowledged that information on disgraced nobles was not worth a Sequence 7 or 8 formula.

Audrey therefore requested something else: knowledge about a Beyonder pathway that she did not already know.

Klein isolated the private exchange.

The World asked about the Demoness Sect.

Audrey admitted that she knew its origins, beliefs, pathways, and the tendencies of its upper echelons, but little more.

The World revealed that the Assassin pathway continued from Sequence 8, Instigator, into Sequence 7 Witch.

Audrey was stunned by the name.

“Witch?”

Then came the truly shocking revelation.

“When a man consumes this potion, he will no longer be considered a male. He will become a true lady.”

Audrey stared in disbelief.

The Witch potion could actually change a man’s sex.

The World explained that women who consumed it would instead become more beautiful and develop enhanced charms, which explained why the higher ranks of the Demoness Sect were predominantly women.

Audrey asked about its flaws.

The acting method required Witch Beyonders to become evildoers who spread calamity, disease, and pain.

Then, unable to suppress her curiosity, Audrey asked whether a female animal consuming the potion would gain charms specifically attractive to its own species.

The World admitted that he had never studied such a situation.

He then revealed the next Sequence.

Sequence 6 was Pleasure, or the Demoness of Pleasure. Its acting method involved giving pleasure to men and women, influencing them, and exercising control over them. Demonesses of this Sequence were particularly skilled at using special spider silk.

Audrey quickly regretted her earlier curiosity.

The World explained that the information could help her identify Demonesses.

Audrey considered the payment sufficient and promised to gather information about the disgraced nobles as quickly as possible. She even offered to submit the information to Mr. Fool so that He could transmit it to The World.

The way she referred to Mr. Fool as “He” immediately caught Klein’s attention.

The World deliberately reacted with surprise.

Audrey merely smiled and offered no explanation.

Then The World asked another question.

Were there any other High-Sequence Beyonders in the Sauron family of Intis?

Klein quietly observed The Sun.

Derrick showed no reaction, confirming that the City of Silver likely had no information about the Sauron family. Justice and The Hanged Man also appeared uncertain.

Klein decided to answer the question himself.

“What can you pay for it?”

“A formula. A Sequence 7 formula.”

“Okay. Deal.”

He isolated the exchange and provided the answer privately.

Afterward, The World bowed.

“Honorable Mr. Fool, thank you for your answer. It was very helpful to me.”

“This is an equivalent exchange. There’s no need to thank me.”

Klein almost shuddered at the awkwardness of hearing himself speak through the puppet.

The remaining discussion lasted only a few minutes before Klein ended the gathering.

Justice, The Hanged Man, and The Sun disappeared.

Klein looked at The World and smiled.

With a separate identity, many things had become far more convenient.

Unfortunately, the All-Black Eye still could not be used outside the gray fog.

Controlling The World had consumed a large amount of Klein’s spirituality, so after returning the puppet and storing the All-Black Eye, he descended to the real world and slept for half an hour.

After waking, he decided not to return to the libraries immediately. Justice’s promise removed the urgency.

Instead, he planned to visit one of Backlund’s cemetery sites after dark.

Klein wanted to test Azik’s copper whistle and discover exactly how broad its influence was and what its limits might be.

He left early because arriving at a cemetery in the evening would attract attention. The actual experiment would have to wait until night.

Key Events

  • The World requests detailed information on disgraced Loen nobles without mentioning the Pound family directly.
  • Justice agrees to gather the information and receives knowledge of another Beyonder pathway as payment.
  • The World reveals that the Assassin pathway’s Sequence 7 is Witch.
  • Klein reveals that the Witch potion can transform a man into a woman and enhance a woman’s beauty.
  • The World reveals Sequence 6, Pleasure, and its dangerous acting method.
  • The World asks whether the Sauron family still has High-Sequence Beyonders in Intis.
  • Klein answers privately in exchange for a Sequence 7 formula.
  • The Tarot gathering ends, with Klein pleased by the usefulness of The World identity.
