Chapter 196 - Spirit Medium Mirror

911 words · 6 min read

Klein hurried out of the hidden compartment and only relaxed after confirming that nothing had immediately followed him. The skeletal statue had left him deeply unsettled. Given what he had already suspected about Madam Sharon, he wondered whether it represented the Primordial Demoness or another evil god.

His unease soon became a genuine warning.

Looking through the bedroom window, Klein saw a carriage approaching the house.

Madam Sharon was back.

Klein immediately realized the danger. If Sharon was a Sequence 7 or higher member of the Demoness Sect, as he suspected, there was no reason to risk confronting her alone. His supplies of powerful charms were limited, and he could not casually rely on the Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem or Azik’s copper whistle. He also did not know whether Azik’s gigantic messenger possessed actual combat ability or merely served as a courier.

More importantly, Klein had already gathered enough evidence for the Nighthawks to intervene.

Why fight Sharon himself when he could simply bring the entire team down on her?

He ended his summoning, returned to his body, quickly extinguished the ritual, removed the spirituality barrier, and fled through the balcony and over the wall. He deliberately avoided resetting the balcony lock and took an expensive night carriage back to Zouteland Street.

Meanwhile, Madam Sharon entered her bedroom.

Her eyes suddenly revealed countless extremely thin, almost invisible threads. They had none of the usual glow associated with spirituality and instead resembled unnaturally altered strands of human hair.

Every thread had been severed.

They lay scattered across the floor.

Sharon immediately focused her attention on the thick steel safe.

...

At the Blackthorn Security Company, Dunn was reading the newspaper when Klein arrived.

Dunn immediately noticed that Klein’s infiltration had not gone according to plan.

Klein told him the important discovery: he suspected that Madam Sharon was a member of the Demoness Sect.

When Dunn asked what evidence he had found, Klein first described the photograph of the young man who looked remarkably similar to Sharon.

Dunn immediately connected the possibility to their earlier suspicions about Trissy and the Demoness Sect.

Klein then described the skeletal statue hidden inside the safe’s concealed compartment. It represented a beautiful woman with extraordinarily long hair, every strand ending in an eye.

Dunn confirmed it.

It was an image of the Primordial Demoness.

The Nighthawks needed to act immediately and seize control of Sharon before she could escape.

Klein agreed and pointed out that if Sharon really was a Mid-Sequence Beyonder, she would probably know someone had infiltrated her bedroom.

He then asked a broader mystical question: why did the seven orthodox gods generally use symbolic emblems without anthropomorphic representations, while the evil gods he knew—the True Creator and Primordial Demoness among them—possessed vivid humanlike images?

Dunn confirmed that this was indeed one distinction between orthodox gods and evil gods.

He then urged them not to waste time.

They needed another Nighthawk.

Dunn instructed Klein to bring Kenley downstairs. With three people, they could request the use of a Sealed Artifact, because Sharon was probably stronger than a Sequence 7.

When Klein asked which artifact they would use, Dunn named 3-0217: the Spirit Medium’s Mirror.

Klein remembered its classification and properties.

It was a dangerous Sealed Artifact that could only be used in operations involving at least three people. It had to be stored in absolute darkness and appeared to be an ordinary mirror whose back was coated in mercury and whose surface had three small cracks.

Its first investigator had seen a crying long-haired girl inside the reflection and then witnessed her climbing out of the mirror.

Subsequent experiments showed that the mirror produced different supernatural phenomena depending on who looked into it. Its most dangerous possibility was seeing one’s own reflection. If nobody looked at it, an image would still appear automatically every three hours as long as light was present.

The artifact had originally belonged to a Spirit Medium who eventually committed suicide while looking into it.

Klein realized that among the artifacts available behind Chanis Gate, 3-0217 was one of the more practical choices for a direct Beyonder confrontation.

That night, Royale was guarding Chanis Gate, Leonard was off duty, and Seeka Tron was patrolling elsewhere. Since the new recruit would not arrive until Sunday, Dunn chose Kenley rather than Frye, reasoning that Sharon’s Demoness pathway had little obvious connection to the powers of the dead.

A few minutes later, Dunn returned carrying the mirror wrapped completely in thick black cloth.

Klein almost could not tell that he was carrying a mirror at all.

Dunn handed the artifact to Kenley.

Only then did Klein truly recognize that he was no longer merely a support member. As a Sequence 8 Clown, he now had sufficient combat ability to fight directly.

He was nervous, but prepared his Slumber Charms and considered borrowing Kenley’s cane since he had left his own behind to make infiltration easier.

On the journey to Osna Street, Kenley admitted that this was his first mission involving a Sealed Artifact. Dunn and Klein both tried to reassure him, though Klein privately felt the same tension.

About twenty minutes later, the three Nighthawks reached Sharon’s home.

The house appeared completely quiet beneath the darkness.

Before entering, Klein used pendulum divination.

“There’s danger inside.”

After repeating the statement seven times, the topaz pendulum rotated clockwise with moderate speed.

There really was danger within.

Not overwhelming danger—but far from something they could safely ignore.
