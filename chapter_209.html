Chapter 209

Chapter 209 - Light

821 words · 5 min read

“Waaa!”

The baby inside Megose cried again, squirming as though it wanted to force its way into the world and save its mother. The black threads summoned from Saint Selena’s ashes recoiled beneath an invisible pressure.

Another cry struck Dunn and Klein. Their throats closed involuntarily, and blood poured from their noses, eyes, and ears as their capillaries ruptured. Klein only remained conscious because of his previous exposure to terrifying mystical voices, while Dunn was supported by his grip on the Saint’s ashes.

Megose’s headless body suddenly turned toward Klein. Her charred flesh peeled away, and the white bone blade extending from her arm looked both sacred and monstrous. Klein’s instincts warned him that she was about to attack before he could activate his final Flaring Sun Charm.

But Megose suddenly halted.

Dunn stood across from Klein, his black windbreaker fluttering. His head was lowered, and grotesque, thick objects resembling tentacles or poisonous snakes had emerged from his back. As a Nightmare, he was forcing Megose’s movements to slow.

With a violent struggle, Megose burst the tentacles apart. Blood sprayed through the room, but the black threads immediately absorbed it. Energized, the threads surged forward and wrapped tightly around Megose, especially her swollen, moving stomach.

Klein saw his chance and prepared to call upon the power of Light.

Then the baby cried again—this time repeatedly.

The black threads instantly recoiled and trembled. Dunn realized they would soon lose their restraint, and Megose would break free.

Without hesitation, he drove his right hand into his own left chest and tore out his still-beating heart.

Klein watched in shock as Dunn placed it inside Saint Selena’s urn.

His vision blurred with tears.

The effect was immediate. The black threads surged forward once more, binding Megose even more tightly. This time they ignored the baby’s cries and even sealed the terrible sound inside her body.

Klein’s tears mixed with blood as he whispered the Hermes word:

“Light!”

He poured almost everything he had left into his final Flaring Sun Charm. His mind went blank from exhaustion, but he still managed to throw the charm at Megose.

The black threads did not retreat. Dunn’s heart continued beating inside the urn.

Then sunlight poured through the enormous hole in the ceiling, descending through all three stories like a solid pillar. The Flaring Sun Charm gathered the sunlight and focused it upon Megose.

The radiance exploded.

Megose’s charred body lost its remaining arm, head, and chunks of flesh before collapsing completely. The half-formed, horrifying entity inside her no longer had a body to support its transformation. It became a furious mass of black gas and dissolved within the white fire.

The building shook violently, but most of the energy remained concentrated rather than spreading like an ordinary explosion.

When Klein opened his eyes, he saw the ruined reception hall. A scorched circle marked where Megose had stood, while a burned placenta lay on the floor. Dunn was still standing nearby in his black windbreaker. His heart continued beating inside the urn. Leonard lay unconscious across the room.

Klein felt an exhausted surge of happiness. They had stopped the descent. Perhaps ritualistic magic could even save Dunn.

Then Dunn turned toward him.

“We saved Tingen.”

His pale face was warm and relaxed. For one final moment, he looked almost like the young man he had once been. He winked at Klein.

Klein froze.

Inside the urn, Dunn’s heart stopped.

It transformed into a brilliant sphere of light before scattering away. Dunn’s arms went limp, and his body fell backward.

The urn dropped from his hands and struck the floor. The dark interior somehow kept the ashes sealed inside as the box rolled toward Klein.

“Captain!”

Klein desperately wanted to shout, but the words would not leave his throat.

We miss you too...

Then sudden pain exploded through his chest.

A pale, blood-covered hand emerged through the left side of Klein’s body.

Megose isn’t dead?

No. Someone else...

The mastermind?

Before Klein could even finish the thought, his consciousness began to fade. The hand withdrew. He saw a pair of unfamiliar leather boots and another pale hand reach down and seize Saint Selena’s urn.

His vision turned black.

Elsewhere, the ruined Blackthorn Security Company fell silent, resembling a cemetery. Minutes later, Leonard slowly regained consciousness.

He struggled upright and looked around.

Dunn was dead.

Klein was dead.

Both had terrible wounds in the left side of their chests.

“No...”

Leonard staggered to Klein and then Dunn, checking them over again and again as though refusing to accept what his eyes showed him. Eventually his knees collapsed.

Tears streamed down his face, washing blood and dust from his cheeks. He clenched his fists and repeatedly slammed them against the ruined floor, overwhelmed by grief, hatred, and self-loathing.

Then hurried footsteps approached.

Through his blurred vision, Leonard saw members of the Mandated Punishers and Machinery Hivemind arriving at the devastated scene.

Key Events

  • The baby’s cries suppress Saint Selena’s ashes, allowing Megose to break free.
  • Dunn sacrifices his own heart by placing it inside the Saint’s ashes, strengthening the restraints on Megose.
  • Klein uses his final Flaring Sun Charm together with sunlight to destroy Megose’s body and interrupt the unborn entity’s descent.
  • Dunn declares, “We saved Tingen,” but dies when his heart stops beating.
  • A mysterious attacker pierces Klein’s chest, takes Saint Selena’s ashes, and apparently kills Klein.
  • Leonard awakens to find both Dunn and Klein apparently dead and breaks down in grief and self-loathing.
  • The Mandated Punishers and Machinery Hivemind arrive at the destroyed Blackthorn Security Company.
