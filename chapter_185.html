Chapter 185 - Spiritual World

931 words · 6 min read

Klein remained calm when Hood Eugen declared that he was waiting for his cake. He casually asked who would bring it, only for Hood’s expression to collapse.

“There’s no cake... There’s no cake! You stole my cake!”

Hood suddenly attacked.

The insane Psychiatrist lunged from the bed with his mouth open, attempting to seize Klein and bite him. Klein reacted almost instinctively. He ducked, drove his shoulder into Hood’s abdomen, and used the Clown potion’s extraordinary coordination to roll away from Hood’s attempted bear hug. He quickly prepared to counterattack, only to realize that Hood had abruptly gone still.

Klein looked toward the corner and saw Dunn standing with his hands clasped and his head lowered.

The Captain had pulled Hood Eugen into a dream.

Klein stopped his attack and immediately used his silver ritual dagger to establish a spirituality barrier around the room. He prepared a simple altar, using three mint-infused candles to represent the Evernight Goddess, the Mother of the Secrets, and himself.

Dunn soon raised his head and explained that Hood’s dreams were an ocean of chaos that could not be guided.

At that moment, Hood’s eyes regained focus. He yawned comfortably and settled back onto the bed.

Klein poured Amantha extract into the candle flame. The calming fragrance immediately spread through the room, suppressing Hood’s nervousness, anger, and relief. The insane Psychiatrist peacefully sat on the edge of the bed, staring blankly at the crimson moon.

Klein knew he needed to weaken Hood’s remaining defenses before attempting spiritual communication. Since he lacked the specialized techniques of a professional Spirit Medium, he chose a more mundane approach.

He pulled out his tarot deck.

The deck contained only the twenty-two Major Arcana. Silver threads reinforced every card, allowing them to harm undead creatures. Klein had obtained them as a practical weapon, though their elaborate designs made them resemble collector’s items.

“Let’s play some card games.”

Hood repeated the word “Cards?” before accepting the deck. He copied Klein’s movements and successfully cut it.

His attention slowly became absorbed in the cards. He turned over the first one.

The Hanged Man.

Taking advantage of the distraction, Klein applied the Eye of the Spirit medicine to the candle flame. Its alcoholic fragrance filled the room and gradually made Hood drowsy and unfocused. The tarot cards slipped from his hands.

Klein himself resisted the medicine’s effects through Cogitation. He then drank the Sedative Agent, its icy liquid immediately sharpening his mind and keeping him lucid.

After preparing the remaining ingredients, Klein prayed for the power of the dark night, the power of mystery, and the Goddess’s grace, asking to communicate with Hood Eugen’s spirituality.

The candles darkened, and a strange “night” spread outward.

Klein allowed himself to be enveloped by it.

His spirit left the protection of his physical body and entered the spiritual world.

He found himself surrounded by limitless darkness, with countless transparent figures and magnificent streaks of color containing apparently infinite knowledge. Klein recognized the place as the Spiritual World.

A foggy realm appeared before him, surrounded by a faint tornado of light.

This represented Hood Eugen’s Body of Heart and Mind.

Klein pushed into the tornado.

Immediately, countless fragments of thought crashed against his spirit. Thousands of voices whispered contradictory and nonsensical things. One moment they praised a beautiful woman; another they described mundane bodily relief; a sorrowful thought could suddenly become ecstatic laughter.

The insane thoughts tried to cling to Klein and assimilate him, but the Sedative Agent and his own mental discipline allowed him to remain lucid. He pushed through the chaotic vortex and reached Hood Eugen’s spiritual world.

A translucent Hood Eugen stood before him in a groggy state.

Klein asked gently:

“Do you know Lanevus?”

“Yes.”

Hood’s answer caused the surrounding spiritual light to transform. Images formed within it, revealing the ordinary-looking, bespectacled Lanevus with his familiar sarcastic smile.

Klein then asked a carefully guided question:

“Why did Lanevus look for you?”

Hood Eugen answered.

Lanevus’s voice suddenly emerged through him, becoming charismatic and slightly manic:

“This is the worst era, but also the best era. As long as you seize the opportunity, we can become the rulers of this world. We can become true immortals!

“As long as you’re willing to help, I’ll tell you how to master your potion and avoid losing control. I’ll also promise that you’ll obtain godhood qualities in the future—immortal godhood qualities!

“You should be able to see the presence behind me. My promise is His promise. In some sense, the Psychology Alchemists are connected to Him.”

Lanevus admitted that the Psychology Alchemists themselves were currently too weak to provide much assistance. Unless Hood remained at his current level forever, they could not give him enough support.

Klein was stunned by the scale of Lanevus’s ambitions. The supposed Swindler was already talking about godhood qualities and claiming the backing of some hidden existence. His schemes clearly went beyond ordinary financial fraud.

Klein pressed further.

“What kind of assistance did Lanevus want you to provide?”

Hood Eugen remained silent.

Then he suddenly laughed.

“Help... Help... Help! Hahaha, I provided help! I provided help! I made...”

His words abruptly stopped.

His spiritual form twisted violently.

The surrounding spiritual light and darkness began transforming into a sinister black altar. A cross appeared atop it. Something seemed to hang from the cross while indistinct objects were piled beneath it.

The image began becoming clearer.

Then the entire spiritual world violently shook.

Klein immediately sensed catastrophic danger.

Without hesitation, he turned around and flew toward the chaotic tornado of thoughts, desperately attempting to escape.

Key Events

  • Klein and Dunn secretly enter Hood Eugen’s room and begin preparing a mediumship ritual.
  • Hood Eugen initially attacks Klein, but Dunn pulls him into a dream and temporarily neutralizes him.
  • Klein uses Amantha extract, tarot cards, Eye of the Spirit medicine, and Sedative Agent to calm Hood and protect his own mind before entering Hood’s spiritual world.
  • Klein enters the Spiritual World and successfully reaches Hood Eugen’s Body of Heart and Mind despite the chaotic thoughts of the insane Psychiatrist.
  • Hood confirms that Lanevus visited him, and Lanevus’s words reveal that he was seeking Hood’s assistance in pursuing a method to master potions and avoid losing control.
  • Lanevus claims that the Psychology Alchemists are connected to a mysterious existence capable of granting “godhood qualities.”
  • Klein realizes that Lanevus’s ambitions extend far beyond ordinary swindling and may involve a much larger hidden conspiracy.
  • When Klein asks what assistance Hood provided, Hood’s spiritual world suddenly manifests a sinister altar and cross.
  • The spiritual world violently shakes, and Klein immediately attempts to flee before the emerging phenomenon can affect him.
