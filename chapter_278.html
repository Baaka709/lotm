Chapter 278 - Free?

674 words · 4 min read

After dinner, Klein entered the billiard room at Bravehearts Bar and found Kaspars practicing alone. The moment Kaspars saw him, he immediately said, “Maric isn’t here. There are no other gatherings you want. I have nothing but weapons.”

Klein almost laughed at how well the man remembered his usual interests.

“Fortunately, I’m here to buy weapons.”

Maric’s absence confirmed Klein’s growing suspicion that he and Miss Bodyguard had been exposed and were changing locations. That meant Klein could no longer easily contact Miss Bodyguard, and it also threatened his plan to borrow Maric’s zombies as assistants for Millet Carter’s underground ruins.

Klein then explained that he needed explosives, specifically the kind commonly used in mines.

Kaspars immediately became stern.

“What do you want to do? I’m warning you, don’t try to do anything condemnable! I won’t allow my customers to challenge Sivellaus Yard.”

Klein reassured him that he only intended to demolish part of a private building while preserving the rest for renovation. He then asked whether Kaspars knew a reliable explosives expert who could tell him where to place the charges without bringing down the entire structure.

Kaspars’s suspicion faded.

Then an ethereal voice sounded from the corner.

“There’s no need.”

Klein turned and saw Miss Bodyguard sitting there without him noticing. Kaspars respectfully addressed her as Madam Sharron.

So her name is Sharron.

Sharron announced that Maric would never return to the bar and that anyone needing to contact him should leave a message through the specified method. Klein asked how he could reach Maric, and she simply answered, “Through Kaspars.”

Then Klein asked what she meant by saying there was no need for a demolition expert.

“I am one.”

Klein was momentarily speechless.

A Sequence 5 Beyonder was also a demolition expert?

Kaspars left to prepare the explosives, leaving Klein and Sharron alone. Klein then explained what he had learned from his visit to Rafter Pound. He believed the baronet had hidden something, because the mysterious people who had controlled the Pound household should not have been able to operate so openly without attracting the attention of the royal family or the churches.

Klein explained his plan to seal the ancient underground structure permanently.

“I’m afraid that I might accidentally release the evil spirit.”

Sharron neither agreed nor disagreed with his conclusions.

Klein then asked how much it would cost if he needed her help with the demolition.

“Free.”

Klein was stunned.

Sharron explained that once the entrance was collapsed, she would be the only one capable of entering and leaving. That was exactly what she wanted.

Klein immediately understood. Sealing the entrance would eliminate outside competition and leave Sharron free to deal with the evil spirit and whatever treasures remained inside.

“Deal!”

He then made another request: could she lend him several of Maric’s subordinates as assistants for appearances before Millet Carter?

“Alright.”

Klein arranged to begin at ten the next morning.

---

On Saturday, Xio rode into East Borough to check the results of her Lanevus search. While waiting for a carriage, she casually bought a newspaper.

Then she saw the headline.

A serious explosion had occurred at 1 Dharavi Street. The tenant in Unit 03-06 had died instantly, and several others had been injured.

Xio froze.

That was Williams’s apartment.

He was dead.

She immediately rejected the idea that it was a normal gas accident. Williams would never recklessly spend money installing gas, and even if Lanevus discovered that someone was looking for him, killing informants through an explosion made no sense.

The murder was too extreme.

“Poor Williams...”

“I-I will definitely avenge you!”

Xio decided to return to Fors, move to a backup residence, disguise herself, and investigate the explosion indirectly.

---

At 10:14, Klein returned to the underground structure with Sharron’s three silent zombie assistants. Sharron had already disappeared from sight.

Millet Carter looked over the three assistants and seemed satisfied.

“Is one of them a snake-repelling expert?”

“Yes,” Klein answered immediately. “They’re very good at repelling snakes.”

How could a zombie be afraid of snakes?
