Chapter 264 - A Five-Person Gathering

869 words · 5 min read

Klein viewed The World as the perfect complement to The Fool. If The Fool represented the beginning of the Tarot Club, then The World represented the completion of a cycle.

With this new identity, Klein would be able to make requests or perform actions without exposing Mr. Fool to unnecessary risks.

After dismissing the puppet, Klein returned to reality and left the All-Black Eye above the gray fog, where it could remain safely hidden.

He looked forward to the next Tarot gathering. Miss Justice should know far more about the aristocracy than he did, making her an ideal source of information about the Pound family. More importantly, The World could ask her directly without damaging Mr. Fool’s mysterious image.

The following day, Klein continued searching libraries for information about nobles. Before three in the afternoon, he returned above the gray fog and practiced controlling The World.

Shortly before the appointed time, he contacted The Sun.

At three, Justice, The Hanged Man, and The Sun appeared.

Audrey immediately noticed the unfamiliar figure seated at the opposite end of the table.

“This is?”

Klein introduced him calmly.

“This is a new member, Mr. World.”

Through the gray fog, Klein observed that The Sun’s astral projection had become purer, suggesting that Derrick had completely digested his Bard potion, though he had not yet reached the standards of Sequence 8.

After the greetings, Klein turned toward Justice and explained that the two candidates she had recommended were still being examined. He gave her a mission to pass on to them: investigate Lanevus, who was currently in Backlund.

Audrey accepted immediately.

Then The World took center stage.

“Mr. Fool told me that I can issue missions and gather ingredients here, is that so?”

Audrey confirmed that he could, though she reminded him that the gathering would first proceed according to Mr. Fool’s established schedule.

Klein turned to The Hanged Man and received six pages of Roselle’s diary.

The first page described the birth of Roselle’s third child, Bornova. Roselle wrote that Bernadette had the freedom to choose her own path, while Ciel had inherited a small amount of Beyonder characteristics and would have to follow Roselle’s pathway. Bornova, however, had inherited characteristics equivalent to those of a Sequence 5 Beyonder and already displayed traits of an Astromancer.

Zaratul visited secretly and praised Bornova as a lovely angel. When Roselle asked about the children’s futures, Zaratul predicted that Ciel’s death might be inevitable while Bernadette would eventually hate and turn against her father and become an important figure in the mysterious world.

Roselle found the prediction absurd and suspected that Zaratul’s divination might simply be wrong.

The next entry concerned Floren of the Sauron family, who had attempted to recruit Roselle as a subordinate. Roselle was furious at the arrogance but was fascinated by the Sauron family’s ancient history.

Their lineage supposedly stretched back more than two thousand years, surviving the Fourth Epoch and the rise and fall of powerful families such as the Antigonus and Zaratul clans.

Roselle recognized that such longevity meant the Saurons must possess deep secrets and possibly terrifying Sealed Artifacts.

Klein immediately wondered how Roselle had managed to overthrow such a powerful family.

The next diary entry revealed Roselle’s political development in Intis. His Civil Code encouraged inventions, protected commerce, and helped stimulate industrial growth. These reforms also improved his relationship with the Church of the Eternal Blazing Sun.

Klein inferred that Roselle’s later claim to the title of Emperor was probably supported by more than one church.

Another entry showed Roselle mocking Floren’s claim that he was destined to become a High-Sequence Beyonder because he resembled an ancestor. Roselle believed that talent mattered less than understanding the acting method and obtaining the required ingredients and rituals.

Klein understood the deeper truth: resemblance to the original owner of a High-Sequence characteristic could reduce the negative spiritual influence attached to it and increase the chance of successful advancement.

The fifth page was even more astonishing.

Roselle described participating in an ancient organization whose members believed individuality slowly disappeared until people “slept.” Their philosophy emphasized observing and remembering oneself in order to achieve purification before the end of the world.

They believed the original Creator was not truly dead and would awaken at the end of the world, reclaim everything, and create a new world and history.

The organization hated the True Creator and considered Him fallen and evil.

Most importantly, they possessed the second Blasphemy Slate and knowledge of the twenty-two divine pathways.

Roselle listed several pathways and wondered about their deeper relationships and whether the predicted apocalypse was real.

Klein desperately wished Roselle had simply written the organization’s name.

The final page revealed why the diary was so extraordinary.

Roselle had created the first Card of Blasphemy.

He intended to hide the ultimate secrets of the twenty-two divine pathways within these cards and scatter them throughout the world. Some would be given to his children, while others would go to people destined to receive them.

Then came the revelation that completely overwhelmed Klein:

Above Sequence 1 was Sequence 0.

Every pathway possessed a Sequence 0.

And Sequence 0 was the level of a True God.

For example:

Sequence 0, Sun.
