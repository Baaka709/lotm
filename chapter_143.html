Chapter 143

1,051 words · 7 min read

Klein paced around the small shooting range, still thinking about the strange secrecy surrounding the Seer pathway within the Church of the Evernight Goddess.

Why had they only provided the Sequence 9 Seer formula while concealing even the names of Sequences 8 and 7? Did they simply not want Nighthawks choosing the pathway to become too powerful? Or was there another reason—perhaps a connection between Seer Beyonders and the remnants of the Antigonus family?

The thought made Klein wary of the Church’s higher-ups. He briefly reconsidered whether openly applying for the Clown potion was safe. If the Church knew the acting method, then his unusually rapid digestion could attract attention.

Still, he found reasons to continue. The Tingen branch already possessed and distributed the Clown formula, so asking for it was perfectly normal. Klein could also explain his rapid progress by pointing to Spirit Medium Daly and Old Neil, claiming that their experience had inspired him.

As long as he did not digest the Clown potion absurdly quickly, reaching the next Sequence should not attract excessive attention.

For now, he would proceed cautiously and investigate further through divination.

Klein then resumed his usual shooting practice and revolver maintenance. The weapon he had received from Welch was surprisingly reliable, while Dunn and Leonard had taught him enough maintenance to keep it in good condition.

Not that he particularly cared if it broke. He could always request reimbursement.

On his way home, Klein noticed Elizabeth waiting outside 2 Daffodil Street.

She happily approached him.

“Good afternoon, Mr. Moretti. I’m guessing you just came back from Lamud Town?”

“No, I came from Zouteland Street.”

Elizabeth hesitated, then brightened.

“I came to tell you that I didn’t have the nightmare last night! I didn’t dream about the black-armored knight anymore. It was exactly like your divination!”

Of course, Klein knew the truth. The wraith had already been destroyed by 3-0782, so there was no longer anything capable of haunting her dreams.

Still, he smiled.

“I’m glad you’re free of your troubles. I’m also very satisfied with my divination.”

Elizabeth thanked him repeatedly before leaving in a carriage. As she departed, she proudly thought that Melissa certainly had no idea how amazing her brother was.

Klein watched her leave with mixed amusement.

Young ladies would rather trust their own conclusions than listen to explanations.

Back in his room, he rested before reviewing everything that had happened during the past week, including mysteries that remained unresolved. Afterward, he burned his notes.

Seeing that there was still time before three, he put on his oldest suit, went to Smyrin Bakery, bought a cup of sweet iced tea from Mrs. Wendy, and returned home.

Fifteen minutes before the Tarot Gathering, Klein sealed his room with spirituality and returned above the gray fog.

He first performed a pendulum divination:

“I should obtain the Clown potion through the Nighthawks.”

The pendulum turned clockwise.

A positive result.

Klein decided that obtaining the Clown potion through official channels was appropriate, though he remained uncertain about what would happen with the higher Sequences.

He then pressed his hand against the crimson star representing Sun.

He intended to summon the young man from the City of Silver early and find out whether Derrick had told his six-member council anything about their encounter. If not, Klein would give him a more reliable method of knowing when the Tarot Gatherings occurred.

---

In the City of Silver, Derrick sat quietly beside his bed waiting for The Fool’s summons.

To avoid being near anyone, he had not left the house since returning. He had nearly exhausted the food stored in his room and was beginning to feel painfully hungry, but he continued waiting.

Then dark-red light spread through the room.

Derrick was swallowed and found himself once again in the gray, boundless world above the fog.

The Fool sat at the head of the ancient table.

“Sun, we meet again.”

Derrick bowed his head.

“You are a Fool who keeps his word.”

“The other members will arrive soon. Before that, I want to confirm a few things.”

Klein deliberately switched to Loen and relied on the mysterious space to translate his words into Jotun.

He immediately discovered another advantage of the gray fog.

The space could translate his speech in real time.

So Justice and The Hanged Man would never have to worry about understanding Derrick’s Jotun.

Klein mentally lamented the burden of being a secret organization’s leader.

He then told Derrick that he could recite The Fool’s honorific name and taught him the incantation:

“The Fool that doesn’t belong to this era, the mysterious ruler above the gray fog; the King of Yellow and Black who wields good luck.”

Derrick carefully memorized the words.

Klein explained that whenever Derrick returned to the City of Silver, he should perform a simple ritual and recite the name. In the future, Klein would notify him before each gathering. Normally, Derrick would not need to isolate himself, but once he received the summons, he should separate himself from others within a thousand heartbeats.

“A thousand heartbeats?” Derrick murmured, memorizing the strange measure.

Klein then gave him a broad explanation of the Tarot Club.

As the gathering approached, Klein checked his pocket watch.

At exactly the appointed time, he pressed the crimson stars representing Justice and The Hanged Man.

Two hazy figures appeared beside Derrick.

Audrey Hall immediately surveyed the unfamiliar surroundings and froze.

Then she heard The Fool’s calm voice.

“This is our newest member. His code name is Sun.

“This is Miss Justice, and that is Mr. Hanged Man.”

Audrey’s surprise instantly turned into delight.

A new member!

The Tarot Club was growing, and she felt almost like the protagonist of a grand story.

Alger, however, frowned inwardly.

The Fool had suddenly introduced a new member without warning them first.

He should have at least mentioned it beforehand...

Still, Alger quickly accepted the situation and greeted Justice and Sun.

Audrey immediately entered her Spectator state and carefully observed Derrick.

He seems young. His movements suggest nervousness and restraint, yet he remains quiet and composed... Almost like a lone wolf.

Her attention then returned to The Fool sitting at the end of the bronze table.

With visible excitement, she spoke.

“Mr. Fool, I’ve collected another two pages of Emperor Roselle’s diary.”
