Chapter 189 - Prayers and Replies

805 words · 5 min read

Back in Tingen, Klein was trying to convince Benson and Melissa to join him at the theater to watch the popular play *The Return of the Count*. Benson seemed interested, while Melissa was hesitant until Klein mentioned that the play featured a famous genius mechanic. She finally agreed.

Just as Klein was imagining other forms of entertainment he might pursue with his improved physical abilities, a buzzing filled his ears and dizziness briefly overcame him.

Someone was praying to him.

Klein immediately excused himself and went upstairs, locking and sealing his bedroom before returning to the world above the gray fog. He connected himself to the crimson star belonging to Justice.

A hazy image appeared.

Audrey was sitting in a secluded corner of Duke Negan’s mansion, her head bowed and hands clasped as she prayed to The Fool. She reported that she was attending the Duke’s ball and had encountered someone she suspected was Qilangos disguised as Baron Gramir.

She explained that subtle differences in Gramir’s behavior had allowed her to recognize the disguise, particularly because she had memorized his usual mannerisms and habits. She suspected Qilangos was using the appearance-changing abilities of the Creeping Hunger.

Klein listened carefully.

Qilangos had actually infiltrated Duke Negan’s ball under another identity, yet he apparently had not realized that one of the guests was a fully digested Spectator capable of noticing tiny behavioral discrepancies.

The situation immediately raised the question of what Qilangos was trying to accomplish.

Klein considered whether he should somehow provide physical assistance, but he had no way to safely transport the Flaring Sun Charm to Backlund yet. He therefore decided that the best course was to pass Audrey’s information to Alger and rely on the powerful Beyonders already present around Duke Negan.

Back in Duke Negan’s prayer room, Audrey received The Fool’s response.

An illusory gray fog appeared around her, revealing the majestic presence of The Fool before she heard his deep voice:

“I’m aware.”

The mysterious vision immediately disappeared.

Audrey’s fear and uncertainty vanished. Reassured that Mr. Fool already knew what was happening, she returned to the dining hall with a much steadier mind.

...

Klein then needed to notify The Hanged Man.

Rather than simply repeating Audrey’s words, he worried that doing so would weaken his image as an unfathomable existence personally communicating with his followers. Instead, he transformed the scene of Audrey’s prayer into a distorted, censored vision and sent that image through The Hanged Man’s crimson star.

Alger was in a simple room at the Holy Wind Cathedral, reviewing reports on Qilangos, when his vision abruptly filled with thick gray fog.

He saw The Fool’s ancient chair and then a blurred image of Audrey praying about Qilangos’s disguise as Baron Gramir.

Alger immediately understood what he was witnessing.

“Praise you, Mr. Fool!”

Everything disappeared.

The experience once again demonstrated to Alger how extraordinary The Fool was. Even within the Holy Wind Cathedral—a place deeply associated with the Church of Storms—The Fool could apparently descend without warning and deliver information directly.

Alger decided to find Ace Snake, the Archbishop of the Backlund diocese and one of the highest-ranking figures in the Church of Storms. If Alger could not personally kill Qilangos, at least ensuring the pirate’s death would be enough.

...

Klein returned to his bedroom and immediately wrote a short letter to Azik.

He informed Azik that, according to an urgent source, Qilangos had used the abilities of Creeping Hunger to disguise himself as Baron Gramir and infiltrate Duke Negan’s ball. Klein did not explicitly ask Azik to intervene; he simply wanted him to know.

He then summoned Azik’s gigantic skeletal messenger with the copper whistle and handed it the letter before dismissing it.

Klein considered the possibility of personally using the summoning ritual to bring the Flaring Sun Charm to Backlund, but rejected it.

Qilangos was a powerful Sequence 6 Wind-blessed carrying Creeping Hunger. The journey would also require an awkward series of rituals to move the charm through the gray fog, and repeatedly relying on such methods could damage Klein’s carefully cultivated image.

More importantly, Klein believed that Duke Negan’s ball was already heavily protected. As one of the most influential nobles outside the royal family and a key figure behind the Conservative Party, Negan would certainly have powerful Beyonders protecting the event. Qilangos would hardly dare infiltrate such a gathering without a carefully planned objective.

Now that Audrey had exposed him early, the authorities would likely be prepared.

Klein’s only remaining uncertainty was how quickly Azik’s messenger traveled. If it could move rapidly through the spirit world, Azik might still arrive before the situation reached its climax. Otherwise, he might only learn about the incident from the newspapers the next day.

With no further action available to him, Klein could only put the matter aside and wait.

Key Events

  • Klein receives Audrey’s urgent prayer warning that Qilangos has infiltrated Duke Negan’s ball disguised as Baron Gramir.
  • Audrey’s Spectator abilities allow her to recognize subtle differences in Gramir’s mannerisms and cologne, exposing Qilangos’s disguise.
  • Klein replies directly to Audrey through the gray fog, reassuring her that he is aware of the situation.
  • Klein forwards the information to Alger by showing him a disguised vision of Audrey’s prayer rather than directly acting as a messenger.
  • Alger realizes the seriousness of the situation and decides to seek assistance from the powerful Archbishop Ace Snake.
  • Klein sends Azik a letter warning him that Qilangos has infiltrated Duke Negan’s ball.
  • Klein considers but rejects personally transporting the Flaring Sun Charm to Backlund because of the danger, inconvenience, and risk to his image.
  • Klein concludes that Duke Negan’s gathering is likely heavily protected and decides there is little more he can safely do for the moment.
