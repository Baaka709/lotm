Chapter 188 - Ball

1,056 words · 7 min read

Qilangos carefully examined his new appearance in the mirror. The transformation produced by his mystical glove was flawless: sharp nose, thin eyebrows, faint blue eyes, and every detail matching Baron Gramir. After practicing the man’s mannerisms, Qilangos dragged the unconscious Baron into a wardrobe and casually snapped his neck, eliminating the possibility of leaving a witness.

He then dressed in a black double-breasted suit, applied amber cologne, and carefully adjusted his hair. When he left the room, he maintained Gramir’s arrogant noble demeanor and warned the butler not to enter because something important was being stored inside.

The butler informed him that his carriage, personal servant, and Duke Negan’s invitation were waiting. Qilangos adopted the Baron’s mannerisms perfectly and headed downstairs.

Internally, however, he was contemptuous of the nobility. Baron Gramir was deeply in debt yet continued maintaining an enormous household, including a butler, personal servant, attendants, numerous maids, workers, a driver, stablemaster, gardener, chef, and sous chef. To Qilangos, these nobles valued appearances and dignity above everything else. He had even been forced to learn their peculiar pronunciation and aristocratic slang to maintain his disguise.

...

Meanwhile, Xio Derecha and Fors Wall were hiding in a cramped apartment in Cherwood.

Their plan had succeeded in exposing Qilangos to the authorities, but they had discovered nothing about his purpose in Backlund. They had reported Darkholme’s murder and secretly informed the police that Qilangos might be responsible. As expected, the case had immediately been transferred to the Mandated Punishers.

Within a day, news that Rear Admiral Hurricane had infiltrated Backlund spread among the various official enforcement organizations. Xio and Fors abandoned their rented home and began operating secretly, since the Nighthawks, Mandated Punishers, and Machinery Hivemind regarded unofficial Beyonders with suspicion and often treated them as potential criminals.

Fors remained confident that patience would eventually produce results. As long as the authorities continued hunting Qilangos, he would eventually make a mistake.

Xio, however, was worried that he might act quickly and escape Backlund before anyone could react. Without capturing him, her progress toward Sequence 8—and eventually Sequence 6 or 5—would be delayed. More painfully, she still had not recovered the things that belonged to her family and had not seen her younger brother for almost a year.

Fors comforted her by joking that she should someday let her turn the entire experience into a novel.

Xio also placed some faith in Audrey’s generosity, believing they would still receive a substantial reward even if Qilangos escaped. Fors then reflected on the nature of “fortuitous encounters.” In the Beyonder world, such opportunities were almost always accompanied by danger. Even the strange ravings Fors experienced during each full moon might eventually cause unknown negative consequences.

Safe opportunities, she concluded, were extremely rare unless someone received the favor of an orthodox deity or a genuinely friendly hidden existence—which itself carried the danger that the supposed benefactor might merely be an evil god or devil in disguise.

Xio responded with a prayer to the Evernight Goddess.

...

At Duke Negan’s mansion in the Empress Borough, a grand ball was underway.

The ground-floor dancing hall was magnificent, with elaborate stone flooring and an excellent ensemble providing music. Guests stood along the second-floor railing and watched couples dancing below, occasionally descending together after an invitation.

Other rooms were available for guests to rest, while another hall at the rear contained food, wine, music, and quieter conversations. Balconies offered views of the garden beneath the crimson moon.

Audrey Hall had participated in the opening dance but soon left the second floor after noticing numerous young men preparing to approach her for dances. She found the endless social rituals tedious and privately observed that dancers sometimes resembled animals competing for mates.

While walking toward the dining hall, Audrey encountered Baron Gramir.

She greeted him with flawless aristocratic etiquette.

Baron Gramir complimented her as the brightest gemstone at the ball before continuing toward the dance floor. Audrey proceeded in the opposite direction.

Then she stopped.

Something was wrong.

The Baron’s behavior differed from his usual habits. Normally, Gramir became noticeably awkward around beautiful women or women of higher social standing. He would avert his gaze, then steal glances repeatedly.

Tonight, he appeared completely confident.

His cologne was also wrong.

Audrey had attended enough gatherings to recognize Gramir’s usual habit: he applied Amber cologne hours before a party, so only the subtle final note remained by the time guests arrived. Tonight, however, the stronger middle note was still present.

As a Spectator who had completely digested her potion, Audrey was exceptionally sensitive to such details.

A possibility suddenly struck her.

Could Baron Gramir actually be Qilangos?

The Creeping Hunger could change appearances.

The more Audrey considered it, the more convincing the idea became. She immediately grew tense and nervous.

If Gramir truly was Qilangos, what was he trying to accomplish here?

Audrey wished she had been able to bring Susie to the ball so the dog could investigate him, but she could not waste time. She hurried into the dining hall and approached her father, Count Hall, who was talking with senior political figures.

She politely borrowed him for a moment and led him onto a secluded balcony.

Audrey carefully explained everything she had noticed about Baron Gramir, emphasizing the behavioral differences and the unusual cologne. She then cautiously mentioned that Viscount Glaint had told her that Qilangos possessed the ability to disguise himself and asked whether the pirate admiral’s presence in Backlund might explain the anomaly.

Count Hall listened closely.

His expression became extremely grave.

Yet he quickly smiled and reassured his daughter that he would handle the matter himself. He instructed Audrey to find her mother and remain with her.

Audrey obediently agreed but remained anxious as she watched her father begin quietly discussing the matter with another noble.

She felt compelled to do more to protect her family.

Instead of returning directly to her mother, Audrey changed direction and went to Duke Negan’s private prayer room. She entered, locked the door, and sat before the symbol of the Lord of Storms.

Then, after finding a secluded position, she clasped her hands and recited softly in Hermes:

“The Fool that doesn’t belong to this era, you are the mysterious ruler above the gray fog; you are the King of Yellow and Black who wields good luck.”
