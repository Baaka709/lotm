Chapter 258 - Murder Case

557 words · 3 min read

Rain had soaked Backlund’s concrete roads, leaving the city wrapped in misty gaslight. Klein sat quietly in a rental carriage, watching the evening streets, when the temperature suddenly dropped. Miss Bodyguard, dressed in her eerie black Gothic dress, silently appeared across from him.

“That Eye of Wisdom sensed my presence,” she said.

Klein wasn’t surprised. Eye of Wisdom possessed several mystical items, and Klein suspected that an organization might stand behind him. Otherwise, it was difficult to explain how a Beyonder with limited power could collect so many powerful artifacts over decades.

Before he could dwell on it, Miss Bodyguard frowned.

“A rich smell of blood.”

Klein looked through the carriage window. At the mouth of a secluded alley lay a woman in a beautiful dress. Her stomach had been brutally cut open, her organs apparently removed, and blood had spread across the wet ground.

Klein’s former Nighthawk experience immediately brought several possibilities to mind. Murders of this nature were often linked to devil worship, particularly the Blood Sanctify Sect, a loose alliance of devil-worshipping clans dating back to the Fourth Epoch.

Miss Bodyguard quietly identified it.

“It looks like it was done by the Blood Sanctify Sect.”

Her figure rapidly faded away.

The carriage continued past the crime scene as the police arrived. Klein deliberately remained an ordinary passerby rather than involving himself. He reminded himself that the authorities would handle the matter.

By the time he returned to Minsk Street, the murder had already slipped from his thoughts. He visited the Sammers and told Mrs. Stelyn that Mary could collect the evidence the following afternoon, then returned home to read newspapers.

The next morning, Klein collected the developed photographs and selected the best one showing Doragu Gale and Erica Taylor. He then went to the Rice Police Station and recovered the ten-pound bail money. Afterward, he withdrew another five hundred pounds from his account.

Before lunch, he handed Miss Bodyguard six hundred pounds, leaving himself only 146 pounds, eight soli, and five pence in liquid funds.

He was enjoying a medium-well T-bone steak when the doorbell rang.

The visitor was an old gentleman in a light-gray overcoat, silk top hat, and carrying a black-and-gold cane. His sharp blue eyes and thick Midseashire accent identified him as Millet Carter.

Klein invited him inside and gave him hot water.

Carter explained that he had purchased a house on Williams Street, formerly owned by a bankrupt viscount. While renovating it, workers had discovered a hidden door in the basement leading to a massive underground structure. Carter had halted construction because he feared there might be danger inside.

He wanted Klein to investigate.

“Why didn’t you call the police?” Klein asked.

“I don’t want too many people to know about this, especially the government.”

Carter offered fifty pounds for the job, provided that Klein use no more than three assistants.

Fifty pounds was an attractive price, but Klein did not immediately accept. He excused himself to the bathroom and performed a divination.

“I should accept this commission.”

The coin gave a positive result.

He then asked Miss Bodyguard for her intuition. She appeared in the mirror and gave a simple assessment.

“There’s a certain amount of danger, but it’s not great.”

Klein put away the coin, washed his hands, and returned to the living room.

“I’ll take this commission.”

Key Events

  • Miss Bodyguard senses a strong smell of blood and identifies a gruesome murder as likely related to the Blood Sanctify Sect.
  • Klein recovers his bail money and withdraws another 500 pounds, then gives 600 pounds to Miss Bodyguard.
  • Klein receives a new commission from Millet Carter involving a hidden underground structure.
  • Carter offers 50 pounds and asks Klein to investigate privately rather than involve the police.
  • Klein performs a divination and confirms that accepting the commission is favorable.
  • Miss Bodyguard senses some danger but considers it manageable.
  • Klein officially accepts the investigation.
