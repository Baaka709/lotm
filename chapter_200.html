Chapter 200 - The Demoness of Pleasure

1,115 words · 7 min read

Klein continued watching the vision, unable to stop thinking about the woman in white’s description of men as a synonym for war and how it might correspond to another Sequence pathway. His knowledge was limited by his status as an official Nighthawk, leaving him aware of only scattered Mid-Sequence names from various sources. He considered five possibilities: the Arbiter pathway, though Sheriff and Interrogator seemed too focused on judgment; the Dark Emperor pathway, whose Lawyer Sequence suggested the manipulation of rules and order; the Hunter pathway, whose Provoker and Pyromaniac abilities were closely connected to conflict and slaughter; the Criminal pathway of the Blood Sanctify Sect; and the Prisoner pathway of the Rose School of Thought.

The vision then changed. Madam Sharon had just finished bathing, her wet hair clinging around her face as she approached a man waiting in bed.

“Do you need me to take care of Maynard?” she asked seductively.

The middle-aged man refused, saying that doing so without leaving traces was impossible.

Klein immediately recognized him as Tingen City’s current mayor, a Conservative Party member whose picture frequently appeared in the newspapers.

The scenes continued, revealing Madam Sharon meeting Members of Parliament, businessmen, and government officials. She acted as a broker, helping them arrange donations, bypass campaign regulations, bribe voters, and obtain protection. Many of these powerful men were also her lovers.

Klein found the whole experience almost absurdly documentary-like: a tour through Tingen’s upper circles accompanied by an extraordinary number of bedroom scenes. More importantly, he wondered whether Sharon’s Beyonder abilities were responsible for making people so vulnerable to her charms.

His earlier divination had already established that these influential figures did not know Sharon’s true identity and had not conspired with her to murder John Maynard. That meant Maynard’s death had apparently been Sharon’s own decision.

But why?

She possessed abilities capable of interfering with divination and producing a death caused by overwhelming sexual pleasure while making it appear natural. Yet Maynard’s position made the murder unnecessarily dangerous. She could have chosen someone far less important without drawing the attention of the Nighthawks and other organizations. Nor had she removed the dangerous white-bone statue and other evidence from her home, despite knowing that Maynard’s furious wife would almost certainly demand an investigation.

Could killing Maynard have been necessary for her “acting”?

With Sharon’s spirit still lingering, Klein performed another Dream Divination, this time asking for her true motive in killing Maynard.

The vision showed Sharon alone with a glass of blood-red wine. She paced around her room in a loose sleeping gown before finally drinking the wine in one swallow, as though she had made an important decision.

The scene ended without explaining her motive.

Klein tried several more divinations, but none gave him a clearer answer. As Sharon’s spirit began fading, he made one final attempt.

“Sequence potion formula of the Demoness pathway.”

He quickly entered a dream with the help of Cogitation.

The dark hall returned, along with the mysterious woman in white. Sharon knelt before her and could see only the woman’s flawless legs. The melodious voice explained that the next Sequence was **Pleasure**, and that upon advancement Sharon would become a Demoness of Pleasure.

The woman explained that irresistible pleasure was itself a form of agony and should become a principle guiding her life. A Demoness of Pleasure would retain and strengthen the Witch’s abilities, become even more beautiful, possess greater seductive power, and be capable of providing extraordinary pleasure. She would also gain the ability to produce strange spider-like threads.

An ancient silver book appeared, revealing the potion formula.

Its main ingredients were a pair of Succubus eyes and an adult Black Widow Spider’s silk gland. The supplementary ingredients included purified water, Black Jimsonweed juice, the remains of a Succubus’s hair, Feynapotter Fly Powder, and Mummy ashes.

Another vision followed.

Sharon had reverted to her former male appearance, appearing as the young man from the earlier photograph. The mysterious woman explained the reason behind the horrifying name of Sequence 7.

“I still can’t believe it’s called Witch!” Sharon protested.

The woman calmly replied that those who wished to approach the Primordial had to become increasingly like “Her.” Since the Primordial was a woman, they too had to become women.

Once someone became a Witch, the transformation was permanent: their appearance and charm would increase greatly, while they gained invisibility, substitutes, basic dark magic, the ability to disrupt divination, and control over black flames and icy frost.

She then gave Sharon the Witch potion’s ingredients: Abyss Demonic Fish blood, an Agate Peacock egg, purified water, Jimsonweed juice, Shadow Lizard scales, and Daffodil Juice.

The visions continued, showing Klein further formulas and the abilities associated with the Assassin and Instigator pathways, until Sharon’s spirit finally dispersed completely.

Klein returned to reality and stopped the ritual. After cleaning the altar and removing the spiritual barrier, he told Dunn everything he had learned, including his suspicions concerning Maynard’s murder.

Dunn agreed that Pleasure did not explain why Sharon would deliberately target someone of such high status. He concluded that they needed to investigate Sharon’s origins and movements over the previous several years, especially the mysterious hall from Klein’s visions. The matter would have to be reported to the Holy Cathedral so that specialized investigators could be assigned.

For the moment, Dunn ordered Klein to inspect the servants on the first floor. Anyone who had awakened would need to be brought in and made to sign a confidentiality agreement. Dunn himself would remain on the second floor.

He covered Sealed Artifact 3-0271 with black cloth.

Klein finally realized why the fierce battle had never awakened any servants: Dunn had placed them into a deep sleep from the very beginning.

Still physically cold and stiff from the battle, Klein descended with painstakingly quiet steps. On the way, he retrieved the tarot cards he had used to block the bedroom door, cleaned them, and returned them to his pocket.

Then he wondered how he could quickly determine whether a person was truly asleep. Divining each servant individually would be far too troublesome. Since Dunn was a Nightmare, Klein decided he would ask his captain for a simple method.

He turned back toward the bedroom.

Before reaching it, he glanced through the partially open door and saw the shattered full-length mirror. Several large palm-sized shards still clung to its frame.

Within one cracked shard, Klein saw Dunn Smith kneeling beside Kenley’s dead body.

Then Dunn suddenly raised his head.

His gray eyes were deep and unreadable.

The corners of his lips were stained with crimson blood.

Klein instinctively turned away, stepped clear of the doorway, and pressed his back against the wall.
