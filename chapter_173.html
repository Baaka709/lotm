Chapter 173

Chapter 173 - Zombiefication

897 words · 6 min read

Before Klein could react, Maynard’s rigid corpse suddenly lunged at him. Thanks to the Clown potion’s enhanced reflexes, agility, and coordination, Klein instinctively jumped onto the back of a high chair to evade the attack. He landed on its narrow edge but immediately adjusted his center of gravity and remained balanced.

As the zombie pounced again, Klein swung his cane into its ribs, knocking it down. He reached for his revolver, but immediately reconsidered. Blowing a hole through a Member of Parliament’s corpse would create an enormous problem for the police and Maynard’s family.

Instead, Klein remembered the Requiem Charm in his uniform. He threw it toward the zombie and activated it with the Hermes incantation “Crimson.” A peaceful aura spread through the room, calming the corpse and suppressing its agitation.

Klein prepared to perform a purification ritual, but Maynard suddenly moved again, once more focusing on Klein’s left pocket.

Klein jumped onto the window ledge as the chair behind him shattered. He realized the zombie was specifically targeting something in his pocket and took out the Slumber Charm. Since the dead were already in a form of eternal sleep, he hoped the charm could force Maynard back into that state.

After reciting “Crimson” and throwing the charm, Klein watched a dark red flame spread through the room. A gentle force of sleep overwhelmed the zombie, causing Maynard to collapse unconscious.

Not daring to trust the situation yet, Klein immediately prepared a full purification ritual. He used Amantha extract, Slumber flowers, chamomile, Drago tree bark, Full Moon Essence Oil, and Holy Night Powder to establish a ritual area around the sleeping corpse.

After lighting the candles and reciting prayers to the Evernight Goddess and the powers of sleep, silence, and the crimson moon, a thin black steam began to rise from Maynard’s body.

Klein then used Spirit Vision and divination repeatedly to confirm that the corpse would not awaken again. Only after receiving reassuring results did he end the ritual and remove the spirituality barrier.

Yet one mystery remained: why had Maynard’s corpse suddenly reanimated in the first place?

For a Seer, a corpse’s return should normally be preceded by some warning or unusual spiritual signs. Klein had experienced none of them. That suggested that some external influence had been involved.

He remembered how the zombie had repeatedly tried to reach for his left pocket.

Klein took out the ancient copper whistle Azik had given him—the object he used to summon Azik’s skeletal messenger.

He immediately suspected the whistle.

After performing a quick divination with a copper penny, he received a positive result. The copper whistle really had been responsible for triggering Maynard’s zombification.

Klein reasoned that Azik was either a descendant of Death or possessed some other strong connection to Death. An object tied to him could therefore have special effects when brought into a corpse-laden or spiritually dangerous environment.

He decided never to carry the whistle casually into cemeteries, ancient castles, or similarly haunted locations. Otherwise, he might simply be creating supernatural trouble for himself.

Afterward, Klein easily lifted Maynard’s naked body back onto the bed. He noticed the obvious mark left by his cane on the corpse and decided to leave that problem to the police.

The Requiem and Slumber Charms he had used could also be reported as mission expenses and reimbursed.

When Klein opened the door, Inspector Tolle immediately asked what had happened. He had heard the commotion from outside.

Klein jokingly claimed that Maynard had come back to life and tried to give him a passionate hug. When Tolle protested, Klein explained the truth in more serious terms: Maynard had inexplicably become a zombie, but Klein had used ritualistic magic to purify the desecration and return him to eternal sleep.

Tolle immediately asked whether the zombification was connected to Maynard’s death.

Klein admitted that he did not know. The supernatural world contained many things that could not easily be explained, and his role was limited to assisting with the autopsy.

He then revealed what he had learned through mediumship. Maynard had been having an affair with a woman and had collapsed at the climax of their encounter, clutching his chest. Klein cautiously said that this was theoretically the cause of death, though the police should wait for the formal autopsy.

He handed Tolle the portrait he had drawn during the ritual.

The moment Tolle saw it, he recognized the woman.

“Madam Sharon!”

Tolle explained that Sharon was the most beautiful and sought-after widow in Tingen. She had been the second wife of Baron Khoy and, after becoming widowed, had become a prominent figure in high society. She was welcomed by both wealthy merchants and aristocrats and could be invited to banquets hosted by both the Conservative Party and the New Party.

More intriguingly, rumors claimed that Sharon maintained “friendly” relationships with numerous nobles and senior civil servants in Backlund, including her stepson, the current Baron Khoy. Her political and social influence was considerable.

Klein concluded that she was an exceptionally powerful socialite.

He then made it clear that investigating or interrogating Sharon was beyond his responsibilities. Tolle would have to handle that side of the case.

Klein also reminded him of one final problem: before the purification, he had struck Maynard’s corpse with his cane.

“That part is yours to deal with,” Klein said, leaving the police to explain the marks.

Key Events

  • Klein uses his cane, Requiem Charm, and Slumber Charm to suppress the zombie without immediately damaging the politician’s body.
  • He performs a full purification ritual and confirms through Spirit Vision and divination that Maynard will not rise again.
  • Klein discovers that Azik’s ancient copper whistle was responsible for triggering Maynard’s zombification.

  • Klein leaves the investigation into Sharon and the explanation for Maynard’s cane injuries to the police.
