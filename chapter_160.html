Chapter 160

1,033 words · 6 min read

On Tuesday morning, Klein had finished his mysticism studies but remained in the break room instead of reading. He was playing cards with Leonard, Kenley, and Royale, though his thoughts were elsewhere.

He had only asked Azik to create an opportunity that would let him take Sealed Artifact 3-0782 out of Chanis Gate. Everything after that would depend on his ability to improvise.

His distraction showed in his game. Within an hour, he had lost five soli. Since purchasing the ingredients needed for Flaring Sun Charms had left his savings below one pound again, and the carriage he rented to search for the red-chimney house cost two soli every day, Klein could not afford to keep losing.

As Kenley shuffled the cards, Klein idly spun a copper penny. He suddenly felt Royale staring intensely at him.

*Does she think I’m using divination to cheat?*

He quickly stopped and slapped the penny onto the table.

Just then, Dunn entered.

“There’s a situation in Morse Town. Leonard, please handle it.”

Morse Town?

Klein immediately became alert but pretended to be curious.

Dunn explained that several paranormal incidents had recently occurred there. People walking near the cemetery heard sobbing and saw vague figures. A widow had encountered what appeared to be her dead husband during the night. An elderly man living alone constantly heard heavy footsteps in his house, only for them to vanish whenever he lit a candle or gas lamp.

Since most residents were followers of the Goddess, the local priest had reported the incidents.

Klein quickly recognized Azik’s handiwork. None of the events had harmed anyone, making them almost seem like elaborate ghost stories.

He suggested that the cases might share a hidden connection and that divination could help uncover it.

“I think I can help Leonard.”

Leonard immediately scrutinized him, but Dunn eventually agreed.

Klein added that some of the phenomena might require ritual purification.

“That makes sense.”

Dunn assigned both of them to Morse Town and then noted that Klein would miss his afternoon combat training.

Perfect. The first step was complete.

But Klein was not satisfied yet.

“Captain, I think we should prepare for the worst. If a powerful wraith is behind this, Leonard and I might not be enough. Morse Town is two or three hours away, and even if we sent a telegram for assistance, we would have to survive until help arrived.”

Dunn asked what he wanted.

“I want another teammate. And according to the rules, a mission involving three or more Nighthawks can apply for a Level 3 Sealed Artifact. 3-0782 is especially suited for this mission.”

Leonard laughed.

“Exactly your style. Careful, cautious, never taking risks.”

Klein ignored the remark and looked toward Dunn.

After considering the recent string of coincidences, Dunn agreed.

“Kenley, join them. Quickly write an application. Once I sign it, retrieve Sealed Artifact 3-0782 from Chanis Gate.”

Klein inwardly celebrated while maintaining a solemn expression.

With Seeka monitoring Hood Eugen and Frye stationed at Chanis Gate, the necessary personnel were available.

Later, Klein waited with Leonard near the stairway to the basement. Leonard suddenly brought up his own secret again, warning Klein that even during a mission, he would never be able to discover his hidden uniqueness.

Klein almost found the man's confidence amusing.

“How can you be so sure that my uniqueness won’t help reveal your secret?”

Leonard smiled.

“It will, huh? I’ll wait for you to discover it.”

Klein silently promised himself that one day, after gathering enough information and items, he would help Leonard perform a divination above the gray fog.

Soon, Kenley appeared carrying the Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem. The moment Klein sensed the artifact’s familiar warmth and purity, he knew the most difficult first step of his plan had succeeded.

He had finally gotten the opportunity to bring the source of his coveted Flaring Sun Charms out into the field.

The three Nighthawks then left the Blackthorn Security Company and headed for their carriage on Zouteland Street.

Kenley nervously wondered whether the artifact’s purifying power would affect their horse.

“I don’t want a horse that can only praise the Sun to pull the carriage...”

Klein explained that 3-0782 only purified living beings possessing a high level of intelligence.

Fortunately, he had no plans to drive. During the three-hour journey, he sat in the carriage with the Sealed Artifact while Leonard and Kenley took turns handling the reins.

At lunchtime, they finally reached Morse Town.

The surrounding plains were covered by endless golden wheat fields, creating a beautiful and peaceful scene. Kenley openly admired the view, while Leonard seemed prepared to recite poetry before settling for the same simple conclusion.

“How beautiful.”

Klein suppressed his amusement and followed them out of the carriage.

A gray-haired, blue-eyed priest in a black gown approached and drew the crimson moon over his chest.

“Praise the Lady. Are you the friends sent by Saint Selena Cathedral to help us?”

Leonard smiled.

“Yes, Priest Siur. May the Goddess bless you. We’re here to deal with the recent paranormal incidents.”

Priest Siur hurriedly emphasized the word “seemingly” when he noticed the growing crowd.

Morse Town was small enough that its inhabitants all knew one another, so news of the strange events had spread quickly. Many residents surrounded the newcomers, anxious to see whether the Church had finally sent help.

Leonard immediately played the part of an experienced supernatural investigator, dramatically producing holy water, silver daggers, Dark Sacred Emblems, and even garlic from inside his clothes.

Klein found the garlic ridiculous.

*Are you planning to stink the spirits to death?*

Priest Siur looked increasingly suspicious that Saint Selena Cathedral had sent him a group of frauds. The townspeople, however, seemed reassured by the display.

Leonard quietly explained to the priest that the locals believed in such things and then suggested having lunch at the church before beginning the investigation.

Klein strongly approved.

Lunch came first, but after that he would have to take turns guarding Sealed Artifact 3-0782. More importantly, he finally had the opportunity to create the Flaring Sun Charms.

*Hopefully everything goes smoothly.*

And if possible, he intended to make the charms during the daytime, when the Sun’s influence would be strongest.

  • Leonard jokes that Klein is overly cautious while continuing to hint at his own mysterious secret.
