Chapter 276 - Rafter Pound

691 words · 4 min read

Sivellaus Street, near the headquarters of Backlund’s police department, was considered a relatively safe place to live.

Baronet Rafter Pound resided there.

The man was in his early forties, with gray temples, puffy eyes, deep wrinkles, and the constant smell of alcohol. He spent his time drinking and indulging in debauchery.

One night, after leaving his warm activity room, Rafter returned to his bedroom.

As he prepared to go to sleep, he suddenly noticed someone sitting beside the curtains.

A man in gray-blue clothing and a cap sat concealed in the shadows.

The stranger raised his head.

His face was painted red, yellow, and white like a clown.

Rafter almost shouted, but a revolver was already aimed at him.

“I advise you not to do anything unwise.

“If you cooperate, I won’t harm you, and I won’t take away your belongings—if you still have any left.”

Rafter obediently closed the door.

“You, what do you want from me?”

“I only want to ask you a few questions.”

The baronet bitterly laughed.

“Here we go again... Am I never going to escape this nightmare?”

Klein immediately became curious.

“Have there been many people coming to you with questions?”

Rafter described his miserable childhood.

After his uncle, the old viscount, died, strange things happened. The old butler mysteriously disappeared, servants and maids changed repeatedly, and unfamiliar people seemed to search the household for something.

Rafter had been very young and terrified.

Later, he claimed, they encouraged him to become a degenerate—drinking, gambling, smoking marijuana, and pursuing women—until he looked like a worthless man.

After he sold the family home, those people disappeared.

Rafter believed they were still secretly watching him and preventing him from contacting the police.

Klein was unsure whether to believe any of this. Rafter’s emotions seemed internally consistent, but he could have constructed the entire story in his mind as a justification for his own degeneration.

Klein asked what those mysterious people wanted.

Rafter said they repeatedly questioned him about the deaths of the old viscount’s two children and the old viscount’s abnormal behavior.

Klein continued questioning him from multiple angles.

Eventually he decided that Rafter really did seem ignorant.

“I’m sorry to bother you, but it’s time for me to leave.”

Klein stood and politely bowed.

At that moment, something changed in Rafter.

For a brief instant, the breakdown and hysteria disappeared from his face. His light-blue eyes became deep and focused as he closely examined Klein.

Then he instantly returned to his earlier performance of grief, madness, and fear.

“Crimson!”

Klein activated a Slumber Charm and threw it at him.

The baronet collapsed onto the bed.

“I’m sorry. The earlier questions were for comparison. Next, I’ll be entering your dream and conducting spirit channeling.”

Klein entered Rafter’s dream with a Dream Charm.

The dream showed faceless servants and maids, an old figure watching him, and Rafter hiding and trembling in terror.

The scenes matched his story.

But Rafter suffered such severe psychological trauma that even minor stimulation made him panic, preventing Klein from learning anything more.

Klein returned from the dream and prepared a mediumship ritual.

After entering Rafter’s spirit channel, Klein asked:

“What did the old viscount say to you before he died?”

“He wants me to maintain the family.”

“What else?”

“He wanted me to remember the glory of the ancestors.”

“And what were those people looking for?”

“I don’t know.”

Klein compared these answers with Rafter’s earlier statements.

The baronet had not lied.

At least, not in the straightforward sense.

Klein ended the ritual, cleaned up the room, and tossed Azik’s copper whistle several times to disrupt possible divination attempts.

“Thank you for your cooperation, Mr. Baronet.”

He climbed through the window and disappeared into the night.

Later, Rafter opened his eyes.

Blood vessels had burst around them.

He stared at the open window.

---

After changing his disguise in East Borough, Klein returned home and immediately entered the gray fog.

He held several strands of Rafter Pound’s hair.

There was one final test.

“Rafter Pound’s future.”

Rather than directly divining the ancient structure itself, Klein decided to use Rafter’s future as an indirect route.

Key Events

  • Klein secretly confronts Rafter Pound in his bedroom.
  • Rafter claims mysterious people searched the Pound household after the old viscount’s death.
  • He says those people psychologically pushed him into a life of drinking, gambling, drugs, and debauchery.
  • Klein tests Rafter through direct questioning, dream probing, and spirit channeling.
  • Rafter appears to genuinely believe and remember the story he tells.
  • Klein discovers that Rafter still contains unexplained mysteries despite apparently passing the tests.
  • Klein secretly collects Rafter’s hair and performs a final divination concerning his future.
  • Klein hopes the future revelation will reveal Rafter’s connection to the Tudor family and clarify the underground ruins.
