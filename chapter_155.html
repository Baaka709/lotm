Chapter 155

1,151 words · 7 min read

After steadying himself, Klein descended to the basement and headed toward Chanis Gate. When he entered the guard room, Royale Reideen had already packed her belongings and stood to greet him before leaving her shift.

Klein casually told her that he had recently made progress in mastering his potion and had shared his experience with Frye and the others.

“You can ask them about it.”

Royale, usually expressionless, showed a rare flicker of surprise.

“Alright.”

Klein secretly found the situation amusing. After what he had told the others, there were already several people in the recreation room probably sitting in a daze, trying to digest his words.

He settled behind the guard desk, prepared a cup of fragrant Fermo coffee, and relaxed while watching the lonely corridor outside. His thoughts wandered to Azik’s upcoming mission and the mysterious Sealed Artifacts hidden beyond Chanis Gate. He wondered where the Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem was stored and what the inside of the sealed vaults looked like. He had never actually entered Chanis Gate and could only imagine the strange and carefully controlled environment required to contain countless bizarre artifacts.

Suddenly, hurried footsteps sounded.

Old Neil appeared in the corridor carrying a black carpet. He entered the guard room, said nothing at first, and simply examined Klein.

“Mr. Neil, did something happen?” Klein asked with a dry chuckle.

Old Neil sighed.

“To think that you found inspiration from the Mystery Pryers’ maxim and Daly...”

Klein replied seriously that he had to praise the Goddess and thank Old Neil for his teachings.

Old Neil sat down, looking unusually depressed. He wished he had discovered these ideas twenty years earlier. He explained that he had once tried to understand the Mystery Pryers’ maxim and gain control over his potion more quickly, but he had failed to grasp the correct direction. When Daly later succeeded, he had assumed she was simply extraordinarily gifted and that ordinary people could not imitate her.

Now, after hearing Klein’s experience, he realized how much he had missed.

Klein understood that Old Neil had possessed a vague understanding of the acting method without recognizing it for what it was. He tried to comfort him by pointing out that the Church did not even possess the Sequence 8 formula corresponding to the Mystery Pryer pathway.

Old Neil speculated that the Holy Cathedral might have it, or perhaps someone in the underground market did. Then he forced a laugh and reminded himself that, at least, he had never lost control and had lived healthily for decades.

After drawing the crimson moon symbol before his chest, Old Neil left.

Klein watched him go and sighed. Once again, he wondered why the Church’s higher-ups deliberately concealed the acting method.

He returned his attention to the Nighthawks’ confidential records. Since learning that the City of Silver still used many ancient names and terms, he had been trying to expand his knowledge in those areas.

Not long afterward, steady footsteps approached. Klein’s enhanced spiritual senses immediately recognized Dunn before he appeared.

Dunn tossed him a letter.

Klein reached for it—but his judgment and reaction speed failed him, and the letter slapped onto the floor.

His hand remained awkwardly extended before he quickly withdrew it and pretended to smooth his hair.

“The gas lamp isn’t bright enough.”

He picked up the letter and recognized the sender as Daxter Guderian, the asylum doctor and his informant from the Psychology Alchemists.

The letter requested an urgent meeting at two that afternoon.

“Has he obtained the Telepathist formula? Or is it about something else?”

Klein informed Dunn, who immediately took the request seriously. He ordered Leonard to temporarily guard Chanis Gate while he accompanied Klein and hid nearby.

“Urgent meetings like this can sometimes be traps. I’ve heard of many similar incidents. Besides, if something important happens, we can act immediately.”

Klein readily agreed.

...

At two in the afternoon, the two met Daxter inside a small shooting range at the Zouteland Street Shooting Club. Klein first asked why Daxter had resorted to contacting mercenaries through the Hound Pub simply to deliver a message.

Daxter nervously revealed the reason.

He believed that Hood Eugen, a patient in the asylum who had once drawn Daxter into the Psychology Alchemists, had recently become abnormal.

“H-he seems to have really gone insane...”

Klein was stunned. Hood Eugen had originally been pretending to be mentally ill so that he could infiltrate the asylum and use the patients to cultivate his mental abilities. Had his madness now become genuine?

Daxter explained that he had previously been able to converse normally with Hood and receive guidance concerning his Beyonder abilities. Recently, however, Hood’s thinking had become bizarre and communication had become nearly impossible. He behaved like the other patients.

Yet there was an important consequence: Daxter had obtained the Telepathist potion formula from him.

“I cannot determine if it’s real or fake,” Daxter admitted. “I’m afraid something uncontrollable might happen.”

Klein quietly relaxed. With the mysterious space above the gray fog, he could eventually determine whether the formula was genuine.

He asked whether Hood had contacted anyone before his condition changed. Daxter could only say that Hood interacted with the asylum’s patients, though Daxter could not guarantee there had been no other contact.

Klein instructed him to investigate immediately, warned that Hood might be testing him, and advised him to report the situation to the Psychology Alchemists to see how the organization’s higher-ups responded. He also promised to arrange secret protection for Daxter.

Daxter regained his Spectator-like composure, adjusted his golden spectacles, and produced a sheet of paper.

“This is the formula for the Telepathist potion, but I cannot guarantee its authenticity.”

Klein examined it.

The main ingredients included the complete pituitary gland of a mature Rainbow Salamander and ten milliliters of spinal fluid from a Farsman Rabbit. The supplementary ingredients were Chestnut Spore, Dragon Tooth Grass Powder, three petals of Pure White Elf Flowers, and one hundred milliliters of pure water.

“Excellent.”

Klein carefully folded the formula and placed it inside his coat.

After a few more questions, he confirmed that the strange voices Daxter had been hearing were gradually subsiding. They then parted.

Klein cautiously returned to the shooting range reserved for the Nighthawks, where Dunn was waiting.

“The informant gave me the Telepathist formula to thank me for helping him control the side effects of his potion,” Klein explained, handing it over. “But he cannot determine whether it’s authentic. He also mentioned something else...”

Dunn reviewed the formula while Klein described Hood Eugen’s sudden deterioration.

After considering everything, Dunn immediately decided to place the asylum under surveillance.

“You haven’t received professional training for this kind of matter, so don’t participate. Return and guard Chanis Gate.”

Then Dunn looked directly at Klein.

“Considering this formula, you don’t need to accumulate any more meritorious achievements. Once you pass the examination, you can directly receive the Clown potion.”

Key Events

  • Old Neil realizes Klein has stumbled onto the acting method and regrets discovering the principle only after reaching an advanced age.
  • Klein receives an urgent letter from Daxter Guderian requesting a meeting.
  • Daxter reports that Hood Eugen appears to have genuinely become mentally unstable.
  • Daxter gives Klein the supposed Telepathist potion formula, though its authenticity remains uncertain.
  • Dunn decides to place the asylum under surveillance and tells Klein to stay out of the investigation.
  • Because of the Telepathist formula, Dunn says Klein will no longer need additional merits and can receive the Clown potion after passing the examination.
