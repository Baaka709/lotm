Chapter 175

Chapter 175 - Deduction

965 words · 6 min read

Klein froze when Daxter revealed that the mysterious “El” was actually Lanevus, the infamous criminal who had escaped with more than ten thousand pounds after cheating numerous victims. The name immediately raised several possibilities in Klein’s mind. Lanevus had visited Hood Eugen three times while the latter was confined to the asylum, meaning he was likely connected to the Beyonder world. Klein recalled that the Marauder pathway’s Sequence 8 was the Swindler, a Beyonder who specialized in deception and fraud. Lanevus being one was therefore highly plausible.

Trying to keep his expression casual, Klein asked when Lanevus had last visited Hood Eugen. Daxter said it had been in early July. That was significant: Lanevus’s crimes had not yet been exposed at the time, and he was still in Tingen.

Klein asked whether Hood Eugen had mentioned Lanevus often. Daxter explained that a Sequence 7 Psychiatrist would carefully consider every word before speaking and would not casually reveal secrets. He had only learned the Telepathist formula because Hood Eugen had gone insane. Klein confirmed that the formula was authentic and offered to help Daxter obtain ingredients when he eventually advanced.

The conversation then returned to Hood Eugen’s strange mention of Lanevus. Klein asked why a Psychiatrist who normally concealed everything would deliberately identify El as Lanevus. Daxter realized that he had overlooked the significance. Perhaps Hood Eugen had wanted him to pass Lanevus’s name to the higher ranks of the Psychology Alchemists as a warning.

The association’s reaction had indeed been strange. After being informed that Hood Eugen had gone insane, they sent a liaison, but after receiving all of Daxter’s information—including Lanevus’s name—they never responded again. It was as though the message had simply vanished into the sea.

Klein agreed that something was likely wrong. If Hood Eugen had somehow anticipated his coming insanity or even death, then Lanevus might have been connected to whatever fate awaited him. Yet why had Hood Eugen not sought help from the organization’s higher-ups?

Klein suggested that some temptation might have driven him to take the risk.

Daxter nodded, observing that greed could make people foolish enough to approach an abyss even when they knew exactly what awaited them. Klein privately likened it to recklessly testing the limits of fate.

He instructed Daxter to continue treating Hood Eugen and, if possible, keep him lucid long enough to extract more information. He also advised Daxter to openly display concern, strengthen his contact with the Psychology Alchemists, and pressure them into dealing with Hood Eugen’s situation. That would be the most natural response and would avoid making their actions suspicious.

Before leaving, Klein asked whether Hood Eugen had displayed any physical changes, such as the emergence of scales.

He knew that “near-insanity,” “insanity,” and “loss of control” represented progressively worse states. Near-insanity could leave someone altered but still rational; insanity destroyed the person’s logic and made communication extremely difficult; complete loss of control transformed both body and mind into a monster. If insanity was not treated, it could eventually lead to true loss of control.

Previously, Dunn had ordered the Nighthawks not to deal with Hood Eugen immediately because doing so might expose their informant within the Psychology Alchemists. Instead, they had kept him under surveillance, ready to intervene if signs of physical transformation appeared.

Daxter reassured Klein that there were no such signs. He watched Hood Eugen very closely and visited the asylum six times a week.

After leaving the shooting range, Klein returned home. He found Melissa sitting motionless on the sofa, staring blankly ahead. After activating Spirit Vision, he confirmed that she was physically healthy.

Melissa explained the reason for her strange mood: Bella had convinced her to try a family breakfast recipe involving throwing leftovers into a pot with water and bread.

Klein immediately recognized it as the kind of improvised dish that seemed to violate every rule of cooking.

Melissa could only insist quietly that food should not be wasted.

Klein suppressed his laughter and asked where Benson was. Benson soon emerged from the bathroom holding a newspaper and enthusiastically offered Klein some breakfast. Klein immediately declined, secretly grateful that he had already eaten before coming home.

Melissa then noticed how late Klein had returned and expressed concern.

Klein cheerfully announced that he had good news. Before he could reveal anything, Melissa correctly guessed that he had passed his examination and received a raise.

Even Benson immediately understood.

Klein was left wondering how he was supposed to surprise them anymore.

He finally announced that his salary had increased severalfold, deliberately concealing the exact amount of his latest raise. He wanted to keep a small private reserve of his own instead of relying entirely on the three hundred pounds hidden in his anonymous account.

Melissa guessed six pounds, while Benson joked that he needed to change jobs. His own studies had become much more serious thanks to the information Klein had given him.

Melissa then happily calculated that Klein’s new salary would allow him to save enough money to meet the financial standards for marriage within a few years, citing standards she had learned from Elizabeth.

Klein was amused by how quickly the conversation had turned toward marriage. He declared that they should celebrate by switching their staple food to white bread and eventually trying various restaurants once his workload became lighter.

Melissa ignored most of this and asked whether Klein wanted to attend Mass with her and Benson at Saint Selena Cathedral.

Klein laughed and declined, saying he needed to catch up on sleep.

He slept until half past twelve, had lunch with his siblings, and then resumed searching houses with red chimneys.

Late that night, Klein sealed his bedroom with spirituality.

He was finally ready to attempt the ritual for summoning Spirit Guide Daly’s messenger.
