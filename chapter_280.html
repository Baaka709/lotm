Chapter 280

Chapter 280 - The First Step

665 words · 4 min read

In a dark corner of East Borough, Xio disguised herself as a dirty, short young man and approached Zeon while he was drunk.

“Xio? Why are you dressed like that?”

She pressed a finger to her lips.

“Keep your voice down. I’ll ask and you answer.”

Xio wanted to know whether anyone who had worked with Williams had died recently.

Zeon thought for a moment.

“Gavin! Gavin was found drowned in the Tussock River this morning.”

Xio immediately asked whether Gavin had accepted Williams’s commission to search for Lanevus.

Of course he had.

Gavin had been spreading Lanevus’s portraits around the East Balam Dock area. The previous evening, he had even visited the Workers Alliance Tavern and showed the portrait to everyone there before going drinking.

Now he was dead.

Xio was deeply disturbed.

The killer was not simply protecting himself. His reaction was completely irrational.

If Lanevus were merely a swindler, he should have escaped as soon as he noticed people searching for him. Killing everyone who investigated him would be far too conspicuous.

Unless Lanevus could not leave.

Xio decided to inform Audrey first, then investigate the Workers Alliance Tavern herself in disguise without directly questioning anyone.

---

Meanwhile, Klein was sitting in the Rice Police Station among thieves and drunkards.

He was annoyed by his bad luck and worried about the charms and herbal powders hidden on his person.

Then he saw Jurgen Cooper.

The young lawyer arrived with a police constable.

“You can leave after signing your name.”

Klein was surprised.

“That’s all?”

Jurgen explained that the police already knew Klein was a well-known detective.

Klein signed the required papers and followed the lawyer outside.

“Thank you. I’ve troubled you again.”

“Two pounds.”

Klein handed over the money.

Jurgen then offered a formal partnership. For ordinary visits to the police station, Klein would only be charged one pound instead of two, although serious cases would cost more.

Jurgen explained that private detectives frequently ended up at police stations and that illegal weapons and trespassing were common occupational hazards.

He praised Klein’s explanation regarding the revolver and gunpowder smell. Because the weapon came from the Quelaag Club, the police had accepted his story and did not have enough evidence to accuse him of illegally carrying it.

Klein agreed to the partnership.

---

Back at home, Klein took a hot bath.

Then he heard another prayer from Miss Justice.

He immediately dried himself, dressed, and ascended above the gray fog.

Audrey explained that the Lanevus investigation had encountered another disaster. She described the Dharavi Street explosion and Gavin’s drowning.

As a Telepathist, she carefully kept her own speculation out of the report.

Klein was troubled by the killer’s behavior.

Lanevus was supposed to be a swindler. If he realized that someone was searching for him, he should simply move elsewhere.

Why murder everyone who followed his trail?

Klein wondered whether the ritual involving the True Creator had affected Lanevus’s mind.

He decided to investigate the Dharavi Street explosion directly.

He performed a dream divination.

The vision showed the damaged apartment where the explosion occurred.

“The clues are at the scene of the crime.”

Klein immediately realized the danger.

The murderer might still be watching the site, waiting for investigators.

But that could also allow Klein to find someone connected to Lanevus.

He considered how to search without being discovered.

His gaze fell upon the All-Black Eye.

Although using it in reality was dangerous because of the lingering corruption from the True Creator, a brief touch might allow him to search for someone hidden nearby.

Klein divined whether the trip would be dangerous.

The answer was positive, but not overwhelmingly so.

Acceptable.

He prepared a disguise, carrying the All-Black Eye inside an iron cigarette case.

Before leaving, he looked into the mirror.

His beard completely concealed his scholarly appearance.

His expression became cold and unfamiliar.

“Captain, this is the first step to avenging both you and me.”

He then saw his reflection smile brightly.

Key Events

  • Xio discovers that Gavin, another Lanevus bounty-hunter contact, has drowned after spreading the search in East Balam.
  • Xio realizes the killer’s response is irrationally extreme and suspects Lanevus may be trapped somewhere.
  • Klein is released from the police station with Jurgen’s assistance.
  • Klein and Jurgen formally establish a lawyer-detective partnership.
  • Audrey informs Klein of the deaths connected to the Lanevus investigation.
  • Klein uses dream divination and discovers that the Dharavi Street crime scene contains an important clue.
  • Klein suspects the murderer may still be monitoring the destroyed apartment.
  • Klein decides to use the dangerous All-Black Eye to locate hidden people at the crime scene.
  • Klein disguises himself and prepares to begin the first step of avenging Captain Dunn and himself.
