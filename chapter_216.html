Chapter 216 - The Same-old Gathering

1,083 words · 7 min read

Klein began his first morning in Backlund beneath cold, damp weather and a thin fog. He ate a miserly breakfast of a liter of cheap tea and two pieces of toast with poor-quality butter before setting out on business.

His first stop was the Backlund Bulletin in Cherwood Borough, where he paid thirty pounds to run a small advertisement for a month:

Sherlock Moriarty. Good at various types of private investigations. Reasonable prices and strictly adheres to the principles of confidentiality. Resides at 15 Minsk Street, Cherwood Borough.

He deliberately avoided the major newspapers. His work was limited to Backlund for now, and he had no desire to make Sherlock Moriarty too famous. The inexpensive, locally popular Backlund Bulletin suited him perfectly.

Afterward, Klein spent another five pounds visiting numerous herb, flower, jewelry, and accessory shops to collect the materials needed for a ritual. Mysticism materials without spirituality could usually be bought from ordinary stores, but they were scattered across the city. Looking at the remaining ninety-two pounds from his original two hundred, he sighed, “Spending money is as easy as drinking water...”

After lunch, he returned to 15 Minsk Street and prepared Holy Night Powder from slumber flower, dragon blood grass, dark red sandalwood, and mint. Without a proper silver ritual dagger, this was the best method he had for creating a wall of spirituality. Klein suspected that he might have to reach Sequence 7 before he could completely overcome that limitation.

His thoughts then turned to the Tarot Club. Miss Justice, Mr. Hanged Man, and The Sun had not requested leave, meaning the gathering would occur as scheduled. He wondered what new surprises Alger would bring and how many more pages of Roselle’s diary he had managed to obtain.

Klein considered his new career as a private detective an ideal combination of income and action. It would expose him to all kinds of people and gradually help him uncover Backlund’s Beyonder circles, which might eventually lead him to the Secret Order. There was also a chance that one such circle could provide him with the Sequence 7 Clown formula and its ingredients.

He had no intention of actively searching for Ince Zangwill for now. He would rather avoid that dangerous enemy, though if he encountered him by chance, Klein would be happy to anonymously inform the Church of the Evernight Goddess.

Money remained a concern. A detective’s income could support a middle-class lifestyle but not the expensive requirements of advancing as a Beyonder. The remaining ninety-two pounds had to be preserved, so Klein began studying newspapers for investment opportunities. He carefully examined notices about inventions and businesses, using his knowledge from Earth and his understanding of the current era, but found nothing trustworthy.

At 2:45, Klein returned to his bedroom, locked the door, drew the curtains, and used the Holy Night Powder to form a wall of spirituality. He performed his usual ritual and stepped four times counterclockwise, arriving above the gray fog. Taking his place on The Fool’s high-backed chair, he extended his spirituality toward the crimson star representing The Sun.

“This is one of the few things in my new life that hasn’t changed...” he sighed.

Far away, outside the City of Silver, nine members of a patrol squad gathered around a bonfire in a partially collapsed tower. Some guarded the perimeter, because darkness in the Forsaken Land of the Gods was never to be taken lightly. Countless Beyonders had learned the lesson through blood: never relax, because monsters might be waiting immediately behind them.

Derrick Berg sat quietly among his companions as they discussed a terrifying humanoid monster whose entire body had been covered in eyes. Their patrol had paid heavily to kill it—five were injured, two seriously.

Then Derrick’s sight suddenly blurred, and a thick gray mist surrounded him. Within it, a vague figure sat on an ancient high-backed chair.

“Prepare for the gathering.”

None of his companions noticed anything. Derrick withdrew his gaze, watched his surroundings for several seconds, then quietly moved to the edge of the fire and pretended to sleep. Since learning that even the six-member council and Elder Lovia could not detect Mr. Fool’s existence, he had gradually become confident that he could attend the Tarot Club as long as he remained careful.

In the Empress Borough of Backlund, Audrey returned to her bedroom under the excuse that she was tired. She was bursting with excitement, though she restrained herself and waited quietly for Mr. Fool’s summons. Her father was busy with Parliament, her Beyonder guard was no longer constantly following her, and two weeks had passed—surely The Hanged Man had obtained the mature Rainbow Salamander pituitary gland she needed to advance to Sequence 8.

The crimson tide soon enveloped her.

At sea, Alger locked himself in the captain’s cabin and carefully memorized a thick stack of secretly copied Roselle diary pages. After leaving Backlund with rewards from his superiors, he had returned to his true domain, the sea. He believed he could soon advance through the Seafarer pathway, though doing so through the “acting method” might reveal that he knew the secret.

Then the summons came. The image of Qilangos’s rotting flesh flashed through his mind, and he lowered his head as the crimson light engulfed him.

Above the gray fog, Klein examined the three summoned members with Spirit Vision. Before Audrey could speak, Alger immediately announced that he had obtained nineteen pages of Roselle’s diary and offered them as compensation for Klein’s “adorer” helping him kill Qilangos.

“It was the principle of equivalent exchange,” Klein replied lightly.

Audrey, increasingly accustomed to thinking of The Fool as a true deity, silently wondered how many powerful High-Sequence Beyonders served him. Alger explained that he could only memorize six pages at a time and would have to provide the rest during later gatherings. Klein accepted.

Derrick watched with curiosity as six pages materialized before The Fool. He remained silent, assuming anything Mr. Fool valued must contain important mysteries.

Klein began reading. The first entry finally continued an earlier mystery:

“16th December. I managed to contact the pathetic fellow who’s trapped in the storm and was lost in the darkness.”

Klein immediately recognized that this concerned Mr. Door. Roselle wrote that the mysterious figure had tried to teach him a complicated ritual so he could return to the real world, offering to fulfill three wishes in exchange. Roselle distrusted the obvious manipulation, but he admitted that Mr. Door’s description of Fourth Epoch history was fascinating.

Key Events

  • Klein established Sherlock Moriarty as a private detective and advertised in the Backlund Bulletin.
  • He spent heavily on ritual supplies and prepared Holy Night Powder.
  • He used the ritual to summon The Sun, while Derrick secretly attended from the City of Silver and Audrey and Alger joined from Backlund and the sea.
  • Alger provided nineteen pages of Roselle’s diary as compensation for Qilangos’s defeat, delivering six pages immediately.
  • The diary revealed that Roselle had once communicated with the mysterious Mr. Door, who offered to return to the real world in exchange for three requests.
