Chapter 265

Chapter 265 - Cards of Blasphemy

504 words · 3 min read

Klein continued reading Roselle’s diary, still shaken by the revelation of the Sauron family’s enormous age.

Roselle wrote that Floren claimed the Sauron lineage had existed for over two thousand years, surviving the upheavals of the Fourth Epoch and remaining powerful into the present. Only a handful of families—the Augustus, Einhorn, and Castiya families—could compare with them. The Antigonus and Zaratul families, once equally powerful, had declined or vanished into obscurity.

Roselle was furious at Floren’s arrogance but admitted that the Saurons must possess extraordinary secrets and terrifying Sealed Artifacts.

Klein immediately considered the implications. Overthrowing such a family could not have been simple. Perhaps the Saurons were already weakening before the Intis revolution, with the revolution merely exposing an internal decline.

He also wondered whether the Sauron family had existed before the Cataclysm. The Sun’s homeland had survived the Dark Ages for more than 2,500 years, and Klein wanted to test Derrick’s reaction to the name.

Another diary entry showed Roselle mocking Floren for claiming he was destined to become a High-Sequence Beyonder because of his resemblance to a great-great-grandfather.

Roselle believed that understanding acting and obtaining ingredients mattered more than natural talent.

Klein, however, now understood why resemblance actually mattered. A High-Sequence Beyonder’s characteristic contained the spirit imprint of its original owner. Someone similar to the original owner could resist that influence more easily, making advancement safer.

Roselle’s later writings revealed his fascination with Intis society and his concerns about the liberal attitudes of future wives.

Then came another major revelation.

Roselle had once attended a gathering of the mysterious ancient organization he had mentioned before. Its members believed that humanity gradually lost individuality and that the world was inevitably heading toward dusk and destruction.

They believed the original Creator remained alive in a dormant state and would awaken when the world ended, reclaiming everything and creating a new world.

The organization possessed the second Blasphemy Slate and access to twenty-two divine pathways. Roselle listed the Bard, Sailor, Reader, and Spectator pathways and wondered about their connections.

Klein was unable to determine whether the organization’s belief in an apocalypse was genuine or simply a form of ideological control.

But Roselle’s final diary entry was the true treasure.

He had completed his first Card of Blasphemy.

He intended to hide the ultimate secrets of the twenty-two divine pathways within twenty-two cards and scatter them throughout the world. Some would be handed to his children, while others would eventually fall into the hands of the fated.

Roselle planned to model the cards after the twenty-two Major Arcana of the tarot, though he intended to adjust some of the names to match the original potion terminology.

He considered the ancient organization his greatest final safeguard.

Then Roselle described the moment he first saw the Blasphemy Slate.

The twenty-two pathways were not merely sequences.

They were paths to divinity.

Each one had a Sequence 0.

Each Sequence 0 possessed its own corresponding potion and ritual.

And each Sequence 0 represented a True God.

“Sequence 0, Sun!”

Key Events

  • Roselle reveals that the Sauron family survived for more than two thousand years and retained enormous power.
  • Klein considers whether the Saurons were already weakening before the Intis revolution.
  • Klein understands the hidden reason why resemblance to a previous Beyonder can increase the chance of successful advancement.
  • Roselle describes the ancient organization’s belief in the original Creator and the inevitable arrival of dusk.
  • The organization possesses the second Blasphemy Slate and knowledge of the twenty-two divine pathways.
  • Roselle reveals that he created the Cards of Blasphemy to preserve the secrets of the twenty-two pathways.
  • He confirms that every pathway possesses a Sequence 0 and that Sequence 0 is the level of a True God.
  • Klein learns the terrifying truth that becoming a god follows a defined pathway with its own potion and ritual.
