Chapter 275 - Pound Family

808 words · 5 min read

After receiving The Sun’s response, Klein was pleased that Derrick seemed satisfied with the weapon.

“Dear Mr. Fool, please tell Mr. World that I’ll try to gather the Mist Treant’s true root and juice as soon as possible.”

Klein passed along the weapon’s characteristics and limitations without overexplaining.

He then returned to the real world.

---

In East Borough, Xio entered a cramped, lively pub on Dharavi Street. The place was packed with drinkers, forcing her to squeeze through the crowd until she reached the bar.

There she found Williams, one of her informants.

“Williams, I have something to talk to you about.”

The drunk young man greeted her warmly and jokingly offered a drink.

The bartender immediately refused on his behalf, remembering how dangerous Xio became when drunk.

Xio tried once again to persuade Williams to give up his drinking and build a stable family life.

Williams bitterly rejected the idea. He believed family warmth depended entirely on money, and he had seen too much misery to believe that marriage would improve his life.

At least drinking and spending money at the pub gave him a few moments of peace.

Xio sighed but stopped arguing.

She handed him Lanevus’s portraits.

“Help me keep an eye out for this man. Find him as soon as possible.”

She gave him five soli for beer and promised another ten pounds if he found Lanevus.

Williams whistled at the amount.

Xio explained that it was the reward for providing useful information.

She had already contacted nearly half the gangs and informants she knew across East Borough, paying several pounds in advance.

As long as one of them succeeded, she would recover everything and earn a large profit.

On her way out, a fight broke out among drunkards.

Xio shouted,

“Stop!”

Her Arbiter power radiated through the pub, instantly silencing the disorder.

She sighed, thinking about how much she wanted to advance to Sheriff.

---

On Thursday morning, Klein traveled to St. George Borough to check on his bicycle investment.

Leppard had already built a crude working prototype based partly on Klein’s suggestions and Roselle’s notes.

Klein tested it and offered several improvements. He promised another investment payment the following week and hoped to attract more investors so the project could enter industrial production.

The only disagreement was the name.

Leppard wanted to call it a “bike” rather than “bicycle.”

Klein didn’t care.

When he returned home around noon, he heard a faint, illusory prayer.

Miss Justice?

Before he could respond, his doorbell rang.

It was Julianne, a maid from the Sammers.

“Mrs. Sammer would like to invite you to a Sunday luncheon. There will be a lot of neighbors attending.”

Klein remembered that he had never received any warning, but realized that the middle class normally delivered invitations through servants rather than extending them personally.

He accepted.

Free lunch was still free lunch.

After closing the door, Klein looked around his increasingly untidy house and decided that hiring a live-in maid would be inconvenient because of his secrets. He might instead ask Mrs. Sammer to arrange for her maid to clean his home twice a week for payment.

He went upstairs and entered the gray fog.

The prayer had indeed come from Audrey.

She had already gathered the information about the disgraced nobles and wanted permission to perform a sacrificial ritual to send it to The World.

Klein agreed.

Audrey carried out the ritual and sent the thick manuscript through the illusory door.

Klein received it and immediately searched for information about the Pound family.

He discovered that the Pound family had received its viscount title after the Battle of the Violated Oath and had remained loyal to the royal family, possessing influence in the military and a significant fief.

Thirty-two years earlier, however, two heirs died one after another from severe illnesses.

The old viscount brought in the child of a distant relative.

Shortly afterward, the old viscount died.

The child was still young, and servants manipulated him into dismissing the old butler and becoming extravagant and dissolute. Within eight years, most of the family fortune disappeared, and the family was reduced to a barony. Their Backlund property was sold.

Eventually even the title was reduced to a baronetcy.

Klein immediately connected the deaths with the underground ruins.

The heirs had likely died inside the innermost chamber, while their Beyonder characteristics and mystical items remained beyond the bloody door.

He suspected the Pound family had discovered the Fourth Epoch structure thirty years earlier and possibly constructed the secret entrance themselves.

But the chamber contained more corpses than the two heirs, indicating that other explorers had died there long before.

Klein decided he needed to speak with Baronet Pound without exposing his identity.

Then he found exactly what he needed.

Baronet Pound currently lived at 29 Sivellaus Street in Empress Borough.
