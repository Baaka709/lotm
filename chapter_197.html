Chapter 197

Chapter 197 - Operation

827 words · 5 min read

Klein’s divination told him that danger remained inside Madam Sharon’s residence—not overwhelmingly dangerous, but serious enough to demand caution. More importantly, Madam Sharon was still there. Klein quickly understood why. Because he had summoned himself to inspect the safe without breaking the lock or triggering any traps, Sharon had no reason to suspect that her secret had actually been uncovered. She would probably assume that someone had merely broken in and failed to find anything useful. For a woman as composed and cunning as her, panicking and fleeing over such a disturbance would make no sense.

Klein shared his deduction with Dunn and Kenley. Dunn agreed that it was the most reasonable conclusion and insisted that they should not rush inside. When Kenley asked why, Dunn reminded them of their previous attempt to capture Instigator Trissy. She had sensed their presence and reacted in time to escape. Klein immediately understood the implication. Bombarding the house would have been the safest method, but Madam Sharon’s residence contained innocent maids. Evacuating them first would alert Sharon, and they also had to assume that, like Trissy, Sharon might possess invisibility.

Dunn therefore proposed a quieter plan. As a Nightmare, he could pull Sharon into a dream from a distance, though doing so within roughly a hundred meters required time and gradual restraint rather than an instant effect. Once Sharon had been subdued enough, Klein and Kenley would move in and capture her. Dunn warned them not to take unnecessary risks and gave them permission to kill her if they could not safely control her. Klein silently admired how clearly his captain thought during critical moments.

Dunn closed his eyes in the street, standing beneath the crimson moon with his hands folded. Inside her luxurious bedroom, Madam Sharon lounged naked in a rocking chair, occasionally admiring herself in a full-length mirror. Beside her sat the strange skeletal statue of the goddess, glowing under warm pink light. Sharon’s movements gradually slowed as Dunn’s power took effect. Her eyelids drooped, and the pull of sleep grew stronger.

After waiting for several minutes, Klein suddenly wondered how Dunn would tell them that the operation could begin without awakening Sharon. The answer came at once. His vision blurred, and he found himself beneath a giant crimson moon with Dunn and Kenley standing nearby. Klein realized that he, too, had been dragged into the dream.

“Captain?”

Dunn nodded. “Madam Sharon has entered a dream. You can take action now.”

He immediately cautioned them to remain careful and not become reckless. The dream shattered, and Klein returned to reality. Kenley emerged from it at the same time, and the two exchanged a glance before beginning the operation.

Kenley was an experienced official Beyonder despite this being one of his more dangerous missions. The night also strengthened a Sleepless, which was another reason Dunn had selected him. Klein, as the Sequence 8, took the lead. The pair crossed the street, climbed the wall, and entered the garden. Klein caught the Spirit Medium Mirror when Kenley tossed it over, but the moment he touched the black-wrapped artifact, his spiritual perception tightened. It felt less like a mirror than a doorway to a dangerous alternate world.

They climbed to the second-floor balcony by the same route Klein had used before. While climbing, Klein unexpectedly flipped himself over with remarkable ease, realizing that the movement felt completely natural. He wondered whether this was another subtle manifestation of the Clown’s abilities.

Kenley uncovered the Spirit Medium Mirror just enough to use it, carefully keeping the reflective surface pointed toward the floor. One of the artifact’s rules was never to use it on oneself or one’s companions. With revolvers ready, the two crept toward Sharon’s bedroom.

Klein pushed the door open while activating his Spirit Vision. Warm gaslight filled the room. Madam Sharon appeared to be reclining in her chair, but she was not asleep. Instead, she wore a faint smile and stared directly at the intruders.

Kenley instinctively raised the Spirit Medium Mirror toward her.

“No!”

Klein remembered that the full-length mirror beside Sharon had disappeared. Too late. The artifact locked onto her for an instant, then her image blurred and transformed into that of the missing mirror. Kenley suddenly saw himself reflected within it—and a sinister, expressionless version of himself appeared inside the Spirit Medium Mirror.

Klein felt his entire body stiffen as though invisible threads had wrapped around him.

At the same time, Madam Sharon appeared beside the full-length mirror, now dressed in a nightgown. She glanced calmly at the two intruders and chuckled.

“If it wasn’t for the fact that the statue happened to be beside me, I should be deep asleep now, waiting for you to wake me up with a kiss.”

Realizing how dire the situation had become, Klein immediately shouted an ancient Hermes word:

“Crimson!”

His left hand was already inside his pocket. With practiced precision, he flicked his fingers and threw out a Slumber Charm.

Key Events

  • Klein deduces that Madam Sharon remains in the building because she has not realized her secrets were exposed.
  • Dunn devises a stealth operation, using his Nightmare ability to gradually pull Sharon into a dream before Klein and Kenley move in.
  • Dunn successfully enters Klein and Kenley’s dreams to signal that Sharon is asleep and warn them to proceed cautiously.
  • Klein and Kenley infiltrate Sharon’s residence through the wall and second-floor balcony while carrying Sealed Artifact 3-0271, the Spirit Medium Mirror.
  • Klein notices the artifact’s ominous nature and displays an unexpected, highly natural Clown-like acrobatic movement.
  • The Spirit Medium Mirror unexpectedly reflects Kenley instead of Sharon, creating a sinister duplicate of him and trapping Klein in place.
  • Madam Sharon reveals that she was never truly asleep and explains that the nearby statue prevented Dunn’s dream attack from fully affecting her.
  • Klein reacts immediately by invoking “Crimson” and throwing a Slumber Charm.
