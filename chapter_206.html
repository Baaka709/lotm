Chapter 206 - Grade 2 Sealed Artifacts

956 words · 6 min read

The nearly four-meter-tall bone messenger disappeared into the quiet basement after taking Klein’s letter. Once the corridor was left with nothing but the steady glow of its gas lamps, Klein put away Azik’s copper whistle and headed toward Chanis Gate.

He had already chosen which Sealed Artifact to take. The Grade 3 artifacts were unlikely to affect the terrifying being inside Megose unless one happened to possess some hidden power like the Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem. Klein had neither the time nor the luxury to gamble on unknown effects, especially since most Sealed Artifacts carried dangerous side effects for their users.

There were no Grade 1 artifacts in the Tingen Nighthawks’ possession, leaving only three Grade 2 Sealed Artifacts: 2-030, 2-078, and 2-105. Dunn had been forced to reveal their existence because of the emergency.

The first, 2-030, was called the “Inexhaustible Poison.” It had been created from the blood of a Beyonder who had gone mad and killed himself. His blood had filled a silver cup with a crystal-clear poison so alluring that even a Sequence 5 Beyonder had drunk it and died instantly. The poison then flowed back together after the man’s death, returning to its original quantity. Holy Cathedral researchers suspected it could even kill a High-Sequence Beyonder. Yet poisoning someone with it was extremely impractical, because the target had to be captured and forced to drink it while the user resisted the artifact’s own overwhelming temptation.

Klein discarded it immediately.

The second, 2-078, was the “Door of Death.” It looked like an ordinary wooden door, but anything alive that passed through it died instantly. It possessed living characteristics, constantly tried to escape, and could disguise itself as another door. A mistake could cause the user to lose control of it, forcing them to avoid every nearby doorway until help arrived or they escaped through a wall. Klein briefly considered it, but a legendary creature should possess an extremely sharp intuition and might easily recognize the true Door of Death.

That left 2-105, the “Blood Vessel Thief.”

The artifact resembled a thick, hardened blood vessel. Anyone who touched it would gradually have their life stolen. Dunn described how a Sequence 5 heretic had once held it for two hours and transformed from a muscular man in his thirties into a bent, white-haired old man with loose skin and missing teeth.

Its greater power, however, was what drew Klein’s attention. The Blood Vessel Thief could steal an ability from a target within a certain range. Even a High-Sequence Beyonder could have an ability stolen, though the chance was lower. For ten minutes, the victim would lose that ability while the artifact’s user could employ it proficiently. Afterward, the stolen ability would disappear and the victim would need several days to recover.

It was risky, but Klein had already made his decision. Any increase in their tiny chance of success was worth taking.

He stopped outside the duty room. He was not going to use 2-105 himself. He intended to give it to Leonard, since Klein already had the Flaring Sun Charm and Azik’s copper whistle—whose usefulness remained uncertain.

“My dear Poet, it’s time for you to show your true secret...” Klein murmured.

Seeka Tron was standing nearby and questioned why Klein was taking the artifact instead of her. She was stronger and more experienced, but Klein calmly pointed out that he was already a Sequence 8 and that remaining upstairs to buy time might actually be safer than staying near the dangerous Sealed Artifacts behind Chanis Gate.

Seeka could only pray for them.

Because Dunn had not had time to prepare the necessary paperwork, Klein could not enter Chanis Gate himself. He watched as Seeka opened a narrow gap and disappeared inside. A few minutes later, she returned carrying the Blood Vessel Thief, a thick, pale vessel stained with blood. The instant Klein took it, he felt a strange current course through his body.

Meanwhile, in the reception hall, Leonard continued talking casually with Megose about Lanevus’s rented house. Megose answered naturally, then suddenly pulled a handful of her own blonde hair from her head and tossed it into the trash.

Leonard froze.

He swallowed hard as cold sweat once again covered his palms.

Klein soon reached the second floor with the Blood Vessel Thief in hand. Outside the Nighthawks’ recreation room stood Dunn Smith, still holding Saint Selena’s ashes. Looking at the Captain brought back memories of their first meeting and of Dunn standing over Kenley’s corpse, blood covering his mouth.

Dunn quietly introduced himself again.

“Reintroducing myself, Nighthawk, Dunn Smith.”

Klein approached and informed him that he had chosen 2-105 and intended to let Leonard use it. Dunn accepted the decision without questioning it.

The Holy Cathedral had already replied. They would immediately gather a team of powerful Beyonders and ordered the Tingen Nighthawks to stall for as much time as possible. There was still no response concerning the mysterious chain of “coincidences”; perhaps the Cathedral had no conclusions, or perhaps the telegram system simply could not convey enough information.

Klein looked toward Leonard and Megose. They appeared to be chatting normally, with nothing suspicious happening yet. He and Dunn remained separated by the corridor, watching and waiting.

Then Dunn suddenly gave a self-deprecating laugh.

“I forgot something.”

Klein looked at him in confusion. Dunn explained that Daly had told him to explain himself to Klein.

Klein froze for a moment before suddenly understanding what that meant. Daly had not responded personally because she considered the matter unimportant; instead, she had simply passed it to Dunn to explain.

That meant there was nothing seriously wrong with the Captain.

At the most dangerous moment of all, Klein suddenly felt a burst of joy.
