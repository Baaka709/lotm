Chapter 219

Chapter 219 - Explanation

667 words · 4 min read

Alger’s caution toward The Fool had made him reluctant to trade for something described as “simple general knowledge.” But once he discovered that he could not properly understand the Law of Similar Sequence Beyonder Characteristics Conservation, curiosity overwhelmed his hesitation.

“Mr. Fool, what kind of payment do you want?”

Audrey immediately indicated that she wanted the answer too, while Derrick remained silent but clearly attentive.

Klein’s smile deepened.

“Information regarding the Secret Order.”

The words immediately caught Alger’s attention. Audrey frowned, having only heard of the organization through Alger, while Derrick did not recognize the name at all.

Klein was not surprised by Derrick’s ignorance. His current historical theory suggested that the City of Silver belonged to a far older era, while the Zaratul family and the Secret Order had emerged during the Fourth Epoch. If Derrick knew about them, Klein’s understanding of the Forsaken Land of the Gods would need to be reconsidered.

After a long pause, Alger accepted the deal. He offered to collect information about the Secret Order and asked whether he could receive the answer in advance.

Klein agreed. If Alger’s information eventually proved more valuable than the explanation, Klein would provide additional compensation.

Audrey immediately joined the transaction as well. Klein welcomed this because she was a native of Backlund with connections to many Beyonder circles and far better access to local information than Alger, who spent most of his time at sea. Klein’s earlier divination had already suggested that his clues regarding the Secret Order would emerge in Backlund.

Derrick then chose to use the compensation Klein had promised from a previous trade.

Klein began explaining the second law. Certain High-Sequence Beyonders could switch between pathways when the corresponding Sequences were similar.

“For example, the Death pathway’s Sequence 5, Gatekeeper, can not only be advanced normally, but one can also choose the Giant pathway, which is the Gatekeeper pathway’s Sequence 4, Demon Hunter.”

Such a transition would not cause the loss of control or the accumulation of madness associated with drinking an incorrect potion. A truly incompatible pathway, however, could produce a semi-deranged condition at best.

Audrey was delighted. The twenty-two divine pathways were not absolutely isolated, and some Sequences could eventually be exchanged. The revelation gave her the exciting possibility of changing direction in the future.

Alger was equally impressed. Many strange experiences from his past suddenly seemed to make sense.

Derrick reacted differently. He had no intention of abandoning the Sun pathway. Yet the revelation reminded him of an incident in the City of Silver involving a former Chief who had once seemed likely to advance beyond Demon Hunter. The man had built a mausoleum, entered it, and gradually disappeared until its gate could no longer be opened. Everyone had assumed he had gone mad during advancement.

Now Derrick wondered whether the Chief had attempted to advance through a similar pathway, perhaps one related to Death. If so, what had happened inside that dark mausoleum?

Audrey then asked which other pathways were compatible with the Spectator pathway.

Sequence 4 Demon Hunter sounds perfect for Justice, she thought excitedly.

Before Klein could answer, Audrey hastily offered to pay in gold pounds, remembering that Mr. Fool’s powerful adorer must require resources. She immediately realized how absurd the offer sounded after seeing what that adorer had done to Qilangos, but it was too late to withdraw.

Klein instead declared that the question would be part of their future transaction after enough Secret Order information had been gathered. Audrey happily agreed.

Alger, who had been preparing to ask a similar question, wisely kept silent.

The conversation then turned to Audrey’s pending advancement. She asked whether Alger had obtained the mature Rainbow Salamander pituitary gland she needed.

He answered that he had obtained it, but neither of them could immediately think of a safe way to transfer it without exposing Audrey’s identity as a Beyonder.

Klein’s heart moved.

“Miss, Sir, are you willing to cooperate with me in making an attempt regarding this matter?”

Key Events

  • Alger and Audrey agreed to collect information on the Secret Order in exchange for Klein’s explanation of Similar Sequence Conservation.
  • Klein revealed that certain High-Sequence Beyonders can switch between compatible pathways without immediately losing control.
  • The revelation gave Audrey greater freedom in planning her future advancement and made Alger reinterpret past mysteries.
  • Derrick connected the law to the disappearance of a former Chief of the City of Silver.
  • Alger confirmed that he had obtained the mature Rainbow Salamander pituitary gland Audrey needed.
  • Klein proposed an experimental method for safely transferring the Beyonder material to Audrey.
