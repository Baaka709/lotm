Chapter 271

Chapter 271 - Golden Rose

519 words · 3 min read

At 19 Hope Street in Cherwood Borough, Mike Joseph pointed out a gray-blue three-story building.

“That’s the Golden Rose, the best legal brothel in this area.”

The entrance was marked only by an inset golden rose.

Klein and Mike entered.

A mixture of perfume and music filled the hall. Bouncers stood throughout the building, while sofas, chairs, a piano, and a dance floor created an atmosphere closer to an elegant ballroom than a brothel.

Because it was only half past three, the place was relatively quiet.

Mike explained that the establishment became much livelier at night, when men could dance with the women, negotiate prices, and then rent rooms upstairs.

He approached a young girl, explaining that Siber had been sixteen and someone close to her age might know more.

Klein remained nearby as his bodyguard.

After several minutes, Mike returned.

“The girl knew Siber,” he whispered, “but Ma’am Lopez forbids them from speaking with anyone about her.”

Mike decided to talk to the owner.

A heavily made-up woman in a beautiful dress introduced herself as Lopez.

Mike initially behaved playfully, then suddenly became serious.

“I’m a reporter. I wish to understand more about Siber’s matter.”

Lopez became irritated. She claimed Siber had been a homeless orphan adopted by her, that a guest had invited Siber to stay overnight, and that Siber died while returning the following morning.

Then Lopez ordered them to leave.

Klein shielded Mike and escorted him away.

“She’s lying.”

He explained that her eyes repeatedly shifted, she refused direct eye contact while monitoring Mike’s reaction, and her defensive posture revealed nervousness.

Mike was impressed by Klein’s deductions.

In reality, Klein’s Spirit Vision had shown Lopez’s emotional colors, allowing him to construct the explanation.

Then Mike noticed Lopez heading toward a side door guarded by two men.

“Can you take care of those two bouncers quickly?”

Klein initially reminded him that his job was only protection.

Mike increased the payment to five pounds for the fight and ten if another fight occurred during their escape.

“Deal!”

They approached the door.

When one bouncer tried to stop them, Klein immediately struck him in the stomach. He then knocked down the second guard. The first guard reacted too late and was also quietly subdued.

Klein and Mike entered the private corridor.

Then they heard Lopez speaking.

“Tell Capim not to send anyone over in the next few days!”

Capim?

Before they could learn more, Lopez approached the corridor.

“Let’s go!”

Klein pulled Mike away, shut the side door, and damaged the lock before they returned through the hall.

Outside, Mike admitted that the investigation had gone surprisingly smoothly.

He paid Klein five pounds and said he needed to investigate who Capim was.

Klein warned that Lopez might try to use the police or newspaper against him.

Mike merely shrugged.

“That’s a fake ID card.”

After Mike left, Klein noticed a familiar rental carriage stop nearby.

The middle-aged man who stepped out was the same gentleman Klein had seen inside Golden Rose.

“Hello, I’m Detective Isengard Stanton. I’m assisting the police in this case. Can we have a chat?”

Key Events

  • Klein and Mike enter Golden Rose to investigate Siber’s death.
  • Mike discovers that Siber’s former associates have been forbidden to discuss her.
  • Ma’am Lopez lies about Siber and becomes visibly defensive.
  • Klein identifies the deception through Spirit Vision and observation.
  • Mike persuades Klein to help him follow Lopez into a restricted corridor.
  • Klein silently defeats two bouncers.
  • They overhear Lopez mentioning a powerful figure named Capim.
  • After leaving Golden Rose, Klein is approached by Detective Isengard Stanton.
