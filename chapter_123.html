Chapter 123

921 words · 6 min read

Vines covered the ruined garden outside the glass windows, while the river flowed quietly beneath the stars and warm lights glowed from nearby buildings. In that stillness, Trissy prepared to leave.

She took a long black hooded robe from the clothes rack, fastened it, and pulled the hood over her head, transforming her appearance into that of an Assassin. With a swipe of her hand, she blurred her features, then scattered shimmering powder over herself while chanting a concealment spell. Her body gradually vanished until even her outline disappeared.

She slipped into the adjoining room, opened an unbarred window, and landed silently on the grass below. Across the field, Corpse Collector Frye was climbing over the steel fence, revolver in hand as he searched for spirits only he could directly perceive.

Trissy moved behind him. A dagger covered in what looked like black paint appeared in her hand.

She thrust it into Frye’s lower back—

—and the entire scene shattered.

Trissy was still standing on the windowsill. Frye was still outside the fence, but now Leonard Mitchell was aiming at her while Dunn Smith stood nearby, hunched over with his eyes closed and one hand pressing against his glabella. Invisible ripples spread from him.

Trissy’s pupils contracted.

It had all been a dream. She had fallen asleep without realizing it.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

Leonard and Frye fired, their bullets striking the invisible target. Trissy’s form appeared for an instant, cracked apart, and shattered into fragments of a rough silver mirror.

Inside the building, the real Trissy had already escaped by means of a substitution spell. She raced down the corridor and stairs, but her concealment had been lost. On the first floor, a freezing wind swept through the building. Transparent, formless spirits wandered through the halls, and every time Trissy passed one, the cold sank into her body.

She finally reached the sacrificial altar.

At its center stood a bone-carved figurine of a beautiful woman. Hair descended from its head all the way to its heels, every strand resembling a poisonous snake or tentacle, with a single eye at each tip. Puppets bearing names and information lay scattered around it, while three yellow-green candles flickered in the unnatural wind.

Trissy bowed and rapidly recited an incantation. She pushed aside the puppets, extinguished the candles, and seized the figurine.

The winds exploded.

Windows shook violently before shattering, sending glass across the room. Frye reached the other side of the building but hesitated to enter the altar recklessly as the cold sapped his strength. Something invisible suddenly seized his heels.

Frye immediately swung his revolver downward and fired.

A silver demon-hunting bullet tore through the unseen spirit, dispersing it and restoring his mobility.

Dunn, meanwhile, tried to approach the second floor from another direction, only to be struck by the same freezing winds. Near a shattered window, a curtain suddenly surged forward and wrapped around his head like the jaws of a beast. Dunn nearly suffocated, but he planted both feet, straightened his body, and tore himself free with brute strength. He shot the other half of the curtain, stopping its attack as dark red liquid seeped from the wounded cloth.

Outside, Leonard was struggling against the same deathly chill. His teeth chattered so badly that he could barely recite his poems. Weeds suddenly twisted around his ankles, and a black shadow hurled itself at him.

Unable to fire in time, Leonard raised an arm to protect himself.

A bright red flower smashed into his forearm, its thorns piercing his skin.

Leonard grimaced and flung it away, then shot into the spreading vines. Dark red liquid oozed from them as he rushed toward the shattered window leading into the altar room. The vines abruptly withdrew, as though fleeing from something they could not see.

Amid the chaos, Trissy destroyed the altar and disrupted the suspension-style ritual. She concealed herself once more, slipping past the Nighthawks’ Spirit Vision and escaping their encirclement. Once she reached a position behind them, she stretched out her hand.

A cold wind carried Leonard’s blood-stained flower directly into her palm.

Without hesitation, she crossed the steel fence and fled toward the Tussock River.

Leonard suddenly stopped. Something seemed wrong.

He pulled back his sleeve and examined the wound. It had already stopped bleeding, leaving only swelling. His expression darkened. He gritted his teeth, pulled out the fingernail of his left index finger, and used it to reopen the wound. Once the nail was soaked in his blood, he wrapped it in several strands of his own hair.

By the river, Trissy stared at the flower. She chanted, and black illusory flames formed in her palm, consuming the flower until nothing remained.

Then she leapt into the river and disappeared beneath the water.

At nearly the same time, Leonard threw the blood-covered fingernail and hair into a corner. They burned with a foul smell before crumbling into dust.

Back inside, Leonard returned through the window and found Dunn and Frye destroying the altar. He told them, “The target has escaped. But it’s alright, our primary objective was to stop the ritual.”

Dunn looked at the puppets scattered across the table and sighed.

“She was very cautious and very powerful. She sensed us approaching her ahead of time, otherwise... she should be, at the very least, a Sequence 7 Beyonder.”

He then instructed them to signal Klein and have him come over.

From their brief encounter inside the dream, Dunn had also established one important fact: their enemy was a woman.

  • Trissy disguises herself, uses concealment magic, and attempts to ambush Frye.
  • The apparent attack is revealed to be a dream created by Dunn’s powers, allowing the Nighthawks to catch her off guard.

  • The altar’s destruction unleashes spirits, freezing winds, and animated objects that hinder Frye, Dunn, and Leonard.
  • Trissy restores her concealment, slips through the Nighthawks’ defenses, and steals the blood-stained flower from Leonard before escaping.
  • Leonard destroys the trace of his blood connection, preventing Trissy from exploiting the flower further.
  • Trissy escapes by diving into the Tussock River.
  • Dunn concludes that the mysterious female enemy is at least a Sequence 7 Beyonder and orders Klein to be summoned.
