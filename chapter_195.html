Chapter 195 - “Lockpicking Expert” Klein

954 words · 6 min read

Klein carefully surveyed Madam Sharon’s bedroom with Spirit Vision and found it extravagantly furnished. The room contained a walk-in cloakroom, thick carpets, a goose-feather blanket, cosmetics, jewelry, fine clothing, and elaborate gold-trimmed decorations.

An unfinished oil painting immediately caught his attention. It portrayed Sharon herself in the nude, combining an innocent, almost deerlike expression with unmistakably mature features. Klein barely looked below her neck. What interested him more were the painting supplies and a full-length silver mirror positioned beside the painting. Their arrangement suggested that Sharon might have painted herself while observing her reflection.

He privately wondered whether the famous socialite was narcissistic before returning his attention to the investigation.

Following the surveillance techniques Leonard and Frye had taught him, Klein wore gloves and carefully memorized the position of every item he disturbed so that he could restore everything afterward. His Seer abilities made remembering the room’s arrangement easy, and he had even performed a preliminary divination before leaving home, confirming that the night’s search should proceed relatively smoothly.

He searched Sharon’s bedroom for about twenty minutes but found nothing suspicious and detected no obvious spiritual traces.

The final object he examined was a massive steel safe. Roughly a meter tall and extremely sturdy, it seemed designed to withstand almost anything. Klein tried opening it normally but failed.

He therefore left it for last and performed a pendulum divination to determine whether the bedroom contained a secret room or hidden compartment.

The pendulum gave a negative response.

Klein expanded the search to the study, living room, greenhouse, and other parts of the house, but found nothing useful. Since he did not know exactly what he was searching for, he did not attempt dowsing.

Eventually, he returned to Sharon’s bedroom and sealed the room with his ritual dagger and spirituality.

He was going to summon himself.

Rather than learning traditional lockpicking, Klein would simply use his Spirit Body to bypass the safe.

He performed his familiar self-summoning ritual, passed through the gray fog, and returned to the mysterious space above the gray fog.

However, before opening the Door of Summoning, he realized that this was an ideal place to perform additional divinations. His connection to the mysterious space removed interference and greatly enhanced his abilities. More importantly, he could treat the location as something permanently connected to Sharon because he was effectively using her own bedroom as the foundation of the ritual.

Klein struggled to formulate a precise question. Asking whether Sharon had “something wrong” with her was too broad, while asking whether she was involved in a crime was also ambiguous, since a socially powerful figure could easily be involved in questionable matters without technically breaking any law.

He therefore decided to verify the earlier divination concerning Maynard’s death.

He wrote:

“John Maynard’s death was due to supernatural influences.”

His previous divination had produced a negative result.

This time, however, the pendulum rotated clockwise.

Positive.

Klein’s expression changed immediately.

His previous divination had been disrupted.

Maynard’s death really had involved supernatural influences.

Klein quickly considered the implications. Perhaps Madam Sharon herself was a powerful Beyonder, or perhaps someone had supported her and helped plan Maynard’s death. Maybe the murder had been politically motivated, intended to eliminate a powerful rival for the mayoral election or weaken the New Party.

Klein then performed another divination:

“Madam Sharon is a Beyonder.”

Again, the pendulum rotated clockwise.

She was a Beyonder.

Klein’s nerves tightened.

He immediately opened the Door of Summoning and entered his Spirit Body state.

Inside Sharon’s bedroom, Klein floated directly to the steel safe and extended his transparent hand through the metal.

Because Sharon was a Beyonder, he remained alert for traps. In his spiritual state, however, his connection to the mysterious space provided an enhanced instinctive warning system whenever he approached danger.

Nothing happened.

Klein passed completely through the safe.

Inside, he found three sections. One contained gold bars, enormous stacks of cash, and valuable jewelry. Another contained sealed documents that he could not open or read in his current state.

The lowest section held only a black-and-white photograph.

The picture showed a handsome young man.

Klein immediately began imagining an elaborate tragic romance: perhaps this was Sharon’s former lover, the man she had been separated from and could never forget, forcing her into a loveless marriage with the old Baron Khoy and eventually leading to her notorious behavior.

But the more Klein examined the picture, the stranger it seemed.

The young man looked remarkably similar to Sharon.

He suddenly wondered whether the figure might actually be her brother—or whether Sharon belonged to the Demoness pathway, like Trissy.

The thought immediately reminded Klein of Trissy’s prolonged stay in Tingen. Perhaps her associate or partner had been in the city.

The realization also destroyed Klein’s ability to look at Sharon’s earlier portrait and mediumship vision with the same mindset.

He recovered and continued searching the safe, carefully examining its edges and corners for hidden compartments.

Eventually, he discovered something unusual.

There was an empty space between the side of the safe and the wall.

A hidden compartment.

After confirming that nothing immediately threatened him, Klein entered it.

Inside were ointments, perfumes, powdered herbs, and other mystical materials.

At the center stood a palm-sized skeletal statue.

It depicted what appeared to be a beautiful girl with extremely long hair extending all the way to her heels. Each strand was thick and unnaturally clear, resembling a venomous snake.

At the tip of every strand was an eye.

Some eyes were open.

Others were closed.

Klein immediately sensed the evil aura coming from the object.

He hurriedly retreated from the hidden compartment.

And finally understood why his earlier divination for a secret room had returned a negative result.
