Chapter 138

1,135 words · 7 min read

“The omnipotent and omniscient God... the Lord that created everything...”

Klein leaned back, turning Derrick’s words over in his mind. He was familiar with the idea of a Creator from the Book of Storms, the Revelation of Evernight, and the beliefs of secret organizations such as the Aurora Order. Yet no major god in the Northern Continent openly claimed to be omnipotent and omniscient.

If the City of Silver truly lay within the Forsaken Land of the Gods, then that sealed land belonged to the same world. The “omniscient and omnipotent God” might therefore have been an ancient title for the Creator used by people in distant eras.

Across the table, Derrick lowered his head as painful memories returned.

“When the sun disappeared, the clouds were torn apart, and lightning and thunder became our rulers, terrifying monsters emerged from the darkness. One city after another in the Kingdom of Silver was destroyed. Humanity entered the Dark Ages.

“The surviving experts of the City of Silver joined forces and relied on two magical items to repel the Things of the Dark. They gradually cleared the monsters within a day’s journey of the city and established a city-state to protect the final light of human civilization.”

Klein privately thought that it sounded like a history textbook.

In the first decades, the city could barely grow plants and suffered from severe food shortages. People survived by hunting dark creatures and mutated animals until they discovered Black-Faced Grass, which could survive the cursed environment and became their most dependable food.

Derrick believed it had been the last blessing left by their magnificent God.

The Dark Ages lasted 2,582 years.

Because their calendar was based on lightning, the people of the City of Silver called periods of frequent lightning “day” and periods when lightning weakened “night.” Exact dates were consequently difficult to establish.

Klein was relieved that he had vaguely referred to “the next two days” earlier instead of mentioning an ordinary tomorrow.

As the population recovered, the number of Beyonders increased. The six-member council began sending elite teams into the surrounding darkness. Eventually, they explored the entire territory around the city and moved deeper into increasingly terrifying regions. They found the ruins of other strange cities, which they believed had been sanctuaries established by surviving humans. None had survived the Things of the Dark.

Then Derrick explained the origin of the City of Silver’s Beyonder pathway.

“The Kingdom of Silver was once ruled by the Giant King. So the Beyonder chain we control is the Giant pathway, also known as the Divine Blood Warrior pathway.”

They had also obtained formulas for other pathways while killing monsters and exploring ruined cities, but those pathways were incomplete.

Klein’s interest immediately sharpened.

The Giant King... the City of Silver and the Northern Continent really do share ancient history.

Derrick listed the Giant pathway’s known Sequences:

“Sequence 9, Beyonder Warrior. Sequence 8, Gladiator. Sequence 7, Weapons Master. Sequence 6, Dawn Paladin. Sequence 5, Guardian. Sequence 4, Demon Hunter. Only the elders of the six-member council know the names of the higher Sequences.”

Sequence 4—Demon Hunter.

Klein was delighted to learn the name of a High-Sequence potion for the first time, though he suspected that it was an ancient version of a name that had changed over time.

More importantly, the pathway sounded extremely familiar.

The Church of the God of Combat controlled Sequence 9 Warrior, Sequence 8 Pugilist, and Sequence 7 Weapons Master. The correspondence was too close to be coincidence.

The complete pathway controlled by the God of Combat was probably the same Giant pathway inherited from the ancient Giant King.

Klein then learned about the City of Silver’s government. Six elders ruled the city, with one serving as Chief while the other five held equal status.

The current council consisted of three Demon Hunters, two Guardians, and a Shepherd.

Klein’s heart tightened.

Three High-Sequence Beyonders.

Even one of them would be an overwhelming opponent for the current Tarot Club. The City of Silver’s upper ranks therefore represented a terrifying level of power.

But Derrick was only an ordinary young man and not even a Sequence 9 Beyonder. The council was unlikely to notice him anytime soon.

Klein focused on the unusual final member.

“Shepherd?”

Derrick explained that Shepherd came from a pathway discovered in one of the ruined cities. The pathway only reached Sequence 5, but Elder Lovia was extremely powerful and frightening. She had supposedly defeated an evil spirit comparable to a High-Sequence Beyonder without being injured, which was why she had been granted a seat on the council despite following an incomplete pathway.

Klein deliberately asked about the Sequences before Shepherd, suspecting that the names might connect to something he already knew.

Derrick answered:

“Sequence 9, Secrets Suppliant. Sequence 8, Whispered. Sequence 7, Shadow Ascetic. Sequence 6, Rose Priest. Sequence 5, Shepherd.”

Klein immediately recognized it.

Secrets Suppliant was the Sequence 9 associated with the Aurora Order’s pathway.

He was quietly delighted. The names Whispered and Listener were also closely related.

The City of Silver, apparently isolated for thousands of years, possessed knowledge that even the Tingen Nighthawks did not have.

Yet Derrick’s history ultimately returned to the curse that defined his people.

“Whether we are ordinary people or Beyonders, when citizens of the City of Silver die, we become evil spirits. A Beyonder’s evil spirit is even stranger, more terrifying, and harder to deal with. There have been many times when this curse nearly destroyed the city.

“The only way to prevent an evil spirit from appearing is for someone of the same bloodline to kill the dying person.”

Klein could do little except offer encouragement.

“That is a cruel fate. I hope you become strong enough to discover a way for the people of the City of Silver to escape it.”

Derrick spoke softly, his voice full of pain.

“That is why I want to become the Sun. When the Sun once shone over the land, we never encountered this curse.”

Klein gave a small nod.

“You will have the chance. Remember, I can pull you here at any time during the next two days. Try not to stay around other people.”

Derrick solemnly agreed.

Before ending their conversation, Klein decided to establish a proper code name for the young man. A deck of tarot cards appeared on the ancient table.

“You can choose any card as your code name, except The Fool, Justice, and The Hanged Man.”

Derrick stepped forward, examined the cards, and chose without hesitation.

“The Sun.”

Klein looked at him and said with solemn authority, “Remember your choice. It will follow you for the rest of your life.”

Then he severed the connection.

The crimson glow gradually faded, and Derrick’s figure became increasingly transparent before disappearing from the palace above the gray fog.
