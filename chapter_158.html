Chapter 158

930 words · 6 min read

Klein listened to Audrey’s cheerful greeting and could not help envying her apparently endless good mood. Then he remembered how effortlessly she had once produced a thousand pounds and decided that maintaining such happiness probably required a fortune.

Derrick immediately answered Klein’s question.

“I have obtained the Telepathist formula.”

During the previous week, Derrick had sold most of the valuable possessions inherited from his parents through the City of Silver’s black market, keeping only the property, furniture, and a few sentimental belongings. The proceeds had been enough to purchase the Telepathist formula and the ingredients for the Bard potion, though his daily meals had become severely restricted.

He remained optimistic. Once he passed the combat examination, he could join expeditions that cleared the Things of the Dark surrounding the City of Silver and earn a decent income. Eventually, he hoped to join the elite squad, explore deeper into the darkness, and find a way to break the city’s curse.

Klein instructed him to recall the formula and use the pen before him to express the contents. Because Derrick came from the City of Silver, the writing instrument that appeared was a quill rather than a fountain pen.

Derrick followed the instructions, and within moments the Telepathist potion formula appeared on the goatskin parchment.

After checking it, he silently pushed it toward Audrey.

Audrey eagerly examined the formula, but the ingredients were unfamiliar. She had been studying Beyonder materials with Fors and Xio, yet several of these names meant nothing to her.

Then she heard a soft tapping from the seat of honor.

Klein was gently tapping the bronze table and indicating the parchment.

Audrey looked again.

Additional notes had appeared beside the unfamiliar names, identifying the Phantom Netherdrake as another name for the Rainbow Salamander and the Half Specter Rabbit as another name for the Farsman Rabbit.

She immediately understood.

“I know all of these!”

Her delight was genuine.

“Thank you, Mr. Fool. You’re really very knowledgeable.”

Alger secretly found the description almost absurd. Calling such an existence merely “knowledgeable” seemed far too modest.

Klein accepted the praise without embarrassment. He had deliberately prepared for exactly this situation ever since recruiting Derrick. Because the City of Silver preserved many ancient names, he had spent considerable effort studying terminology and comparing the available information. Even without Daxter’s formula, he had already prepared enough to annotate it correctly.

*Preparedness averts peril,* he thought smugly.

Audrey then copied another page of Emperor Roselle’s diary and offered it to The Fool.

“You deserve this. In addition, I’ll give your adorer another 300 pounds. Is that enough?”

She felt somewhat guilty because the three diary pages had cost her only twenty pounds, while the Sheriff formula she needed cost 450. She viewed the diary as an astonishing bargain and believed each page was worth at least fifty pounds.

Klein calmly accepted.

“A reasonable deal. This is my adorer’s information.”

Rather than verbally discussing bank accounts, he caused the information to appear on the parchment before Audrey. He had already visited the Tingen branch of Backlund Bank in disguise and opened an anonymous account. Only an account number and password were required to withdraw funds from any branch.

For additional security, he had chosen not to add his signature and thumbprint. Instead, the password was the full ancient Hermes incantation invoking The Fool.

Anyone attempting to copy it would effectively recite his name, immediately alerting Klein to the theft attempt.

He was pleased with the arrangement. The only drawback was that it revealed a little more about The Fool’s existence.

Audrey carefully memorized the account details, wondering about the mysterious adorer’s gender, Sequence, and strength. She guessed that the person was extremely powerful, perhaps no weaker than Rear Admiral Hurricane Qilangos.

Then Klein casually explained that she did not need to memorize the information perfectly.

“When you get home, recite my name and you’ll be able to write the information directly.”

Audrey was astonished. After realizing that this was consistent with The Fool’s supernatural abilities, she accepted it, though she was puzzled about why she had needed to memorize the potion formula herself.

Klein then turned toward Derrick.

“What compensation would you like?”

Derrick thought carefully.

“I don’t have anything I urgently need. I should digest the Bard potion soon. I’ll wait until then to request my compensation. Perhaps I can use it for the Sequence 8 formula or its ingredients.”

Klein immediately recognized the significance of the word “digest.” The Sequence 8 of Derrick’s pathway was Light Suppliant, a formula Klein already possessed, though he had no practical means of delivering the necessary ingredients.

More importantly, this confirmed that the City of Silver knew the acting method.

Audrey noticed the same thing.

“Mr. Sun, are you aware of the acting method?”

Derrick looked confused by the question.

“There’s nothing strange about it. The general education classes in the City of Silver teach the acting method.”

Audrey and Alger exchanged a silent glance.

The City of Silver taught the acting method as ordinary knowledge?

Alger immediately followed up.

“Do you also teach any important points about the acting method?”

“Yes.”

Derrick answered without hesitation.

“It’s clearly stated in our general education classes. The one and only key point is: ‘Remember that you’re only acting.’”

Klein quietly smiled.

As he had expected, the acting method allowed Beyonders to circumvent the danger of remnants within their potions rather than submitting to them. Derrick, despite his sincerity, had casually revealed extremely valuable information.

Klein lowered his gaze toward Roselle’s diary page, already beginning to wonder what secrets it contained.

Key Events

  • Derrick successfully obtains the Telepathist formula by trading much of his family inheritance.
  • Klein reveals his prior preparation by annotating unfamiliar City of Silver terminology on the formula.
  • Audrey provides another page of Roselle’s diary and agrees to give The Fool’s adorer 300 pounds.
  • Klein establishes a secret anonymous Backlund Bank account and uses the full Fool incantation as its password.
  • Derrick reveals that the City of Silver routinely teaches the acting method in general education.
  • The key principle of the acting method is revealed: “Remember that you’re only acting.”
  • Klein realizes Derrick has unintentionally disclosed major information about how Beyonders avoid losing control.
