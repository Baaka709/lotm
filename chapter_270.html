Chapter 270

Chapter 270 - Reporter

496 words · 3 min read

At the Quelaag Club shooting range, Klein fired a rented revolver with remarkable accuracy. Even his worst shot struck the eight-ring.

If he kept practicing for several months, he believed he could become a true sharpshooter.

Talim then prepared to test Klein’s fighting ability. The equestrian instructor confidently adopted a boxing stance and revealed that he had trained as a trainee knight from childhood.

As a Sequence 8 Clown, Klein silently thought that losing to an ordinary trained man would be humiliating.

Talim attacked.

Klein blocked the blow, seized his arm, twisted his body, and redirected his momentum in a smooth throw.

Thud.

Talim crashed onto his back.

“Impressive!” he said as he rose. “As expected of a famous detective. Your marksmanship and fighting skills are excellent.”

Klein privately wondered how such an easy victory had earned him such praise.

He asked about the commission.

Talim explained that his friend was Mike Joseph, a reporter for the Daily Observer who would probably need short-term protection.

Klein continued his firearms practice, trying a hunting rifle, a single-shot rifle, and a repeating rifle in addition to the revolver.

Around noon, he went downstairs and enjoyed the Quelaag Club’s generous buffet. He ate chicken, steak, seafood rice, oyster broth, cream cheese lobster, fruit salad, and Marquis black tea.

Talim eventually returned with Mike Joseph.

Mike looked to be in his late twenties, with rough skin, sparse eyebrows, blue eyes, and a thin mustache.

After quickly filling two plates with food, he sat down with Klein.

“Detective Moriarty, have you heard about the recent serial murders?”

Klein immediately thought of the woman he had seen lying in the alley.

“The ones which had organs removed?”

Mike showed him the latest issue of the Daily Observer.

The headline announced:

“11! Another lady killed! Sivellaus Yard is helpless!”

Eleven victims.

The latest had been another woman found with her intestines removed.

Klein immediately suspected a Devil-pathway Beyonder or devil worship. The case should have drawn the attention of the Nighthawks, Mandated Punishers, or Machinery Hivemind, yet the killer remained free.

Mike clarified that he did not want Klein to investigate the killer.

He wanted to complete a newspaper report.

His research had uncovered a disturbing commonality: every victim had once been a street girl. Some later became maids, textile workers, tailors, or teachers.

Mike claimed that one in six Backlund women between fifteen and fifty-five was either currently or formerly a street girl.

Klein found the statistic almost unbelievable.

Of the eleven victims, ten had been former street girls. One, a sixteen-year-old named Siber, was still working as a prostitute.

Mike wanted to investigate Siber’s workplace, Golden Rose.

“I’m worried that my questions will anger the people there, so I plan to ask you to protect me temporarily.”

He offered one pound if nothing happened and five pounds if a fight broke out.

Klein agreed to consider it, then excused himself.

In the bathroom, he performed a divination.

The answer was positive.

Key Events

  • Klein demonstrates excellent shooting and easily defeats Talim in hand-to-hand combat.
  • Talim introduces Mike Joseph, a reporter seeking temporary protection.
  • Mike reveals that the Backlund serial killer has already killed eleven women.
  • The victims all share a history of prostitution or street work.
  • The youngest victim, sixteen-year-old Siber, was still working at Golden Rose.
  • Mike wants to investigate Golden Rose rather than directly pursue the murderer.
  • Klein agrees to protect him and confirms the commission through divination.
