Chapter 281

Chapter 281 - Clue

595 words · 4 min read

In an abandoned medical school building west of North Borough, Audrey and Fors attended a strange Beyonder gathering.

Both were dressed in white gowns and surgical masks.

Audrey was startled by what she saw inside.

The classroom contained skeletal specimens, glass coffins filled with preserved corpses, and a glass pillar containing the floating corpse of a man in a black scholar’s robe.

Several people in similar medical attire sat silently around the room.

Audrey felt frightened.

But she was also excited.

This was what the life of a Beyonder should be like.

Eventually, the corpse in the glass pillar opened its eyes.

“Let’s begin.”

---

Meanwhile, Klein entered East Borough’s Dharavi Street wearing his disguise.

The district was dark and miserable. Homeless people wandered the streets after being driven away by the authorities.

The scene reminded Klein of an abyss.

He reached the destroyed apartment and studied the buildings around it. The best places to observe the crime scene were directly across from the damaged building.

He entered one apartment and climbed to the third floor.

Then he opened the iron cigarette case.

The All-Black Eye immediately assaulted his mind with terrifying murmurs.

The black threads appeared again.

Klein traced them through nearby rooms and found only ordinary residents: men, women, and children sleeping in cramped conditions.

There was no hidden murderer.

He moved from room to room, repeatedly touching the All-Black Eye, trying to find a position that could clearly observe the crime scene.

Nothing.

The repeated exposure severely affected him. Klein eventually crouched on a balcony, panting, tears streaming from his eyes and his nose running.

Still, the corruption had not worsened into true mental damage.

After recovering, he searched another building.

Again, nothing.

Klein began wondering whether his interpretation of the divination had been wrong.

He returned to the street and finally decided to test the crime scene itself.

He touched the All-Black Eye.

His head suddenly felt as though it were being smashed.

The black lines were difficult to perceive at that distance, but they clearly gathered somewhere inside the destroyed apartment.

Then Klein saw something.

A black line emerged from the third floor of the damaged building and disappeared into the air.

Someone was inside.

The murderer was still there.

Klein immediately withdrew his hand and calmed himself.

He realized that the murderer was insane enough to wait at the crime scene for investigators.

His previous search had failed because he had assumed the murderer would avoid the scene.

Klein walked around the neighborhood and eventually entered the building as though he were a resident.

He climbed to the third floor.

Near the destroyed apartment, Klein touched the All-Black Eye again.

The same terrifying murmurs returned.

A black thread extended from the ruined room.

Following it, Klein discovered a man completely merged into the shadows.

He was nearly two meters tall, wearing a black priest’s robe. His expression was ferocious, his cold eyes resembling those of a wild beast.

It wasn’t Lanevus.

Klein immediately withdrew his hand.

He casually entered the public bathroom nearby, wiped away his sweat, and waited for the symptoms of the All-Black Eye to weaken.

Then he escaped through the window and quickly left the area.

He knew the hidden man would eventually realize that someone who had entered the bathroom had not come back.

Klein ran through a long detour and returned to his East Borough apartment.

After using the gray fog to confirm that he was safe, he conjured a portrait of the mysterious man.

Then he contacted Miss Justice.

“This is a clue.”

Key Events

  • Audrey and Fors attend a strange gathering in an abandoned medical school classroom surrounded by corpses and anatomical specimens.
  • Audrey receives no immediate response from Mr. Fool while preparing for the gathering.
  • Klein enters Dharavi Street in disguise and searches nearby apartments using the All-Black Eye.
  • Repeated contact with the All-Black Eye causes severe physical and mental pain but does not yet corrupt Klein irreversibly.
  • His initial searches fail to locate the murderer.
  • Klein finally discovers a black thread emerging from the destroyed crime scene.
  • He infiltrates the building and identifies a nearly two-meter-tall man hiding inside the ruined apartment.
  • The man is not Lanevus but is clearly connected to the case.
  • Klein escapes without alerting him and sends his portrait to Justice as a crucial clue.
