Chapter 269

Chapter 269 - Clues to the Psychology Alchemists

577 words · 4 min read

On an empty horse track outside Empress Borough, Audrey arranged a meeting with Xio and Fors while pretending to speak privately with Viscount Glaint.

Audrey looked striking in riding clothes, while Susie sat obediently by her feet. Xio and Fors were disguised as servants.

Glaint joked that horse-riding was dull compared with hunting, while Audrey reminded him that hunting season would not arrive for several months. He then stepped away, allowing Audrey to speak privately with the two women.

“Honorable Miss Audrey, why have you called us here?” Xio asked.

Audrey smiled elegantly.

“It’s a simple request this time.”

She indicated the small leather satchel attached to Susie.

Xio immediately stepped forward to retrieve the papers. She briefly considered patting Susie’s head, but the golden retriever neatly turned around and presented the satchel herself.

Xio opened it and found several portraits of a young man with neatly combed hair, round glasses, and mocking brown eyes.

He looked familiar.

Then Xio saw the description.

“Formerly used name: Lanevus, wanted swindler.”

She immediately remembered him.

Before meeting Audrey, Xio had made money by studying newspapers, wanted notices, and gang information to hunt criminals with valuable bounties. She had even considered searching for Lanevus because he carried a hundred-pound bounty and had stolen more than ten thousand pounds.

She had simply become too busy with Audrey’s work.

“How much is this request worth?”

Audrey hesitated. She had forgotten to discuss payment because she regarded the mission as a test ordered by Mr. Fool.

“Uh... 100 pounds?”

“Deal!”

Xio and Fors answered together.

If they caught Lanevus, they could receive Audrey’s hundred pounds and potentially claim his official bounty as well.

Xio casually asked why Audrey wanted Lanevus, but Audrey avoided answering and instead explained that she had information suggesting he was currently in Backlund.

She then produced more portraits showing possible disguises—different hairstyles, beards, and the removal of his glasses.

Xio was delighted.

“This is really good news!”

Audrey then asked about the Psychology Alchemists.

Fors explained that she had joined another Beyonder gathering where rumors suggested Spectator and Telepathist potion formulas had appeared. She suspected that one of its members might belong to the Psychology Alchemists.

“I’ll apply to bring you with me the next gathering.”

Audrey was openly pleased. Through their emotional states and reactions, she had already confirmed that both women were genuinely interested and had not lied.

Glaint then jokingly asked Audrey about his own potion formula.

Fors explained that there were still no clues, since the Apothecary pathway was concentrated mainly in the Feynapotter region.

Glaint laughed that he was young enough to wait.

Audrey eventually departed with Susie.

---

The following morning, Klein left home after placing a note outside saying he would return after five. He mainly wanted to prevent Millet Carter from discovering that the detective hired for fifty pounds was actually sitting around with nothing urgent to do.

He planned to practice shooting, read newspapers, enjoy lunch at the Quelaag Club, rest, and later visit Bravehearts Bar.

He deliberately avoided looking for Maric because Miss Bodyguard might belong to the same hidden circles. Selling potion formulas around people like that would attract unwanted suspicion.

At the Quelaag Club, Klein encountered Talim Dumont, who immediately informed him that Mary and Doragu were getting divorced.

Talim then explained that a friend needed help and asked how good Klein was at shooting and fighting.

It sounded like a violent commission.

Klein agreed to demonstrate.

Key Events

  • Audrey gives Xio and Fors the task of locating Lanevus.
  • Xio recognizes Lanevus as a wanted swindler and eagerly accepts the 100-pound mission.
  • Audrey provides multiple portraits showing possible Lanevus disguises.
  • Fors reveals a possible connection to the Psychology Alchemists through a new Beyonder gathering.
  • Klein spends the next day keeping himself occupied while waiting for new information.
  • Talim Dumont asks Klein to demonstrate his shooting and fighting skills for a friend’s potential protection request.
