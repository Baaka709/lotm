Chapter 181

Chapter 181 - Different State

1,125 words · 7 min read

Klein did not immediately dispel the spirituality barrier after returning to his room. Instead, he calmly prepared another ritual, placing a sandalwood-infused candle on his desk and surrounding it with ingredients symbolizing mystery and good luck.

He lit the candle, stepped back, and began the incantation in the language of the giants before switching to Hermes.

“I summon in my name:

‘The Fool that doesn’t belong to this era, the mysterious ruler above the gray fog; the King of Yellow and Black who wields good luck.’”

The candle flame suddenly fused with the surrounding mystical scent, forming an illusory vortex that rapidly absorbed spirituality. The vortex eventually stabilized into a palm-sized circle of grayish-white fog.

Klein immediately followed the same procedure he had used to reach the mysterious space before, taking four counterclockwise steps.

He returned to the world above the gray fog.

As expected, ripples of light were spreading from the high-backed chair, highlighting the mysterious symbol carved into its back—the Pupil-less Eye surrounded by partially distorted lines.

After using Cogitation to steady himself, Klein reached toward the target of his ritual.

The incantation he had just recited echoed back to him. Surging spirituality and rippling light merged into an illusory door. Unlike his previous attempt, this time the door was completely formed and covered in mysterious patterns identical to the symbol on the back of The Fool’s chair.

Klein concentrated and willed it open.

The normally motionless gray fog and palace suddenly rippled like a pond struck by a stone. A heavy scraping sound followed as the mysterious door slowly opened.

Beyond the narrow gap lay an unimaginably dark realm filled with countless transparent, indescribable figures and magnificent streaks of color that seemed to contain infinite knowledge.

Then an irresistible force came from beyond the doorway.

Klein was pulled forward.

Damn! Are you not giving me the choice?

Before he could react, his body passed through the opening and vanished into the darkness.

When the madness and roaring voices faded, Klein found himself standing before a young man.

The young man had black hair, brown eyes, an ordinary face, and the bearing of a scholar.

Klein immediately realized that it was himself.

He looked around and found himself in his own bedroom. His bed, clothes, bookshelf, desk, candle, and other familiar objects were all exactly where they should be. Yet Klein himself was floating in front of the small circle of gray-white fog.

He quickly understood the situation.

Only his spirit body had been summoned. His physical body remained behind, while his projected appearance was effectively an Astral Projection.

This also explained why, while inside the world above the gray fog, he had been able to observe the Astral Projection surfaces of Audrey, Alger, and Derrick and use them to determine that they were Beyonders and estimate their Sequences.

Klein studied his own physical body and then began experimenting with his spirit form.

He moved through the room like a ghost, enjoying the sensation of flight. He wondered whether he could serve as a messenger in this state and whether he could physically carry objects.

At first, he could not.

His hand passed directly through ordinary books and sheets of paper. The sensation was strange and slightly resistant rather than simply passing through empty air, suggesting that with greater power or stronger resonance with the mysterious space, he might eventually be able to move physical objects.

Klein then tested objects containing his own spirituality.

His Slumber Charms and Requiem Charms could be sensed, but he still lacked enough strength to pick them up. The Flaring Sun Charms were different. Because they contained both his spirituality and power derived from divine blood, they responded much more strongly.

Klein managed to pull one from his pocket.

Warmth spread throughout his spiritual body, making his form more stable and his thoughts clearer. In the bedroom mirror, the charm seemed to float through the air on its own, as though moved by an invisible ghost.

Klein discovered another ability by experimenting with the mirror itself.

He could pass through it.

His perspective instantly shifted, and he found himself observing the reflected room from the mirror’s other side. Since it was only an ordinary mirror rather than a supernatural gateway, he could immediately move back into the room.

The success gave him confidence, so he attempted to touch Azik’s copper whistle.

The result was dramatically different.

The moment his spirit touched the ancient artifact, his spirituality suddenly expanded and became cold and powerful. The illusory eyes of his spirit transformed into dark flames.

Klein felt that he had become stronger.

His appearance resembled a wraith, though without the overwhelming hatred and vengeance usually associated with one. Thanks to the Clown’s abilities, he could also better project and control his appearance.

With the copper whistle empowering him, Klein could now pick up lighter objects, including sheets of paper and his Slumber Charms. He could even carry the silver ritual dagger, though his revolver was still too heavy.

Klein then tested his supernatural abilities in this spiritual state. He discovered that he could use two spells: one was an intangible howl capable of shaking the souls of others, while the second could induce a freezing-like condition through direct contact.

However, his experiments soon revealed another limitation.

Klein wondered whether he could move around during the day as a spirit. He cautiously opened the curtain and allowed sunlight into the room.

The result was immediate.

Under the sunlight, his spirit began boiling with black mist while his powers rapidly drained away. He quickly closed the curtain.

He then wondered whether the Flaring Sun Charm, empowered by the blood of the Eternal Blazing Sun, could protect him.

When he tried combining it with the copper whistle, however, the two powers violently conflicted. The warm, purifying power of the Flaring Sun Charm clashed against the cold spirituality generated by the copper whistle.

Klein was forced to throw the charm away.

He realized that the power of Azik’s copper whistle and the Flaring Sun Charm could not coexist within his spirit body at the same time. When he set the whistle aside, his spirituality immediately weakened and the dark flames in his eyes disappeared.

His two spiritual spells were also weaker in this state.

After another round of testing, Klein picked up the Flaring Sun Charm instead. Its warm and purifying power stabilized his spirit body.

He cautiously approached the window again and passed through the curtain while carrying the charm.

This time, the sunlight felt merely warm.

The charm could protect him from the harmful effects of daytime sunlight.

Encouraged by the discovery, Klein gave a complicated smile and moved through the wall of spirituality, floating out of his house to continue experimenting.

Key Events

  • Klein successfully performs the ritual to summon himself into the world above the gray fog.

  • Klein experiments with moving through objects and discovers he can pass through mirrors and fly like a spirit.
  • His Flaring Sun Charms can be physically manipulated in his spirit state and stabilize his spiritual body.

  • The copper whistle’s power and the Flaring Sun Charm cannot coexist within his spirit simultaneously, forcing Klein to choose between their effects.
