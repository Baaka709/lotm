Chapter 223 - Three Matters

621 words · 4 min read

After hearing Ian’s account, Klein asked whether the Zreal seen by other detectives might actually have been an impostor.

Ian considered the possibility but rejected it as unlikely. The gathering had been filled with experienced detectives, and ordinary disguises such as wigs, beards, or cosmetics would hardly fool such people. Klein silently noted that Beyonder abilities could easily change that conclusion, but Ian’s response gave him no indication that the boy understood much about mysticism.

Ian proposed another explanation: the real Zreal had been present but was somehow being controlled, leaving him unable to send distress signals. His failure to respond to their agreed method of communication could itself have been a hidden request for help.

Klein considered the theory reasonable. Ian then formally asked him to investigate Zreal and determine his current condition.

Because Ian seemed to be an experienced information gatherer, Klein was interested in establishing a connection with him. However, he also warned that the investigation might be dangerous. They eventually settled on a contract in which Ian would provide five pounds up front and three favors afterward.

Ian signed the agreement and handed over detailed information about Zreal. When Klein asked how to contact him in case of an emergency, Ian simply said that Klein would not need to contact him because he would appear at the appropriate time.

Ian then counted out the payment from a carefully organized stack of money. Klein noticed the notes were arranged with almost absurd precision and estimated that the boy had less than three pounds remaining.

To Klein’s amusement, he deliberately folded the money messily, provoking a small, almost imperceptible reaction from Ian.

After Ian departed, Klein attempted a divination to determine whether supernatural elements were involved. The copper coin slipped from his fingers, signaling failure.

Ian had clearly concealed more information than he had revealed.

That night, Klein went to Zreal’s home disguised as a laborer. He wore a cheap blue work outfit, a rough black beard, and an Intis-style cap pulled low over his face.

Zreal lived alone in a dark terrace house. He had come to Backlund from Southville and lived there as a bachelor, occasionally hiring temporary maids to clean the house.

Before entering, Klein used his spirit pendulum and repeated, “There’s danger inside.” The pendulum turned clockwise, but slowly, suggesting danger that was present yet manageable.

After checking his equipment, Klein silently crossed the road, climbed the building, and used a tarot card to unlock the balcony door.

Following the floor plan Ian had supplied, he reached Zreal’s bedroom. With Spirit Vision activated, Klein detected three humanoid auras behind the wooden door.

Three people were waiting inside.

Klein immediately retreated to the balcony and retrieved one of the Slumber Charms he had made earlier. Because he had created it through his connection to The Fool rather than the Evernight Goddess, he knew the charm’s power was limited, but it was still useful.

He activated it with the ancient Hermes word “Crimson,” opened the bedroom door slightly, and threw the charm inside.

After counting down, he entered.

The three people were all asleep.

The room itself appeared ordinary, furnished with a bed, closets, a desk, sofas, and a coat rack. A man in a black coat slept beside the bed, while two others had collapsed near the sofa and closet.

Klein confirmed that the three had been successfully subdued.

He then searched near the bed and found several strands of short yellowish-brown hair, matching Ian’s description of Zreal. He picked them up, sat down on the sofa, and prepared to use dream divination to locate the missing detective.

In the dim crimson moonlight, Klein leaned back and allowed himself a dry smile.

“Deduction, my good man...”

Key Events

  • Ian argues that the Zreal seen by other detectives probably was the real man, but that Zreal may have been under control.
  • Klein suspects Ian knows more than he has admitted, especially after the failed divination.
  • Klein begins investigating Zreal’s home under disguise.
  • A spirit pendulum confirms danger inside, though it does not appear overwhelming.
  • Klein secretly infiltrates the house and uses a self-made Slumber Charm.
  • He discovers three people lying in ambush inside Zreal’s bedroom and puts them to sleep.
  • Klein finds hair matching Zreal’s description.
  • He prepares to use dream divination to determine Zreal’s whereabouts and condition.
