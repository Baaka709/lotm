Chapter 162

1,143 words · 7 min read

Weak sunlight entered Morse Cathedral through narrow windows high above, illuminating the dark interior in thin shafts. Klein sat quietly in the front pew, studying the altar. Instead of a statue of the Goddess, there was only a massive Dark Sacred Emblem—a black base bearing a crimson half-moon surrounded by points of light. Sunlight passing through openings behind it formed tiny specks of brightness, creating the illusion of a star-filled night sky.

Klein reflected that the traditional Gods never left behind literal images. Their symbols were worshiped instead, perhaps embodying the warning not to look directly upon God.

He deliberately waited before acting. Leonard and Kenley could still return during the first few minutes, and he wanted to avoid being interrupted while performing his dangerous experiment.

After twenty minutes, Klein finally moved to a hidden corner and turned his back to the altar. He took out Sealed Artifact 3-0782, the golden Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem, and placed it on the floor. Before it, he set a small sandalwood candle to represent himself.

Then he arranged the ritual materials: a carving knife, two thin gold sheets, Sun essential oil, Golden Hand fingered citron powder, and rosemary powder. Using his silver ritual dagger, he established a spiritual barrier around the ritual.

Klein lit the candle with spirituality and added the Sun-related ingredients. A faint mist smelling of sunlight spread through the air.

He raised the carving knife and golden sheets and began an incantation in Hermes:

“The blood of the Eternal Blazing Sun.

“You are the Inextinguishable Light, the Embodiment of Order, the God of Deeds, the Guardian of Businesses.”

He was deliberately invoking the blood contained within the sacred emblem rather than directly calling upon the Eternal Blazing Sun. Otherwise, the deity might recognize the person who had dared to gaze directly at “Him.”

Yet Klein still felt intense pressure as he continued.

“I pray to you.

“I pray for you to give me strength.

“Give me strength to complete the Flaring Sun Charm.”

He could not divine beforehand whether stealing divine power would succeed, so he could only endure his fear and finish the ritual.

Suddenly, the golden emblem erupted with brilliant light.

The cathedral seemed to contain a second sun.

An unbearable heat enveloped Klein. His hair nearly caught fire, his feet felt as though they stood on sun-heated sand, and scorching winds rushed around him. His instincts told him that unless he released the energy somewhere, he would literally burn like a candle.

Without hesitation, he raised both hands and began carving.

His movements relied on spirituality, instinct, the wind, and the ritual’s guidance. He engraved symbols, Path Numbers, magical characteristics, and ancient incantations onto both sides of the gold sheets.

Outside, Leonard and Kenley suddenly noticed the sunlight becoming dramatically stronger. The sky was perfectly clear, yet the light became as fierce as midsummer.

“What strange weather,” Kenley remarked.

Leonard glanced toward the cathedral.

“Luckily Rozanne isn’t here. She’d complain about the sun tanning her skin.”

The intense light lasted for several minutes before gradually returning to normal.

Inside the cathedral, Klein completed the final stroke. The spiritual powers on both sides of the gold sheets merged, and the concentrated radiance settled into the metal.

The burning sensation finally disappeared.

Klein examined the two newly created Flaring Sun Charms. Their golden surfaces were now dimmer, their patterns ancient and complicated, and a warm sensation gently seeped into his hands.

“Not bad. I finally have a more impressive trump card.”

He designated “light” in Ancient Hermes as their activation incantation, amused by the coincidence.

The charms should retain their power for at least a year, perhaps longer. He kept them separate from his Slumber, Requiem, and Dream Charms because their powerful aura would shorten the other charms’ effectiveness.

Klein then restored everything, removed the spiritual barrier, and examined himself. His clothes were soaked with sweat, his hair slightly curled from the heat, but he had survived.

*Thankfully...*

Completely exhausted, he returned to his original seat and fell asleep.

He awoke when Leonard entered.

“You don’t look alright.”

“I’m nearing my limit.”

Klein checked his pocket watch and immediately handed Leonard the Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem.

“Just in time. It’s your turn to look after Sealed Artifact 3-0782.”

Leonard’s casual attitude vanished once he took charge. He carefully examined the artifact, clearly puzzled that nothing seemed different.

After the shifts were completed, the three Nighthawks began the journey back to Tingen. Before leaving, they instructed Priest Siur to keep monitoring Morse Town and immediately contact Saint Selena Cathedral by telegram if further paranormal events appeared.

They reached Zouteland Street around 7:20 that evening, returned 3-0782, and ensured that Dunn noticed nothing unusual. Klein then went home.

When he opened the door, he found an unfamiliar young woman cleaning the dining room. She looked to be barely twenty, wore a plain grayish-white dress, and had ordinary black hair, brown eyes, and unremarkable features.

Klein quickly realized that she must be one of the maidservants visiting for the trial.

Benson lowered his newspaper and remarked that a company that refused to let employees leave on time was annoying.

“But it provides a salary capable of compensating for any dissatisfaction,” Klein replied jokingly.

He was already thinking about the 300 pounds Audrey would send. Once it arrived, he intended to tell Benson and Melissa about his increased salary of six pounds a week so they would worry less about the family’s finances.

Klein asked whether they had chosen a maidservant.

Benson nodded.

“Bella. Five soli a week. She’s willing and capable of learning to cook, and she hopes to become a home chef eventually, which would double her income. Her father works at the Tingen Steelworks Union Factory, and her mother is a laundry worker.”

There was another reason. The other two candidates followed the Lord of Storms, while Bella was a believer in the Goddess. Benson personally did not care much, but Melissa had strongly preferred a believer of the Goddess.

Benson then told Klein that since he was home, they should have dinner.

...

The following morning, Klein arrived at the Blackthorn Security Company in a good mood.

Rozanne quickly looked around before lowering her voice.

“Old Neil is sick. Let’s visit him at noon.”

Klein was surprised.

*Could the ritual he uses to treat diarrhea have caused severe constipation?*

He quickly dismissed the joke and instead remembered that Old Neil was elderly, and the emotional shock of learning about the acting method might have affected him badly.

Rozanne explained that Old Neil had formally asked Dunn for leave.

Klein agreed that they should visit him and expressed sympathy. He remembered that Old Neil’s wife had died early and that his son worked in another city, leaving him alone when ill.

Rozanne suddenly stared at him.

“When did Old Neil get married?”
