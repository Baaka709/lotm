Chapter 208 - Cry

895 words · 6 min read

The telegram in Dunn’s office suddenly began beeping, but Klein and Leonard paid it no attention. Their eyes were red and brimming with tears as they counted down the seconds.

“10.”

“9.”

“8.”

Dunn entered the reception hall carrying the silver, bone-like box containing Saint Selena’s ashes. Megose, already having torn a wound deep enough to expose bone while ripping out her hair, suddenly pointed at him and screamed, “You want to kill my child!”

The shriek struck Klein like a hammer. His head throbbed, his vision reddened, and blood began flowing from his nose. Leonard was in an even worse state, blood covering his eyes, nose, and lips.

Klein forced himself to continue counting.

“5.”

“4.”

Dunn was suffering as well. Blood vessels bulged across his face, and blood flowed from his ears, yet his will remained unshaken. He opened the urn.

Inside was profound darkness filled with countless grains of radiant sand, resembling a starry night trapped inside the box. Instantly, the reception hall plunged into darkness. Countless cold, smooth black threads emerged from the air and rushed toward Megose, wrapping around her like the tentacles of some enormous unseen creature.

Megose had already torn out one of her own eyes, leaving it dangling from her face. She glared at Dunn and shrieked, “You must die!”

An invisible force blasted Dunn backward into a wall. Bricks flew loose as blood poured from his mouth, but he stubbornly kept both hands around Saint Selena’s urn.

The black threads tightened around Megose. Blight-tainted flames erupted from her body, and her skin began secreting a liquid that reeked of brimstone, yet neither could harm the threads.

“3!”

“2!”

“1!”

Klein and Leonard burst into the reception hall. Klein held the Flaring Sun Charm, while Leonard aimed the Blood Vessel Thief at Megose.

By then, Megose had become barely recognizable as human. Two grotesque lumps of flesh had emerged from her shoulders, resembling the heads of children, with cracks spreading across them into crude eyes.

She opened her mouth, preparing to unleash the Blaspheme Curse against anyone threatening her child.

Leonard twisted his wrist.

His face became livid, veins bulging horribly beneath his skin.

The curse died in Megose’s throat.

The Blood Vessel Thief had stolen her Blaspheme Curse.

Klein immediately seized the opening. He uttered an ancient Hermes word.

“Light!”

The golden charm in his hand became scorching hot and erupted with dazzling radiance. Pouring more than half his spirituality into it, Klein hurled the Flaring Sun Charm toward Megose.

The reception hall was flooded with brilliant sunlight. Darkness vanished, and even the black threads shrank away instinctively.

Above Megose, the ceiling had been torn open during the battle, exposing the bright blue sky. The natural sunlight combined with the Flaring Sun Charm, transforming its radiance into a gigantic sphere of swirling flames.

Boom!

The entire building shook violently. Windows in nearby buildings shattered, yet the destructive power remained concentrated on Megose.

When the light finally faded, Klein saw flames still burning and black ashes drifting through the air.

Megose was gone.

So was the baby.

The coffee table, glass, newspaper, and sofa around them had all been annihilated.

Could it really be over?

Klein barely had time to wonder before his Clown instincts screamed of danger.

He rolled aside without hesitation.

A long arm tipped with an extraordinarily sharp white bone blade cleaved through the space he had just occupied.

The slash tore through Klein’s clothing, skin, flesh, and bone, nearly splitting him in two. Only his premonition had saved his life. Even so, agony flooded his mind and scattered his thoughts.

Megose emerged from the flames.

Her hair and clothing had burned away. Her body was charred and peeling, her nose melted, and empty eye sockets flickered with pale flames. The two malformed heads had vanished, while her left arm had transformed into the monstrous white bone blade.

Ignoring Dunn, Leonard, and the incoming black threads, she shot toward Klein and raised the blade to cut his throat.

“Submit!”

Leonard raised his hand. The Blood Vessel Thief around his wrist had transformed into a swollen crimson mass.

He had successfully stolen Megose’s Blaspheme Curse and was using it against her.

The power was finally strong enough to restrain her. Megose’s body bent as her knees trembled, her movements halted under the stolen curse. The black threads immediately rushed toward her.

Klein seized the opportunity and rolled away, leaving a trail of blood. Despite the agony, he reached into his pocket and pulled out his final Flaring Sun Charm.

He would end this before the baby could be born.

Boom!

Megose’s head suddenly exploded, scattering charred flesh and bone. Yet her headless body broke free of Leonard’s stolen Blaspheme Curse.

It transformed into a projectile and slammed directly into Leonard.

Leonard’s body crashed through the wall. His bones cracked, blood poured from his mouth, and he immediately lost consciousness.

Megose raised her bone blade again as the black threads rushed back toward her.

Dunn was still gripping Saint Selena’s urn, his face deathly pale. The threads had yet to completely restrain Megose.

Klein ignored his own terrible wounds and drew out the final Flaring Sun Charm. He was just about to recite the Hermes incantation when the room fell strangely quiet.

Then, from the darkness, came a sound that froze everyone.

“Waaa!”

A baby’s cry.

Key Events

  • Dunn activates Saint Selena’s ashes, unleashing countless black threads that restrain Megose.
  • Klein and Leonard attack once the ten-second countdown ends; Leonard uses 2-105 to steal Megose’s Blaspheme Curse.
  • Klein combines the Flaring Sun Charm with direct sunlight, apparently destroying Megose and the unborn child.
  • Megose unexpectedly survives in a heavily burned, monstrous form and nearly kills Klein with her transformed bone blade.
  • Leonard uses the stolen Blaspheme Curse to restrain Megose, but she breaks free and violently crashes into him, knocking him unconscious.
  • Klein prepares his final Flaring Sun Charm while Dunn struggles to keep Megose restrained.
  • A baby suddenly cries from the darkness, revealing that the terrifying child may have been born—or is about to descend.
