Chapter 139

1,109 words · 7 min read

When the crimson connection disappeared, Derrick found himself back in his room, still holding the pure crystal ball.

Crack!

The crystal ball shattered from within. Some fragments became illusory beams of light that vanished into the air, while the rest scattered across the floor.

Derrick stared blankly at the remains. In the bronze mirror, he could still see the blood streaking his face. Then he noticed a crimson symbol appearing on the back of his right hand—a circle with lines extending outward. The strange mark sank into his skin and vanished.

After several flashes of lightning, he finally recovered his senses.

He left his bedroom and stepped outside, looking up at the endless darkness of the City of Silver. Lightning briefly illuminated the city in silver, followed by thunder that shook the world.

Derrick clenched his fists. His grief over his parents remained, but he was no longer lost.

---

Above the gray fog, Klein shook his head at the thought of the Tarot Club.

Phew. I’ve tricked another person into joining. No—recruited another member.

Yet he could not ignore the absurd disparity between his own strength and that of his newest member’s home.

The Fool was only a Sequence 9 Seer who had just finished digesting his potion, while the City of Silver possessed at least three High-Sequence Beyonders at Sequence 4.

Klein knew he needed to advance soon.

“After I mention the acting method one more time, I can tell the Captain everything and submit my special application. At least I won’t remain on support duty once I become a Clown.”

He descended from the gray fog and returned to his rented room.

After confirming that Dunn and Frye had not yet returned, Klein returned the key downstairs. The innkeeper looked at the clock and gave him an approving thumbs-up.

“Well done!”

Klein nearly explained the misunderstanding behind the hourly room, but decided against it.

At least he won’t mention that I rented another room in front of the Captain.

He later performed a quick divination and returned to the inn at the appropriate time. Dunn and Frye were already discussing their investigations.

Dunn confirmed that the wraith had appeared within the past three months.

Klein shared the results of his own investigation and mentioned the local resident, Scoundrel Gray, who claimed to possess an antique portrait of the first Baron Lamud.

“Don’t tell me you bought it?” Dunn asked in surprise.

“No,” Klein replied dryly. He explained that his historical and archaeological knowledge gave him enough experience to recognize obvious forgeries.

Then he casually added the most important detail:

“The person in the portrait looked somewhat like my history teacher, Mr. Azik.”

Dunn did not seem particularly interested. Lamud was a town near an important historical site, so fake antiques were apparently everywhere. He had even encountered someone selling supposed silver wine glasses belonging to Baron Lamud.

Frye added that someone had tried to sell him a supposed Lamud family insignia.

Klein asked whether either of them had bought the items.

Silence followed.

They quickly moved on.

The next issue was Sealed Artifact 3-0782.

Because its purification could turn ordinary people within its range into mindless devotees of the Sun, it had to be kept far from the inn. Klein volunteered to carry it outside for the next two-hour shift.

Frye handed him the Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem.

The dark-gold metal was warm and seemed to contain flowing heat. A gentle, pure force spread from it in waves, cleansing Klein’s spirituality and removing impurities.

Klein was fascinated, but he also remembered the fundamental rule surrounding Sealed Artifacts: every one of them carried danger, and a careless mistake could result in death—or something worse.

After checking his revolver, charms, and cane, Klein left the inn with Frye.

They found an isolated location beside a sparse forest and confirmed that nobody was nearby.

“I’ll come back in two hours,” Frye said. “Chase away anyone who approaches.”

Once Frye left, Klein chose a large boulder, cleaned its surface, and sat down.

Why stand when you can sit?

After several quiet minutes, however, the dark forest began to feel more ominous. Klein took out several containers of herb powder and essential oils and scattered them around the boulder.

He then recited an ancient Hermes incantation and formed a spiritual barrier around himself.

The barrier had two purposes: preventing a surprise attack from corpses or spirits and, more importantly, keeping insects away.

This is a hundred times better than insect repellent!

With his preparations finished, Klein took out 3-0782 and began examining it carefully.

He wondered whether divination could reveal its origin.

He wrote:

“The origin of the Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem in my hands.”

After the standard seven repetitions and a journey into a dream, he saw nothing useful—only fragmented pieces of light.

Klein sighed.

The Church had almost certainly tried this before. The fact that no origin appeared in any records probably meant previous Seers had received no useful result either.

But then another thought struck him.

What if he removed the interference?

His curiosity grew stronger until he decided to try.

After entering the world above the gray fog, Klein sat in the ancient palace and summoned both the Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem and his divination materials.

The object felt just as real as it had in the outside world.

Klein wondered whether the mysterious space simply reconstructed things according to his perception.

He repeated his previous divination.

This time, the dream produced an image.

A drop of glowing golden liquid floated above an altar before a man dressed in a white ceremonial robe.

The man had his back turned to Klein and appeared lifeless as he collapsed toward the altar.

The Sun Sacred Emblem in his hand touched the golden liquid.

The liquid immediately seeped into the emblem.

Then the dream ended.

Klein awakened with a sudden understanding.

So that golden liquid is what gave the Sacred Emblem its extraordinary and seemingly uncontrollable purification power.

Decades had passed since the artifact was discovered, yet its strength had never weakened.

What exactly was that golden liquid? A powerful Beyonder ingredient?

Klein stared at the Sacred Emblem and became increasingly curious.

Then, guided by the feeling from the dream, he tried to separate the golden liquid from the artifact.

He succeeded almost instantly.

The dark-gold emblem lost its warmth and purity as several drops of glowing golden liquid separated from it and floated in the air.

Klein stared in shock.

Even if this separation only existed within the mysterious space, its ability remained astonishing.

He quickly took up his pen again.

“The origin of this drop of golden liquid...”
