Chapter 277 - Finalizing a Plan

831 words · 5 min read

Klein held Rafter Pound’s hair and entered a deep slumber through Cogitation.

In the dream, he saw a narrow, irregular cave.

Rafter crawled out of it, his gray hair and puffy eyes clearly visible.

Then Rafter retrieved a black iron emblem and placed it on his chest.

The emblem bore a hand gripping a scepter.

“Tudor!”

Klein immediately woke from the dream.

According to Miss Bodyguard, the emblem was the coat of arms of the Fourth Epoch Tudor Empire—and it was identical to the emblem he had seen in the underground structure.

Rafter Pound was far more complicated than he appeared.

Klein reviewed his previous investigations.

Direct questioning had revealed nothing.

Dream probing had revealed nothing beyond Rafter’s apparent trauma.

Spirit channeling had also produced consistent answers.

Without the fourth test, Klein might have accepted Rafter’s story completely.

One possibility was that Rafter truly knew nothing now and might only encounter the Tudor connection in the future.

But another possibility seemed much more likely.

Rafter already knew something.

He might have deliberately adopted a decadent and insane appearance to conceal his true knowledge. Perhaps some Beyonder ability allowed him to manipulate his own behavior so thoroughly that even Klein’s dream and spirit channeling could be deceived.

Klein then formed a more ambitious hypothesis.

Could Rafter actually be descended from the Tudor family?

If his connection with the old viscount was genuine, perhaps the Pound family had once been a hidden branch of the Tudor bloodline.

They might have changed their surname to escape pursuit, eventually becoming aristocrats and secretly searching for the Tudor Empire’s lost remains.

Decades earlier, they may have discovered the underground ruins beneath what later became Millet Carter’s property.

Then disaster struck.

The evil spirit inside did not recognize their supposed Tudor bloodline. The old viscount’s two heirs died in the innermost chamber, and their Beyonder characteristics and mystical items were left behind.

The old viscount might have obtained substitute characteristics to conceal the true cause of their deaths.

That would explain why the royal family and churches did not simply intervene openly.

Klein also reconsidered the mysterious people who had watched Rafter.

Perhaps they were not enemies at all.

Perhaps the royal family and churches had sent them to investigate.

The Tudor and Trunsoest families were connected to the Dark Emperor pathway, whose Sequence 9 was Lawyer.

Lawyers specialized in exploiting rules and loopholes.

That could explain how Rafter had somehow bypassed Klein’s dream and spirit-channeling abilities while still technically telling the truth.

His statements—“maintain the family” and “remember the glory of the ancestors”—could refer not merely to the Pound family, but to the Tudor family.

Klein then assessed the current danger.

If the evil spirit could escape by itself, Rafter would probably already have tried to solve the problem. The collapsed passage had not weakened the seal.

Therefore, as long as the innermost chamber remained intact, the evil spirit should remain contained.

Klein formed a plan.

He would purchase explosives from Kaspars and collapse the entrance to the ruins so no one could enter again.

The evil spirit would remain buried underground.

Once Klein became stronger, he could consider finishing the problem and perhaps recover valuable mystical items in the process.

He still needed to learn how to perform the demolition safely.

Kaspars might know someone experienced with explosives.

Klein decided to settle the matter within a week.

As for Rafter Pound’s connection to Tudor, he decided not to investigate further.

“It’s none of my business!”

---

On Friday, Klein spent the morning pretending to be busy while actually practicing shooting and reading newspapers at the Quelaag Club.

Later, Mary Gale and Talim Dumont arrived with a group of other gentlemen and ladies, including Mike Joseph.

Mike greeted Klein and explained that he had finished his investigation.

“I’ve roughly figured out who Capim is. I’ll tell you in detail when I have the time.”

Mike had concluded that Siber’s death was probably caused by a copycat killer rather than the serial killer.

He also arranged an interview with Mary Gale concerning Backlund’s air pollution and possible solutions.

Klein realized the meeting was connected to the new National Atmospheric Pollution Council and the political and business networking of the club.

He chose not to become involved.

---

That evening, Williams returned to his cramped apartment in East Borough.

A black shadow suddenly stood by the window.

“Who told you to look for Lanevus?”

Williams nervously explained that a bounty hunter friend had asked him.

“Who is she?”

After hesitating, Williams revealed the name.

“Xio Derecha. I don’t know where she lives.”

“Very good. Very honest.”

The shadow casually patted him.

Williams secretly relaxed, believing his experience with the underworld had helped him survive.

Then the figure snapped its fingers.

Pa!

Williams’s body suddenly shattered into pieces, covering the room with blood and flesh.

The killer broke the gas pipe, lit the candle, and left.

Minutes later, the candle ignited the leaking gas.

Boom!

The explosion destroyed everything.
