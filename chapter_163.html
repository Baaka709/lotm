Chapter 163 - Various Signs

981 words · 6 min read

Klein was left dumbfounded by Rozanne’s question. He distinctly remembered visiting Old Neil only a month earlier and seeing a piano in his living room. Neil had explained that his deceased wife had loved music. Yet Rozanne firmly recalled that when she and Mrs. Orianna visited him earlier in the year, there had been no piano at all. More unsettlingly, when she had once asked why he remained single, Neil had replied that he simply had not met a woman he wished to marry.

The contradiction immediately alarmed Klein. He asked when Rozanne had last visited Old Neil, and she answered that it had been around the beginning of June, before Kenley became a Nighthawk and Viola resigned. Her busy schedule had kept her away ever since.

Klein’s heart sank. He pulled a halfpence coin from his pocket, formulated a divination—“There’s something wrong with Old Neil’s current situation”—and entered Cogitation. After flicking the coin into the air, he caught it in his palm. George III’s portrait was facing upward: a positive result. Something really was wrong.

As he clenched the coin, memories he had almost overlooked surged back. When he had first become a Beyonder and experimented with Spirit Vision, he had seen a pair of cold, merciless eyes hovering behind Old Neil. Neil had said they were a characteristic of ritualistic magic. Klein also remembered an almost formless human figure near the doorway, its aura blending into the darkness. Later, after fully digesting the Seer potion, he had secretly altered how he activated Spirit Vision, tapping his left molar—and once, when he did so while looking at Neil, the old man had suddenly begun coughing violently.

The scattered details now connected into something deeply disturbing.

Rozanne nervously asked whether Old Neil had lost control, protesting that despite his stinginess and obsession with getting reimbursed, he was still a good man who rarely became angry. Klein could not reassure her completely. He believed Neil was not quite beyond saving, but was perilously close to losing control.

He immediately entered Captain Dunn Smith’s office and explained everything: Neil’s contradictory story about his wife, the strange figures seen through Spirit Vision, and the successful divination. Dunn listened carefully and asked why Klein had not simply divined whether Neil had already lost control.

Klein explained that during the past month, Neil had behaved like an ordinary Beyonder. He had even assisted Klein in dealing with a Mandated Punisher who had lost control. Klein had also observed Neil’s aura several times and found him generally healthy apart from the frailty of age. For those reasons, he believed Neil was still in the dangerous stage just before a full loss of control.

Dunn agreed that it was a reasonable deduction. They would visit Neil immediately, taking care not to agitate him. Once there, they would attempt to control the situation and use ritualistic magic to stabilize him.

The mention of controlling Neil sparked an idea in Klein’s mind: Sealed Artifact 3-0611, the Peaceful Hair Strands.

Dunn recalled the artifact’s details. It was a highly dangerous object consisting of black strands of hair. Direct contact with a living being stripped away every desire and emotion—hunger, anger, grief, pain, hatred, joy, greed, and even the desire to escape. A victim would simply remain where they were. Breaking contact could restore them, but only if they had been affected for less than two hours. Beyond that point, the victim would remain silent forever. The highest tested Sequence was 5, and gloves could prevent accidental contact.

The strands had been left behind after a Nighthawk Captain failed an advancement to Sequence 6.

Dunn immediately approved the idea. He told Klein to find Royale while he retrieved the artifact from Chanis Gate and applied for its use. Klein rushed to the recreation room and called for the normally expressionless Sleepless. When he told Royale their mission was to visit Old Neil, her eyes widened instinctively. Neither needed to say much more. The possibility of what they might find weighed heavily over them.

A few minutes later, Dunn returned wearing black gloves and carrying the tangled mass of dark hair. Along with Klein and Royale, he set out with the chauffeur Cesare for Neil’s bungalow.

The rainy road and silent carriage made the journey oppressive. At last, Dunn revealed a painful part of Neil’s past. When Neil was younger, he had once had a partner whom he was about to marry, but she suddenly became terminally ill. Desperate, the inexperienced Neil had risked revealing Beyonder secrets and attempted to use ritualistic magic to save her. He had failed.

The Nighthawks had once feared that the tragedy would drive him to lose control, but Neil had somehow maintained his sanity and continued appearing normal.

Klein could only pray that this was another false alarm. He drew the crimson moon of the Evernight Goddess over his chest and prayed that She would watch over Neil. Dunn and Royale joined him.

By the time they reached the bungalow, the sky was beginning to brighten as the dark clouds dispersed. Dunn sent Cesare away and approached the front door with his cane in one hand and the Peaceful Hair Strands in the other. Klein and Royale followed through the rose and mint garden.

Klein rang the bell several times. The pleasant chime echoed through the silent house, but no one came to answer. After several minutes, he forced a smile and suggested that perhaps Neil had gone to see a doctor.

Then music suddenly began playing inside.

A piano.

The melody was strangely beautiful and serene, like a still lake hidden beneath moonlit mist. Dunn’s expression turned grave, and Klein’s heart sank. Before he could perform another divination, he noticed liquid flowing from beneath the door. It was initially transparent and pure—but as it spread outward, it gradually turned a deep crimson, dark and unmistakably bloodlike.
