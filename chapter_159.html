Chapter 159

1,032 words · 6 min read

Messy Chinese characters covered the yellow-brown goatskin as Klein continued reading Roselle’s diary.

The entries revealed that Roselle had witnessed the power of a High-Sequence Beyonder and found the transformation terrifyingly profound. He compared such beings to deities and suggested that “Legendary beings” might describe them better than “Demigods.”

Another entry troubled him even more. Roselle questioned the Seven Major Churches’ strange approach to potion formulas. For lower and middle Sequences, the Churches freely provided formulas, ingredients, and even detailed instructions. Yet for higher Sequences, they only supplied completed potions.

It seemed completely backwards.

Roselle suspected a hidden secret behind this arrangement.

Then came an even more alarming entry. He had personally initiated the Industrial Revolution and brought about the Age of Steam and Machinery, but now feared that these changes might have created the conditions required for an Evil God to descend upon the world.

Klein immediately focused on that sentence.

*What does he mean by creating the conditions necessary for an Evil God to descend?*

He considered attempting a divination, but the subject involved entities on the level of deities, making the risks impossible to estimate. Even a simple question concerning divine danger could itself become dangerous. Klein decided to keep the matter in mind and investigate through observation instead.

Roselle’s comments also increased his anticipation of reaching the higher Sequences. As for the Churches’ potion system, perhaps he could eventually uncover something through Daly if she rose to a position near the Church’s core.

Klein put the diary aside and allowed the Tarot Club members to begin their discussion.

Alger immediately revealed new information about Qilangos. The pirate admiral had infiltrated Backlund for a difficult mission and might remain there for quite some time. Worse, the mission involved an extremely important and mysterious object—something that Qilangos believed would allow him to become comparable to a High-Sequence Beyonder.

Audrey immediately questioned whether he would not fear losing control.

Alger explained that this was precisely why the object mattered. Qilangos was currently only a Sequence 6 Wind-blessed, but he apparently believed that completing the mission would make him equal to Nast, the King of the Five Seas. That would effectively elevate him into a position comparable to the Pirate Kings, most of whom were either High-Sequence Beyonders or possessed comparable combat strength through mysterious items and Beyonder ships.

Klein listened silently. He knew Nast was a Sequence 4 Beyonder, though he did not know the exact name of his potion.

Derrick listened with growing fascination. The talk of pirates, seas, and distant lands revealed an entirely different world from the isolated City of Silver. Yet he remembered The Fool’s warning not to pry into other people’s secrets.

Audrey agreed that Alger’s deduction made sense, while Alger emphasized two key points: Qilangos would remain in Backlund for some time, and the mission involved an extraordinarily important object.

He was obviously trying to tempt The Fool into taking action.

Klein understood immediately. Unfortunately, his current circumstances made direct interference difficult. He could not simply leave Tingen, and his status as The Fool prevented him from openly asking Azik for help.

He considered other possibilities. Daly might be able to organize the Nighthawks if he quietly passed along information about Qilangos’s location and intentions. Azik was another powerful possibility, but Klein had no justification for involving him.

For the moment, he could only say, “I’m aware.”

Audrey then summarized the investigation she and Xio had conducted. They had narrowed down Qilangos’s likely area of activity, but needed more information—especially his habits and preferences.

Alger supplied a surprisingly detailed profile.

Qilangos loved fish, especially raw sea fish, and preferred hard liquor while disliking champagne and red wine. Whenever he went ashore, he frequently sought women, and his extraordinary physical strength meant one was rarely enough. He preferred cold weapons to firearms and could not remain away from water for long. Every few days, he needed to swim or dive.

Audrey carefully memorized every detail, building a complete mental profile of the pirate.

“Let’s hope the investigation succeeds. It’s a pleasure working with you.”

“My pleasure,” Alger replied, forcing himself to trust her ability to act within Backlund.

Throughout their conversation, however, Klein’s thoughts had shifted to a different problem.

He needed a way to deliver Beyonder ingredients to Derrick.

His understanding of mysticism naturally led him toward ritual magic. The Nighthawks’ records contained examples of Churches bestowing holy objects upon followers through rituals, as well as evil gods and devils sending items into the real world.

*Could I bestow something when answering a prayer?*

His previous communication with the Tarot Club had only allowed him to transmit images and voices. That did not necessarily mean he could never transmit physical objects. Perhaps Sequence 8 would change his abilities.

But another possibility interested him more.

*Can objects from the real world be brought into the gray fog?*

He remembered that rituals involving evil gods and devils often included sacrifices.

*What if I sacrifice something to myself?*

If that worked, he might be able to move physical objects into the mysterious space, obtain items from Justice, The Sun, or The Hanged Man, and then bestow them to himself—or potentially use the same method to provide materials to others.

Unfortunately, sacrifice was a more advanced ritual technique that he did not yet understand.

For now, the answer was simple: he needed to become stronger.

The discussion eventually shifted from Qilangos to various monsters and their characteristics. After some time, Klein brought the gathering to an end.

“Let’s end it here for today.”

“By your will.”

Justice, The Hanged Man, and The Sun simultaneously rose as their connections were severed.

Klein immediately descended from the gray fog and returned to his room. He dismissed the spiritual wall and opened the curtains, letting sunlight pour through the window.

Two matters dominated his thoughts.

First, he needed to undergo his examination and advance to Sequence 8.

Second, he needed to create Flaring Sun Charms. Their power might exceed that of ordinary Sequence 7—or even Sequence 6—abilities.

Klein looked toward the bright world outside with anticipation.

Tomorrow, he should finally receive news about the paranormal disturbance Azik had created in Morse Town.
