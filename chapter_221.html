Chapter 221 - The Tarot Club at a Higher Level

565 words · 4 min read

The Blue Avenger rolled steadily across the Sonia Sea while Alger Wilson prepared himself for a ritual he had once never imagined possible. In the captain’s cabin, he carefully drew the mysterious symbol The Fool had given him, arranged the candles according to dualism, cleaned the altar, and surrounded the area with a sealing wall.

Then, enduring the pain caused by the ritual’s ancient language, Alger scattered the sesame-like granules from a dark bottle into the wind. The gale strengthened and divided into silver and black streams before merging into the candle representing The Fool. The flame suddenly burst open into an illusory door.

At the same moment, Klein sensed the disturbance above the gray fog. When he extended his spirituality, the blurry door behind his high-back chair slowly opened.

Alger saw through that door an indescribably mysterious scene: countless shadowy figures containing tremendous knowledge, the gray fog above them, and the ancient palace overlooking everything. Trembling with fear and excitement, he presented the Rainbow Salamander’s pituitary gland he had prepared. A sudden suction pulled the Beyonder material away.

Only after hearing The Fool’s voice say, “You did well,” did Alger dare look up. The door had vanished.

As he sat alone afterward, Alger couldn’t help thinking about how far The Fool had progressed. At first, The Fool could only pull people into the mysterious world. Later, he could respond to prayers. Now sacrifices and bestowments were possible. It seemed as though The Fool was gradually breaking free from some unseen predicament and approaching the real world.

The thought frightened Alger, yet it also brought him pride. He was one of the earliest members of the Tarot Club.

Above the gray fog, Klein examined the Rainbow Salamander’s pituitary gland with genuine satisfaction. He could already see how much more miraculous the Tarot Club might become.

Meanwhile, Audrey waited impatiently in her bedroom for news of the ritual. When Klein appeared before her, he told her the sacrifice had succeeded and asked whether she had prepared spiritual materials. She immediately began preparing her own ritual.

The illusory door opened before her, revealing the mysterious world beyond. Audrey felt intoxicated by the sight, viewing it as something beyond anything she had ever pursued. Then she noticed the reward waiting for her: a Rainbow Salamander’s pituitary gland.

After completing the ritual, she eagerly examined the material before returning to her room to prepare the potion. She drank the Telepathist potion and successfully endured the integration of the Beyonder characteristics, advancing in Sequence.

Her senses became clearer and her spirituality greatly increased. After stabilizing her Sequence through Cogitation, Audrey happily let Susie into the room, only to face the dog’s suspicion over how long she had been gone.

Audrey quickly changed the subject and asked Susie how she could secretly tell Xio and Fors about something without revealing her identity while still making them interested. Human and dog stared at each other and fell into thought together.

Afterward, Klein returned to reality and spent some time preparing for his detective career. He bought glasses, wigs, and various fake mustaches, then rented a small one-bedroom house in East Borough. The poverty and danger of the district left a deep impression on him.

Over the next two days he practiced using his spirituality to create charms and perform rituals. Then, on Thursday morning, his new career finally received its first visitor.

Key Events

  • Alger successfully performs the sacrificial ritual and sends the Rainbow Salamander’s pituitary gland to The Fool.
  • The illusory door allows Alger to glimpse the mysterious space above the gray fog, strengthening his awe toward The Fool.
  • Audrey performs her ritual, receives the pituitary gland, and successfully advances to the Telepathist Sequence.
  • Audrey immediately begins considering how to carry out The Fool’s new assignment involving Xio and Fors.
  • Klein prepares his detective identity, rents a room in East Borough, and begins making charms.
  • His preparations finally pay off when a client arrives at his door.
