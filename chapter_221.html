Chapter 221 - The Tarot Club at a Higher Level

1,078 words · 7 min read

The Blue Avenger rose and fell with the waves of the Sonia Sea, steady despite the restless water. In the captain’s cabin, Alger Wilson paced uneasily before finally returning to his desk. He drew the strange symbol The Fool had given him, then carefully prepared the sacrificial altar according to dualism principles.

He placed one candle above the combined symbol of the Pupil-less Eye and twisted lines, another representing himself, then cleansed the altar and surrounded the entire arrangement with a wall of spirituality. After lighting the candles, Alger lowered his head and began reciting in ancient Hermes:

“The Fool that doesn’t belong to this era,

You are the mysterious ruler above the gray fog;

You are the King of Yellow and Black who wields good luck.

Your devoted servant prays for your attention,

I pray for you to take his offerings.

I pray for you to open the gates to your Kingdom.”

The ancient incantation stirred the wind violently around him. Enduring the pain as though blades were cutting his skin, Alger opened a dark-brown bottle and scattered its metallic, sesame-like granules into the gale.

The wind intensified, then separated into silver and black streams. They collided and intertwined before pouring into the candle flame representing The Fool. An illusory door slowly formed, bearing the same mysterious symbol.

Above the gray fog, Klein immediately sensed the disturbance. A hazy door had appeared behind The Fool’s high-backed chair, and the surrounding spirituality was agitated by the ritual. He instinctively extended his spirituality into it.

Creak.

The indistinct door slowly opened.

On the other side, Alger saw an extraordinary sight: endless, barely visible shadows filled with vast knowledge beneath the dense gray fog, while the ancient palace seemed to overlook reality itself. The spectacle caused him to tremble with both fear and excitement.

He hurriedly lifted the mature Rainbow Salamander’s pituitary gland toward the door. The object was palm-sized, soft and continuously shifting in color. A sudden suction pulled it away, leaving Alger’s hands empty.

Only after The Fool’s deep voice sounded did he dare raise his head.

“You did well.”

“It’s my honor,” Alger replied.

The door vanished, the gale stopped, and the candle flames returned to normal. Once the ritual ended, Alger sat quietly, troubled by his thoughts.

At first, The Fool could only pull people into the mysterious space. Then He learned to respond to prayers. Now sacrifices and bestowments were possible. Alger could not escape the conclusion that The Fool was gradually freeing Himself from some great restriction and might eventually descend into the real world.

The possibility frightened him, yet he also felt a strange sense of pride.

At least I’m a member of the Tarot Club—one of the earliest members...

Above the gray fog, Klein held the Rainbow Salamander’s pituitary gland and watched its shifting colors reflect across his face. The faint tingling in his palm filled him with accomplishment, and he smiled sincerely.

In the future, the Tarot Club would become even more miraculous.

He then sent his will toward Audrey.

Back in her bedroom, Audrey had been unable to sit still. She alternated between reading books, staring into mirrors, and anxiously waiting for news. She desperately hoped the sacrificial experiment would succeed, yet she feared failure.

Eventually, gray fog filled her vision, and The Fool appeared on His familiar chair.

“Miss Justice, the attempt was successful. Have you prepared the materials that contain spirituality?”

“That’s great! As expected of Mr. Fool!”

Audrey immediately confirmed that she had the necessary materials. Klein then asked when she wished to perform the ritual, emphasizing that there could be no other Beyonders nearby.

“I can do it now.”

Klein taught her the modified prayer:

“Your devoted servant prays for your attention,

I pray for you to open the gates to your Kingdom.

I pray for you to give me strength.”

She also had to follow the dualism method.

Audrey eagerly began. When the illusory door appeared, revealing a mysterious scene deeper and more unreal than a starry sky, she was intoxicated by the experience. This was the mystical world she had always yearned for.

She then noticed something resting upon the altar: the Rainbow Salamander’s pituitary gland.

Her eyes lit up, and she nearly rushed forward to grab it, but her etiquette held her back. After expressing her gratitude to The Fool, she completed the ritual and carefully examined the material several times.

Our Tarot Club is at a higher level than all the other secret organizations...

Audrey wasted no time. Soon she had prepared the Telepathist potion, a shimmering liquid whose shifting brilliance seemed capable of illuminating the depths of anyone’s heart.

She drank it confidently.

The integration of the Beyonder Characteristics went smoothly. Audrey successfully advanced to Sequence 8 Telepathist. Her senses sharpened, her perceptions seemed clearer, and she immediately used Cogitation to restrain her scattered spirituality until her Sequence stabilized.

She then opened the bedroom door and allowed Susie inside.

The golden retriever looked suspiciously at her.

“You took far longer than usual.”

Audrey laughed awkwardly before changing the subject. She asked Susie how she could secretly inform Xio and Fors about a particular matter without exposing herself, while still making them curious enough to investigate.

Before she finished, she had already begun seriously thinking about Mr. Fool’s assignment.

Audrey and Susie looked at each other.

Both fell into thought simultaneously.

After accomplishing his objectives above the gray fog, Klein returned to reality and slept for a little over an hour before leaving the house. He spent one pound on gold-rimmed glasses, wigs, and removable mustaches, preparing a collection of disguises.

Before dinner, he traveled to East Borough, Backlund’s most crowded and dangerous district. There he rented a small one-bedroom house for four soli and three pence per week, paying two weeks’ rent and a deposit totaling seventeen soli.

Only then were his initial preparations complete.

East Borough deeply affected Klein. Vast areas resembled Tingen’s Lower Street, but the poverty-stricken territory was many times larger. Residents wore worn clothing, their sallow faces and thin bodies reflecting hunger and hardship. The miserable conditions allowed gangs to flourish.

Returning to Cherwood Borough made Klein feel as though he had stepped from hell into heaven.

For the next two days, he experimented with using his spirituality to perform rituals and create charms. He stopped praying to the Goddess and simply waited for the results of his advertisement.

Then, on Thursday morning, the doorbell finally rang.

Key Events

  • Alger successfully performed The Fool’s sacrificial ritual and transferred the mature Rainbow Salamander’s pituitary gland through the illusory door.
  • Alger concluded that The Fool was gradually overcoming His mysterious restrictions.
  • Klein successfully received the Beyonder material and sent it to Audrey through a bestowment ritual.
  • Audrey advanced successfully from Sequence 9 Spectator to Sequence 8 Telepathist.
  • Audrey began planning how to discreetly bring Xio and Fors into contact with The Fool’s name.
  • Klein bought multiple disguises and rented a safe house in the impoverished East Borough.
  • After two days of preparation and experimentation, Klein finally received a visitor at 15 Minsk Street.
