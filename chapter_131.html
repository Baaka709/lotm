Chapter 131

1,204 words · 8 min read

Audrey looked at Mr. A with mild superiority. Compared with The Fool, he seemed almost ordinary to her; only gods or demigods were worthy of being compared with Mr. Fool.

She quietly asked Fors and Xio, “Are there any stories about this man?”

Xio answered seriously, “There have been several incidents. Sequence 8 Beyonders, and even some Sequence 7s, have tried to deal with Mr. A. Every one of them mysteriously disappeared.”

“So he truly is powerful,” Glaint murmured.

Once they entered the gathering, the guards shut the door behind them. Under the gas lamps, Audrey noticed two blackboards covered with anonymous requests and items for sale.

Fors explained that many participants hid their identities and possessions to avoid becoming targets. They simply listed what they wanted or wished to sell, together with approximate prices.

Audrey scanned the left board.

A mature Manhal Fish’s eyes. Dust left by vengeful spirits for 165 pounds. Three pages of Emperor Roselle’s notebook for 20 pounds.

Her eyes widened.

These prices are too cheap!

She continued reading.

“Tears of an Infant flower, 200 pounds.”

“Mummy Powder, 10 grams, 5 pounds.”

“Fishman Secretion, 30 ml, 29 pounds.”

“Formula for Sequence 8 potion Sheriff, 450 pounds.”

Nearly every Beyonder ingredient here was cheaper than 300 pounds.

Audrey could barely contain her excitement. Xio leaned toward her.

“Do you have anything you want?”

Audrey immediately remembered one of Roselle’s famous declarations: I want it all!

Despite having two elder brothers and little chance of inheriting the family title directly, Audrey was extraordinarily wealthy. Properties, farmland, pastures, mines, jewelry, stocks, and bonds assigned to her were worth around 300,000 pounds. Though much of it remained inaccessible until her father died or she married, her trust fund still provided her with roughly 15,000 to 25,000 pounds every year, making her one of Loen’s wealthiest noblewomen.

She restrained herself.

“For now, I have my sights on Emperor Roselle’s notebook. I admire him, and I believe the strange characters and writings he created contain some hidden significance.”

Audrey secretly felt that she was becoming more and more hypocritical.

A young man nearby suddenly stood up excitedly.

“Yes! I completely agree! I finally found someone who thinks the same way. I have the three pages of the notebook, and I can sell them to you now!”

Audrey was momentarily stunned, then smiled.

“Then please allow me to express my gratitude.”

She handed him two ten-pound notes and received the three pages. After checking them, she confirmed that the handwriting matched the Roselle pages she had previously obtained.

She then quietly asked Xio, “Who should I look for if these notes are fake? Mr. A?”

“Yes. Mr. A won’t permit fraud during his gathering. I can also help mediate privately.”

“I understand.”

Audrey entered her Spectator state and calmly observed the room.

Many people were now looking toward her and Glaint after seeing the transaction. Their hoods concealed their identities well, however.

The furnishings were ordinary sofas and chairs facing the blackboards. The setting suggested that Mr. A was not a noble and cared little for appearances. Given his confidence, he hardly needed an impressive venue.

Then Audrey studied Mr. A himself.

He repeatedly looked at the women present, especially those considered attractive.

He’s lecherous... Why does he keep looking at me? Can he see through my robe?

For an instant, Audrey felt disgusted.

But she soon realized that his eyes were not actually seeing through the clothing. His vision simply seemed unusually sharp, as though he could observe people from extremely close range. Her disguise would still hide her features, but she understood that she could not depend entirely on the hood.

A facilitator eventually approached their group and explained that requests could be submitted on paper or written on the blackboard during the break.

Fors then asked Audrey, “Have you decided which Sequence 9 formula you want?”

Since Fors had already told Audrey and Glaint about all the pathways she knew, Audrey had to decide how much to reveal.

“Spectator,” she said. “I want to become a Spectator, and I also want the advancement formula for Telepathist.”

She knew she would likely encounter Fors and Xio frequently in the future and that they might eventually discover she was a Beyonder. Revealing herself as a Spectator while completely hiding the Tarot Club seemed like the safest choice.

At the same time, Audrey noticed Xio repeatedly staring at the blackboard, her expression full of longing and frustration.

Xio had previously told her that the Sequence 8 advancement of Arbiter was Sheriff, whose formula was priced at 450 pounds.

Audrey quickly connected the clues. Xio had been an Arbiter for more than a year and should already have digested her potion through acting. Yet she obviously lacked the money to advance.

So Xio is short on money.

Glaint then announced his own choice.

“Apothecary. I want the Sequence 9 Apothecary formula.”

When everyone looked at him, he laughed.

“To me, health and avoiding serious illness are the most important things.”

“A rational decision. I once dreamed of becoming an Apothecary,” Fors said with a languid smile.

Once the choices were made, Audrey and the others wrote their requests on slips of paper and handed them to the facilitator. He gathered everyone’s requests, shuffled them, and passed them to another facilitator, who copied them onto the blackboards.

“I need the formulas for Spectator and Telepathist. The price will be negotiated face-to-face...”

The announcement was repeated three times. Interested sellers could privately request a room where facilitators would help complete the transaction.

But no one immediately responded to Audrey or Glaint.

Then a folded note arrived for Audrey.

“It’s from Mr. A,” the facilitator whispered.

The message asked whether Audrey was interested in other Sequence 9 formulas.

She wrote back coldly, “I am only interested in Spectator.”

Mr. A read her response without comment.

Yet Audrey noticed him secretly burn the note and let the ashes fall to the floor.

Fifteen minutes later, Mr. A announced, “Now we will have a break. You can interact with the other participants freely.”

The young man who had sold Audrey Roselle’s pages immediately approached her.

“I’ve deciphered some of Emperor Roselle’s special characters and tattooed them onto my body. They’ve given me remarkable abilities. Are you interested?”

Audrey remembered asking Mr. Fool whether the strange symbols possessed special powers. Mr. Fool had told her they were useless unless some deity suddenly took an interest in them.

Still, she asked, “What remarkable abilities?”

The young man proudly answered, “I’ve become stronger and healthier!”

Audrey looked at him sympathetically.

“I’m sorry, but I have more confidence in my own research.”

During the remainder of the gathering, Audrey continued observing the participants. She learned little beyond some vague impressions that several of them had ordinary professions such as doctors or lawyers.

After another half hour, she and Glaint left with Fors and Xio and returned to Viscount Glaint’s mansion, waiting until the ball ended.

Audrey finally returned home around ten that night. She was about to ask Annie to prepare hot water when Susie, her dog, suddenly shot her a meaningful look.

Audrey froze.

My dog just gave me a look...

Her emotions instantly became complicated.
