Chapter 198 - Appropriating Uniqueness

1,074 words · 7 min read

The silver Slumber Charm suddenly became ice-cold, its surface seeming to grow a layer of frost. Klein shivered, but the shock also snapped him out of his fear and agitation. He quickly poured his spirituality into the charm and pushed it from his pocket.

A crimson flame burst into the air amid a series of faint explosions, and a profound, serene sensation spread across the bedroom. The Slumber Charm did not distinguish between friend and foe. Ordinarily, one would throw it at the enemy and remain outside the strongest part of its effect, but Klein’s arms were trapped by countless invisible threads. He had no choice but to let himself fall asleep alongside Madam Sharon and Kenley.

Yet Klein had prepared for this possibility. His body possessed a unique quality unlike that of ordinary Low-Sequence Beyonders: whenever an ability involving dreams or hypnosis was used on him, he could remain consciously aware of himself. He had already discovered this through Dunn’s Nightmare abilities and Daly’s spirit channeling.

The instant his eyes closed, he knew he was dreaming.

Kacha!

Klein forcibly tore himself out of the dream and awoke. The invisible threads binding his body had loosened. Madam Sharon was still dazed, while Kenley lay on the floor with the Spirit Medium Mirror overturned nearby and his revolver thrown toward the door.

An opportunity!

Klein snapped his fingers, producing a faint blue spiritual flame that burned through the threads restraining him. At the same time, he drew his revolver and fired twice.

Bang! Bang!

Two silver demon-hunting bullets flew toward Madam Sharon. Klein did not stop to check their effect. He leaped toward Kenley, tearing free of the remaining threads. The gunshots were partly meant to alert Dunn that the situation had gone wrong, though Klein naturally hoped the bullets would hurt Sharon as well.

They did not.

The bullets struck Madam Sharon’s body, but her figure shattered like the reflection of the crimson moon on disturbed water. The full-length mirror beside her cracked apart, scattering numerous fragments shaped strangely like palms.

A substitute? A Beyonder ability from the Demoness pathway?

Before Klein could think further, Madam Sharon vanished.

Then the unconscious-looking Kenley suddenly grabbed his own throat.

His fingers tightened until saliva dripped from his mouth and his tongue protruded. Yet Klein’s Spirit Vision showed nothing around him. He immediately remembered the Spirit Medium Mirror’s most dangerous warning: the greatest danger came when one saw oneself.

Could Kenley have seen his own reflection in the mirror through Sharon’s full-length mirror?

With no time to hesitate, Klein pulled out a triangular silver charm.

“Crimson!”

He infused the Requiem Charm with spirituality and threw it. At the same time, he pressed his hand down and grabbed the Spirit Medium Mirror, carefully ensuring that its surface was facing the floor rather than himself.

Icy-blue flames bloomed as the charm released a quiet, tranquil darkness. The violent emotions around Kenley dissipated, and his grip relaxed. Klein himself also entered an intensely peaceful state, as though he were standing at the oriel window of his own home and gazing down upon a silent street.

Then his Clown instincts gave him a warning.

Madam Sharon is going to attack my right waist!

Klein immediately rolled left. A dagger wrapped in black flames stabbed into the place where he had just been. Madam Sharon reappeared.

While rolling, Klein raised the Spirit Medium Mirror and aimed it at her.

This had been part of his plan from the beginning. He had approached Kenley not merely to rescue him, but to recover the Sealed Artifact. He had anticipated waiting for Dunn’s reinforcement would be too dangerous, and the Flaring Sun Charm would not be especially useful against a living Beyonder like Sharon. If necessary, he was even prepared to risk using Azik’s copper whistle.

Fortunately, Sharon chose to assassinate rather than interrupt him.

Klein deliberately used the serenity of the Requiem Charm to strengthen his Clown’s battle foresight. Then, after avoiding her attack, he immediately used the Spirit Medium Mirror to counterattack.

The cracked mirror rippled.

A black-haired woman emerged from its surface, her face hidden by thick hair. Klein tilted the mirror so that its reflective side faced upward. A pale hand extended from within, followed by a woman dressed in a white, sheet-like garment who sprang toward Sharon.

Madam Sharon’s expression darkened. Seven black flames ignited around her.

One flame shot toward the woman.

Whoosh!

The woman caught fire, screamed, and vanished.

The remaining flames immediately flew toward Klein. He rolled desperately, but fine invisible threads began wrapping around him once again, gradually slowing his movements. The ability was almost a perfect counter to his Clown-like agility.

The black flames missed him and landed on the bed without burning it, apparently affecting only living targets or objects containing spirituality.

Klein had barely registered his success when another premonition struck him. He twisted his body and changed his intended forward roll into a sideways one.

An instant later, a transparent ice spear pierced the carpet exactly where he would have landed.

Frost rapidly spread outward, striking Klein despite his altered movement. His body stiffened, and although he could still move, his actions became much slower.

Madam Sharon once again surrounded herself with black flames while condensing another transparent ice spear. Klein reached into his pocket and gripped Azik’s copper whistle.

Before he could use it, Kenley finally awoke fully.

His expression was blank and unsettling, a deep shadow covering his face. He suddenly lunged toward Sharon.

Thud! Thud! Thud!

Madam Sharon sent black flames toward him.

They vanished against Kenley like snowflakes and failed to injure him.

Klein immediately understood that something about Kenley had changed. He raised his revolver and fired.

Bang!

Sharon dodged before the shot arrived and hurled the frost spear at Kenley. It pierced his clothing without entering his skin, leaving him unaffected by its freezing power.

Klein fired again.

Sharon slipped behind the broken full-length mirror, seized a palm-sized shard, and continued avoiding the bullets. She then used the fragment to reflect Kenley as he charged toward her.

A heartbeat later, she swiped the fragment with a black-flame-covered palm.

Klein’s revolver was empty. He threw it aside and rolled toward Kenley’s fallen weapon.

Then he heard a horrible scream.

Kenley stopped in mid-motion and bent over, vomiting violently.

First came bile.

Then a red heart.

Then his lungs and stomach, all of them burning with black flames.

Key Events

  • Klein uses the Slumber Charm despite being trapped, relying on his unique resistance to dream and hypnotic effects to awaken consciously.
  • He breaks free of the invisible threads and fires at Madam Sharon, only to discover that she used a substitute and escaped the bullets.
  • Klein realizes Kenley is being affected by the Spirit Medium Mirror after seeing his own reflection and uses a Requiem Charm to calm him.
  • Using the Requiem’s serenity to enhance his Clown foresight, Klein predicts Sharon’s assassination attempt and successfully uses the Spirit Medium Mirror against her.
  • Sharon counters with black flames, invisible threads, ice attacks, mirror fragments, and other Demoness abilities, steadily restricting Klein’s mobility.
  • Kenley regains consciousness and attacks Sharon, seemingly unaffected by her black flames.
  • Sharon uses a mirror fragment against Kenley, causing his internal organs to be violently expelled while burning with black flames.
