Chapter 261 - The Innermost Room

631 words · 4 min read

After leaving the room containing the statue of the Evernight Goddess, Klein kept his left hand free, ready to retrieve his charms, tarot cards, and Azik’s copper whistle at the slightest sign of danger.

The next door bore a different symbol.

A baby surrounded by wheat, flowers, and spring water.

“The Sacred Emblem of Mother Earth,” Klein said solemnly.

He opened the door and discovered another huge chamber designed around a giant statue.

A beautiful, plump woman stood four or five meters tall. Wheat grew around her feet, spring water surrounded her, and herbs, flowers, and animals adorned her dress. She held a swaddled infant against her chest.

It was clearly a statue of Mother Earth.

They left the room and inspected the four doors on the right.

Behind them were the Sacred Emblems and statues of the remaining orthodox gods: the Lord of Storms, the Eternal Blazing Sun, the God of Combat, and the God of Knowledge and Wisdom.

Each was represented by a humanoid statue.

Klein was stunned.

Apart from the God of Steam and Machinery, all six of the other major orthodox gods seemed to be represented in this enormous ancient hall.

How could the churches of these six true gods possibly have allowed their deities to share a single structure?

Was this simply a Fourth Epoch custom?

And why had the gods been represented as human-like figures rather than the abstract symbols commonly used today?

Klein’s thoughts wandered into increasingly strange possibilities.

Miss Bodyguard ignored his questions and pointed out the remaining central door.

Klein immediately became cautious. The central door was likely connected to the core of the structure and therefore represented the greatest danger.

“What are your thoughts on that area?”

Miss Bodyguard closed her eyes and sensed the surroundings.

“Very dangerous.

“But the danger is subdued.

“Once you venture deeper, don’t touch anything.”

Subdued danger.

Klein wondered whether something had been sealed inside.

They approached the central doorway.

The deeper they went, the darker and heavier the atmosphere became. After roughly thirty steps, the passage was blocked by rubble. Another path had completely collapsed.

Miss Bodyguard passed through the obstruction and returned with a simple conclusion.

“It’s completely collapsed.”

That left only one possible route.

Klein found a narrow opening in the remaining stone door and peered through it. Spirit Vision immediately revealed at least four powerful spiritual lights—two dark-gold and two dark-blue.

Inside lay piles of bones covered with rotten clothes.

Several of the remains still emitted powerful spiritual light.

Condensed Beyonder characteristics?

Mystical items?

At the far end stood a pair of bloody black doors, streaked with what looked like fresh blood.

Before Klein could investigate further, Azik’s copper whistle suddenly became freezing cold.

A profound sense of death surged through him.

Then his right forearm went numb and began swelling.

Klein instinctively pulled out a tarot card and slashed his arm.

Instead of blood, tiny black worms poured from the wound.

They writhed across the floor before sizzling and dissolving under the lantern light.

Klein quickly stabilized the wound.

Miss Bodyguard’s expression became unusually serious.

Yet the whistle remained cold.

Then Klein saw something impossible.

Miss Bodyguard had a shadow.

She had never possessed one before.

“Run!”

The two immediately fled.

A black shadow began swallowing the lantern’s light behind them. It expanded rapidly, becoming thicker and closer while Klein raced toward the hall.

He hurled himself forward and rolled across the threshold.

The moment he reached the main hall, the firelight brightened. The terrifying sensation vanished, and Azik’s whistle returned to normal.

Miss Bodyguard stared into the darkness.

“Evil spirit...”

Klein’s heart tightened.

In the mystical world, evil spirits were terrifying beings. The strongest could reach the level of High-Sequence Beyonders.

And one was apparently imprisoned inside this ancient structure.
