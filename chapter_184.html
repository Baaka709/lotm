Chapter 184

Chapter 184 - Behind the Gate

1,020 words · 6 min read

Klein met Dunn in the Captain’s office, where Dunn was eating breakfast. Klein reported that Daxter Guderian, the asylum doctor and Psychology Alchemists member, had revealed a critical name: Lanevus had repeatedly visited Hood Eugen before the Psychiatrist went insane.

Dunn initially struggled to remember Lanevus until Klein reminded him that he was the swindler who had cheated victims out of more than ten thousand pounds. Klein explained the scam in detail: Lanevus had falsely claimed to have discovered and purchased a highly profitable iron mine, collected investments from people in Tingen, and disappeared with their money. Someone from Klein’s Divination Club had been among the victims, while another young woman had been deceived into an engagement and was now pregnant with Lanevus’s child.

Dunn quickly connected Lanevus to the Marauder pathway’s Sequence 8, Swindler. Klein admitted that he had reached the same conclusion.

The case had ultimately fallen outside the Nighthawks’ jurisdiction because Lanevus’s alleged steelworks operation was in the south and his victims belonged to different faiths. Any confirmed Beyonder involvement would instead have been handled by the Mandated Punishers.

Klein then stated his intention: he wanted to perform a mediumship ritual on Hood Eugen to discover why Lanevus had sought him out and whether that encounter was connected to Hood Eugen’s descent into insanity.

Dunn agreed that such an investigation would eventually be necessary, but warned Klein that Daly had emphasized the danger. He offered to request assistance from the Backlund diocese and postpone the attempt.

Klein refused to give up the opportunity. He was confident in his knowledge of mediumship and requested several preparations: Amantha extract, Eye of the Spirit medicine, and especially the Sedative Agent.

Dunn recognized the seriousness of the request and approved it. Klein could submit the necessary application and retrieve the preparations from behind Chanis Gate. If a finished Sedative Agent was unavailable, he could obtain its ingredients and concoct it himself.

Dunn then explained how they would approach Hood Eugen. He happened to be responsible for monitoring the asylum that evening, but they could not simply walk in. Members of the Psychology Alchemists might be disguised as doctors, nurses, cleaners, or patients, and the organization might also be watching Hood Eugen themselves. They therefore had to keep the operation secret to avoid exposing Daxter as their informant.

The plan was to infiltrate the asylum at dawn, with Dunn standing guard while Klein performed the ritual.

Klein felt reassured. If Hood Eugen was secretly pretending to be insane, using a mediumship ritual on him would be extraordinarily dangerous.

After filling out his request on the Akerson Model 1346 typewriter and obtaining Dunn’s signature and stamp, Klein descended into the basement. Only then did he realize that this would be his first time venturing beyond Chanis Gate.

He arrived at the massive black gate and handed his authorization to Seeka Tron for registration. After she signed the document, Klein knocked. A hollow echo came from the other side.

Within half a minute, the Chanis Gate opened.

An elderly Nighthawk guard wearing a black robe and carrying a barn lantern stood inside. His icy blue eyes and expressionless face were illuminated by the dim light. Klein recognized him as one of the aging Nighthawks who voluntarily guarded the sealed area.

After inspecting Klein’s authorization, the old guard allowed him through.

The moment Klein entered, an unnatural chill swept over him—not the ordinary cold of winter, but a sensation that seemed to make his very spirituality shiver.

Inside, silver candles with blue flames illuminated a broad ancient-stone walkway. Doors on either side were labeled with sections such as “Ingredients,” “Medicine,” and “Information.” A staircase at the far end descended into darkness and presumably connected to deeper sealed areas containing dangerous Sealed Artifacts.

Klein activated Spirit Vision.

The entire interior was covered with countless fine black lines, swaying and intertwining so densely that there appeared to be no gaps anywhere.

He immediately understood that these were likely the sealing forces behind Chanis Gate.

Under the escort of the old guard, Klein entered the Medicine Room. Following the alphabetized storage system, he quickly located Amantha extract, Eye of the Spirit medicine, and the Sedative Agent.

The Sedative Agent was contained in a translucent glass bottle filled with blue liquid. Strangely, looking at it gave Klein a sensation resembling being held in a mother’s embrace. Fortunately, the medicine was still within its expiration date.

Klein collected three small bottles and returned to the gate with the guard. As soon as he emerged, he felt relieved to escape the pervasive cold, oppressive sealing power, and supernatural sensation within.

Looking back at the gate, he wondered whether prolonged exposure would damage both body and soul. He finally understood why those guards had to volunteer for the duty rather than being permanently assigned there.

...

At dawn, Klein secretly locked his bedroom, opened the oriel window, and jumped down from the second floor. His improved body allowed him to land flawlessly.

Dunn’s carriage was waiting across the street.

Without unnecessary conversation, they traveled to Tingen Asylum. Dunn had chosen a dark, unlit corner as their entry point and confirmed that no one was nearby.

The two Nighthawks infiltrated the asylum using their enhanced physical abilities. Klein’s Clown-like balance and agility allowed them to get over the wall quickly and quietly.

They crossed the hospital grounds, entered the three-story building, and reached the top floor where Hood Eugen was confined.

Because Hood Eugen was considered potentially violent, he had been given a private room. The Nighthawks had previously copied the key during their surveillance, allowing Dunn to open the door without raising suspicion.

Inside, Hood Eugen sat on his bed.

His face was long and gaunt, his eye sockets deeply sunken, and his blond hair disheveled. He stared through the barred window at the crimson moon hanging outside.

Klein quietly closed the door and casually asked, “Why aren’t you sleeping?”

Dunn remembered that Klein was now a Sequence 8 Clown and stepped aside, leaving the interaction to him.

Hood Eugen turned toward Klein, chuckled foolishly, and answered:

“I’m waiting for my cake.”
