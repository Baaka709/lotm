Chapter 190 - The Assortment of Abilities

806 words · 5 min read

Inside Duke Negan’s mansion, Qilangos stood disguised as Baron Gramir, casually watching the ball while secretly studying his real target: Duke Pallas Negan.

The Duke was the Conservative Party’s most influential supporter, the brother of the Prime Minister, and one of the richest and most powerful men in Loen. More importantly, Qilangos had accepted the assassination because the reward was extraordinary. Success would make his fame spread across the Northern and Southern Continents, potentially surpassing even the Four Kings, and would earn him a mysterious card containing one of the secrets created by Emperor Roselle.

Qilangos’s confidence came largely from Creeping Hunger. The artifact had allowed him to rise to the rank of Pirate Admiral despite being only Sequence 6, although it was significantly weaker than a genuine Shepherd. It switched souls more slowly, each soul could only use one to three abilities before being exhausted, Qilangos could not choose exactly which abilities appeared, and the artifact could only contain five souls rather than a Shepherd’s seven.

Still, Qilangos had spent years carefully selecting and adjusting his five souls so their abilities complemented one another. This had led to his reputation as an “omnipotent” pirate.

Unfortunately, he had failed to locate the mysterious Traveler he had been searching for. If he had captured that person, he would have gladly sacrificed one of his existing souls to obtain the Traveler’s abilities.

For the assassination, Qilangos was currently using the soul responsible for changing appearances. It offered little combat ability, but its transformation power was too valuable to discard. Regardless of which soul he controlled, however, he could still use his own Wind-blessed abilities.

Qilangos studied Duke Negan’s surroundings and identified the likely Beyonder guarding him. He reasoned that Negan was a devoted follower of the Lord of Storms and too important a political figure to be left unprotected.

He then made his move.

Qilangos summoned a powerful gust over the dance floor and used concealed wind blades to sever the chain supporting the enormous crystal chandelier. The chandelier crashed down, plunging the hall into chaos while injuring and frightening numerous guests.

In the darkness, Creeping Hunger transformed, and Qilangos targeted the mysterious protector beside Duke Negan. A burst of his power struck the man directly, causing the protector to scream and collapse while clutching his head.

Qilangos immediately charged toward Duke Negan.

But Negan did not panic.

He calmly raised his hand and spoke an ancient Hermes command:

“Imprison!”

A transparent barrier suddenly formed around Qilangos, trapping him like an insect sealed in amber.

Negan followed with another command.

“Flog!”

Invisible lashes struck Qilangos repeatedly, tearing his clothing and cutting his flesh down to the bone.

Then the Duke declared:

“Death!”

His fist slammed forward with numerous afterimages and struck Qilangos’s head with crushing force.

Qilangos’s head shattered.

But so did the entire scene.

Duke Negan had merely destroyed an illusion.

Qilangos had already switched his active ability and dragged the Duke into a Nightmare-like state while retaining the ability to move his physical body. He appeared behind Negan and drove a wind-wrapped fist directly through his chest.

His fist pierced the Duke’s heart.

Yet the figure immediately became transparent and dissipated like a summoned spirit.

Duke Negan then appeared safely beside a French door on the opposite side of the corridor, watching Qilangos with a probing smile.

There was another Beyonder.

Qilangos finally understood that the authorities had anticipated his attack and prepared an ambush specifically for him.

Although shocked, he did not lose his composure.

Creeping Hunger transformed again. Golden scales appeared across his left hand, his eyes became pale with vertical pupils, and a terrifying wave of power swept across the mansion.

Guests were overwhelmed with uncontrollable fear. They fled their hiding places and ran aimlessly, creating complete chaos.

The defenders could not immediately attack because doing so might injure their own relatives and friends.

Qilangos seized the opportunity.

He moved like a hurricane, smashed through a guest-room door, burst through an oriel window, and escaped the mansion on the wind. Once outside, he immediately ran toward a nearby municipal forest he had previously selected as an escape route.

If he could shake off his pursuers, he could change his appearance again and disappear into Backlund’s enormous population.

That had been the foundation of his confidence in accepting such an audacious assassination mission.

But help soon arrived.

A powerful gale swept toward Duke Negan’s mansion as Ace Snake, the Archbishop of Backlund and “Spellsinger of God” of the Church of the Lord of Storms, arrived with several Mandated Punishers.

Unfortunately, he had not arrived quickly enough to stop the escape.

Among those who reached the mansion was Alger.

When Alger saw the shattered windows and the Beyonders and guests streaming out of the building, his mood immediately sank.

Rear Admiral Hurricane Qilangos had escaped.

Key Events

  • Qilangos reveals that he infiltrated Duke Negan’s ball to assassinate Duke Pallas Negan in exchange for an enormous reward and a mysterious Roselle-created card.
  • Creeping Hunger’s limitations are explained: it can hold five souls, switches abilities more slowly than a true Shepherd, and gives Qilangos less control over which powers each soul provides.
  • Qilangos uses the appearance-changing soul to infiltrate the ball while retaining his own Wind-blessed abilities.
  • He sabotages the crystal chandelier, creating chaos and using the distraction to attack Duke Negan’s protector.
  • Duke Negan is revealed to be a powerful Beyonder capable of using “Imprison,” “Flog,” and “Death.”
  • Qilangos survives by using a Nightmare-like ability and attempts to kill Duke Negan, only to discover that the Duke’s apparent body was an illusion or spiritual projection.
  • Qilangos unleashes a fear-inducing ability to prevent the defenders from attacking freely, then escapes through a window and flees toward a municipal forest.
  • Archbishop Ace Snake and the Mandated Punishers arrive after the attack, but Qilangos has already escaped.
  • Alger arrives at the mansion and realizes with disappointment that Rear Admiral Hurricane has successfully escaped.
