Chapter 192

Chapter 192 - Attention

1,064 words · 7 min read

Audrey rapidly pieced together the events surrounding Qilangos’s death. She reasoned that Qilangos trusted no one and would have told no one about his assassination plan. She herself had only informed her father and Mr. Fool that Baron Gramir was suspicious. Although the mansion possessed a telegraph and could theoretically have called for assistance, Count Hall’s confusion suggested that the person who killed Qilangos had not been expected by the defenders.

Everything pointed toward one conclusion: Mr. Fool’s mysterious adorer had killed Qilangos.

The killer’s ability to eliminate a Sequence 6 Wind-blessed carrying Creeping Hunger so quickly suggested either a High-Sequence Beyonder or an extremely dangerous Sealed Artifact. Either possibility demonstrated just how powerful the force behind Mr. Fool was.

Audrey was delighted by another realization. She had provided the key information leading to Qilangos’s death, meaning the Hanged Man should fulfill his promise and give her the Rainbow Salamander’s pituitary gland. Their first major Tarot Club undertaking had effectively resulted in the death of one of the Seven Pirate Admirals.

Count Hall, however, immediately warned Audrey not to involve herself in the matter any further. Her curiosity about mysticism was usually tolerated, but encountering a terrifying Beyonder or hidden organization was entirely different. With the Queen preparing to introduce Audrey into Backlund’s highest social circles, she needed to understand that such powers represented genuine danger.

Audrey obediently agreed, while privately thinking that she understood the situation better than her father.

Count Hall then revealed another astonishing development.

Audrey was effectively the heroine of the evening and partly responsible for Qilangos’s death. Her contribution could earn her half of the bounty—ten thousand pounds. If nobody else claimed the remaining half, she could potentially receive the entire amount. On top of Loen’s reward, bounties offered by Intis, Feysac, and other countries could bring the total to roughly twenty thousand pounds.

Duke Negan had also promised her his holiday estate at Desi Bay, complete with a large rubber plantation.

Audrey was stunned by the scale of the rewards. Despite already possessing a substantial inheritance, tens of thousands of pounds remained an enormous sum.

Then she realized the problem: publicly receiving the bounty would expose her identity as Justice to Alger, destroying one of the Tarot Club’s most important advantages—mystery.

Audrey immediately adopted a worried expression and told her father that she feared Qilangos’s allies or whoever had commissioned the assassination might retaliate against her.

Count Hall agreed to hire additional protection. He also decided that Duke Negan should keep Audrey’s role secret and arrange for someone else to officially receive the bounty, with Audrey being compensated privately.

He jokingly praised his daughter for making forty thousand pounds so easily, saying she had earned more than a tenth of her existing wealth.

When Audrey asked whether she was as capable as he had been at her age, Count Hall laughed and admitted that she was much better. His own first business venture had only earned sixty pounds.

Audrey’s happiness surged. She had helped complete an extraordinary mission, earned her father’s praise, contributed to Qilangos’s death, expected the Rainbow Salamander ingredient from Alger, and stood to receive an enormous financial reward.

Yet she quickly decided not to report the full situation to Mr. Fool. A mysterious High-Sequence Beyonder had killed Qilangos, and someone might be watching for clues about the incident. Showing unusual behavior could expose her.

She also concluded that Mr. Fool’s adorer would already know the outcome if he really was the killer, making another report unnecessary.

As for the money, Audrey reasoned that transferring the bounty to the mysterious adorer would be too conspicuous. The Hanged Man should bear the responsibility for the promised reward anyway, especially since he had promised access to more of Roselle’s diary.

Audrey decided to gather additional diary pages instead as a more appropriate way to repay Mr. Fool.

...

In a secret study within Duke Negan’s mansion, the Duke, Archbishop Ace Snake, Prime Minister Aguesid, and other senior figures discussed Qilangos’s death.

Ace Snake explained that they still could not identify the High-Sequence Beyonder responsible. The killer was not one of the known High-Sequence Beyonders or a member of the Numinous Episcopate. They therefore had to assume that an unknown and extremely powerful Beyonder was operating in Backlund, though the possibility of a dangerous Sealed Artifact remained.

Duke Negan suggested that a hidden organization might stand behind the killer. The assassin had somehow predicted Qilangos’s movements accurately enough to intercept him after the attack, and one of the ball’s guests might even have belonged to such an organization.

Prime Minister Aguesid emphasized that the government needed to determine the killer’s identity, purpose, and reason for killing Qilangos.

The existence of an unknown High-Sequence Beyonder wandering around Backlund was serious enough to attract the attention of the government and the major Churches. Such beings possessed mysterious powers that made them potentially more dangerous than entire military forces.

Ace Snake decided to coordinate with the Church of the Evernight Goddess, the Church of Steam and Machinery, and the Machinery Hivemind. Aguesid promised that the military and intelligence services would also cooperate.

...

In a hotel in Backlund’s North Borough, Azik stared at a strange glove.

It was extremely thin and resembled human skin, appearing almost as though it could become a hand if supplied with flesh and blood.

Azik stared at it for a long time before his expression twisted with sudden pain.

“I seem to... seem to have cooperated with them before...”

The fragment of memory left him visibly distressed.

...

Klein, meanwhile, spent an uneasy night.

He received no reports from Audrey or Alger and still had no response from Azik. He began wondering whether Azik had become involved in the confrontation, whether something had gone wrong, or whether Qilangos had somehow harmed him.

On his way to Zouteland Street the next morning, Klein heard a newspaper boy shouting about a major headline:

“Extra! Extra! Rear Admiral Hurricane Qilangos killed in Backlund!”

Klein immediately bought the Tingen Morning Post.

The headline stated:

*Pirate Qilangos shot dead by Duke’s bodyguard in Backlund.*

Klein stared at the report.

Qilangos was dead.

His immediate thought was that perhaps Azik had been responsible.

Then he could only bitterly laugh at the absurdity of his situation.

As the supposed mastermind behind the scenes, he had learned what happened by reading it in the newspaper.

Key Events

  • Audrey concludes that Mr. Fool’s mysterious adorer was almost certainly responsible for killing Qilangos.
  • Count Hall warns Audrey to stay away from the dangerous Beyonder forces involved but reveals that she could receive up to tens of thousands of pounds in bounty rewards and Duke Negan’s Desi Bay estate.
  • Audrey deliberately avoids publicly claiming the bounty so that her identity as Justice remains secret.
  • She decides that collecting more of Roselle’s diary pages is a better way to repay Mr. Fool than attempting a conspicuous monetary transfer.
  • Duke Negan, Ace Snake, and Prime Minister Aguesid determine that an unknown High-Sequence Beyonder or powerful Sealed Artifact killed Qilangos and begin coordinating a wider investigation.
  • Ace Snake plans cooperation with the major Churches and Machinery Hivemind, while the government prepares military and intelligence assistance.
  • Azik examines a strange skin-like glove and suddenly recalls that he may have cooperated with the people connected to it in the past.
  • Klein spends the night anxiously waiting for information from Audrey, Alger, and Azik.
  • The next morning, Klein learns from a newspaper that Qilangos has been killed and realizes he has discovered the outcome after the fact.
