Chapter 161

1,138 words · 7 min read

Most of Morse Town’s buildings followed the style popular a century earlier, with the black spire of its cathedral standing out above everything else.

After a quick lunch of bread, toast, bacon, butter, and coffee, Klein, Leonard, and Kenley discussed how long they could continue carrying Sealed Artifact 3-0782. They estimated that they had another two hours and thirty-five minutes before they needed to recover from its purifying influence.

Kenley suggested dealing with the three paranormal incidents first, then returning to the church to take turns watching over the artifact while recovering.

Their first case was the elderly Noah, who lived alone and had repeatedly heard heavy footsteps in his house.

Leonard explained that he had chosen Noah’s case because it was closest to the church. He had learned this during lunch after casually speaking with a trainee priest.

*He really is experienced at handling missions,* Klein thought.

They reached Noah’s home almost immediately. The old man, who had lost his left hand during a war and later left the army, eagerly welcomed them. He had barely slept for two nights and was relieved that the Church had finally sent help.

Once inside, Leonard quickly surveyed the room. Klein soon sensed a cold, sinister aura lingering in the house.

“There really was an impure being here,” Kenley whispered.

“Very weak,” Leonard replied.

Klein could also feel 3-0782 rapidly purifying the remaining traces.

The townspeople had gathered outside, eagerly watching the Nighthawks. Leonard cleared his throat and proclaimed that, under the Goddess’s protection, the impure beings would soon disappear. Then he quietly instructed Klein to perform the purification ritual.

*Why me?*

Klein had little choice. He took the Dark Sacred Emblem, holy water, silver dagger, and garlic. He placed the emblem before his chest, peeled the garlic, and scattered the cloves throughout the house.

The townspeople became fascinated by the spectacle.

“Is that really how garlic dispels ghosts?”

“It’s different from what the newspapers say.”

Klein almost felt like a circus performer. *It’s useless. I’m only acting!*

He closed his eyes, splashed holy water around with the silver dagger, and recited a solemn invocation to the Evernight Goddess:

“The Mother of Secrets... The Lady of Crimson... Empress of Disaster and Horror... Mistress of Calm and Silence...”

The audience fell silent. Then they began noticing something else.

“It feels warm.”

“Like sunlight.”

“No, like looking at a pure sky.”

The purifying power of 3-0782 had spread through the entire house, filling the space with a serene sensation. The townspeople were astonished and quickly became convinced that the visiting Nighthawks were highly capable.

*3-0782 is doing all the actual work,* Klein thought. *We only need to stand around long enough for it to finish.*

Once every trace of the sinister aura had vanished, Klein put away his dagger and drew the crimson moon before his chest.

“Praise the Lady!”

“Praise the Lady!” the townspeople echoed reverently.

Leonard then informed them that the Nighthawks still needed absolute silence to finish their work. The crowd obediently withdrew, followed by Priest Siur. Even Noah was persuaded to leave temporarily, despite complaining that he had wanted to take a nap.

Once the house was empty, Leonard turned to Klein.

“Do a divination on the cause of this incident.”

Klein readily agreed. He already knew Azik was responsible, but suspected that any attempt to divine the truth about a being who had lived for more than a thousand years would be heavily distorted.

He wrote:

“The cause of the haunting at Noah’s house.”

Sitting at a round table, he entered a dreamlike state.

A black mausoleum appeared before him.

It resembled an inverted pyramid, buried almost entirely beneath the ground, with dense black mist filling the ancient structure.

Klein woke abruptly.

When Kenley asked whether he had discovered anything, Klein described the vision and added that the mausoleum was not characteristic of the Northern Continent or the Fifth Epoch.

Leonard immediately recognized it.

“That’s an Inverted Pyramid from the Southern Continent. It represents entering the nether realm from the world of the living. Such mausoleums were erected by the so-called Descendants of Death in the old Balam Empire and its surrounding states.”

He called it a symbol of Death.

Since ghosts were naturally associated with Death, Leonard concluded that Klein’s divination had clearly pointed toward the correct source.

But the result sparked a different thought in Klein.

*Could Azik be a descendant of Death? Or did he once make some kind of transaction with Death to obtain his extraordinary lifespan?*

According to the Nighthawks’ records and the Revelation of Evernight, Death had been a malevolent god who caused catastrophe in the Northern Continent near the end of the Fourth Epoch, the period remembered as the Pale Era. Death had supposedly fallen through the combined efforts of the Seven Gods.

Lamud Castle could not have existed before that era, making the possibility of a connection especially intriguing. Perhaps it could even relate to the mysterious culprit behind the red-chimney house and the theft of Azik’s child’s skull.

Of course, Klein also considered the possibility that the association between Death and the Southern Continent had been distorted by the Northern Continent’s colonial interests.

The Nighthawks soon moved on.

At the other two locations, they repeated essentially the same process. 3-0782 dispelled the traces of dead spirits, but the underlying cause remained undiscovered.

Leonard questioned the townspeople about any strangers who had recently visited Morse Town. Nobody recalled anyone suspicious.

*Azik must have entered and left without anyone noticing. He’s extremely cautious.*

Klein also wondered whether Azik had already predicted that the spirits would disappear naturally by Wednesday, regardless of whether the Nighthawks arrived.

When the three returned to the cathedral, they still had about an hour and forty-five minutes before they needed to fully recover from 3-0782.

“We’ll take one-hour shifts,” Klein suggested, carefully hiding his excitement. “Let’s try to return to Tingen in time for dinner.”

Leonard agreed, but suggested that they maintain two people around the artifact while one rested.

Klein froze.

He immediately launched into an elaborate mathematical explanation about determining the optimal rotation schedule, calculating recovery rates, comparing efficiencies, and establishing an algorithm with an unknown variable.

Leonard’s expression slowly collapsed into pure alarm.

“Wait! In that case, let’s just watch it one at a time!”

He proposed that whoever was on duty remain inside the cathedral, while the other two stood outside and prevented anyone from approaching. Priest Siur and the townspeople would have to stay elsewhere during the process.

Kenley immediately agreed.

Klein reluctantly nodded.

His real objective had been to find a way to make his companions leave him alone with 3-0782. If they had refused, he would have needed to secretly bargain with Leonard and reveal some of his own information.

Fortunately, Leonard had surrendered first.

The problem was solved.

Key Events

  • The Nighthawks begin investigating the paranormal incidents in Morse Town while carrying Sealed Artifact 3-0782.
  • Klein uses a staged purification ritual while 3-0782 actually eliminates the lingering spiritual traces.
  • Klein’s divination reveals an inverted black mausoleum, which Leonard identifies as a symbol associated with Death from the Southern Continent.
  • Klein realizes the vision may provide clues concerning Azik, the red-chimney house, and the stolen skull.
  • The team clears all three haunting sites but discovers no direct culprit or suspicious visitor.
  • Klein maneuvers the group into adopting one-person shifts while guarding 3-0782, creating the opportunity he needs to use the artifact for his own purposes.
