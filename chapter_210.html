Chapter 210

Chapter 210 - Story

1,163 words · 7 min read

In a suburban house in Tingen, beneath a crimson chimney and beside a garden beginning to wither in September, a slightly pale hand opened an ordinary notebook. The pages were filled with a story whose words described every coincidence, deviation, and hidden hand that had shaped Tingen’s fate.

The Antigonus family’s notebook had first been sold accidentally by Regence of the Secret Order, disguised as an ordinary ancient book because exhaustion and illusions had influenced his judgment. Its bloodline had then drawn the notebook from owner to owner until it reached Sirius Arapis and Hanass Vincent of the Aurora Order. They copied the potion formulas that temporarily appeared within it before selling it onward to avoid attracting the Secret Order’s diviners. Through a chain of coincidences, Hanass sold it to Welch McGovern.

Sirius became interested in the treasures of the Hornacis mountain range and, while researching them, met Lanevus, who was preparing a massive scam involving iron mines. Impressed by Lanevus’s hidden madness and talent for deception, Sirius considered recruiting him into the Aurora Order. He told Lanevus about a ritual that could bring the True Creator’s offspring into the world.

Lanevus was fascinated. Despite Sirius believing the ritual had almost no chance of success, Lanevus intended to perform it alongside his plan to establish a steelworks company. He secretly approached Hood Eugen, an old acquaintance, while being aware that Sirius himself was suspicious.

The Antigonus notebook was eventually awakened completely through a dark divination. Welch and his classmate died, while Klein Moretti—supposedly destined to die as well—survived through circumstances the notebook’s owner could not explain. Klein joined the Nighthawks after meeting Dunn Smith during the investigation.

Meanwhile, the desperate Bacchus brothers kidnapped Elliott, the son of tobacco merchant Vickroy. Their chosen hideout happened to be opposite Ray Bieber’s apartment. Ray, already half-insane from attempting to digest the power left by his ancestors, remained in Tingen and unknowingly became another thread in the story.

Vickroy’s butler sought help from a security company, eventually leading Klein and Leonard to the Blackthorn Security Company. They rescued Elliott, and Klein’s spirituality later guided the Nighthawks toward the clues hidden in Ray Bieber’s apartment.

The arrival of Sealed Artifact 2-049 helped Dunn and the Nighthawks locate Ray before his digestion was complete. Ray transformed into a monster, but the team defeated him, only to face an attack from a Secret Order member. Leonard intended to intervene, yet Klein inexplicably killed the Sequence 7 Beyonder first. Dunn then touched the Antigonus notebook and became secretly contaminated by it.

Lanevus continued preparing his ritual with Hood Eugen. Megose was deceived into becoming the vessel for the True Creator’s offspring. Yet the ritual would have killed her, so the secretly helpful Ince Zangwill intervened, planting half of a characteristic from a descendant of Death inside her beforehand. Hood Eugen placed Megose into a semi-conscious condition and convinced her that the deity’s illusory projection was Lanevus.

The ritual succeeded.

The resentment, oppression, and gloom of the factory district nourished the pregnancy. The True Creator recognized the hidden coincidences but allowed the ritual to proceed because “He” wished to break free of the constraints imposed by the seven deities. Hood Eugen became contaminated.

Once Lanevus regained his sanity, he understood the terrible truth: when the deity’s child descended, he himself would have to become a sacrifice. A mortal fathering a deity’s child was the ultimate blasphemy. He fled Tingen and deliberately left clues that would allow the official Beyonder organizations to clean up the disaster. He avoided directly sending them a warning because he considered that too foolish, instead hiding his letter among his rented houses. He also informed Sirius, who only barely believed him.

Other events followed. Selena Wood accidentally found a genuine magic mirror divination ritual and nearly caused a catastrophe before Klein intervened. During the investigation of Hanass Vincent, Dunn glimpsed the True Creator in a dream. Though the incident did not directly contaminate him, it worsened the influence of the Antigonus notebook, increasing his forgetfulness and instability.

Klein eventually noticed Ince Zangwill’s hidden influence and discovered the mystery surrounding the crimson chimney. His investigation repeatedly veered away from the actual house through apparent coincidence. He also met Sirius at the library and was forced to kill him, severing the trail toward Lanevus and delaying the discovery of the true danger.

Then Dunn’s condition unexpectedly improved.

Klein had taught him the acting method after drawing inspiration from Daly Simone and Old Neil. This altered Ince Zangwill’s plans. At the same time, Azik departed for Backlund to recover his lost memories, while Klein and Dunn found new clues pointing toward Hood Eugen.

Madam Sharon later murdered Member of Parliament John Maynard. Her motives were not entirely rational, but her transformed state and desire to vent her madness pushed her into action. Maynard’s wife hired the Blackthorn Security Company, and the Nighthawks uncovered Sharon’s abnormality. Dunn, whose strength approached Sequence 6, led the operation with Klein and Kenley.

Their plan failed because Madam Sharon possessed the statue of the Primordial Demoness. Kenley accidentally reflected himself in a mirror and died, though Sharon was ultimately defeated. Dunn blamed himself and consumed Kenley’s Beyonder characteristic as he had done with his former comrades. His digestion was disrupted, worsening his mental state.

Then Leonard and Klein discovered Lanevus’s letter.

Megose was mysteriously summoned to Zouteland Street and arrived at the Blackthorn Security Company while the child inside her had reached a critical stage. Dunn developed a detailed plan, but he made one fatal mistake: he removed Saint Selena’s urn from Chanis Gate instead of waiting within its protective environment.

The deity’s child sensed the threat posed by the Saint’s ashes and began drawing strength from Megose in order to force an early birth.

Azik was in Backlund and could not return in time.

The final battle followed. Megose became a monster, and the Nighthawks fought her using Saint Selena’s ashes, the Blood Vessel Thief, and powerful charms. They succeeded in killing Megose and exorcising the deity’s offspring, but Dunn died, and the Saint’s ashes were badly depleted.

This was exactly what Ince Zangwill wanted.

He had never needed to appear directly. After the battle, he killed Klein—the man who had repeatedly interfered with his plans—and took Saint Selena’s ashes. Using the remaining half of the Death descendant characteristic, he performed a ritual, consumed the Saint’s ashes, and advanced from Sequence 5 Gatekeeper to Sequence 4 Nightwatcher, gaining godhood characteristics and becoming a Demigod.

Above Tingen, the sun continued shining brightly. Almost no ordinary citizen realized how close the city had come to destruction.

At last, the notebook reached its final page.

The man holding it was middle-aged, with dark blond hair, nearly black dark-blue eyes, a tall nose, tightly pressed lips, and a flawless face resembling a statue. Without dipping his quill into ink, he calmly wrote one final sentence:

“Tingen’s story ends here.”

He closed the notebook.

Only its plain brown cover remained.

Key Events

  • The Antigonus notebook traces and records the chain of coincidences behind Tingen’s major supernatural events.
  • Sirius introduces Lanevus to the True Creator ritual, which ultimately results in Megose becoming pregnant with the deity’s offspring.
  • Klein, Dunn, Leonard, and the Nighthawks repeatedly disrupt Ince Zangwill’s plans, though many apparent coincidences are secretly manipulated.
  • Dunn becomes increasingly contaminated by the Antigonus notebook before briefly improving after learning the acting method from Klein.
  • Lanevus abandons Tingen after realizing that his own ritual will ultimately demand his life as a sacrifice.
  • Megose’s transformation and the final battle destroy the deity’s offspring, kill Dunn, and severely weaken Saint Selena’s ashes.
  • Ince Zangwill kills Klein, takes the remaining Saint’s ashes, and successfully advances to Sequence 4, becoming the Demigod Nightwatcher.
  • The mysterious author of the notebook concludes: “Tingen’s story ends here.”
