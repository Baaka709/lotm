Chapter 295 - All Ingredients Gathered

1,172 words · 7 min read

Audrey stared at the enormous creature in front of her in complete confusion. In her mind, a Rainbow Salamander had always meant a palm-sized pituitary gland—soft, grooved, and constantly changing colors. What she had been given, however, was a three-meter-long monster.

When Susie yelped, Audrey quickly composed herself and pretended to be satisfied.

“This is exactly the animal specimen I need. Um... it’s just a little bigger than I thought. Just a little. Take the servants and move it to the warehouse. I’ll study it when I’m free.”

Once she and Susie reached the study, Audrey dismissed the maids and immediately began thinking about the real problem. After dissecting the creature, she would have two sets of Rainbow Salamander pituitary glands. One could be exchanged for Farsman Rabbit material, giving her enough ingredients to finally prepare the Telepathist potion.

But before she advanced Susie, Audrey suddenly realized something important.

She had no idea whether Susie had fully digested her Spectator potion.

“If it hasn’t been completely digested, consuming the Telepathist potion would easily lead to a loss of control,” Audrey thought anxiously. “She isn’t like a human who can endure through it. Wait... how did she endure it the first time?”

She turned toward the enormous golden retriever sitting beside her.

“Susie, have you completely digested the potion?”

Susie tilted her head.

“Digest?”

Audrey carefully explained the strange sensation that accompanied potion digestion: an illusory feeling within the body breaking down and merging with the mind, accompanied by the appearance of countless imaginary stars that attracted one another.

Susie listened, then cheerfully replied that she had experienced exactly that.

Audrey was stunned.

“When did you digest it?”

“Last month, the month before last, or perhaps even earlier...” Susie struggled to remember. Seeing Audrey’s increasingly strange expression, she quickly wagged her tail. “I don’t remember... I’m just a dog. I won’t purposely remember such things. Woof.”

Audrey maintained her elegant smile and praised her for doing well.

...

After returning from Leppard’s residence, Klein was taking a leisurely afternoon nap when strange cries suddenly woke him.

He immediately wondered whether The Hanged Man or The Sun had obtained one of the final ingredients for his potion. He quickly performed his usual four-step ritual and arrived above the gray fog.

The crimson star belonging to The Hanged Man was pulsing. Klein connected to it and heard Alger’s prayer.

He had already obtained the Dark Patterned Black Panther Spinal Fluid and the Spring of the Elves Marrow Crystals and wanted permission to perform a sacrificial ritual so that Mr. Fool could pass them to Mr. World.

“That’s pretty fast...” Klein thought. Alger had previously hinted that a major pirate event was coming soon, but apparently “soon” meant now. Klein permitted the ritual.

The two ingredients appeared on the ancient bronze table.

The Dark Patterned Black Panther Spinal Fluid was a translucent liquid whose clarity increased in distinct layers toward the bottom, almost perfectly arranged for someone with obsessive tendencies.

The Spring of the Elves Marrow Crystals resembled a faded egg with an incredibly thin shell. Even without shaking it, Klein could hear liquid sloshing inside.

He expected to obtain three hundred pounds and perhaps a clue toward another potion formula in return. His own Magician advancement was now missing only the true root and juice of the Mist Treant.

The Sun did not keep him waiting long.

On Wednesday evening, Derrick prayed to report that he had obtained the Mist Treant’s true root and juice and asked Klein to pass them to Mr. World.

The true root was heart-shaped, brown, wrinkled, and about the size of a palm. It resembled an old man’s skin on one side but was smooth and gemlike on the other. It even seemed to expand and contract slightly as though it were alive.

The juice was light green and sparkling.

Klein looked at the ingredients with satisfaction.

Sequence 7 was the threshold of a Mid-Sequence Beyonder. Reaching it meant gaining a substantial amount of supernatural power instead of merely possessing abilities that made one somewhat stronger than an ordinary person.

He returned to his bedroom and summoned himself so that he could bring the ingredients into the real world.

He did not bother preparing elaborate equipment. He simply washed an iron pot in the kitchen and began concocting the potion, following the proper order for the supplementary and main ingredients.

His Clown-enhanced physical control made the process easy. He soon added the Dark Patterned Black Panther Spinal Fluid and Mist Treant root.

Sizzle!

Pale white fog suddenly surged upward before being forcibly drawn back into the pot.

Once the reaction settled, Klein poured the entire mixture into a transparent bottle.

Inside, the liquid seemed filled with miniature fireworks. Red, orange, yellow, green, and other colors constantly appeared, vanished, and reappeared.

The Magician potion.

Before drinking it, Klein used a one-pound coin to perform a simple divination.

The coin landed with its portrait facing upward.

Positive.

The potion had been successfully concocted.

Klein sat down in the darkened living room. He entered Cogitation to calm himself completely, suppressing his emotions before the advancement.

Then he raised the bottle and drank.

Gulp after gulp, the icy potion passed through his throat.

Immediately, countless bubbles seemed to burst throughout his body while enormous amounts of information flooded his mind like fireworks. His forehead veins bulged, and his head felt as though it might split apart.

Yet Klein had endured far worse.

The mad ravings he encountered before entering the gray fog, and the terrifying roars of the True Creator, had been more frightening.

Then the familiar whispers returned.

“Hornacis... Flegrea... Hornacis... Flegrea...”

Klein’s mind seemed to expand and contract under their influence, but he steadily restrained his thoughts, visualized the spherical light, and entered Cogitation.

Eventually, his consciousness stabilized.

Then he noticed something strange.

His entire body itched, especially his arms.

He rolled up his sleeves.

One arm was deeply wrinkled, resembling that of a centenarian. The other had become strangely translucent, allowing him to see his own blood vessels, muscles, and veins.

Klein immediately became wary.

Had he nearly lost control?

No. This should be the potion’s remaining negative influence rather than true loss of control.

He remained seated in the darkness, watching his abnormal arms slowly return to normal. After five or six minutes, the wrinkles disappeared, the translucency faded, and his body completely recovered.

Klein quietly sighed in relief.

He had only attempted the advancement after fully digesting his Sequence 8 potion, yet the negative effects had still been this severe. For Beyonders who advanced simply by slowly enduring their potion over time, the process must be extraordinarily difficult.

No wonder Dunn had needed nine years.

No wonder Swain, the former Captain of a Mandated Punisher Squad and owner of the Evil Dragon Bar, had never dared to consume the Sequence 7 Seafarer potion.

After sitting silently for several more seconds, Klein finally stood.

He had crossed the threshold.

He was now a Mid-Sequence Beyonder.

He was now a Magician.
