Chapter 205 - Urgent Arrangement

1,117 words · 7 min read

Klein instinctively reached into his pockets, gripping Azik’s copper whistle in one hand and the Flaring Sun Charm in the other. The gentle Beyonder feedback from the whistle had inexplicably vanished, as though something invisible were suppressing it, while the charm remained warm and reassuring. Using that comfort to steady himself, Klein entered a half-Cogitation state and pushed his anxiety aside. He glanced at Leonard and subtly indicated Megose before turning toward her with a perfectly controlled smile.

“Do you want coffee, black tea, or nothing at all?”

Megose stroked her pregnant stomach as though listening to something inside. “A cup of warm water. I suddenly thought of chatting with you guys about Lanevus. I have the feeling that you know a lot.”

“Who told you that?” Leonard asked, his usual carefree attitude gone.

Megose giggled. “My child told me. He knows a lot. He’s very smart!”

Klein suppressed the urge to curse and signaled Leonard to keep her calm. Leonard forced a smile and invited her to sit while saying that they also wanted to discuss Lanevus.

Klein immediately rushed into Dunn Smith’s office and shut the door behind him. Dunn, startled, quickly became serious when Klein said, “Captain, something serious has happened. I know what Lanevus meant by bomb!”

Dunn guessed the subject was Megose. Klein explained that when he had attempted to activate Spirit Vision and examine her mental state, his own spirituality had stopped him and repeatedly warned that looking would result in death. That made him recall the saying, “You may not look directly at God.” Whatever was inside Megose, it was certainly beyond the level of an ordinary Beyonder—possibly a legendary creature, or worse.

Connecting Megose to Hood Eugen’s memories, the black altar, his Psychiatrist abilities, and Lanevus’s descriptions of the misery surrounding the factories, Klein laid out his theory. Lanevus had likely obtained a True Creator-related ritual from an Aurora Order member. With Hood Eugen’s help, he had transformed Megose into a vessel to gestate some terrible power. The resentment, oppression, and gloom surrounding the factories were not merely circumstances but fuel for the ritual, allowing the power to rapidly mature.

Dunn silently considered the explanation before reaching his decision.

“I’ll ask for assistance from the Holy Cathedral immediately. Let’s hope that the baby in Megose’s stomach can still wait!”

He then ordered Klein to have Leonard keep Megose calm and entertained while Mrs. Orianna, Rozanne, and the other non-combatants were evacuated. Dunn would send a telegram for help, then head behind Chanis Gate to prepare for the worst. As Captain of the Tingen Nighthawks, he had emergency authority to use Saint Selena’s ashes.

The mention of a High-Sequence Beyonder’s ashes eased Klein’s fear somewhat. He immediately thought of another possibility: the Mandated Punishers and Machinery Hivemind should possess powerful holy items as well. Lanevus’s case had originally fallen under the Mandated Punishers, and Klein suddenly remembered the senior member who had lost control during the investigation.

“Captain, can you ask the Mandated Punishers if the member who lost control was tracking or keeping Megose under surveillance?”

Dunn understood the implication: perhaps that Beyonder had been corrupted by whatever was growing inside Megose. He agreed to investigate while simultaneously contacting the Holy Cathedral, the Mandated Punishers, the Machinery Hivemind, and the police, hoping the authorities could create an excuse to evacuate the surrounding civilians.

Before leaving, Klein remembered another disturbing pattern. The strange coincidences surrounding his investigations had always been subtle—the Antigonus notebook, Ray Bieber failing to leave Tingen, Hanass Vincent exposing himself, and an Aurora Order member dying simply because he happened to encounter Klein. But Megose arriving immediately after they discovered Lanevus’s letter was different. The coincidence was too blatant.

“I think that the person behind this will soon take center stage!” Klein warned. He also wondered whether Madam Sharon’s dangerous decision to assassinate Member of Parliament Maynard was another such coincidence.

Dunn solemnly agreed to include the point in his telegram.

Klein hurried to evacuate the clerks. Mrs. Orianna was still preparing the budget when he told her they were taking the day off and that she should go home immediately. She quickly obeyed, and before leaving, traced a crimson moon over her chest and wished them the Goddess’s blessing. Klein then warned Rozanne to leave as well. She initially froze, but his stern expression convinced her. As she departed, she quietly admitted that she respected the Nighthawks despite hating people who became Beyonders.

With the mundane staff gone, Klein brought Megose warm water and told her he needed to settle something. Leaning close to Leonard, he whispered, “Keep her here.” Leonard continued chatting with Megose, though he noticed she had become increasingly restless and distracted.

When Klein returned to Dunn’s office, the Captain had already gone underground. A telegram lay waiting.

“Yes. We will be there immediately.”

The Mandated Punisher had confirmed it: their member really had lost control because of Megose. Klein’s anxiety intensified. He paced the corridor, uncertain whether he was waiting for Dunn to retrieve Saint Selena’s ashes or for reinforcements to arrive.

Then the gas lamps began to glow with a faint blue hue.

Dunn emerged from the underground passage carrying a small, square box. It looked like silver, yet also like polished human bone, and was covered in mysterious carvings. The closer Klein drew, the colder he felt, as though the chill were seeping directly into his blood. Dunn explained that Saint Selena’s ashes were inside and ordered Klein to retrieve the most offensively powerful Sealed Artifact available behind Chanis Gate. Since Seeka and the Keepers could no longer leave their posts while the ashes were removed, Klein would have to make the choice himself, among the dangerous Grade 2 artifacts stored there.

Klein immediately realized that most of the Grade 3 artifacts would probably be ineffective against the terrifying entity inside Megose. Even the Mutated Sun Sacred Emblem took too long to exert its effect. The three Grade 2 Sealed Artifacts were extraordinarily dangerous, but Klein estimated their strength to be comparable to his Flaring Sun Charm.

He touched the charm and the copper whistle in his pockets. To his surprise, the whistle’s familiar sensation had returned.

At the intersection, Klein stopped and quickly wrote a message for Azik. The person who had disrupted his fate and stolen Azik’s child’s skull had finally appeared. He had arranged for Megose to enter the Blackthorn Security Company at 36 Zouteland Street, and there was a high possibility that she was carrying the son of an evil god.

“The situation is very urgent.”

He folded the message, took out the copper whistle, and blew into it.

A gigantic skeletal messenger appeared before him.

Key Events

  • Klein realizes Megose contains something extraordinarily dangerous, possibly a legendary creature or the offspring of an evil god.
  • Klein concludes Lanevus used a True Creator-related ritual to turn Megose into a vessel whose power feeds on the misery surrounding the factories.
  • Dunn urgently contacts the Holy Cathedral, Mandated Punishers, Machinery Hivemind, and police while ordering the Nighthawks’ non-combatants to evacuate.
  • The Mandated Punishers confirm that a member lost control because of Megose, strongly supporting Klein’s theory of corruption.
  • Dunn retrieves Saint Selena’s ashes, while Klein prepares to obtain a powerful Grade 2 Sealed Artifact from Chanis Gate.
  • Klein realizes Megose’s sudden arrival may be another deliberate “coincidence” connected to the hidden mastermind behind the recent events.
  • With Azik’s copper whistle restored, Klein summons the giant skeletal messenger and sends Azik an urgent warning about Megose and the imminent danger.
